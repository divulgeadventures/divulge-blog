## 
This Nelion and Batian climb is an 8-day technical expedition on Mount Kenya. You trek up the Sirimon route, acclimatise at Shipton’s Camp and on Point Lenana (4,985 m), then base at Austrian Hut for two climbing days on Nelion (5,188 m) and, conditions permitting, across the Gate of the Mists to Batian (5,199 m). You descend on the Chogoria side. 
It suits experienced alpine rock climbers who can climb around UIAA grade IV and abseil unassisted. 

## Why choose the Nelion and Batian climb? 
Nelion’s Normal Route on the south-east face is the best-known technical line on Mount Kenya: about 400 m of roped climbing and scrambling, with a crux of around UIAA IV-. From the top of Nelion, strong teams can cross the Gate of the Mists, a notch between the two summits, to reach Batian. 
Two climbing days are built in, so you have a spare day for weather or to attempt Batian after Nelion. 

## What to expect on the Nelion and Batian climb 
Days 1 to 3 cover the Sirimon trek to Shipton’s Camp, with an acclimatisation day there. On day 4 you climb Point Lenana and move to Austrian Hut. Days 5 and 6 are climbing days on Nelion and, if conditions allow, Batian, returning to Austrian Hut each night. Days 7 and 8 take you down past Lake Ellis to the Chogoria side and back by road. 

### When is the best time to climb Nelion? 
December to March is the classic season for the south-east face and Nelion, with drier, sunnier days. The Gate of the Mists can hold snow and ice, especially around December, and may need a night in the small Howell Hut on Nelion’s summit.

Day 1 : 
Nairobi to Old Moses Camp 

Drive to Nanyuki and Sirimon Gate, then walk about 3 to 4 hours through forest to Old Moses Camp at about 3,300 m. 

Day 2 : 
Old Moses Camp to Shipton's Camp 

Trek across the moorland and up Mackinder's Valley to Shipton's Camp at about 4,200 m. 

Day 3 : 
Acclimatisation day at Shipton's Camp 

Spend the day acclimatising with short hikes towards the Hausberg area, returning to Shipton's Camp for a second night. 

Day 4 : 
Point Lenana and on to Austrian Hut 

Climb Point Lenana (4,985 m) early for sunrise, then settle into Austrian Hut below Nelion. Your climbing guide joins you here for a route briefing. 

Day 5 : 
Climbing day: Nelion 

Start before dawn on the Normal Route up the south-east face of Nelion. After the summit, abseil back down and return to Austrian Hut. 

Day 6 : 
Second climbing day: Batian or reserve day 

Use this day for the Gate of the Mists traverse to Batian if conditions allow, or as a reserve day if weather stopped the first attempt. 

Day 7 : 
Austrian Hut to the Chogoria side 

Descend past the Gorges Valley and Lake Michaelson to the Chogoria side, camping near Lake Ellis or the lower moorland. 

Day 8 : 
Chogoria to Nairobi 

Walk down to the roadhead, then transfer by 4x4 to Chogoria town and on to Nairobi.

How technical is the Nelion and Batian climb? 

The Nelion and Batian climb is a serious alpine rock route. Nelion’s Normal Route has about 400 m of roped climbing and scrambling with a crux around UIAA IV-, followed by abseils on the descent. The traverse to Batian is harder and often icy. 

When is the best time to climb Nelion and Batian? 

The best time to climb Nelion and Batian is December to March, when the south-east face is usually dry and sunny. From late June to early October the North Face is the better choice. 

What equipment should I bring? 

Bring a harness, helmet, stiff boots that take crampons, rock shoes, a belay and abseil device and personal slings. Crampons and an ice axe are useful for the Gate of the Mists. Your guide carries group ropes and protection. 

Where do we sleep during the climb? 

You camp at Old Moses and Shipton’s Camp on the way up, then stay three nights at Austrian Hut below Nelion. On the way down you camp near Lake Ellis or the Chogoria side. 

Can a climbing club book this trip? 

Yes. We plan climbs for clubs and groups with a maximum of two climbers per climbing guide, plus trekking guides and porters. Send us your numbers and preferred dates.
