## Overview 
This short escape is luxury gorilla trekking Rwanda style: a comfortable private transfer from Kigali, a night at an elegant lodge overlooking the Virunga volcanoes and a once-in-a-lifetime hour with a mountain gorilla family. After a leisurely lunch in Kigali, you drive north to your lodge to unwind with fireplaces, fine food and attentive service. The next morning, you step into Volcanoes National Park for your trek, returning in the afternoon for an evening flight. It is designed for travellers who want Rwanda’s greatest wildlife encounter with comfort, privacy and minimal time away.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is how your 2-day luxury gorilla trekking Rwanda safari unfolds. 

Day 01 : 
Kigali - Musanze (Volcanoes National Park) 

Upon arrival at Kigali International Airport, you will be met by your Divulge Adventures driver guide for meet, greet and gorilla trekking Rwanda safari briefing. Enjoy lunch at a popular Kigali restaurant before departing for Musanze, approximately 2.5 hours away, with stops to admire the terraced hills and the first views of the Virunga volcanoes. 
Check in at your luxury lodge and spend the afternoon relaxing, perhaps with a massage or a drink on your private terrace as the sun sets over the volcanoes. Enjoy a gourmet dinner before an early night. Dinner and overnight at Bisate Lodge | Virunga Lodge 

Day 02 : 
Gorilla Trekking - Kigali 

After an early breakfast at 6:45 AM, drive to the Kinigi park headquarters for park formalities and your gorilla family assignment. Your trek through bamboo and montane forest can take 1 to 6 hours, and once you find the gorillas you will spend one unforgettable hour in their company. 
After the trek, return to the lodge to freshen up, then drive back to Kigali, approximately 3 hours, with an optional stop for souvenir shopping. You will be transferred to Kigali International Airport for your evening flight, which we recommend for this itinerary. This marks the end of your luxury gorilla trekking Rwanda safari by creating unforgetable memories.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

What are the best luxury lodges for gorilla trekking in Rwanda? 

Top choices near Volcanoes National Park include Bisate Lodge, Virunga Lodge, Singita Kwitonda Lodge and One&Only Gorilla’s Nest. All offer volcano views, fine dining and easy access to the park headquarters. 

Is the gorilla permit included in this luxury safari? 

Yes. One gorilla trekking permit per person (USD 1,500) is included and booked by Divulge Adventures in advance. 

Can I fly out of Kigali on the same day as my gorilla trek? 

Yes, an evening flight works well. Treks usually finish by early afternoon and the drive back to Kigali takes about 3 hours. 

Do luxury lodges help with gorilla trekking? 

Most luxury lodges provide packed snacks, walking sticks, gaiters and boot cleaning, and some offer a massage after your trek.
