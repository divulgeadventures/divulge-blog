## 
This 5 day Namibia celebration safari is for groups marking a milestone together. It packs two of Namibia’s highlights into a short trip: game drives at Etosha’s waterholes and the red dunes of Sossusvlei, with a stop at the AfriCat Foundation and a group sundowner on Elim Dune. 

## Why choose a 5 day Namibia celebration safari? 
Namibia’s scenery makes every photo special. The sundowner on Elim Dune and the sunrise climb of Dune 45 give the group two shared moments, and Deadvlei’s white clay pan and dead trees make a striking backdrop for celebration photos. 
We can arrange a celebration dinner, a cake or decorations at the lodge, and add extra nights for guests who want to stay longer. 

## What does the celebration itinerary include? 
Two nights around Etosha, one night in Windhoek after the AfriCat visit, and one night near Sossusvlei, with the drive back to Windhoek on day 5. 

### Can the trip be longer? 
Yes. Many groups add Swakopmund on the coast for dune activities and a dinner by the sea, or extra nights in Etosha. Tell us how many days you have. 
Read our Namibia safari guide and best time to visit Namibia . 

## Who is the 5 day namibia celebration safari best for? 
The 5 day namibia celebration safari is best for milestone birthdays, anniversaries, reunions, retirements and wedding parties. Tell us the occasion and we will plan the special moments with the lodges, so the 5 day namibia celebration safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## How many guests can join the 5 day Namibia celebration safari? 
We plan vehicles and lodges around your numbers. Larger groups travel in several vehicles or a larger safari truck and can split between lodges of the same standard.

Day 1 : 
Windhoek to Etosha 

Your 5 day namibia celebration safari begins today. Meet our representative in Windhoek or at the airport and drive north to southern Etosha. After check-in and lunch, head out on an afternoon game drive. 

Day 2 : 
Etosha National Park 

A full day of game drives around Etosha's waterholes, where elephants, rhinos, lions and many birds come to drink. 

Day 3 : 
Etosha to Windhoek via AfriCat 

Drive south to Okonjima Nature Reserve and visit the AfriCat Foundation to learn about Namibia's carnivores and meet the ambassador cheetahs. Continue to Windhoek for a free evening. 

Day 4 : 
Windhoek to Sossusvlei 

Drive south into the Namib Desert. In the evening, the group climbs Elim Dune for a sundowner over the desert. 

Day 5 : 
Sossusvlei to Windhoek 

Climb Dune 45 at sunrise, then explore Sossusvlei and the white clay pan of Deadvlei. Visit Sesriem Canyon before driving back to Windhoek. This is the end of your 5 day namibia celebration safari.

What occasions suit the 5 day Namibia celebration safari? 

The 5 day Namibia celebration safari suits milestone birthdays, anniversaries, reunions, retirements and wedding parties. We plan a sundowner and a celebration dinner around your occasion. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 5 day namibia celebration safari. 

How much driving is there? 

Namibia is large, so there are some long drives, such as Etosha to Windhoek and Windhoek to Sossusvlei. The roads are good and we stop along the way. Light-aircraft flights can replace some drives. 

Can guests pay separately? 

Yes. Each guest or couple can pay their own share. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. 

Can we add a balloon flight over the dunes? 

Yes. A sunrise balloon flight over the Namib Desert can be added near Sossusvlei at an extra cost. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 5 day namibia celebration safari. 

When is the best time for a celebration safari in Namibia? 

May to October is dry, with clear skies and animals at the waterholes. The desert is very hot from December to February. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 5 day namibia celebration safari.
