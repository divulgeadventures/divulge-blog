## Overview 
This eight-day Tanzania Luxury Safari offers a comprehensive balance of premier wildlife viewing and deep cultural immersion across the country’s top northern circuit destinations. Starting from Kilimanjaro, the journey ventures through the massive elephant herds of Tarangire National Park and into the legendary plains of the Serengeti for two full days of tracking the Great Migration. 
Travelers will then experience unparalleled biodiversity inside the dramatic Ngorongoro Crater, home to Africa’s Big Five and the rare black rhino, before connecting with the ancestral lifestyle of the Hadzabe hunter-gatherer tribe at Lake Eyasi. Concluding with a stay in Arusha, this carefully paced itinerary blends thrilling game drives, stunning UNESCO World Heritage landscapes, and rich cultural interactions into an unforgettable East African adventure.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
Arrival in Tanzania 

Upon your arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport, your will be received by Divulge representative driver who will meet you with a big welcome! Ready to start your Tanzania Luxury Safari. From there, you’ll be picked up from the Airport to your hotel, where you can unwind and immerse yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of Africa.You will then head out to explore the cit. Next day marks the beginning of your safari adventure! Diner and overnight at Raha wildlife resort 

Day 02 : 
Tarangire National Park 

Your Tanzania Luxury Safari begins with a visit to Tarangire National Park, famous for its massive elephant herds and ancient baobab trees. As you explore, you’ll see elephants using the baobab trunks as water sources and witness wildlife flocking to the Tarangire River, especially during the dry season. Be prepared to see large gatherings of elephants, zebras, wildebeest, and more in this picturesque, rugged landscape. Dinner and overnight at Manyara Secret Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Serengeti National Park 

Around day time you will arrive at Serengeti National Park. Enjoy an exhilarating half day game drive as you drive to the park’s breathtaking landscapes. Known for its incredible wildlife spectacle, the Serengeti is known for its migration of over 1.5 million wildebeest and countless zebras. Witness the raw beauty of nature of the Tanzania Luxury Safari as these animals bravely cross crocodile-infested waters, giving birth to over 8,000 calves each day. Dinner and overnight at Seronera Wildlife Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Serengeti National Park 

Following another delicious African breakfast, dive deeper into Tanzania Luxury Safari where the wilderness with a full day of game driving along the Great Migration trails. This is mostly when you visit during migration time. Your knowledgeable driver-guide will share captivating insights into this extraordinary phenomenon, solidifying Tanzania’s reputation as Africa’s best safari destination. This UNESCO World Heritage site is alive with the sounds of over six million hooves echoing across the plains, as zebras and Thomson’s gazelles join the throng in search of fresh pastures.Dinner and overnight stay at Seronera Wildlife Lodge 

Day 05 : 
Serengeti to Ngorongoro Crater 

Wake up early for a sunrise game drive of the Tanzania Luxury Safari, catching the Serengeti’s wildlife at its most active time. Afterward, return to camp for brunch before heading to Ngorongoro Crater. This UNESCO World Heritage Site is home to nearly all of East Africa’s wildlife, including the elusive black rhino, you will be able to spot the rhino here. Spend the night near the crater, preparing for tomorrow’s descent. Dinner and overnight at Marera Mountain View Lodge 

Day 06 : 
Ngorongoro Crater 

Travel to the worldwide known Ngorongoro Crater, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Africa’s greatest wildlife havens. Descend into the crater, home to a rich variety of animals including lions, zebras, buffalo, and the rare black rhino. With its stunning scenery and dense concentration of wildlife, the Ngorongoro Crater offers an exceptional safari experience, you will have time to check the crater outside and inside with a safari vehicle Later afternoon drive to the Lodge. Diner and overnight at Kitela Lodge Karatu 

Day 07 : 
Lake Eyasi & The Hadzabe Tribe 

Rise early for an extraordinary day with the Hadzabe tribe, one of Africa’s last true hunter gatherer communities. Immerse yourself in their daily activities, whether it’s joining the men in hunting or the women in foraging. After this incredible cultural experience, visit the tranquil Lake Eyasi before returning to Arusha city. Dinner and overnight at Melia Arusha 

Day 08 : 
Departure 

After breakfast and some leisure time at the lodge, your guide will escort you to Kilimanjaro International Airport for your journey home. If you’re not ready to leave yet, consider extending your adventure with a trip to the pristine beaches of Zanzibar Islands for a relaxing island escape.
