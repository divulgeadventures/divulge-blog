## Overview 
This 8 day Serengeti Ngorongoro and Lake Eyasi safari is a 7-night northern Tanzania lodge safari from Kilimanjaro International Airport. You game drive in Tarangire, spend 2 nights at Seronera in the central Serengeti, tour the Ngorongoro Crater and meet the Hadzabe at Lake Eyasi, ending at Melia Arusha. The page gives no fixed season, and it costs from USD 3,588 per adult. 
It suits couples, first-time visitors and families who want the classic northern parks plus a cultural morning, travelling in a modified 4WD Land Cruiser with a photographic roof hatch, with a professional English-speaking guide and full board at the lodges. 

## Why choose an 8 day Serengeti Ngorongoro and Lake Eyasi safari? 
The 8 day Serengeti Ngorongoro and Lake Eyasi safari covers three very different wildlife areas and one of East Africa’s most memorable cultural visits. Tarangire is known for its massive elephant herds and ancient baobab trees along the Tarangire River. The Serengeti gets a half-day drive on arrival, a full day on the migration trails and a sunrise game drive. 
The Ngorongoro Crater is a UNESCO World Heritage Site with lions, zebras, buffalo and the rare black rhino in one volcanic bowl. The trip then spends a morning with the Hadzabe, one of Africa’s last hunter-gatherer communities, beside Lake Eyasi. 
- Wildlife lovers: Tarangire elephants, the Serengeti plains and the crater floor in one week. 
- Culture seekers: a morning joining the Hadzabe in hunting or foraging. 
- Comfort seekers: lodges on full board and a final night at Melia Arusha. 

## What to expect on the 8 day Serengeti Ngorongoro and Lake Eyasi safari 
Day 1 starts with a welcome at Kilimanjaro International Airport and a transfer to Raha Wildlife Resort, with time to explore the city. On day 2 you game drive in Tarangire National Park among baobabs, elephants, zebras and wildebeest, with the night at Manyara Secret Lodge. Day 3 is the drive to Serengeti National Park, with a half-day game drive on arrival and the night at Seronera Wildlife Lodge. 
Day 4 is a full day of game drives on the Serengeti migration trails, following the herds when they are in the area, with a second night at Seronera Wildlife Lodge. On day 5 you have a sunrise game drive and brunch, then drive towards the Ngorongoro Crater for the night at Marera Mountain View Lodge. 
Day 6 is the Ngorongoro Crater, with time on the rim and a descent to the crater floor by safari vehicle, then the night at Kitela Lodge in Karatu. On day 7 you rise early to join the Hadzabe, visit Lake Eyasi and return to Arusha for the night at Melia Arusha. Day 8 is your transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport, or the start of a Zanzibar beach extension. 

### Is 8 days enough for the Serengeti, Ngorongoro and Lake Eyasi? 
Eight days are enough for the Serengeti, Ngorongoro and Lake Eyasi if 2 Serengeti nights suit you, because Tarangire and the crater each get their own day and Lake Eyasi adds a cultural morning. Day 3 is the longest travel day. Compare places to stay in our Serengeti camps guide and Ngorongoro Crater lodges guide , and check the best time to visit Tanzania for your dates. Day 01 : 
Arrival in Tanzania 

Upon your arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport, your will be received by Divulge representative driver who will meet you with a big welcome! Ready to start your Tanzania Luxury Safari. 
From there, you’ll be picked up from the Airport to your hotel, where you can unwind and immerse yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of Africa.You will then head out to explore the cit. Next day marks the beginning of your safari adventure! Diner and overnight at Raha wildlife resort 

Day 02 : 
Tarangire National Park 

Your Tanzania Luxury Safari begins with a visit to Tarangire National Park, famous for its massive elephant herds and ancient baobab trees. As you explore, you’ll see elephants using the baobab trunks as water sources and witness wildlife flocking to the Tarangire River, especially during the dry season. 
Be prepared to see large gatherings of elephants, zebras, wildebeest, and more in this picturesque, rugged landscape. Dinner and overnight at Manyara Secret Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Serengeti National Park 

Around day time you will arrive at Serengeti National Park. Enjoy an exhilarating half day game drive as you drive to the park’s breathtaking landscapes. Known for its incredible wildlife spectacle, the Serengeti is known for its migration of over 1.5 million wildebeest and countless zebras. 
Witness the raw beauty of nature of the Tanzania Luxury Safari as these animals bravely cross crocodile-infested waters, giving birth to over 8,000 calves each day. Dinner and overnight at Seronera Wildlife Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Serengeti National Park 

Following another delicious African breakfast, dive deeper into Tanzania Luxury Safari where the wilderness with a full day of game driving along the Great Migration trails. This is mostly when you visit during migration time. Your knowledgeable driver-guide will share captivating insights into this extraordinary phenomenon, solidifying Tanzania’s reputation as Africa’s best safari destination. 
This UNESCO World Heritage site is alive with the sounds of over six million hooves echoing across the plains, as zebras and Thomson’s gazelles join the throng in search of fresh pastures.Dinner and overnight stay at Seronera Wildlife Lodge 

Day 05 : 
Serengeti to Ngorongoro Crater 

Wake up early for a sunrise game drive of the Tanzania Luxury Safari, catching the Serengeti’s wildlife at its most active time. Afterward, return to camp for brunch before heading to Ngorongoro Crater. 
This UNESCO World Heritage Site is home to nearly all of East Africa’s wildlife, including the elusive black rhino, you will be able to spot the rhino here. Spend the night near the crater, preparing for tomorrow’s descent. Dinner and overnight at Marera Mountain View Lodge 

Day 06 : 
Ngorongoro Crater 

Travel to the worldwide known Ngorongoro Crater, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Africa’s greatest wildlife havens. Descend into the crater, home to a rich variety of animals including lions, zebras, buffalo, and the rare black rhino. 
With its stunning scenery and dense concentration of wildlife, the Ngorongoro Crater offers an exceptional safari experience, you will have time to check the crater outside and inside with a safari vehicle Later afternoon drive to the Lodge. Diner and overnight at Kitela Lodge Karatu 

Day 07 : 
Lake Eyasi & The Hadzabe Tribe 

Rise early for an extraordinary day with the Hadzabe tribe, one of Africa’s last true hunter gatherer communities. Immerse yourself in their daily activities, whether it’s joining the men in hunting or the women in foraging. After this incredible cultural experience, visit the tranquil Lake Eyasi before returning to Arusha city. Dinner and overnight at Melia Arusha 

Day 08 : 
Departure 

After breakfast and some leisure time at the lodge, your guide will escort you to Kilimanjaro International Airport for your journey home. If you’re not ready to leave yet, consider extending your adventure with a trip to the pristine beaches of Zanzibar Islands for a relaxing island escape.
