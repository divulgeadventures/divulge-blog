## 
This Namibia honeymoon gives you the country’s most romantic settings in one week: starry desert nights at Sossus Dune Lodge right inside the Sesriem gate, two relaxed nights in the seaside town of Swakopmund at The Delight Swakopmund, and game drives in Etosha National Park from Mokuti Etosha Lodge. A private guide handles the driving so you can enjoy the views. It suits couples who want wide open spaces, photography and adventure rather than crowds. 
Price: from USD 2,470 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Sunrise on the Sossusvlei dunes from Sossus Dune Lodge inside the park 
- Two nights by the Atlantic in Swakopmund 
- Optional dune, kayak or dolphin activities at Walvis Bay 
- Game drives in Etosha National Park 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Windhoek to Sossusvlei 

Collection in Windhoek and drive to the Namib Desert. Sunset over the dunes. Dinner and overnight at Sossus Dune Lodge. 

Day 2 : 
Sossusvlei and Deadvlei 

Enter the park at first light for the dunes and Deadvlei, then visit Sesriem Canyon. Dinner and overnight at Sossus Dune Lodge. 

Day 3 : 
Sossusvlei to Swakopmund 

Drive through the desert via Solitaire and Walvis Bay to Swakopmund. Overnight at The Delight Swakopmund. 

Day 4 : 
Swakopmund 

Free day for optional activities such as a Walvis Bay dolphin cruise, kayaking or a living desert tour. Overnight at The Delight Swakopmund. 

Day 5 : 
Swakopmund to Etosha 

Drive north via Uis and Outjo to the eastern side of Etosha. Dinner and overnight at Mokuti Etosha Lodge. 

Day 6 : 
Etosha National Park 

Full day of game drives between Etosha's waterholes. Dinner and overnight at Mokuti Etosha Lodge. 

Day 7 : 
Etosha to Windhoek 

Drive back to Windhoek for your onward flight.

Is Namibia a good honeymoon destination? 

Yes. It is safe, uncrowded and very scenic, with some of the clearest night skies in the world. 

When is the best time for a Namibia honeymoon? 

April to October brings dry, sunny days and the best game viewing in Etosha.
