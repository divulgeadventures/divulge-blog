## Overview 
This 7 day Central Kalahari and Nxai Pan safari is a 7-day, 6-night camping expedition from Maun. You spend 3 nights in Nxai Pan National Park, with a visit to Baines Baobabs, and 3 nights in the Central Kalahari Game Reserve. The green season, roughly December to April, is the classic time for the Kalahari. It is priced from USD 3,600 per adult and suits adventurous travellers. 
Nights are spent at campsites under the desert sky, with all meals, park fees and activities included. 

## Why choose a 7 day Central Kalahari and Nxai Pan safari? 
The Central Kalahari Game Reserve is one of the largest protected areas in Africa, a world of grassy fossil riverbeds, pans and red sand where black-maned Kalahari lions, cheetahs, brown hyenas, gemsbok, springbok and meerkats live. Nxai Pan National Park, to the north, protects open grassland and salt pans, and the famous Baines Baobabs stand on the edge of Kudiakam Pan inside the park. 
The Kalahari is a very different Botswana from the Okavango Delta: wide horizons, few other vehicles and some of the clearest night skies on the continent. 
- Repeat Botswana visitors: desert-adapted wildlife you will not see in the delta. 
- Photographers: ancient baobabs, salt pans and golden dune light. 
- Adventurous travellers: remote camping, campfire storytelling and guided nature walks. 

## What to expect on the 7 day Central Kalahari and Nxai Pan safari 
Day 1 starts in Maun with a safari briefing, then a scenic drive to Nxai Pan National Park, looking out for zebras, elephants, ostriches and springbok on the way. You settle into camp for your first night under the African sky. Days 2 and 3 are spent exploring Nxai Pan, including the Baines Baobabs, the ancient trees painted by explorer Thomas Baines, the surrounding salt pans, photography stops and nature walks. 
On day 4 you leave early for the Central Kalahari Game Reserve, watching the land change from grassland to red sand, with an evening game drive for gemsbok, wildebeest, cheetahs and brown hyenas. Day 5 continues through the wider Makgadikgadi and Kalahari landscape to a deeper part of the reserve, ending with a bush dinner and stories around the campfire. 
Day 6 is a full day of game drives and nature walks in the Central Kalahari, looking for Kalahari lions, meerkats, giraffes and foxes, with a San cultural visit where available. On day 7 you drive back to Maun, arriving in the afternoon. 

### When is the best time for a Kalahari safari? 
The Kalahari is at its best in the green season, roughly December to April, when rain brings fresh grass and springbok, gemsbok and other antelope gather on the open pans, followed by predators. The dry months from May to October are cooler at night and wildlife is more spread out, but skies are very clear. Our best time to visit Botswana guide explains each month.

Day 01 : 
Maun-Nxai Pan National Park 

Arrival in Maun, followed by a safari briefing before departing for Nxai Pan National Park. Enjoy a scenic drive through Botswana’s wilderness while spotting wildlife such as zebras, elephants, ostriches, springboks, and antelopes along the route. Upon arrival, settle into the campsite and enjoy dinner while experiencing your first night under the African sky. 

Day 02 : 
Explore Nxai Pan National Park 

After breakfast, travel to the iconic Baines Baobabs, a group of ancient baobab trees made famous by explorer Thomas Baines. Explore the surrounding salt pans and enjoy breathtaking views of one of Botswana’s most unique landscapes. Guests will have time for photography, nature walks, and learning about the geological history of the region before settling at camp for dinner. 

Day 03 : 
Explore Baines Baobabs 

After breakfast, travel to the iconic Baines Baobabs, a group of ancient baobab trees made famous by explorer Thomas Baines. Explore the surrounding salt pans and enjoy breathtaking views of one of Botswana’s most unique landscapes. Guests will have time for photography, nature walks, and learning about the geological history of the region before settling at camp for dinner. 

Day 04 : 
Nxai National Park to Central Kalahari Game Reserve 

Depart early and journey deeper into Botswana toward the legendary Central Kalahari Game Reserve, one of Africa’s largest protected wilderness areas. The drive offers changing landscapes from open grasslands to red sand dunes characteristic of the Kalahari ecosystem. Arrive in the reserve and enjoy an evening game drive while searching for desert-adapted species including gemsbok, wildebeests, cheetahs, and brown hyenas. 

Day 05 : 
Makgadikgadi to Central Kalahari Game Reserv 

Continue exploring the remote desert landscapes while driving through parts of the greater Makgadikgadi Pans ecosystem toward deeper sections of the reserve. Along the way, observe desert vegetation, ancient dry riverbeds, and wildlife adapted to extreme climatic conditions. Arrive at camp, enjoy a traditional bush dinner, and experience storytelling around the campfire in the heart of the desert. 

Day 06 : 
Exploration of Central Kalahari Game Reserve 

Spend the day discovering the untouched beauty of the Central Kalahari Game Reserve through guided game drives and nature walks. Search for famous black-maned Kalahari lions, meerkats, giraffes, antelope species, foxes, and rare birdlife. Learn about desert survival techniques and, where available, experience cultural interaction with the indigenous San People, known for their traditional tracking skills and deep connection to the land. 

Day 07 : 
Central Kalahari Game Reserve to Maun 

Enjoy an early morning breakfast before beginning the return journey to Maun. Take in the final views of the vast Kalahari landscapes and make brief stops for wildlife viewing and photography along the route. Arrive in Maun in the afternoon, marking the end of an unforgettable Kalahari Expedition filled with adventure, wildlife encounters, cultural discovery, and authentic African wilderness experiences.
