## 
This Bwindi birding safari is for birders who want depth rather than distance. Six nights in and around Bwindi Impenetrable National Park give you the best possible chance at the Albertine Rift endemics. 
The trip starts with the shoebill at Mabamba and the savannah of Lake Mburo, and ends with Queen Elizabeth National Park and the forest of Kibale. 

## Why choose this Bwindi birding safari? 
- Ruhija: four nights for Mubwindi Swamp, the bamboo zone and African green broadbill. 
- Buhoma: two nights on the main trail for Neumann’s warbler and equatorial akalat. 
- Lake Mburo: African finfoot and savannah birds by boat and on foot. 
- Kibale and Bigodi: green-breasted pitta and an optional chimpanzee trek. 

## Where you stay 
Sophie’s Hotel in Entebbe, Mantana Tented Camp, Trekker’s Tavern Cottages at Ruhija, Engagi Lodge at Buhoma, Savannah Resort near Queen Elizabeth, Primate Lodge and Tooro Resort, or similar. 

## Best time for a Bwindi birding safari 
Bwindi can be birded all year. June to August and December to February are drier and trails are easier. March to May and October to November bring rain but very active birds. 
Plan your trek with our Bwindi gorilla trekking guide , see our gorilla trekking safaris , or browse all birding safaris . The Uganda Wildlife Authority manages Bwindi and its permits.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Entebbe 

Evening arrival and transfer to Sophie's Hotel. 

Day 2 : 
Mabamba to Lake Mburo 

Boat trip at Mabamba for the shoebill, then drive to Lake Mburo for an evening game drive. Overnight at Mantana Tented Camp. 

Day 3 : 
Lake Mburo to Ruhija 

Morning savannah birding and a boat ride for African finfoot, then drive to Ruhija. Overnight at Trekker's Tavern Cottages. 

Day 4 : 
Mubwindi Swamp 

A full day on the trail to Mubwindi Swamp for African green broadbill and Grauer's rush warbler. 

Day 5 : 
Gorilla trek or forest birding 

Optional gorilla trek, or forest birding along the mine trail for regal sunbird and antpeckers. 

Day 6 : 
Ruhija bamboo zone 

Search the bamboo for handsome francolin and Rwenzori batis, with afternoon birding near camp. 

Day 7 : 
Ruhija to Buhoma via the Neck 

Bird the Neck on the way to Buhoma. Overnight at Engagi Lodge. 

Day 8 : 
Buhoma main trail 

A full day on the main trail for owlets, cuckoos and forest warblers. 

Day 9 : 
Buhoma to Queen Elizabeth via Ishasha 

Drive north through Ishasha, looking for tree-climbing lions. Overnight at Savannah Resort. 

Day 10 : 
Queen Elizabeth to Kibale 

Birding along the way to Primate Lodge near Kibale. 

Day 11 : 
Kibale and Bigodi Wetland 

Optional chimpanzee trek and forest birding, then a walk in Bigodi Wetland. Overnight at Tooro Resort in Fort Portal. 

Day 12 : 
Kibale to Entebbe 

Morning forest birding west of Fort Portal, then drive to Entebbe for your flight.

Why spend so long in Bwindi? 

Bwindi holds most of Uganda’s Albertine Rift endemics, and many are shy. More time means better chances at birds like African green broadbill. 

Is gorilla trekking included in the Bwindi birding safari? 

It is optional. We can book a gorilla permit for day 5 if you want to trek. 

Which lodges are used? 

Sophie’s Hotel, Mantana Tented Camp, Trekker’s Tavern Cottages, Engagi Lodge, Savannah Resort, Primate Lodge and Tooro Resort, or similar. 

How hard is the Mubwindi Swamp trail? 

It drops about 300 m over 4 km and must be climbed back up. It is steep but manageable at a slow pace. 

Is chimpanzee tracking included? 

It is optional on day 11 and can be added. 

What should I wear in the forest? 

Long trousers, waterproof boots, a rain jacket and gloves for grabbing vegetation. 

Can I see the shoebill on this trip? 

Yes, Mabamba Swamp on day 2 is one of the best shoebill sites in Uganda.
