## 
This Kenya and Tanzania birding safari is a two-country journey for birders who also want the great game parks. It starts in Tsavo West, crosses into Tanzania near Amboseli and visits every park on the northern circuit. 
Tanzania adds several birds you will not see in Kenya, such as the ashy starling and Fischer’s lovebird, while Kenya’s dry south adds Taveta golden weaver and many arid-country species. 

## Why choose this Kenya and Tanzania birding safari? 
- Tsavo West and Amboseli: sandgrouse, pygmy falcon and Taveta golden weaver. 
- Angata plains: a search for the rare Beesley’s lark. 
- Arusha NP and Tarangire: turacos, trogons, ashy starling and yellow-collared lovebird. 
- Ngorongoro: highland widowbirds, then crowned cranes and bustards in the crater. 
- Serengeti and Manyara: vultures, raptors, flamingos and pelicans. 

## Where you stay 
Kitani Safari Camp in Tsavo West, Amboseli Sentrim Camp, Impala Hotel in Arusha, Momella Lodge, Tarangire Sopa Lodge, Ngorongoro Sopa Lodge, Serengeti Sopa Lodge and Lake Manyara Hotel, or similar. 

## Best time for a Kenya and Tanzania birding safari 
November to April is excellent, with migrants present and the southern Serengeti full of wildebeest. June to October is dry, with great game viewing in Tarangire and the Serengeti. 
Read our guide to Tanzania national parks , see all Tanzania safaris or browse birding safaris . For park rules, see Tanzania National Parks .

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Nairobi to Tsavo West 

Morning pick-up at Nairobi airport and drive to Tsavo West with birding stops near Hunter's Lodge. Overnight at Kitani Safari Camp. 

Day 2 : 
Tsavo West to Amboseli 

Drive through dry scrub to Amboseli. Evening game drive. Overnight at Amboseli Sentrim Camp. 

Day 3 : 
Amboseli National Park 

Morning and evening drives for pygmy falcon, Taveta golden weaver and elephants below Kilimanjaro. 

Day 4 : 
Amboseli to Arusha 

Cross into Tanzania at Namanga, with a stop on the Angata plains for Beesley's lark. Overnight at Impala Hotel. 

Day 5 : 
Arusha National Park 

Forest, scrub and crater lakes for Hartlaub's turaco, bar-tailed trogon and flamingos. Overnight at Momella Lodge. 

Day 6 : 
Momella Lakes to Tarangire 

Morning at the Momella Lakes, then drive to Tarangire Sopa Lodge. 

Day 7 : 
Tarangire National Park 

A full day among baobabs for ashy starling, yellow-collared lovebird and secretary birds. 

Day 8 : 
Ngorongoro highlands 

Climb to the crater rim for widowbirds, Schalow's turaco and sunbirds. Overnight at Ngorongoro Sopa Lodge. 

Day 9 : 
Ngorongoro Crater 

A crater floor game drive for crowned cranes, kori bustards and Fischer's lovebirds. 

Day 10 : 
Ngorongoro to the Serengeti 

Drive into the central Serengeti. Overnight at Serengeti Sopa Lodge. 

Day 11 : 
Central Serengeti 

Plains and kopje birding for Hildebrandt's starling and rosy-patched shrike. 

Day 12 : 
Ndutu area 

A full day at Ndutu for vultures, eagles and spotted eagle-owl. 

Day 13 : 
Serengeti to Lake Manyara 

Drive to Lake Manyara. Overnight at Lake Manyara Hotel. 

Day 14 : 
Lake Manyara National Park 

Lakeside birding for flamingos, pelicans and silvery-cheeked hornbill. 

Day 15 : 
Manyara to Nairobi 

Drive to Arusha and take the shuttle to Nairobi airport for your evening flight.

Do I need visas for both countries? 

Yes. You need a Kenya eTA and a Tanzania visa. We will send you the current requirements when you book. 

Where do we cross the border? 

At Namanga, between Amboseli and Arusha. Your guide helps with the formalities. 

Which lodges are used on the Kenya and Tanzania birding safari? 

Kitani Safari Camp, Amboseli Sentrim Camp, Impala Hotel, Momella Lodge, Tarangire Sopa, Ngorongoro Sopa, Serengeti Sopa and Lake Manyara Hotel, or similar. 

How many species can I expect? 

Most groups record 400 to 500 species in 15 days. 

Is the Ngorongoro Crater fee included? 

Yes, park and crater fees are included in your quote. 

How do I get back to Nairobi? 

On the last day a shuttle runs from Arusha to Nairobi airport, or you can fly from Arusha or Kilimanjaro. 

Is there a yellow fever requirement? 

Tanzania may ask for a yellow fever certificate depending on your route. We will advise you before you travel.
