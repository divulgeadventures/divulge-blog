## Overview 
Experience the ultimate safari thrill tracking the elusive black leopard across the rugged terrains of Laikipia. This exclusive four-day expedition at Laikipia Wilderness Camp offers an intimate look at one of Africa’s most elusive predators. Guided by expert trackers, you will navigate ancient valleys and rocky outcrops where the melanistic black leopard roams. Combine intense tracking with luxury tented accommodation and breathtaking views. Perfect for photographers and wildlife enthusiasts, this package promises a profound connection with Kenya’s wild heart. Book your adventure today. 

### Safari Highlights 
- Black Leopard Tracking 
- Ewaso Enclave Conservancy 
- Laikipia Wilderness 
- Mpala Research Centre 
- Night Game Drive 
- African Wild Dogs 

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Capture the raw essence of Kenya’s northern frontier on this exclusive photography expedition. Track the legendary black leopard of Laikipia through rugged rocky outcrops and riverine thickets, then witness Africa’s most iconic giants at 
Solio Reserve, a premier sanctuary for hundreds of black and white rhinos. It is a masterclass in wilderness immersion and high-impact wildlife documentation. 

Day 01 : 
Nairobi - Laikipia Wilderness 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by Divulge Adventures representative for meet, greet and safari briefing. Afterward, you will depart Nairobi for Ewaso Enclave Conservancy in Laikipia for the elusive black leopard tracking. You will arrive in time for lunch, check in followed by lunch and afternoon siesta. 
Later, you will set out for an afternoon game drive in this completely unique ecosystem. Stay on the lookout for the special five comprising of gravy zebra, reticulated giraffe, somali ostrich, gerenuk and beisa oryx. Other animals include the vulturine guinea fowls, lions, leopard, stripped hyenas among other. Afterwards, return to the camp for dinner and overnight at Sidai Camp . 

Day 02 & 03 : 
Two Full Days Tracking the Black Leopard 

For these two full days you will have a task to accomplish. This includes tracking the famous and elusive black leopard and the special five at the Mpala Research Centre. You will have an amazing moment by sighting the only Kenya’s black panther in its wild habitat. In addition, track the famous, vulnerable and endangered wild dogs. 
These activities require much patience and calmness to spot these kind of animals. At Ewaso Enclave Conservancy you are guaranteed of such sightings in addition to other endangered wildlife like the stripped hyenas, lesser kudu, as well as large numbers of African elephants. While at this conservancy, you will enjoy a remarkable night game drive to spot the nocturnal animals which become active during the night. Dinner and Overnight at Sidai Camp . 

Day 04 : 
Laikipia Wilderness - Solio Reserve 

After three good nights of tracking the black leopard, you will have your breakfast, and proceed towards one of the most populated rhino area, Solio Game Reserve . Approximately, 3 hours’ drive, you will check in at Solio Game Reserve home to over 300 white and black rhinos for full day game drive at this reserve. 
Be ready to spot hundreds of white and black rhinos, waterbucks, impalas, buffaloes, lions as well as the common plain zebras. You will have a picnic Lunch and proceed with the afternoon game drive till late in the evening. Afterwards, exit the reserve to your hotel for dinner and overnight at Rhino Watch Lodge 

Day 05 : 
Solio Game Reserve - Nairobi 

After a steamy hot delicious breakfast head to Solio Reserve for a morning game drive, giving you a chance to click the last pictures of the amazing rhinos before dearting to Nairobi by around noon time. You will have your lunch enroute to Narobi. Drop off at your Nairobi Hotel or at the airport for your outbound flight.
