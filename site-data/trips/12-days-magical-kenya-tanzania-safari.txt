## Overview 
Embark on an unforgettable 12 Day Magical Kenya & Tanzania Safari, a journey through East Africa’s most iconic wildlife destinations. This Kenya and Tanzania Safari combines Kenya’s legendary Maasai Mara, Amboseli, and Lake Naivasha with Tanzania’s world-famous Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater. Witness dramatic landscapes, diverse wildlife, and the awe-inspiring movements of migrating herds. Perfect for adventure seekers and nature lovers, this Kenya and Tanzania Safari promises exhilarating game drives, rich cultural encounters, and breathtaking scenery, creating memories that will last a lifetime. 

### Safari Highlights 
- Lake Naivasha - visited for birdwathing, boat ride and nature walks 
- Lake Nakuru National Park - famous for both black and white rhinons 
- Masai Mara National Reserve - famous for big cats and wildlife dramas 
- Serengeti National Park - visited for wildebeest migration three-quaters of the year 
- Ngorongoro Crater Conservation Area - Famous for rhinos and torpography uniqueness 
- Tarangire National Park - commonly visited for large elephant herds and torpography 
- Amboseli National Park - famous for Large Elephant herds and Mt Kilimanjaro Views. 

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

This 12 Days Magical Kenya & Tanzania Safari offers an immersive journey across two iconic wildlife destinations, perfectly aligned with the world-famous migration routes. From the Maasai Mara to the Serengeti, this Wildebeest Migration Safari promises thrilling wildlife encounters and breathtaking landscapes. Enjoy unforgettable moments as this Wildebeest Migration Safari brings you face-to-face with nature’s most dramatic spectacle. 

Day 01 : 
Arrival at Nairobi 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by a Divulge Adventures representative for a meet, greet, and be transferred to your hotel in Nairobi for Dinner and overnight Jacaranda Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Nairobi - Lake Naivasha - Lake Nakuru National Park 

At your Nairobi Hotel, you will be met by a Divulge Adventures representative for the Kenya and Tanzania safari briefing. Afterward, you will depart Nairobi for Lake Naivasha, which is approximately 2 hours away. There will be a stop to view the Great Rift Valley scenery as you proceed to Lake Naivasha. Upon arrival, proceed for a boat safari at Lake Naivasha and a n ature safari walk at the river banks of Lake Naivasha before exiting for Lake Nakuru National Park, approximately a 1-hour drive. Arriving at around lunchtime, have lunch. Afterwards, proceed for an afternoon game drive at Lake Nakuru National Park. The park comes alive with the sights and sounds of wildlife. Visitors can spot diverse animals, including rhinos, giraffes, lions, and flamingos. The park's stunning landscapes, ranging from lush forests to expansive grasslands and the picturesque lake itself, create a serene backdrop for an adventurous evening exploring Kenya's rich biodiversity. Afterwards, return to the camp for dinner and overnight at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Lake Nakuru National park - Masai Mara National Reserve 

After breakfast, check out of the hotel and proceed to the Masai Mara National Reserve, approximately a 5-hour drive, arriving around lunch time. Check in at the camp for lunch, followed by afternoon relaxing moments. In the evening, visit a Masai Village; Immerse yourself in the rich culture of the Masai people by visiting a traditional village. Learn about their customs, traditions, and way of life, witness their intricate beadwork and warrior dances, and perhaps even purchase a handcrafted souvenir to support their community. Afterwards, return to the resort for dinner and overnight. No evening game drive. Dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp 

Day 04 : 
Full Day at Masai Mara National Reserve 

After breakfast, you will have a full day at Masai Mara National Reserve for game viewing during your stay. This allows you a chance to thoroughly explore this wonderful reserve while you're here. Stay on the lookout for the big cats with magical scenes when hunting, elephants, buffaloes, zebras, wildebeests, giraffes, hippos, lion prides, thousands of impalas, topi, grants, and Thomson's gazelles, and different bird species could all be seen. If lucky, you will get to witness millions of wildebeests cross the famous Mara River from Serengeti National Park in Tanzania to Masai Mara National Reserve in Kenya. During this moment, be ready for the epic scenery of the dangerous giant Mara River Nile crocodiles feeding on the wildebeests, and zebras as they cross the river of death (Mara River. In addition, an optional balloon safari over the Masai Mara, which overs you a magnificent aerial view of the park and allows you to view the wildlife and the meandering Mara River from above. Later, return to the camp for dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp 

Day 05 : 
Masai Mara National Reserve - Serengeti National Park 

After an early and steamy breakfast, you will check out and bid the Masai Mara goodbye with an en route game drive as you head to Isebania, cross-border into Tanzania, approximately a 4-hour drive. At 1000hr, pick up at Isebania border and drive to Serengeti National Park, approximately a 4-hour drive, with an en route game drive to central Serengeti. Dinner and overnight at Tamba Tented Camp . 

Day 06 : 
Full Day at Serengeti National Park 

You will have two full days of game drives at Serengeti National Park. Full-day game drive, a journey deep into the heart of this legendary park, where iconic wildlife roams free, and nature's grandeur unfolds at every turn. Keep your eyes peeled for the open roof as you drive through diverse landscapes, searching for amazing wildlife. Elephants trumpeting, giraffes munching on leaves, and playful meerkats popping out of their burrows are just some of the sights that might await you. In the afternoon, you will stop for lunch at a picnic area, then continue your game drive until sunset, casting a warm glow over the plains. Afterwards, return to the camp for dinner and overnight at Tamba Tented Camp 

Day 07 : 
Serengeti National Park - Ngorongoro Crater Conservation Area 

After breakfast at your camp, you will transfer to Ngorongoro, approximately a 4-hour drive. Arriving for lunch and then proceeding to the Ngorongoro Conservation Area for a game drive. The drive takes you to the breathtaking Ngorongoro Crater, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the most iconic wildlife destinations on Earth. Prepare to be awestruck by the Crater, the world's largest intact volcanic caldera. This natural wonder is a haven for a diverse array of animals, including the Big Five: lions, elephants, buffaloes, leopards, and rhinos. Keep your eyes peeled as you might also spot zebras, giraffes, and a plethora of other fascinating creatures roaming the crater floor. Your experienced guide will be there to share his knowledge and insights, making this a truly enriching experience. After an exhilarating day exploring the Ngorongoro Crater, you will be driven to Karatu for a delicious dinner and overnight at Ngorongoro Coffee Lodge 

Day 08 : 
Ngorongoro Conservation Area - Tarangire National Park - Arusha 

After your morning breakfast, check out with packed lunches and drive towards Tarangire National Park, Tanzania's third largest national park and sanctuary for an unusually large elephant population. Majestic baobab trees are an interesting feature of the park, dwarfing the animals that feed beneath them. Animals concentrate along the Tarangire River, which provides the only permanent water supply in the area. There is a great diversity of wildlife, including lion, leopard, and cheetah, and up to six thousand elephants. Lunch boxes will be provided to you at the picnic point. After the game drive, drive to Arusha for dinner and overnight at Mount Meru Hotel 

Day 09 : 
Arusha - Amboseli National Park 

After breakfast at your hotel, you will check out and bid Arusha Town goodbye as you head to Amboseli National Park via Namanga, Kenya-Tanzania cross-border, approximately a 3-hour drive. Upon clearance with customs, you will proceed to Amboseli National Park, approximately a 2-hour drive. You will have your lunch at Amboseli Sopa Lodge, Hyrax Hill, and afterwards continue with an afternoon game drive at Amboseli National Park to your lodge. Dinner and overnight at the Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 10 : 
Full Day at Amboseli National Park 

After breakfast, set out for a full-day game drive into the home of large herds of elephants and the Kilimanjaro royal court. The game drive takes you to Kenya's premium park, which is rich in wildlife, from up close free-ranging African Elephants to experiencing the epic view of the tallest mountain in Africa, Mt Kilimanjaro. You will also visit Observation Hill, where you can view the entire park in 360 degrees from the top. Lunch will be served as you proceed with the game drive and later return to the lodge for dinner, bonfire, and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 11 : 
Amboseli national Park - Nairobi 

After breakfast, check out and embark on a journey back to Nairobi, approximately a 4-hour drive. Relish the opportunity to enjoy the view of Mt Kilimanjaro and soak in the scenic beauty one last time. This leisurely drive offers a final glimpse of the park’s wonders before concluding your unforgettable safari adventure. Drop off at your Nairobi hotel for dinner and overnight at Jacaranda Hotel 

Day 12 : 
Departure 

After your breakfast, you will check out of the Hotel. Afterwards, you will be dropped off at the airport for your Outbound Flight. This marks the end of your Kenya and Tanzania Safari.
