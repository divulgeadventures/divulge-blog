## Overview 
This 12 day Kenya and Tanzania safari is a private road safari from Nairobi through seven parks. You spend 2 nights in the Masai Mara, 2 in the Serengeti, 1 near Ngorongoro Crater, 2 in Amboseli and 1 at Lake Nakuru, with a Tarangire game drive and a Lake Naivasha boat ride. The page lists January to June as the best season, and the price is on request. 
It suits first-time visitors to East Africa who want the Masai Mara, Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater in one trip, travelling in a 4×4 Land Cruiser with a professional driver guide, on full board in the parks, in 3-star rated lodges and camps. 

## Why choose a 12 day Kenya and Tanzania safari? 
A Kenya and Tanzania safari links the two halves of the Mara-Serengeti ecosystem, so you see the Masai Mara and the Serengeti on one trip, crossing overland at Isebania and returning at Namanga. Adding Ngorongoro Crater, Tarangire and Amboseli gives you rhinos, big elephant herds and views of Mount Kilimanjaro as well as big cats. 
The route needs no internal flights, because every transfer is by road. Check entry rules for both countries in our Tanzania visa guide before you travel. 
- First-time visitors: the Masai Mara, Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater in one itinerary. 
- Elephant lovers: Tarangire’s baobab country and a full day in Amboseli. 
- Culture: a Maasai village visit in the Masai Mara is included. 

## What to expect on the 12 day Kenya and Tanzania safari 
Day 1 is your arrival and transfer to Jacaranda Hotel in Nairobi. On day 2 you drive about 2 hours to Lake Naivasha for a boat ride and nature walk, then about 1 hour to Lake Nakuru National Park for an afternoon game drive, staying at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge. Day 3 is a drive of about 5 hours to the Masai Mara and an evening Maasai village visit, and day 4 is a full day in the reserve, staying at PrideInn Mara Camp. 
On day 5 you drive about 4 hours to the Isebania border, cross into Tanzania and drive about 4 more hours to central Serengeti, staying 2 nights at Tamba Tented Camp with a full day of game drives on day 6. Day 7 is a drive of about 4 hours to Ngorongoro with a crater game drive, and a night at Ngorongoro Coffee Lodge in Karatu. 
Day 8 is a game drive in Tarangire National Park with packed lunches, then Mount Meru Hotel in Arusha. On day 9 you cross back into Kenya at Namanga for 2 nights at Amboseli Sopa Lodge, with a full day in Amboseli and Observation Hill on day 10. Day 11 is a drive of about 4 hours to Jacaranda Hotel in Nairobi, and day 12 is your airport transfer. 

### When is the best time for a Kenya and Tanzania safari? 
The trip page lists January to June as the best season for this Kenya and Tanzania safari. Mara River crossings usually happen between July and October, so if the crossings are your main goal, ask us about those months. See where the herds usually are in our Great Migration month by month guide . 

## What is included in this Kenya and Tanzania safari? 
The safari includes a meet-and-greet and safari briefing, all airport transfers, all transport in a 4WD Land Cruiser, 11 nights of accommodation (2 in Nairobi on bed and breakfast), all park entry and conservation fees, full board meals as per the itinerary, an English or bilingual driver guide, drinking water and game drives as per the itinerary. The Maasai village visit, Lake Naivasha boat ride and nature walk are included free. 
Not included are the optional hot air balloon safaris (USD 500 over the Masai Mara and USD 600 over the Serengeti per person), travel insurance, visas, international flights, tips (about USD 10 per person per day is suggested), drinks and personal items. Learn what the Tanzanian parks charge in our Tanzania park fees guide .

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

This 12 Days Magical Kenya & Tanzania Safari offers an immersive journey across two iconic wildlife destinations, perfectly aligned with the world-famous migration routes. From the Maasai Mara to the Serengeti, this Wildebeest Migration Safari promises thrilling wildlife encounters and breathtaking landscapes. Enjoy unforgettable moments as this Wildebeest Migration Safari brings you face-to-face with nature’s most dramatic spectacle. 

Day 01 : 
Arrival at Nairobi 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by a Divulge Adventures representative for a meet, greet, and be transferred to your hotel in Nairobi for Dinner and overnight Jacaranda Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Nairobi - Lake Naivasha - Lake Nakuru National Park 

At your Nairobi Hotel, you will be met by a Divulge Adventures representative for the Kenya and Tanzania safari briefing. Afterward, you will depart Nairobi for Lake Naivasha, which is approximately 2 hours away. There will be a stop to view the Great Rift Valley scenery as you proceed to Lake Naivasha. 
Upon arrival, proceed for a boat safari at Lake Naivasha and a n ature safari walk at the river banks of Lake Naivasha before exiting for Lake Nakuru National Park, approximately a 1-hour drive. Arriving at around lunchtime, have lunch. Afterwards, proceed for an afternoon game drive at Lake Nakuru National Park. 
The park comes alive with the sights and sounds of wildlife. Visitors can spot diverse animals, including rhinos, giraffes, lions, and flamingos. The park's stunning landscapes, ranging from lush forests to expansive grasslands and the picturesque lake itself, create a serene backdrop for an adventurous evening exploring Kenya's rich biodiversity. 
Afterwards, return to the camp for dinner and overnight at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Lake Nakuru National park - Masai Mara National Reserve 

After breakfast, check out of the hotel and proceed to the Masai Mara National Reserve, approximately a 5-hour drive, arriving around lunch time. Check in at the camp for lunch, followed by afternoon relaxing moments. In the evening, visit a Masai Village; Immerse yourself in the rich culture of the Masai people by visiting a traditional village. 
Learn about their customs, traditions, and way of life, witness their intricate beadwork and warrior dances, and perhaps even purchase a handcrafted souvenir to support their community. Afterwards, return to the resort for dinner and overnight. No evening game drive. Dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp 

Day 04 : 
Full Day at Masai Mara National Reserve 

After breakfast, you will have a full day at Masai Mara National Reserve for game viewing during your stay. This allows you a chance to thoroughly explore this wonderful reserve while you're here. 
Stay on the lookout for the big cats with magical scenes when hunting, elephants, buffaloes, zebras, wildebeests, giraffes, hippos, lion prides, thousands of impalas, topi, grants, and Thomson's gazelles, and different bird species could all be seen. 
If lucky, you will get to witness millions of wildebeests cross the famous Mara River from Serengeti National Park in Tanzania to Masai Mara National Reserve in Kenya. 
During this moment, be ready for the epic scenery of the dangerous giant Mara River Nile crocodiles feeding on the wildebeests, and zebras as they cross the river of death (Mara River. 
In addition, an optional balloon safari over the Masai Mara, which overs you a magnificent aerial view of the park and allows you to view the wildlife and the meandering Mara River from above. Later, return to the camp for dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp 

Day 05 : 
Masai Mara National Reserve - Serengeti National Park 

After an early and steamy breakfast, you will check out and bid the Masai Mara goodbye with an en route game drive as you head to Isebania, cross-border into Tanzania, approximately a 4-hour drive. At 1000hr, pick up at Isebania border and drive to Serengeti National Park, approximately a 4-hour drive, with an en route game drive to central Serengeti. Dinner and overnight at Tamba Tented Camp . 

Day 06 : 
Full Day at Serengeti National Park 

You will have two full days of game drives at Serengeti National Park. Full-day game drive, a journey deep into the heart of this legendary park, where iconic wildlife roams free, and nature's grandeur unfolds at every turn. Keep your eyes peeled for the open roof as you drive through diverse landscapes, searching for amazing wildlife. 
Elephants trumpeting, giraffes munching on leaves, and playful meerkats popping out of their burrows are just some of the sights that might await you. In the afternoon, you will stop for lunch at a picnic area, then continue your game drive until sunset, casting a warm glow over the plains. 
Afterwards, return to the camp for dinner and overnight at Tamba Tented Camp 

Day 07 : 
Serengeti National Park - Ngorongoro Crater Conservation Area 

After breakfast at your camp, you will transfer to Ngorongoro, approximately a 4-hour drive. Arriving for lunch and then proceeding to the Ngorongoro Conservation Area for a game drive. The drive takes you to the breathtaking Ngorongoro Crater, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the most iconic wildlife destinations on Earth. 
Prepare to be awestruck by the Crater, the world's largest intact volcanic caldera. This natural wonder is a haven for a diverse array of animals, including the Big Five: lions, elephants, buffaloes, leopards, and rhinos. Keep your eyes peeled as you might also spot zebras, giraffes, and a plethora of other fascinating creatures roaming the crater floor. 
Your experienced guide will be there to share his knowledge and insights, making this a truly enriching experience. After an exhilarating day exploring the Ngorongoro Crater, you will be driven to Karatu for a delicious dinner and overnight at Ngorongoro Coffee Lodge 

Day 08 : 
Ngorongoro Conservation Area - Tarangire National Park - Arusha 

After your morning breakfast, check out with packed lunches and drive towards Tarangire National Park, Tanzania's third largest national park and sanctuary for an unusually large elephant population. Majestic baobab trees are an interesting feature of the park, dwarfing the animals that feed beneath them. 
Animals concentrate along the Tarangire River, which provides the only permanent water supply in the area. There is a great diversity of wildlife, including lion, leopard, and cheetah, and up to six thousand elephants. Lunch boxes will be provided to you at the picnic point. 
After the game drive, drive to Arusha for dinner and overnight at Mount Meru Hotel 

Day 09 : 
Arusha - Amboseli National Park 

After breakfast at your hotel, you will check out and bid Arusha Town goodbye as you head to Amboseli National Park via Namanga, Kenya-Tanzania cross-border, approximately a 3-hour drive. Upon clearance with customs, you will proceed to Amboseli National Park, approximately a 2-hour drive. 
You will have your lunch at Amboseli Sopa Lodge, Hyrax Hill, and afterwards continue with an afternoon game drive at Amboseli National Park to your lodge. Dinner and overnight at the Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 10 : 
Full Day at Amboseli National Park 

After breakfast, set out for a full-day game drive into the home of large herds of elephants and the Kilimanjaro royal court. The game drive takes you to Kenya's premium park, which is rich in wildlife, from up close free-ranging African Elephants to experiencing the epic view of the tallest mountain in Africa, Mt Kilimanjaro. 
You will also visit Observation Hill, where you can view the entire park in 360 degrees from the top. Lunch will be served as you proceed with the game drive and later return to the lodge for dinner, bonfire, and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 11 : 
Amboseli national Park - Nairobi 

After breakfast, check out and embark on a journey back to Nairobi, approximately a 4-hour drive. Relish the opportunity to enjoy the view of Mt Kilimanjaro and soak in the scenic beauty one last time. This leisurely drive offers a final glimpse of the park’s wonders before concluding your unforgettable safari adventure. Drop off at your Nairobi hotel for dinner and overnight at Jacaranda Hotel 

Day 12 : 
Departure 

After your breakfast, you will check out of the Hotel. Afterwards, you will be dropped off at the airport for your Outbound Flight. This marks the end of your Kenya and Tanzania Safari.

The 12 day Kenya and Tanzania safari includes airport transfers, a 4WD Land Cruiser with a driver guide, 11 nights of accommodation, full board in the parks, all park and conservation fees, drinking water, a Lake Naivasha boat ride and a Maasai village visit. Flights, visas, insurance, tips, drinks and balloon flights are extra. 

Where do you stay on the Kenya and Tanzania safari? 

On this Kenya and Tanzania safari you stay at Jacaranda Hotel in Nairobi, Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge, PrideInn Mara Camp, Tamba Tented Camp in the Serengeti, Ngorongoro Coffee Lodge in Karatu, Mount Meru Hotel in Arusha and Amboseli Sopa Lodge. Is the 12 day Kenya and Tanzania safari suitable for families? 

Yes, families can join the 12 day Kenya and Tanzania safari, as it is a private trip. Several days include drives of 4 hours or more and two border crossings, so it suits children who are comfortable in a vehicle. Tell us their ages and we will advise on family rooms and pacing.
