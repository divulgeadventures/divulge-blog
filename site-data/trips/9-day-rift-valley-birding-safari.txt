## 
This Rift Valley birding safari is a nine-day journey from Nairobi through the lakes of the Great Rift Valley to the Masai Mara. It suits keen birders and nature lovers who also want classic game drives. 
The route moves from the gardens of Nairobi to soda and freshwater lakes, the cliffs of Hell’s Gate and the open grassland of the Mara. Each stop adds very different species to your list. 

## Why choose this Rift Valley birding safari? 
- Sunbirds from day one: Variable, Bronze and Collared Sunbirds in Nairobi’s gardens. 
- Soda and freshwater lakes: avocets, godwits and flamingos at Elementaita, fish eagles and ospreys at Naivasha. 
- Raptors at Hell’s Gate: cliffs where vultures and eagles ride the thermals. 
- Lake Nakuru: water birds plus rhinos and a fair chance of leopard. 
- Masai Mara: more than 450 recorded bird species and the Big Five. 

## Where you stay 
We use well-placed lodges with good gardens for early-morning birding: Nairobi Safari Club, Lake Elementaita Lodge, Lake Naivasha Lodge, Lake Nakuru Lodge and Mara Sopa Lodge, or similar. 

### Pace and style 
Mornings start early, when birds are most active. Midday is for rest, and afternoons bring a second walk or drive. Your private 4×4 has a pop-up roof, and your guide carries a field guide and a scope. 

## Best time for a Rift Valley birding safari 
Birding is rewarding all year. From October to April, migrants from Europe and Asia join the residents, and many birds are in breeding colours during the rains. June to September is drier, with easy roads and great game viewing in the Mara.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Nairobi and the National Museum 

Pick-up at Jomo Kenyatta Airport and transfer to Nairobi Safari Club. In the afternoon, visit the bird gallery at the National Museum and walk through the botanic garden, where sunbirds, weavers and palm swifts are common. 

Day 2 : 
Nairobi to Lake Elementaita 

Drive into the Rift Valley to Lake Elementaita Lodge. Late-morning and afternoon walks along the shore bring avocets, black-tailed godwits, glossy ibises and flamingos. 

Day 3 : 
Lake Elementaita to Lake Naivasha 

A short drive to Lake Naivasha, with a bird walk on arrival. In the afternoon visit Elsamere and scan the lake edge for herons, fish eagles, ospreys and black crakes. Overnight at Lake Naivasha Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Hell's Gate National Park 

Morning walk along the Naivasha shoreline, then an afternoon in Hell's Gate. Look for raptors on the cliffs, plus wheatears and batises in the scrub. Return to Lake Naivasha Lodge. 

Day 5 : 
Lake Naivasha to Lake Nakuru 

Drive to Lake Nakuru National Park and check in at Lake Nakuru Lodge. Afternoon game and bird drive along the shore, with flamingos, pelicans and rhinos. 

Day 6 : 
Full day in Lake Nakuru National Park 

A full day of birding in the acacia woodland and around the lake, with a late game drive for a chance of leopard. Overnight at Lake Nakuru Lodge. 

Day 7 : 
Lake Nakuru to the Masai Mara 

Cross the Rift Valley to the Masai Mara and check in at Mara Sopa Lodge. Afternoon game drive and birding in the warm evening light. 

Day 8 : 
Full day in the Masai Mara 

An early start for birds and big cats, with a picnic lunch on the plains. Look for bustards, secretary birds, raptors and colourful rollers. 

Day 9 : 
Masai Mara to Nairobi 

Drive back to Nairobi with a lunch stop, then a day room before your evening flight. An optional farewell dinner can be arranged.

How many birds can I see on this Rift Valley birding safari? 

Most guests record between 250 and 350 species in nine days, depending on the season and how much time you spend birding. 

Is this trip only for expert birders? 

No. It suits beginners and experienced birders. Your guide helps you find and identify birds, and the game drives keep non-birders happy too. 

Which lodges are used? 

Nairobi Safari Club, Lake Elementaita Lodge, Lake Naivasha Lodge, Lake Nakuru Lodge and Mara Sopa Lodge, or similar lodges of the same standard. 

Is a boat ride on Lake Naivasha included? 

A boat ride is optional and can be added. It is one of the best ways to see fish eagles and water birds up close. 

When is the best time for birding in the Rift Valley? 

October to April brings migrant birds and breeding plumage. June to September is drier, with easier travel and excellent game viewing. 

Will I see the Big Five? 

Lions, buffaloes and elephants are common in the Mara, and rhinos live at Lake Nakuru. Leopards are seen often but never guaranteed. 

Can the itinerary be changed? 

Yes. We can add nights, swap lodges or include extra stops such as Lake Bogoria or Lake Baringo.
