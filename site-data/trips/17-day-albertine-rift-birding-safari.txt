## 
This Albertine Rift birding safari is a 17-day journey through western Uganda for birders who want the region’s endemics and forest specials. It also includes chimpanzee tracking and a mountain gorilla trek. 
The Albertine Rift runs along Uganda’s border with Congo and Rwanda. Its mountain forests hold dozens of birds found nowhere else, and Semliki adds species from the great Congo Basin rainforest. 

## Why choose this Albertine Rift birding safari? 
- Kibale: green-breasted pitta, forest robins and chimpanzees. 
- Semliki: Congo Basin hornbills, Nkulengu rail and Congo serpent eagle. 
- Rwenzori foothills: crimsonwings and purple-breasted sunbird. 
- Queen Elizabeth: Kazinga Channel boat trip, skimmers and black bee-eaters. 
- Bwindi and Ruhija: African green broadbill, Rwenzori turaco and mountain gorillas. 

## Where you stay 
Lodges include Lindsay Cottages in Entebbe, Ndali Lodge near Kibale, Semliki Safari Lodge, Margherita Hotel in Kasese, Mweya Safari Lodge, Buhoma Homestead or Gorilla Forest Camp, and Trekker’s Tavern at Ruhija, or similar. 

### The Mubwindi Swamp walk 
From Ruhija, a steep trail drops to Mubwindi Swamp, the best site in Uganda for the rare African green broadbill. It is a demanding walk, but a highlight for serious birders. 

## Best time for an Albertine Rift birding safari 
Birding is good all year. The drier months, June to August and December to February, make forest trails easier. March to May and October to November are wetter, but many birds are breeding and singing.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Entebbe 

Meet your birding guide at Entebbe Airport and transfer to Lindsay Cottages. Early arrivals can bird the Entebbe Botanical Gardens. 

Day 2 : 
Entebbe to Kibale National Park 

An early start west to Kibale, birding along the way. Check in at Ndali Lodge or similar. 

Day 3 : 
Kibale Forest 

Morning in the forest for green-breasted pitta, forest robins and barbets, with optional chimpanzee tracking. Afternoon birding along the main road through the forest. 

Day 4 : 
Kibale to Semliki 

Drive to Semliki National Park, a lowland forest linked to the Congo Basin. Overnight at Semliki Safari Lodge. 

Day 5 : 
Kirumia Trail, Semliki 

A full day on the Kirumia Trail with a packed lunch, searching for hornbills, kingfishers, malimbes and honeyguides. 

Day 6 : 
Semliki to Kasese 

Morning birding in Semliki, then drive to Kasese at the foot of the Rwenzori Mountains. Overnight at Margherita Hotel. 

Day 7 : 
Rwenzori foothills 

A full day birding the lower slopes of the Rwenzoris for crimsonwings, sunbirds and forest flycatchers. 

Day 8 : 
Queen Elizabeth National Park 

Drive into Queen Elizabeth National Park for savannah and wetland birds. Overnight at Mweya Safari Lodge. 

Day 9 : 
Kazinga Channel 

Morning game drive, then a two-hour afternoon boat trip on the Kazinga Channel for skimmers, storks, kingfishers and hippos. 

Day 10 : 
Queen Elizabeth to Bwindi 

Drive south, optionally through the Ishasha sector, to Buhoma in Bwindi. Bird the Munyaga trail if time allows. 

Day 11 : 
Bwindi, Buhoma section 

A full day on the forest trails for Albertine Rift endemics, turacos and greenbuls. 

Day 12 : 
Gorilla trekking 

Briefing at park headquarters, then track a habituated gorilla family and spend one hour with them. Packed lunch. 

Day 13 : 
The Neck to Ruhija 

Bird the road through the 'Neck', a lush valley full of forest birds, and continue up to Ruhija. Overnight at Trekker's Tavern or similar. 

Day 14 : 
Mubwindi Swamp 

A full day on the trail down to Mubwindi Swamp for African green broadbill, Grauer's warbler and other endemics. 

Day 15 : 
Ruhija bamboo zone 

Bird the bamboo and montane forest along the main road for highland endemics. 

Day 16 : 
Return to Entebbe 

A long drive back to Entebbe with birding stops. Overnight at Lindsay Cottages or Lake Victoria Hotel. 

Day 17 : 
Departure 

Transfer to Entebbe Airport for your flight.

What is the Albertine Rift? 

It is the western arm of the Great Rift Valley, along Uganda’s border with Congo and Rwanda. Its forests hold many endemic birds found nowhere else. 

Is gorilla trekking included in the Albertine Rift birding safari? 

Yes. One gorilla permit per person is included. Chimpanzee tracking in Kibale is optional and can be added. 

How difficult are the forest walks? 

Most trails are moderate. The Mubwindi Swamp trail at Ruhija is steep and can be muddy, so good fitness helps. 

Which lodges are used? 

Lindsay Cottages, Ndali Lodge, Semliki Safari Lodge, Margherita Hotel, Mweya Safari Lodge, Buhoma Homestead or Gorilla Forest Camp, and Trekker’s Tavern, or similar. 

How many endemics can I see? 

Many birders see 15 to 20 Albertine Rift endemics on this route, depending on luck and weather. 

Is Uganda safe for travellers? 

Yes. Uganda is a popular and welcoming destination. We plan routes with experienced local guides and follow park advice. 

What is the best time for birding in Uganda? 

All year. The drier months make trails easier, while the wetter months bring breeding birds and lush scenery.
