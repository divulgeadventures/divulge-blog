## Overview 
This 7 day Amboseli Nakuru and Masai Mara safari is a private road safari from Nairobi through four parks: 2 nights in Amboseli National Park, a Lake Naivasha boat ride, 1 night at Lake Nakuru and 3 nights in the Masai Mara. It runs all year, with the Great Migration usually in the Mara from July to October. The price starts from USD 2,711 per adult, and it suits first-time visitors, couples and families. 
You travel in a 4WD Land Cruiser with an English or bilingual driver guide, stay at Amboseli Sopa Lodge, Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge and Prideinn Mara Camp on full board, and finish with a Maasai village visit and the Giraffe Centre in Nairobi. 

## Why choose a 7 day Amboseli Nakuru and Masai Mara safari? 
The 7 day Amboseli Nakuru and Masai Mara safari covers Kenya’s classic southern circuit in one week. Amboseli gives you big elephant herds with Mount Kilimanjaro behind them, Lake Naivasha adds a boat safari among hippos and water birds, Lake Nakuru is known for rhinos, Rothschild’s giraffes and flamingos, and the Masai Mara offers the Big Five and, in season, the Great Migration. 
- First-time visitors: four very different parks and lakes in seven days. 
- Big Five seekers: two full days in the Masai Mara National Reserve. 
- Value seekers: the Lake Naivasha boat ride, Maasai village visit and Giraffe Centre are included. 

## What to expect on the 7 day Amboseli Nakuru and Masai Mara safari 
Day 1 starts with a meet-and-greet in Nairobi and a drive of about 4 hours to Amboseli, with an evening game drive and the night at Amboseli Sopa Lodge. Day 2 is a full day in Amboseli for elephants, Kilimanjaro views and the climb up Observation Hill, with a second night at Amboseli Sopa Lodge. 
Day 3 is a drive of about 5 hours to Lake Naivasha, with a Great Rift Valley viewpoint stop, a boat safari and a nature walk, before the night at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge. Day 4 opens with a morning game drive in Lake Nakuru National Park, then a drive of about 5 hours to the Masai Mara and Prideinn Mara Camp. 
Days 5 and 6 are full days in the Masai Mara with picnic lunches on the plains and an optional hot air balloon safari. On day 7 you visit a Maasai village, drive about 5 hours to Nairobi, feed giraffes at the Giraffe Centre and are dropped at your hotel or the airport. 

### Is 7 days enough for Amboseli, Nakuru and the Masai Mara? 
Seven days is enough for this route because the Masai Mara gets two full days and Amboseli gets one, which is where most of the wildlife time matters. The trade-off is long road transfers of 4 to 5 hours on most travel days. To see when the herds are in the Mara, read our Great Migration month by month guide , and for the first park on the route see our Amboseli National Park guide . 

## What is included in this Kenya safari? 
The price includes a meet-and-greet and safari briefing, all transport in a 4WD Land Cruiser, 6 nights of accommodation (2 in Amboseli, 1 at Lake Nakuru and 3 in the Masai Mara), all park entry and conservation fees, full board meals, an English or bilingual driver guide, unlimited game drives on the itinerary and drinking water. The Maasai village visit, Lake Naivasha boat ride and Giraffe Centre are included free. 
Not included are the hot air balloon safari over the Masai Mara (USD 500 per person), travel insurance, visas, international flights, tips (about USD 10 per person per day is suggested), drinks and personal items. ## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Prepare for an unforgettable Kenya safari as you journey through four of the country's most celebrated wildlife destinations. This 7-day itinerary begins in Amboseli National Park, where colossal elephant herds roam beneath the majestic, snow-capped Mount Kilimanjaro. 
The adventure continues to the tranquil freshwater oasis of Lake Naivasha, offering a unique boat safari experience, followed by a quest for rhinos and flamingos in Lake Nakuru National Park. Conclude your ultimate Kenya safari in the vast, rolling savannahs of the Masai Mara National Reserve, the pinnacle of wildlife viewing, known for the Big Five and the breathtaking Great Migration (seasonal). 
This comprehensive Kenya safari guarantees diverse ecosystems and iconic African moments. 

Day 01 : 
Nairobi - Amboseli National Park 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by Divulge Adventures representative for meet, greet and Kenya safari briefing. Afterward, you will depart Nairobi for Amboseli National Park, approximately 4 hours . drive. 
Arriving at around lunchtime for check-in and lunch, followed by afternoon siesta. Later in the evening set out for evening game drive at Amboseli National Park, and later return to the lodge for dinner and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 02 : 
Full Day Game Drive at Amboseli National Park 

After breakfast, set out for a full-day game drive into the home of large herds of elephants and the Kilimanjaro royal court. The game drive takes you to Kenya's premium park, which is rich in wildlife, from up close free-ranging African Elephants to experiencing the epic view of the tallest mountain in Africa, 
Mt Kilimanjaro. You will also visit Observation hill, where you can view the entire park in 360 degrees from the top. Lunch will be served as you proceed with the game drive and later return for dinner, and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Amboseli National Park - Lake Naivasha - Lake Nakuru National Park 

After a steamy hot delicious breakfast depart Amboseli for Lake Naivasha, which is approximately 5 hours away. There will be a stop to view the great rift valley scenery as you proceed to Lake Naivasha. You will arrive in time for lunch. 
After lunch, proceed for boat safari at Lake Naivasha and nature safari walk at the river banks of Lake Naivasha before heading to Nakuru for check in at the Hotel, dinner and overnight at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Lake Nakuru National park - Masai Mara National Reserve 

After an early and steamy breakfast, set out for Lake Nakuru National Park morning game drive. As the sun rises, the park comes alive with the sights and sounds of wildlife. Visitors can spot diverse animals, including rhinos, giraffes, lions, and flamingos. 
The park's stunning landscapes, ranging from lush forests to expansive grasslands and the picturesque lake itself, create a serene backdrop for an adventurous evening exploring Kenya's rich biodiversity. 
Later in the afternoon exit for Masai Mara Reserve, approximately 5 hours drive arriving late in the evening. Check-in at camp/ lodge, followed by relaxing moments. No evening game drive. Dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp 

Day 05 & 06 : 
Two Full Days at Masai Mara National Reserve 

You will have two full days at Masai Mara National Reserve for game viewing during your Kenya safari. This allows you a chance to thoroughly explore this wonderful reserve while you're here. Stay on the lookout for the big cats with magical scenes when hunting, elephants, buffaloes, zebras, wildebeests, giraffes, hippos and dangerous crocodiles feeding on the wildebeests as they cross the Mara River. 
Elephants, giraffes, lion prides, thousands of impalas, topi, grants and Thomson's gazelles, and different bird species could all be seen. So, make sure to carry your phone or camera to capture these moments. You will have picnic lunch served in the vast plains of Maasai Mara National Reserve. 
In addition, optional balloon safari over the Masai Mara which offers you a magnificent aerial view of the park and viewing the wildlife and meandering Mara River from above. Dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp 

Day 07 : 
Masai Mara National Reserve - Nairobi 

After breakfast at your camp, you will check out and head for Masai Village Visit; Immerse yourself in the rich culture of the Masai people by visiting a traditional village. Learn about their customs, traditions, and way of life, witness their intricate beadwork and warrior dances, and perhaps even purchase a handcrafted souvenir to support their community. Afterwards, exit Masai Mara for Nairobi approximately 5 hours’ drive. 
Arriving late in the afternoon, upon arrival, proceed to giraffe center where you get a chance to feed giraffes with your own hands and later drop off at your Nairobi hotel or at the airport for your outbound flight. This marks the end of your lifetime memorable Kenya safari.

When is the best time for the 7 day Amboseli Nakuru and Masai Mara safari? 

The 7 day Amboseli Nakuru and Masai Mara safari runs all year. For the Great Migration in the Masai Mara, travel from July to October, when the herds are usually in the reserve. January to March is also good for dry-season game viewing in Amboseli and the Mara, while April and May are the long rains, with greener parks and fewer vehicles. 

What is included in the 7 day Amboseli Nakuru and Masai Mara safari? 

The 7 day Amboseli Nakuru and Masai Mara safari includes a 4WD Land Cruiser with a driver guide, 6 nights on full board, all park and conservation fees, unlimited game drives on the itinerary, drinking water, a Lake Naivasha boat ride, a Maasai village visit and the Giraffe Centre. Flights, visas, insurance, tips, drinks and the USD 500 balloon safari are extra. 

Where do you stay on this Amboseli, Nakuru and Masai Mara safari? 

On this Amboseli, Nakuru and Masai Mara safari you stay 2 nights at Amboseli Sopa Lodge, 1 night at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge and 3 nights at Prideinn Mara Camp in the Masai Mara, all on full board. If a lodge is full for your dates, we offer a similar option and confirm it in your written quote. 

Is this 7 day Kenya safari suitable for families with children? 

Yes, this 7 day Kenya safari suits families, because it is a private trip with a flexible pace, lodges with family-friendly rooms and varied activities such as the boat ride and Giraffe Centre. Some transfers take about 5 hours, so tell us your children’s ages and we will plan breaks and seating for them.
