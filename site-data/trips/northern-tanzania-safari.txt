## Overview 
This 6 day Tarangire Serengeti and Ngorongoro safari is a 5-night road safari on Tanzania’s northern circuit. You spend a day in Tarangire National Park, 3 nights at Moyo Tented Camp in the Serengeti and a night at Rhino Lodge before a full Ngorongoro Crater game drive. The page gives no fixed season, so it runs all year. From USD 2,649 per adult, it suits first-time visitors to Tanzania. 
It suits couples, families and small groups who want the three best-known northern parks in under a week, travelling in a modified 4WD Land Cruiser with a photographic roof and a professional English-speaking guide, with meals as per the itinerary. 

## Why choose a 6 day Tarangire Serengeti and Ngorongoro safari? 
Tarangire, the Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Crater are the core of the northern circuit, and this trip gives each one a proper game drive. Tarangire is known for big elephant herds along the Tarangire River and its baobab trees. The Serengeti gets two full days and two more half days, which is the most time of any park on the trip. 
The Ngorongoro Crater ends the safari. It is a collapsed volcanic caldera with walls about 2,000 ft high, and one of the few places where the rare black rhino can be seen. With some luck, the Big Five can be seen in a single crater game drive. 
- First-time visitors: Tarangire, the Serengeti and the crater in 6 days. 
- Wildlife lovers: 3 nights in one Serengeti camp, so less packing and more driving time. 
- Rhino seekers: a full crater tour with a picnic lunch on the crater floor. 

## What to expect on the 6 day Tarangire Serengeti and Ngorongoro safari 
Day 1 is a full day of game drives in Tarangire National Park, about 120 km southwest of Arusha, with a picnic lunch, looking for elephants, lions, buffaloes, zebras and wildebeest. In the evening you drive to Omega View Hotel for dinner and the night. 
Day 2 crosses the Ngorongoro Conservation Area to the Serengeti, with an optional stop at Olduvai Gorge and a picnic lunch on a kopje, then a game drive to Moyo Tented Camp. Days 3 and 4 are full days in the Serengeti, starting early to catch predators returning from the night’s hunt, with picnic lunches and both nights at Moyo Tented Camp. 
Day 5 starts with a morning game drive in the central Serengeti, then a game drive en route to Rhino Lodge inside the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Day 6 is a crater tour with a picnic lunch on the crater floor, then a late afternoon drive back to Arusha for the flight to Zanzibar. 

### When is the best time for a Tarangire, Serengeti and Ngorongoro safari? 
The Tarangire, Serengeti and Ngorongoro safari works all year, because the crater and the central Serengeti hold resident wildlife in every month. Tarangire is usually at its best in the dry months, when elephants gather along the river. The wildebeest herds move around the Serengeti through the year, so tell us your dates and we plan the game drives around them. Read our best time to visit Tanzania guide for month-by-month advice.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
Northern Tanzania safari - Tarangire National Park 

After breakfast ,begin your Northern Tanzania safari with a drive to Tarangire National Park with picnic lunches for a day of game drive . 
This Park is located 120km southwest of Arusha. In this astonishing park, expect to see substantial herds of Elephants gathering alongside the Tarangire River along with many other animal species e.g. 
Wildebeest, Lions, Buffaloes, Zebras and prolific birds. In the evening drive for dinner and overnight at Omega view Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Tarangire- Serengeti National Park 

After breakfast begin your Northern Tanzania safari with a drive to Serengeti National Park via Ngorongoro Conservation Area Authority a designated the UNESCO World Heritage. On the way you will stop at the Olduvai Gorge (optional) where anthropologists Dr. Louis and Mary Leakey made significant discoveries contributing to the theory that human life originated in Africa. 
After stopping for your picnic lunch on a kopje (rock outcropping) in Serengeti you will continue further into the park game drive en route arriving in time for dinner and overnight at Moyo Tented Camp 

Day 03 : 
Serengeti Full Day- The Ultimate Northern Tanzania safari Experience 

Early morning game drive to see also nocturnal predators returning from hunting and animals waking up in early morning sunrise followed by full day Serengeti National Park tour putting you in a position to see lots of the animals. 
Serengeti in Maasai meaning the endless plain; 14763sq km in size is the home to many animals including Lions, Elephants, Giraffes, Wildebeests, Zebras, Leopards, Cheetahs, Warthogs, Hyenas plus a lots of bird life. 
The great migration is taking place around Serengeti and during that particular time millions of Wildebeests, Zebras, Thompson’s Gazelles, Grants Gazelle, Elands and Impalas are mingling together followed by predators. Dinner and overnight Moyo tented Camp 

Day 04 : 
Serengeti Full Day-Northern Tanzania safari 

After breakfast, another full day game drive in Serengeti National Park with picnic lunch. Dinner and overnight at Moyo Tented Camp Serengeti 

Day 05 : 
Central Serengeti- Ngorongoro 

After breakfast at the lodge set off to the Northern Tanzania safari for a morning game drive. After lunch proceed to Ngorongoro game drive en route arriving in time for dinner and overnight at Rhino lodge inside Ngoro Ngoro conservation Area. 

Day 06 : 
Ngorongoro Crater- Arusha 

After breakfast you will descend into the crater for a wonderful crater tour with a picnic lunch inside the Ngorongoro Crater often described as one of the wonders of the world a designated UNESCO World Heritage. It is one of the few places where the rare black rhino can be observed in their natural setting. 
As very few animals migrate in and out of the crater with its 2000 ft. high walls, you can expect to see lions, elephants, zebras, hippos, flamingos, jackals, rhinos, antelopes, many birds and other species. With a bit of luck, the ‘Big Five’ can be spotted during one game drive only. 
Then late afternoon drives back to Arusha for Zanzibar flight .
