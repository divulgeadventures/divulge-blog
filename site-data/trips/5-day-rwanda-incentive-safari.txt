## 
This 5 day Rwanda incentive safari is a short, high-impact reward trip for corporate groups. It starts in Kigali, one of Africa’s cleanest and best-connected cities, then moves to Volcanoes National Park for a mountain gorilla trek and golden monkey trekking, with nights in luxury lodges. 

## Why choose a 5 day Rwanda incentive safari? 
Rwanda is easy to reach and easy to organise. Kigali has direct international flights, conference venues and good hotels, and Volcanoes National Park is only about 2.5 hours away, so the whole trip fits into a working week. 
Gorilla trekking is a once-in-a-lifetime experience that guests talk about for years, which makes it a strong reward for top performers. We can add a conference day, an awards dinner or a helicopter transfer. 

## What does the incentive itinerary include? 
One night in Kigali on arrival, two nights near Volcanoes National Park and a final night in Kigali after the golden monkey trek. The Kigali Genocide Memorial visit on day 2 gives important context to Rwanda’s recovery. 

### How many guests can trek gorillas? 
Each gorilla family can be visited by up to 8 people a day, so larger groups trek to different families at the same time. Permits must be booked early, so tell us your numbers as soon as possible. 
Read our guides to gorilla trekking in Rwanda and the Rwanda gorilla permit . 

## Who is the 5 day rwanda incentive safari best for? 
The 5 day rwanda incentive safari is best for companies rewarding top sales teams, dealers, partners or long-serving staff. We plan the programme with your HR or events team, so the 5 day rwanda incentive safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## Can the 5 day Rwanda incentive safari include a conference? 
Yes. Kigali is a popular conference city, and we can plan the safari before or after your meeting, with transfers for the whole group. We can also add Akagera National Park for a Big Five game drive. 

## How do I get a group quote? 
Price on request. Group prices depend on your dates, the number of travellers and the rooms you need, so we quote every group personally. Day 1 : 
Arrival in Kigali 

Your 5 day rwanda incentive safari begins today. Meet your guide at Kigali International Airport and transfer to your hotel. Dinner and overnight at The Retreat by Heaven or Kigali Marriott Hotel. 

Day 2 : 
Kigali Genocide Memorial to Volcanoes National Park 

Visit the Kigali Genocide Memorial, then drive about 2.5 hours to Volcanoes National Park. A helicopter transfer can be arranged. Dinner and overnight at Singita Kwitonda Lodge or similar. 

Day 3 : 
Mountain gorilla trekking 

Transfer to Kinigi for the ranger briefing, then trek through bamboo and montane forest to spend one hour with a gorilla family. In the afternoon, visit the Gorilla Guardians Village. Dinner and overnight at Singita Kwitonda Lodge or similar. 

Day 4 : 
Golden monkey trekking and return to Kigali 

Trek to find golden monkeys and spend an hour with them, then return to Kigali by road or helicopter. Dinner and overnight at The Retreat by Heaven or Kigali Marriott Hotel. 

Day 5 : 
Departure 

Transfer to Kigali International Airport for your flight home. This is the end of your 5 day rwanda incentive safari.

What is included in the 5 day Rwanda incentive safari? 

The 5 day Rwanda incentive safari includes a 4×4 safari vehicle with a guide, luxury accommodation, meals as per the itinerary, gorilla and golden monkey permits, the Kigali Genocide Memorial and the Gorilla Guardians Village. Helicopter transfers and private events can be added. 

How much is a Rwanda gorilla permit? 

A Rwanda gorilla permit costs USD 1,500 per person and is included in this package. Permits sell out early in the busy months, so book as soon as your dates are fixed. 

Can we hold a meeting or awards dinner? 

Yes. Kigali has conference hotels and venues, and luxury lodges near the park can host a private dinner. Tell us your programme early so we can confirm the venue. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 5 day rwanda incentive safari. 

How fit do guests need to be? 

Gorilla treks take 30 minutes to over 4 hours on steep forest trails. Porters can carry bags and help on the slopes, and rangers try to match less active guests with closer gorilla families. 

How far ahead should we book? 

Book 6 to 12 months ahead to secure gorilla permits and lodge rooms for the whole group. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 5 day rwanda incentive safari.
