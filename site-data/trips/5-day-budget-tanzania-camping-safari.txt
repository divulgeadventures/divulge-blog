## 
This budget Tanzania safari is a classic camping trip through the northern circuit. From Arusha you visit Tarangire’s elephant herds, camp two nights in the heart of the Serengeti at Seronera public campsite, and spend a morning on the floor of Ngorongoro Crater before camping on the crater rim. A cook travels with you and prepares all meals. It suits travellers who want the Serengeti and the crater for less and are happy with simple tents. 
Price: from USD 1,280 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Two nights camping in the central Serengeti 
- Ngorongoro Crater game drive 
- Tarangire National Park and its elephant herds 
- Camping crew and cook with all meals 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Arusha to Tarangire 

Morning pick-up in Arusha and drive to Tarangire National Park for a game drive among baobabs and elephants. Dinner and overnight at Twiga Campsite in Mto wa Mbu. 

Day 2 : 
To the Serengeti 

Drive over the Ngorongoro highlands into the Serengeti with a game drive on the way to Seronera. Dinner and overnight at Seronera public campsite. 

Day 3 : 
Serengeti National Park 

Full day of game drives in the central Serengeti, known for lions, leopards and cheetahs. Dinner and overnight at Seronera public campsite. 

Day 4 : 
Serengeti to Ngorongoro 

Morning game drive, then drive to the Ngorongoro Crater rim. Dinner and overnight at Simba Campsite. 

Day 5 : 
Ngorongoro Crater to Arusha 

Descend into the crater for a morning game drive, then drive back to Arusha in the afternoon.

How much does this budget Tanzania safari cost? 

It starts from USD 1,280 per adult with a shared vehicle, based on current park and camping fees. We confirm the price for your dates when you enquire. 

What are the campsites like? 

Public campsites have shared toilets and showers; we provide dome tents, mattresses and a cook who prepares all meals. 

Is camping in the Serengeti safe? 

Yes. Campsites are used by many safari groups every night, and guides advise on staying safe around wildlife.
