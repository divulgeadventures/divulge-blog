## Overview 
This luxury Rwanda safari brings together two of the country’s most celebrated camps. You spend two nights at Magashi Camp in a private part of Akagera National Park, then two nights at Bisate Lodge on the edge of Volcanoes National Park. 
Both are run with a strong focus on conservation and community, and both offer the kind of guiding and service that makes a safari feel effortless. 

## Why choose this luxury Rwanda safari? 
Magashi sits in a remote area of northern Akagera overlooking Lake Rwanyakazinga, where the camp’s own guides run game drives, boat trips and catch-and-release fishing. Bisate’s thatched forest villas sit on an eroded volcanic cone with sweeping views of the Virungas. 
- Exclusive: a private concession with few other vehicles. 
- Iconic lodges: two of Rwanda’s most admired camps. 
- Complete: Big Five, gorillas and golden monkeys. 

## What to expect on your luxury Rwanda safari 
After a warm welcome in Kigali, you drive to Akagera’s north gate on day two, where Magashi’s guides meet you. Settle into your tent overlooking the lake and choose your activities, from morning and evening game drives to boat cruises. 
Day three is a full day at Magashi, tracking lions, elephants, buffaloes, rhinos and the area’s many hippos with expert guides. On day four you travel to Bisate Lodge, about five hours by road or quicker by optional helicopter. 
Day five is your gorilla trek, with one magical hour beside a habituated family. On day six you trek golden monkeys before returning to Kigali for your evening flight. 

### How fit do you need to be? 
Activities at Magashi need no special fitness. Gorilla treks last one to six hours, and porters are available to carry your bag and help on steep ground. 

## Best time for a luxury Rwanda safari 
June to September and December to February are the most popular months, and both camps book up early. We recommend reserving six to twelve months ahead, especially for gorilla permits. 
See more lodge options on our luxury gorilla trekking Rwanda safari , and read our guide to gorilla trekking in Rwanda . Park facts are on Visit Rwanda’s Akagera National Park page .

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is your 6-day exclusive luxury Rwanda safari day by day. 

Day 01 : 
Arrival in Kigali 

Upon arrival at Kigali International Airport, you will receive a warm welcome from the Divulge Adventures team. After exchanging money if needed, transfer to your hotel and enjoy the rest of the day at leisure. Dinner and overnight at Kigali Marriott Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Kigali - Akagera National Park (Magashi Camp) 

After an early breakfast, drive to Akagera National Park, approximately 3 hours, where the camp's guides will meet you at the north gate for the short drive to camp. Settle into your tent overlooking the lake, then choose from the camp's activities, including morning and evening game drives, boat cruises on Lake Rwanyakazinga and catch-and-release fishing. Dinner and overnight at Magashi Camp 

Day 03 : 
Akagera Safari Activities 

Spend a full day enjoying Magashi's activities with expert guides: track animal signs, learn to identify birds by their calls and search for lions, elephants, buffaloes, rhinos and the area's abundant hippos. Relax between activities with lake views from your private deck. Dinner and overnight at Magashi Camp 

Day 04 : 
Akagera - Volcanoes National Park (Bisate Lodge) 

After breakfast, check out and drive to Volcanoes National Park, approximately 5 hours, with a lunch stop in Kigali. Arrive at your lodge and settle into your villa with views of the volcanoes. In the afternoon, you may visit the Ellen DeGeneres Campus of the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund. Dinner and overnight at Bisate Lodge 

Day 05 : 
Mountain Gorilla Trekking and Gorilla Guardians Village 

After an early breakfast, report to the Kinigi park headquarters for the 7:00 AM briefing, with your trek starting at about 8:00 AM. Hikes can last from 30 minutes to over 4 hours before you spend one magical hour with the gorillas. Return to the lodge for lunch. In the afternoon, visit the Gorilla Guardians Village for traditional dances and stories. Dinner and overnight at Bisate Lodge 

Day 06 : 
Golden Monkey Trekking - Kigali (Departure) 

After breakfast, set out on a golden monkey trek, spending one hour with these lively monkeys. Return to the lodge for lunch and check out, then drive back to Kigali, approximately 2.5 hours, with an optional shopping stop before your evening flight from Kigali International Airport.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

What is Magashi Camp? 

Magashi is a luxury tented camp in a private area of northern Akagera National Park, overlooking Lake Rwanyakazinga, with exclusive game drives, boat trips and fishing. 

What is special about Bisate Lodge? 

Bisate Lodge has distinctive thatched forest villas on an eroded volcanic cone near Volcanoes National Park, with sweeping views and a strong reforestation programme. 

Is a luxury Rwanda safari worth it? 

For many travellers, yes. Luxury camps offer exclusive locations, expert guiding and personalised service that make the wildlife experiences even more special. 

Can I fly between Akagera and Volcanoes National Park? 

Yes. Private helicopter or charter transfers can be arranged at extra cost to save driving time. 

What is included at Magashi Camp? 

Your stay includes the camp’s game drives, boat cruises and fishing, plus meals. Premium drinks and spa treatments may be extra. 

How far ahead should I book a luxury Rwanda safari? 

Book six to twelve months ahead for the best rooms and gorilla permit dates, especially for travel in the dry season. 

What is the minimum age for gorilla trekking? 

Visitors must be at least 15 years old to trek mountain gorillas in Rwanda.
