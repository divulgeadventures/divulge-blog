## 
This Botswana honeymoon safari keeps the group small and the scenery wild. Fly from Maun to Pom Pom Camp in a private concession of the Okavango Delta for three nights of game drives, mokoro trips and bush dinners, then fly to Kasane for two nights at Chobe Chilwero above the Chobe River, famous for its elephant herds. It suits couples who want a quiet, exclusive Delta experience, and it combines easily with Victoria Falls. 
Price: from USD 4,030 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Three nights in a private Okavango Delta concession at Pom Pom Camp 
- Mokoro trips and game drives 
- Two nights at Chobe Chilwero above the Chobe River 
- Light aircraft flights between camps 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Maun to the Okavango Delta 

Fly by light aircraft from Maun to Pom Pom Camp. Afternoon activity. Dinner and overnight at Pom Pom Camp. 

Day 2 : 
Okavango Delta 

Morning and afternoon game drives or mokoro trips, depending on water levels. Dinner and overnight at Pom Pom Camp. 

Day 3 : 
Okavango Delta 

A full day exploring the concession by vehicle, boat or on foot. Dinner and overnight at Pom Pom Camp. 

Day 4 : 
Fly to Chobe 

Fly to Kasane and transfer to the lodge. Sunset cruise on the Chobe River. Dinner and overnight at Chobe Chilwero. 

Day 5 : 
Chobe National Park 

Morning game drive and afternoon boat safari. Dinner and overnight at Chobe Chilwero. 

Day 6 : 
Departure 

Transfer to Kasane airport or continue by road to Victoria Falls.

How much does this Botswana honeymoon safari cost? 

It starts from USD 4,030 per adult sharing, based on low-season camp rates. Prices rise from July to October; we confirm the price for your dates when you enquire. 

Can we add Victoria Falls? 

Yes. Victoria Falls is about an hour by road from Kasane and makes a good two-night extension. 

What is a mokoro? 

A traditional dugout canoe, now usually made of fibreglass, poled through the shallow Delta channels by a local guide.
