## Overview

Enjoy thrilling game drives, stunning scenery, and close encounters with Africa’s Big Five. Book now to see the Kenya Wildebeest Migration in its full glory

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day-to-day, explore Amboseli, Lake Naivasha, Lake Nakuru, and the Maasai Mara on this 10 Days Classic Kenya Wildebeest Migration Safari. Enjoy game drives and witness the spectacular Kenya Wildebeest Migration up close. 

Day 01 : 
Arrival 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by a Divulge Adventures representative for a meet, greet, and Kenya wildebeest migration safari briefing. Afterwards, you will be transferred to your hotel in Nairobi for dinner and overnight at Argyle Grand Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Nairobi National Park, Elephan Orphanage and Giraffe Center 

After a delicious breakfast at your hotel, embark on a full-day Nairobi National Park excursion filled with unforgettable wildlife encounters and cultural insights. Begin your adventure at Nairobi National Park , the only national park located within a capital city, where you may spot lions, rhinos, giraffes, and zebras roaming against a backdrop of city skyscrapers. Continue to the renowned David Sheldrick Elephant Orphanage , where rescued baby elephants are cared for and rehabilitated before being reintroduced into the wild. Later, visit the Giraffe Center , home to the endangered Rothschild giraffes, and enjoy the rare experience of feeding these gentle giants up close. Afterwards, return to your hotel for a late lunch followed by evening relaxing at the hotel. Dinner and overnight at Argyle Grand Hotel 

Day 03 : 
Nairobi - Amboseli National Park 

After a delicious breakfast at your hotel, you will depart for Amboseli National Park, approximately 4 hours away . drive. Arriving at around lunchtime for check-in and lunch, followed by an afternoon siesta. Later in the afternoon, set out for an afternoon game drive at Amboseli National Park , and later return to the lodge for dinner and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Full Day at Amboseli National Park 

After breakfast, embark on a full-day game drive through the home of large elephant herds and the majestic Kilimanjaro, marking the start of your Kenya Wildebeest Migration Safari. Explore this premier park, encountering free-ranging African elephants and breathtaking views of Mt. Kilimanjaro. Visit Observation Hill for a 360-degree panorama, enjoying lunch during the game drive. This introduction highlights the excitement of your Kenya Wildebeest Migration Safari. Later returning for dinner, and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 05 : 
Amboseli National Park to Lake Naivasha 

After a steamy, hot, delicious breakfast, depart Amboseli for Lake Naivasha, which is approximately 5 hours away . There will be a stop to view the Great Rift Valley scenery as you proceed to Lake Naivasha. You will arrive in the afternoon and check into your hotel, followed by lunch. After lunch, proceed for a boat safari at Lake Naivasha and a nature safari walk at the Crescent Island. Afterwards, head back to the hotel to relax and enjoy a moment, followed by dinner and overnight at Lake Naivasha Resort 

Day 06 : 
Lake Naivasha to Lake Nakuru National Park 

After a sweet and steamy hot delicious breakfast, check out of your hotel and leave for Hells Gate National Park for cycling and walking in the gorges. It is located near Lake Naivasha in Kenya’s Rift Valley, is a small but dramatic park known for its towering cliffs, rugged gorges, volcanic formations, hot springs, and geothermal activity. Unlike most parks, it allows visitors to walk, cycle, and even rock climb alongside wildlife such as zebras, giraffes, buffalo, and antelopes, while over 100 bird species, including vultures and eagles, soar above the cliffs. Highlights include hiking through the Hell’s Gate Gorge, cycling safaris on the open plains, and exploring Fischer’s Tower, a striking volcanic plug. Its breathtaking scenery even inspired the landscapes in Disney’s The Lion King, making it a unique destination for both adventure and nature lovers. Afterwards, head for Lake Nakuru National Park, which takes approximately a 2-hour drive. Arrival at Lake Nakuru National Park you will check in for lunch and in the evening head out for an afternoon game drive at Nakuru Park. Later in the evening, return to the hotel for dinner and overnight at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge 

Day 07 : 
Lake Nakuru National Park to Masai Mara National Reserve 

After a steaming, delicious breakfast, depart Lake Nakuru National Park and drive approximately five hours to the Maasai Mara Reserve. Check in at your camp by lunchtime. In the afternoon, enjoy a Masai Village visit and immerse yourself in the vibrant culture of the Maasai people. Learn about their customs, traditions, and warrior dances, admire their intricate beadwork, and support the community with handcrafted souvenirs. This day perfectly complements your Kenya Wildebeest Migration Safari experience, offering cultural insight alongside the thrilling wildlife encounters the Kenya Wildebeest Migration Safari is renowned for. Later, return to the camp for dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp 

Day 08 & 09 : 
Two Full Days at Masai Mara National Reserve 

Enjoy two full days of game viewing at the world-renowned Maasai Mara National Reserve, giving you plenty of time to explore its stunning landscapes and abundant wildlife. Spot big cats hunting, elephants, buffaloes, zebras, giraffes, wildebeests, hippos, and crocodiles preying on wildebeests crossing the Mara River. Capture lion prides, impalas, topi, gazelles, and diverse bird species on camera. Picnic lunches are served in the vast plains, with an optional balloon safari offering breathtaking aerial views. This unforgettable experience is a key highlight of your Kenya Wildebeest Migration Safari, providing intimate encounters with the epic Kenya Wildebeest Migration. Dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp 

Day 10 : 
Masai Mara National Reserve to Nairobi (Departure) 

After breakfast at your camp, check out and drive approximately five hours to Nairobi. Arrive in the afternoon and proceed to the airport for your outbound flight. This marks the conclusion of your unforgettable Kenya Wildebeest Migration Safari, leaving you with lasting memories of thrilling wildlife encounters and the spectacular migration across the Maasai Mara.
