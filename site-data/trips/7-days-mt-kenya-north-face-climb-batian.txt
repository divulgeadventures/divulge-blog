## 
Rated excellent by our travellers ★★★★★ 4.9/5 on TripAdvisor (74 reviews) · ★★★★★ 5.0/5 on SafariBookings (66 reviews) 
This 7 day Batian North Face climb takes experienced climbers from Nairobi to Batian (5,199 m), the highest point in Kenya, and back in a week. You walk up the scenic Sirimon route, base yourself at Kami Hut below the North Face, climb the standard North Face route over two days with a bivouac, and walk out on the Chogoria side. 
It is aimed at fit, competent rock climbers comfortable at UIAA grade IV to IV+ who already have some altitude experience, because the schedule is shorter than our 10-day version. 

## Why choose the 7 day Batian North Face climb? 
The Sirimon route is the gentlest and most scenic way into the northern side of the peaks, through forest, moorland and Mackinder’s Valley. It leads naturally to Kami Hut, the traditional base for the North Face. 
If you have more time, our 10-day Kamweti version adds a longer walk-in and a Point Lenana acclimatisation summit. Choose the 7-day trip if you are short on time and already acclimatised, for example after another high trek. 

## What to expect on the 7 day Batian North Face climb 
Day 1 is a drive to Nanyuki and a short walk to Old Moses Camp (about 3,300 m). Day 2 crosses the moorland and Mackinder’s Valley to Shipton’s Camp (about 4,200 m), and day 3 is a short hop to Kami Hut. Day 4 is the main climbing day to a bivouac near Firmin Tower, and on day 5 you reach Batian around midday, abseil down and return to Shipton’s. 
Days 6 and 7 take you over Simba Col and down to Chogoria, then back to Nairobi by mid-afternoon. 

### When is the best season? 
Late June to early October is the classic North Face season, when the face is sunny and dry. Outside that window, ask us about the Nelion route on the south-east face.

Day 1 : 
Nairobi to Old Moses Camp via Sirimon Gate 

Drive about four hours to Nanyuki for lunch, then on to Sirimon Gate. Walk about 9 km (3 to 4 hours) on a forest track to Old Moses Camp at about 3,300 m. 

Day 2 : 
Old Moses Camp to Shipton's Camp 

Cross the moorland and follow Mackinder's Valley to Shipton's Camp (about 4,200 m), around 6 to 7 hours of walking with the peaks rising ahead. 

Day 3 : 
Shipton's Camp to Kami Hut 

A short walk of about two hours to Kami Hut, the base for the North Face. The afternoon is for rest, a gear check and a route briefing with your climbing guide. 

Day 4 : 
North Face climb to the bivouac 

Leave around 5:30 a.m., reach the foot of the face within an hour and start climbing at first light. After roughly ten rope lengths you spend the night at a bivouac near Firmin Tower. 

Day 5 : 
Batian summit and back to Shipton's Camp 

Finish the climb to Batian (5,199 m), usually by midday. Abseil down the route and walk back to Shipton's Camp. 

Day 6 : 
Shipton's Camp to Chogoria over Simba Col 

Cross Simba Col and descend through the Gorges Valley area to the Chogoria side, about 7 hours. Overnight in bandas near the gate. 

Day 7 : 
Chogoria to Nairobi 

Walk a short way down through the moorland, then a 4x4 takes you to Chogoria town and on to Nairobi, arriving in the afternoon.

Is the 7 day Batian North Face climb suitable for beginners? 

Rated excellent by our travellers ★★★★★ 4.9/5 on TripAdvisor (74 reviews) · ★★★★★ 5.0/5 on SafariBookings (66 reviews) 
No. The 7 day Batian North Face climb is for experienced rock climbers only. You need to be comfortable on multi-pitch rock up to about UIAA grade IV+, to abseil on your own and to climb at over 5,000 m. 

What is the best time for the North Face of Mount Kenya? 

Rated excellent by our travellers ★★★★★ 4.9/5 on TripAdvisor (74 reviews) · ★★★★★ 5.0/5 on SafariBookings (66 reviews) 
The best time is late June to early October, when the North Face is in the sun and usually dry. In January and February the south-east face routes on Nelion are generally in better condition. 

How do you acclimatise in only seven days? 

Rated excellent by our travellers ★★★★★ 4.9/5 on TripAdvisor (74 reviews) · ★★★★★ 5.0/5 on SafariBookings (66 reviews) 
The Sirimon route gains height steadily, and nights at Shipton’s Camp and Kami Hut help. Because the schedule is short, it suits climbers who have recently been at altitude. Otherwise we recommend our 10-day version with a Point Lenana summit. 

What gear do I need to bring? 

Rated excellent by our travellers ★★★★★ 4.9/5 on TripAdvisor (74 reviews) · ★★★★★ 5.0/5 on SafariBookings (66 reviews) 
Bring a harness, helmet, climbing shoes or stiff boots, belay and abseil device, slings and warm clothing for a cold bivouac. The guide provides group ropes and protection. Sleeping bags and trekking gear can be hired in Nanyuki. 

Can friends or a club book this climb together? 

Rated excellent by our travellers ★★★★★ 4.9/5 on TripAdvisor (74 reviews) · ★★★★★ 5.0/5 on SafariBookings (66 reviews) 
Yes. We plan climbs for groups and clubs, keeping rope teams to two climbers per guide and adding guides as needed. Send us your group size and dates for a quote.
