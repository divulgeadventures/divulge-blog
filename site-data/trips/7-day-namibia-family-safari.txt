## 
This 7 day Namibia family safari spends six nights inside and around Etosha National Park, moving from the south through the centre to the east. Floodlit waterholes at the lodges let children watch elephants, rhinos and lions after dinner, and the game drives are easy for all ages. 

## Why is this 7 day Namibia family safari good for children? 
Etosha is one of Africa’s easiest parks for families. Animals gather at waterholes in the dry months, so sightings are frequent and drives can be short. Several lodges have waterholes you can watch from the terrace, day and night. 
The roads are good and the route moves slowly across the park, so there are no long transfer days after the first drive from Windhoek. 

## What does the family itinerary include? 
Two nights in southern Etosha, one in central Etosha and three in eastern Etosha, with a night drive on day 4 and packed lunches on the longer drives. 

### Which lodges are used? 
We choose family-friendly lodges with waterholes and pools in each area and confirm family rooms and child rates in your quote.

## Who is the 7 day namibia family safari best for? 
The 7 day namibia family safari is best for families who want to share a safari, from young children to grandparents. Tell us everyone’s ages and we will match vehicles, rooms and activities to your family, so the 7 day namibia family safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## Can several families share the 7 day Namibia family safari? 
Yes. Families can share one vehicle or travel in two vehicles that meet at the lodges, and each family can pay separately. We can also add Sossusvlei’s dunes or the coast at Swakopmund.

Day 1 : 
Windhoek to southern Etosha 

Your 7 day namibia family safari begins today. Your guide collects you from the airport or your Windhoek hotel and drives north to southern Etosha for an afternoon game drive. 

Day 2 : 
Southern Etosha 

Morning and afternoon game drives among the savannah species of the south. After dinner, watch elephants, antelopes and rhinos at the lodge's floodlit waterhole. 

Day 3 : 
Southern to central Etosha 

A relaxed morning in the south, then move to central Etosha's mopane woodland, where leopards are more often seen. Afternoon game drive and an evening by the lodge waterhole. 

Day 4 : 
Central to eastern Etosha 

An early drive in the central area, then continue east, where natural springs attract plenty of wildlife. After dinner, join a night drive to look for nocturnal animals. 

Day 5 : 
Eastern Etosha 

A full day of game drives in eastern Etosha with a packed lunch, including the northern areas of the park. 

Day 6 : 
Eastern Etosha 

Another day exploring the east, with a lunch break at the lodge and an afternoon drive to search for wildlife. 

Day 7 : 
Eastern Etosha to Windhoek 

Drive back to Windhoek for your onward journey. This is the end of your 7 day namibia family safari.

Is the 7 day Namibia family safari suitable for young children? 

Yes. The 7 day Namibia family safari has short drives, good roads and lodges with floodlit waterholes, so children can watch wildlife without long days in the car. Some activities, such as the night drive, may have minimum ages. 

What animals can we see in Etosha? 

Elephants, black and white rhinos, lions, giraffes, zebras, springbok and many birds. Leopards and cheetahs are seen less often, especially in central Etosha. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 7 day namibia family safari. 

Do children get lower rates? 

Many lodges offer child rates and family rooms, but rules vary by age and season. We include the right rates for your family in your quote. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 7 day namibia family safari. 

Can two families travel together? 

Yes. On group bookings the seventh place is free for every six paying travellers, and each family can pay separately. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 7 day namibia family safari. 

When is the best time for a family safari in Namibia? 

May to October is the dry season, when animals gather at waterholes. July and August are cooler and match northern school holidays. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 7 day namibia family safari.
