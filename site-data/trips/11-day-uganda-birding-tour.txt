## 
This Uganda birding tour is a well-paced 11-day route for travellers who want great birding with time to enjoy each place. It covers wetland, rainforest, savannah and mountain forest, with chimpanzees and an optional gorilla trek. 
Two nights around Entebbe give you a gentle start, with Lake Victoria’s shoreline and the famous shoebill at Mabamba. 

## Why choose this Uganda birding tour? 
- Entebbe: hornbills, kingfishers and palm-nut vultures by Lake Victoria. 
- Mabamba: canoe trip for the shoebill. 
- Kibale: chimpanzees, grey parrots and Narina trogon. 
- Queen Elizabeth: skimmers, martial eagles and the Kazinga Channel launch. 
- Bwindi and Lake Mburo: forest endemics, then savannah birds and zebras. 

## Where you stay 
An Entebbe hotel by the lake, a lodge near Kibale, Mweya Safari Lodge in Queen Elizabeth, a camp in Buhoma and a lodge at Lake Mburo, such as Mantana Tented Camp, or similar. 

## Best time for a Uganda birding tour 
Uganda is good all year. June to August and December to February are the driest months. The wetter months bring breeding birds and lush green scenery. 
See our Uganda national parks guide , the Bwindi gorilla trekking guide and all Uganda safaris . Permits are managed by the Uganda Wildlife Authority .

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Entebbe 

Airport pick-up and transfer to your hotel near Lake Victoria. 

Day 2 : 
Entebbe birding 

Bird the lakeshore and forest edges for casqued hornbills, palm-nut vultures, long-crested eagles and kingfishers. 

Day 3 : 
Mabamba to Kibale 

Canoe trip at Mabamba for the shoebill, then drive west to a lodge near Kibale. 

Day 4 : 
Kibale National Park 

Chimpanzee tracking and forest birding for grey parrot, African emerald cuckoo and Narina trogon. 

Day 5 : 
Kibale to Queen Elizabeth 

Morning in Kibale, then drive to Mweya Safari Lodge in Queen Elizabeth National Park. 

Day 6 : 
Queen Elizabeth and Kazinga Channel 

Morning game drive and an afternoon launch trip for skimmers, herons and hippos. 

Day 7 : 
Queen Elizabeth to Bwindi 

Drive south to Buhoma for forest birding in Bwindi. 

Day 8 : 
Bwindi forest or gorilla trek 

Forest birding for alethes, warblers and wattle-eyes, or an optional gorilla trek. 

Day 9 : 
Bwindi to Lake Mburo 

Drive east to Lake Mburo with birding stops. 

Day 10 : 
Lake Mburo National Park 

Savannah birding for go-away-birds, francolins and lovebirds, plus a boat ride. 

Day 11 : 
Lake Mburo to Entebbe 

Final morning of birding, then drive to Entebbe for a farewell dinner and your evening flight.

Is gorilla trekking included in the Uganda birding tour? 

It is optional on day 8. We can add a permit for you. 

Is chimpanzee tracking included? 

Yes. One chimpanzee tracking permit is included in Kibale. 

How many birds will I see? 

Most guests record 350 to 450 species in 11 days. 

Where do we stay? 

An Entebbe hotel, a lodge near Kibale, Mweya Safari Lodge, a Buhoma camp and a Lake Mburo lodge, or similar. 

Is this trip suitable for beginners? 

Yes. The pace is relaxed and your guide helps you find and identify every bird. 

How long are the drives? 

Most drives are three to six hours, broken up with birding stops. 

Can I add Semliki? 

Yes. Semliki can be added for two nights after Kibale for Congo Basin forest birds.
