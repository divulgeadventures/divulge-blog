## 
This Kenya migrant birding safari is planned for the months when Kenya’s bird list is at its biggest. From October to December, millions of migrants from Europe and Asia arrive and join more than 1,000 resident species. 
The route covers the forests of Mount Kenya, the dry north, the Rift Valley lakes, Kakamega’s rainforest and the Masai Mara. 

## Why choose this Kenya migrant birding safari? 
- Mount Kenya: Mackinder’s eagle-owl, white-starred robin and turacos. 
- Samburu and Buffalo Springs: Somali specials and cream-coloured courser. 
- Nakuru and Baringo: flamingos, white rhinos, owls and coursers. 
- Kakamega: three nights in the rainforest. 
- Masai Mara: migrant raptors, storks and wintering waders. 

## Where you stay 
A Nairobi hotel, Serena Mountain Lodge, Samburu Lodge, Naro Moru River Lodge, Lake Nakuru Lodge, Soi Lodge, Rondo Retreat, Mara Serena Lodge or Mara West Camp, Sarova Mara Camp and Lake Naivasha Country Club, or similar. 

## Best time for a Kenya migrant birding safari 
October to December is the peak, as migrants arrive and the short rains start. January to March is also excellent, with migrants still present and many residents breeding.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Nairobi 

Airport pick-up and transfer to your Nairobi hotel. 

Day 2 : 
Thika to Mount Kenya 

Bird the Blue Post grounds, Chania River and Wajee Camp for Hinde's babbler, then continue to Serena Mountain Lodge. 

Day 3 : 
Mount Kenya to Samburu 

Early birding at the lodge for white-starred robin and mountain greenbul, then drive to Samburu Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Samburu National Reserve 

A full day in semi-desert and river woodland for Somali bunting and Somali bee-eater. 

Day 5 : 
Buffalo Springs to Naro Moru 

Morning in Buffalo Springs, a stop at Meru Forest, then Naro Moru River Lodge. 

Day 6 : 
Mount Kenya to Lake Nakuru 

Climb into the montane forest for Mackinder's eagle-owl, then drive to Lake Nakuru Lodge. 

Day 7 : 
Lake Nakuru to Lake Baringo 

Morning at Nakuru with white rhinos, then north to Soi Lodge. 

Day 8 : 
Lake Baringo 

Lake and cliff birding for Verreaux's eagle, Heuglin's courser and white-faced owl. 

Day 9 : 
Kerio Valley to Kakamega 

Birding stops in the Kerio Valley on the way to Rondo Retreat. 

Day 10 : 
Kakamega Forest 

Rainforest birding and primates. 

Day 11 : 
Kakamega Forest 

More forest birding for flufftails, bee-eaters and akalats. 

Day 12 : 
Lake Victoria to the Masai Mara 

Papyrus birding near Kisumu, then drive to the Mara. Overnight at Mara Serena Lodge or Mara West Camp. 

Day 13 : 
Masai Mara 

A full day of game drives and birding. 

Day 14 : 
Hippo Pools 

Cross the Mara River and visit Hippo Pools. Overnight at Sarova Mara Camp. 

Day 15 : 
Siana to Lake Naivasha 

Morning birding near Siana, then drive to Lake Naivasha Country Club. 

Day 16 : 
Lake Naivasha to Nairobi 

Boat trip on Lake Naivasha and stops at Kinangop, Gatamaiyu and Limuru, then transfer to the airport.

When is the migrant season in Kenya? 

Most migrants arrive from October and stay until March or April, with numbers peaking from November to February. 

What migrants might I see? 

White storks, European rollers, harriers, wagtails, wheatears, warblers and many waders. 

Which lodges are used on the Kenya migrant birding safari? 

A Nairobi hotel, Serena Mountain Lodge, Samburu Lodge, Naro Moru River Lodge, Lake Nakuru Lodge, Soi Lodge, Rondo Retreat, Mara Serena or Mara West, Sarova Mara Camp and Lake Naivasha Country Club, or similar. 

Will it rain? 

The short rains usually begin in November, with afternoon showers. Mornings are often clear and birds are very active. 

How many species can I expect? 

Many birders record 500 or more species on this route in the migrant season. 

Is this trip good for beginners? 

Yes, but it is a full programme. Beginners may prefer a shorter trip. 

Can I add the coast? 

Yes. Arabuko-Sokoke and Mida Creek can be added at the end.
