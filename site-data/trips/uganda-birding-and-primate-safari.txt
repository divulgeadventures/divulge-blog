## 
This Uganda birding and primate safari is a balanced trip for travellers who love birds but also want Uganda’s famous primates. In 12 days you search for the shoebill, trek mountain gorillas and track chimpanzees. 
Uganda has more than 1,000 recorded bird species in a country smaller than many others in Africa. Short drives between very different habitats make it ideal for birders. 

## Why choose this Uganda birding and primate safari? 
- Mabamba Swamp: a canoe trip through the papyrus to find the shoebill. 
- Lake Mburo: African finfoot, zebras and savannah birds. 
- Bwindi: Albertine Rift endemics and a mountain gorilla trek. 
- Queen Elizabeth: Kazinga Channel boat cruise and tree-climbing lions in Ishasha. 
- Kibale: chimpanzee tracking and forest birding. 

## Where you stay 
Lodges include the Sheraton in Kampala, Mihingo Lodge or Mantana Tented Camp at Lake Mburo, Gorilla Forest Camp or Buhoma Homestead in Bwindi, Mweya Safari Lodge or Jacana Safari Lodge in Queen Elizabeth, and Primate Lodge in Kibale, or similar. 

### Group size 
We keep groups small, with a maximum of six guests per vehicle, so everyone has a window seat and time with the guide. 

## Best time for a Uganda birding and primate safari 
Uganda is a year-round destination. June to August and December to February are drier and best for trekking. The wetter months bring lush forest, breeding birds and fewer visitors. 
Plan ahead with our Bwindi gorilla trekking guide and Uganda national parks guide , or see all Uganda safaris . Permits are issued by the Uganda Wildlife Authority .

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Entebbe 

Meet your birding guide at Entebbe Airport and transfer to your hotel. Early arrivals can bird the Entebbe Botanical Gardens. 

Day 2 : 
Mabamba Swamp 

Drive to Mabamba Swamp and explore the channels by canoe with local guides, looking for the shoebill, papyrus gonolek and lesser jacana. 

Day 3 : 
Lake Mburo National Park 

Drive west with a stop at the equator. Afternoon birding and game viewing at Lake Mburo, with zebras, elands and African finfoot. 

Day 4 : 
Lake Mburo to Bwindi 

Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, birding along the way. Overnight at Gorilla Forest Camp or Buhoma Homestead. 

Day 5 : 
Birding in Bwindi 

A full day on the Buhoma forest trails for Albertine Rift endemics, turacos and sunbirds. 

Day 6 : 
Gorilla trekking 

Trek with rangers to a habituated gorilla family and spend one hour with them. The trek can take most of the day. 

Day 7 : 
Bwindi to Queen Elizabeth via Ishasha 

Drive north through the Ishasha sector, famous for tree-climbing lions. Overnight at Mweya Safari Lodge or Jacana Safari Lodge. 

Day 8 : 
Kazinga Channel boat cruise 

Morning birding in the park, then an afternoon boat cruise on the Kazinga Channel for skimmers, spoonbills, plovers and hippos. 

Day 9 : 
Queen Elizabeth to Kibale 

Morning game drive and birding, then drive to Kibale and check in at Primate Lodge. 

Day 10 : 
Chimpanzee tracking in Kibale 

Early chimpanzee tracking, then forest birding for pittas, greenbuls and sunbirds. 

Day 11 : 
Kibale to Kampala 

Drive back to Kampala with birding stops along the way. Overnight at the Sheraton. 

Day 12 : 
Departure 

Transfer to Entebbe Airport for your flight.

Are gorilla and chimpanzee permits included? 

Yes. One gorilla permit and one chimpanzee tracking permit per person are included. 

Will I see a shoebill? 

Mabamba Swamp is one of the most reliable shoebill sites in Uganda, and most visitors see one, but sightings can never be guaranteed. 

How fit do I need to be for gorilla trekking? 

Moderate fitness is needed. Treks can take from one hour to most of the day on steep, muddy trails. Porters are available to help. 

Which lodges are used on the Uganda birding and primate safari? 

Sheraton Kampala, Mihingo Lodge or Mantana Tented Camp, Gorilla Forest Camp or Buhoma Homestead, Mweya Safari Lodge or Jacana Safari Lodge, and Primate Lodge Kibale, or similar. 

What is the maximum group size? 

Six guests per vehicle, so everyone has a window seat. 

What is the minimum age for gorilla trekking? 

You must be at least 15 years old to trek gorillas in Uganda. 

Can I add more birding days? 

Yes. We can add Semliki, Murchison Falls or Ruhija for more species.
