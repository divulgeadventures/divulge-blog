## 
This 7 day Kenya incentive safari rewards top performers with a fly-in trip to three of Kenya’s best wildlife areas. Light aircraft take your group from Nairobi to Samburu, the Masai Mara and Amboseli, so there are no long drives, and each stop has luxury camps, private game drives and time to relax together. 

## Why choose a 7 day Kenya incentive safari? 
An incentive trip has to feel special from the first day. Flying between parks saves about 15 hours of road travel and gives guests views of the Rift Valley and Mount Kenya from the air. Each day ends with shared moments that build the team: a bush lunch in the Mara, a sundowner facing Kilimanjaro and evenings around the fire. 
We can add a private dinner, an awards evening, branded welcome packs or a short meeting session at a lodge. Tell us what your programme needs and we will plan it into the itinerary. 

## What does the incentive safari itinerary include? 
You spend two nights at Sasaab in Samburu, two nights at Angama Mara above the Mara Triangle and two nights at Tortilis Camp in Amboseli, or similar. Days 4 and 6 are full days in the parks, and an optional balloon flight can be added in the Mara. 

### How large can the group be? 
Scheduled light aircraft suit small and medium groups. For larger groups we arrange a private charter so everyone flies together, and we can split the group between two camps of the same standard if one camp is full. 
See our guide to luxury safari lodges and our Samburu vs Masai Mara comparison for more on each stop. 

## Who is the 7 day kenya incentive safari best for? 
The 7 day kenya incentive safari is best for companies rewarding top sales teams, dealers, partners or long-serving staff. We plan the programme with your HR or events team, so the 7 day kenya incentive safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## Can the 7 day Kenya incentive safari be shortened? 
Yes. A 4 or 5 day version can skip Samburu or Amboseli and keep the Masai Mara as the centrepiece. We can also add a beach extension in Diani or Zanzibar, or a team-building day near Nairobi before or after the safari.

Price on request. Group prices depend on your dates, the number of travellers and the rooms you need, so we quote every group personally. Day 1 : 
Fly from Nairobi to Samburu 

Your 7 day kenya incentive safari begins today. Transfer to Wilson Airport for a morning flight north to Samburu. Your guide meets the group at the airstrip for the drive to camp, followed by an afternoon game drive along the Ewaso Ng'iro River. Overnight at Sasaab or similar. 

Day 2 : 
Samburu 

Morning and afternoon game drives in search of reticulated giraffe, Grevy's zebra, gerenuk and elephants, with a guided walk and free time by the pool overlooking the river. Overnight at Sasaab or similar. 

Day 3 : 
Fly from Samburu to the Masai Mara 

Fly to the Mara Triangle via Nairobi. After settling into camp, head out on an afternoon game drive across the plains. Overnight at Angama Mara or similar. 

Day 4 : 
Full day in the Masai Mara 

A full day of private game drives with a bush lunch set up on the plains, a highlight for most incentive groups. An early-morning balloon flight can be added. Overnight at Angama Mara or similar. 

Day 5 : 
Fly from the Masai Mara to Amboseli 

Take a morning game drive, then fly to Amboseli via Nairobi. Afternoon drive among the elephant herds. Overnight at Tortilis Camp or similar. 

Day 6 : 
Amboseli 

Sunrise game drive for clear views of Kilimanjaro and the park's elephants, then free time at camp. In the evening, the group gathers for a sundowner with the mountain in view. Overnight at Tortilis Camp or similar. 

Day 7 : 
Fly back to Nairobi 

An early game drive, then the flight back to Wilson Airport, where we transfer the group to your hotel or Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. This is the end of your 7 day kenya incentive safari.

What is included in the 7 day Kenya incentive safari? 

The 7 day Kenya incentive safari includes scheduled light-aircraft flights, airstrip transfers, luxury camps on full board, game drives with professional guides, all park fees and drinking water on drives. Balloon flights, premium drinks and tips are extra. 

Can you charter a plane for a large corporate group? 

Yes. For larger groups we arrange a private charter so the whole group flies together and keeps to its own timetable. Charter costs depend on aircraft size and routing, and we include them in your quote. 

How much luggage can each guest bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. We can store extra luggage in Nairobi or book an extra seat for bags if needed. 

Can we hold a meeting or awards dinner during the safari? 

Yes. Most luxury camps can host a private dinner, an awards evening or a short meeting in a quiet space. Tell us your programme early so we can confirm the venue and set-up with the camp. 

How far ahead should we book a corporate incentive safari? 

Book 6 to 12 months ahead for July to October, when the Masai Mara is busiest and camps fill early. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the rooms and flights, and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel.
