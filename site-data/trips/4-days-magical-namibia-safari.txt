## Overview 
This 4 day Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay safari is a guided loop from Windhoek with 1 night near Sossusvlei, 1 night in Swakopmund and 1 night in Windhoek. It covers the Sossusvlei dunes, Deadvlei, the Walvis Bay lagoon and a choice of a dolphin and seal cruise or Sandwich Harbour. It runs all year, costs from USD 1,990 per adult and suits first-time visitors and short stays. 
The trip travels by 4×4 safari vehicle with an English or bilingual driver guide, staying in 3-star and 4-star accommodation, and starts and ends at Hosea Kutako International Airport. 

## Why choose a 4 day Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay safari? 
The 4 day Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay safari pairs Namibia’s two big contrasts in a long weekend: the red dunes and white clay pans of the Namib Desert, then the cold Atlantic coast, where flamingos feed in the Walvis Bay lagoon and seals and dolphins follow the boats. 
The route also shows you the country between them, from the Khomas Hochland highlands and the Spreetshoogte Pass to the desert stop at Solitaire, so even the drives are part of the sightseeing. 
- Short stays: desert and coast in 4 days, from the airport and back. 
- Photographers: dune light at Sossusvlei, Deadvlei and Sandwich Harbour. 
- Marine wildlife fans: Cape fur seals, dolphins and flamingos at Walvis Bay. 

## What to expect on the 4 day Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay safari 
Day 1 starts with a meet-and-greet at Hosea Kutako International Airport, then a drive of about 370 km over the Kupferberg and Spreetshoogte passes onto the Namib plains, with dinner and the night at Le Mirage Desert Lodge near Sossusvlei. 
Day 2 begins early in the Sossusvlei dunes and at Deadvlei, before a drive of about 370 km to the coast with a stop in Solitaire and flamingos on the Walvis Bay lagoons. You stay at the Atlantic Villa Guest House in Swakopmund. 
On day 3 you choose the Dolphin and Seal Cruise or the Sandwich Harbour tour, both ending around midday, or leave early for a Bushmen walk in Usakos. You then drive about 360 km to The Weinberg Hotel in Windhoek. Day 4 offers a relaxed morning or a Windhoek city tour before your airport transfer. 

### Is 4 days enough for Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay? 
Four days is enough to see the main sights of Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay, but each of the first three days includes a drive of about 360 to 370 km, partly on gravel. If you want a second desert night and a game drive, compare our 5 day Sossusvlei and Swakopmund tour . Our best time to visit Namibia guide explains the seasons month by month.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Experience the ultimate Namibia Safari on this 4-day adventure. Discover the towering Sossusvlei Sand Dunes and the haunting beauty of Deadvlei. Visit Walvis Bay for a spectacular dolphin and seal cruise before exploring the coastal charm of Swakopmund. 

Day 01 : 
Start of Magical Namibia Safari 

Upon landing at Hosea Kutako International Airport, you will be greeted by our professional representative from Divulge Adventures for a brief orientation ready to start your Magical Namibia Safari adventure. 
Your Namibia safari begins as you depart Windhoek, ascending the scenic Kupferberg Pass through the Khomas Hochland highlands. 
This rugged terrain, sitting up to 2,000 meters above sea level, offers stunning vistas of deep, eroded valleys. 
After a brief photo stop, you will descend the dramatic Spreetshoogte Pass onto the vast Namib Desert plains. The drive continues south, framed by the majestic Naukluft Mountains and the first glimpse of towering orange dunes. 
This 370-kilometer journey serves as a breathtaking introduction to your Namibia safari before you arrive for dinner and your overnight stay at Le Mirage Desert Lodge Sossusvlei. 

Day 02 : 
Sossusvlei Sand Dune - Deadvlei Swakopmund 

After enjoying an early breakfast, you will venture deep into the ancient Sossusvlei desert to scale its magnificent dunes. 
This highlight of your Namibia safari continues at Deadvlei, where skeletal camel thorn trees stand frozen in time against a backdrop of brilliant blue skies and vivid orange sands. 
After exploring these clay pans, you will embark on a 370-kilometer journey toward the coast. Along the way, enjoy a brief stop in the quirky settlement of Solitaire, famous for its iconic apple pie. 
As you approach the Atlantic, keep an eye out for elegant flamingos gathered in the Walvis Bay lagoons. 
Your day of discovery on this Namibia safari ends in the coastal town of Swakopmund, where you can choose between optional activities or a sunset beach stroll before retiring to the Atlantic Villa Guest House , Swakopmund. 

Day 03 : 
Swakopmund - Windhoek 

Start your morning with a selection of exhilarating coastal activities before heading back to the capital. You can choose to join a "Dolphin and Seal Cruise" or explore the breathtaking Sandwich Harbour, with both excursions concluding around midday. 
These experiences offer a perfect maritime contrast to your Namibia safari adventures. Alternatively, those preferring an early departure can opt for an insightful "Bushmen walk" in Usakos. 
Afterward, you will travel approximately 360 kilometers across the changing landscapes, arriving back in the city by late evening. Whether you spent the morning at sea or in the bush, this final leg of your Namibia safari concludes with a luxurious dinner and stay at The Weinberg Hotel , Windhoek. 

Day 04 : 
End of Magical Namibia Safari 

After a final breakfast, enjoy a relaxing morning at your hotel or take the opportunity to explore the landmarks of the capital on a Windhoek city tour. 
When it is time for your departure, you will be transferred to Hosea Kutako International Airport for your outbound flight. This seamless transition marks the conclusion of your remarkable 4-day magical Namibia safari, signifying the end of safari.

When is the best time for the 4 day Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay safari? 

The 4 day Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay safari runs all year, and the trip page does not set a season. May to September is cooler for climbing the Sossusvlei dunes, while December to February is hot inland. The coast at Walvis Bay and Swakopmund stays mild, often with morning fog, so pack a warm layer for the boat cruise. 

What is included in the 4 day Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay safari? 

The 4 day Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay safari includes airport transfers, a 4×4 safari vehicle with an English or bilingual driver guide, park and conservation fees, bottled water and the listed activities, including Sossusvlei, Sandwich Harbour and the Pink Salt Lake. Flights, visas, insurance, tips, premium drinks, optional activities and extra nights are not included. 

Where do you stay on the 4 day Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay safari? 

On this Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay safari you stay 1 night at Le Mirage Desert Lodge near Sossusvlei, 1 night at the Atlantic Villa Guest House in Swakopmund and 1 night at The Weinberg Hotel in Windhoek. We confirm each property in your written quote, based on availability for your dates. 

Is the 4 day Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay safari suitable for families? 

Yes, families can join the Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay safari, and the dune climbs are optional. Children usually enjoy the seals and dolphins on the Walvis Bay cruise. The first three days each include a drive of about 360 to 370 km, so tell us your children’s ages and we will plan stops to suit them.
