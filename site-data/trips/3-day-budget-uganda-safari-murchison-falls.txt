## 
This budget Uganda safari shows you Murchison Falls National Park, Uganda’s largest park, in three days from Kampala. Stop to track white rhinos on foot at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary on the way, then enjoy a game drive on the northern savannah, a boat trip up the Nile to the foot of the falls, and a walk to the top of Murchison Falls where the river forces its way through a narrow gorge. You stay at the simple Red Chilli Rest Camp inside the park. 
Price: from USD 570 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Rhino tracking on foot at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary 
- Game drive for giraffes, elephants and lions 
- Boat trip on the Nile to the foot of Murchison Falls 
- Hike to the top of the falls 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Kampala to Murchison Falls 

Morning drive north with a stop at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary to track white rhinos on foot. Continue to Murchison Falls National Park. Dinner and overnight at Red Chilli Rest Camp. 

Day 2 : 
Game drive and Nile boat trip 

Early crossing of the Nile for a game drive on the northern bank, then an afternoon boat trip to the foot of the falls. Dinner and overnight at Red Chilli Rest Camp. 

Day 3 : 
Top of the falls and return to Kampala 

Visit the top of Murchison Falls, then drive back to Kampala in the afternoon.

How much does this budget Uganda safari cost? 

It starts from USD 570 per adult with a shared vehicle, based on low-season rates. We confirm the price for your dates when you enquire. 

Can we add chimpanzee tracking? 

Yes. Budongo Forest, on the way to Murchison, offers chimpanzee tracking for an extra fee. 

When is the best time for Murchison Falls? 

December to February and June to September are the driest months for game drives.
