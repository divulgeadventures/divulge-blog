## 
This 10 day Kilimanjaro Lemosho route is the route we recommend for the best chance of reaching Uhuru Peak (5,895 m). The 10-day trip starts and ends in Moshi. Eight days on the mountain mean nights at Mti Mkubwa, Shira 1 and Shira 2 before joining the southern circuit at Lava Tower, Barranco and Karanga, with acclimatisation walks most afternoons. 
It suits first-time climbers, older trekkers and anyone who wants to enjoy the mountain at an unhurried pace. 

## Why choose the 10 day Kilimanjaro Lemosho route? 
Time is the most important factor in reaching the summit of Kilimanjaro. The 10 day Lemosho route gains height slowly and uses ‘climb high, sleep low’ walks on almost every day, so summit success rates are among the highest of any itinerary. It is also one of the most beautiful and least crowded routes. 

## What to expect on the 10 day Kilimanjaro Lemosho route 
Day 2 walks through rainforest to Mti Mkubwa (about 2,780 m), day 3 climbs to Shira 1 (about 3,505 m), and day 4 crosses the plateau to Shira 2 (about 3,900 m). Day 5 goes over Lava Tower to Barranco, day 6 to Karanga with an afternoon walk, and day 7 to Barafu. Summit night reaches Uhuru Peak at sunrise, then you descend to Millennium Camp and walk out to Mweka Gate on day 9.

Day 1 : 
Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport 

Transfer to your hotel in Moshi. In the evening your guide gives a briefing and checks your gear. 

Day 2 : 
Londorossi Gate to Mti Mkubwa Camp 

Drive to Londorossi Gate (about 2,200 m) and walk 3 to 5 hours through rainforest to Mti Mkubwa Camp at about 2,780 m. 

Day 3 : 
Mti Mkubwa to Shira 1 Camp 

Leave the forest and climb through heath to Shira 1 Camp (about 3,505 m), 5 to 6 hours. 

Day 4 : 
Shira 1 to Shira 2 Camp 

Cross the Shira Plateau to Shira 2 Camp (about 3,900 m), 3 to 4 hours, with an afternoon acclimatisation walk. 

Day 5 : 
Shira 2 to Lava Tower and Barranco Camp 

Climb to Lava Tower (about 4,630 m), then descend to Barranco Camp (about 3,960 m), 6 to 8 hours. 

Day 6 : 
Barranco Camp to Karanga Camp 

Scramble up the Barranco Wall and continue to Karanga Camp (about 4,035 m), with an afternoon acclimatisation walk. 

Day 7 : 
Karanga Camp to Barafu Camp 

A short climb to Barafu Camp (about 4,640 m), with an optional walk towards Kosovo Camp. Early dinner and rest. 

Day 8 : 
Summit night: Uhuru Peak, then Millennium Camp 

Leave around midnight for Stella Point and Uhuru Peak (5,895 m) at sunrise, then descend to Millennium Camp (about 3,820 m), 8 to 12 hours. 

Day 9 : 
Millennium Camp to Mweka Gate and Moshi 

Walk down to Mweka Gate, collect your summit certificate and transfer to Moshi. 

Day 10 : 
Departure 

Transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport for your flight home, or continue on safari.

Why choose the 10 day Kilimanjaro Lemosho route? 

The 10 day Kilimanjaro Lemosho route gives your body the most time to adjust to altitude, with two nights on the Shira Plateau and acclimatisation walks most afternoons. That usually means a higher chance of reaching Uhuru Peak and a more enjoyable climb. 

Is the 10 day Lemosho route suitable for first-timers? 

Yes. The 10 day Lemosho route is one of the best choices for first-time high-altitude trekkers, because the slow pace reduces the risk of altitude sickness. You still need good fitness for summit night. 

When is the best time to climb the Lemosho route? 

The best months are January to mid-March and June to October, when the mountain is driest. January to February is often clearer and colder; July to September is busier. 

What are the daily walking times? 

Most days on the 10 day Kilimanjaro Lemosho route are 3 to 6 hours, with optional afternoon acclimatisation walks. Summit night is the longest, at 10 to 14 hours including the descent. 

Can a group climb the Lemosho route together? 

Yes. The Lemosho route is popular with clubs, charities and groups of friends. We add guides so the group can split by pace on summit night, and plan rooms and transfers for everyone. Contact us with your numbers and dates.
