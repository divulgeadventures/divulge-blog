## Overview 
Experience one of Africa’s most remarkable wilderness areas with an unforgettable journey through the breathtaking Okavanga Delta Exploration .This unique safari offers a perfect combination of water-based adventures, wildlife encounters, and immersive nature experiences in a pristine ecosystem known for its incredible biodiversity and scenic beauty. 
As you glide silently through the crystal-clear channels aboard a traditional mokoro (dugout canoe), you will discover the peaceful waterways that make the Okavango Delta Exploration special. The gentle pace allows you to appreciate the tranquility of the surroundings while observing birds, aquatic life, and wildlife along the riverbanks. The absence of engine noise creates an intimate connection with nature, making every moment on the water truly memorable. 

The Okavanga Exploration continues with guided walking safaris led by experienced local guides. These walks provide a unique opportunity to learn about the Delta’s flora and fauna, animal tracks, medicinal plants, and the intricate relationships within this fragile ecosystem. Walking through the wilderness offers a thrilling perspective that cannot be experienced from a vehicle alone. 

Game drives complement the water and walking activities, allowing guests to cover larger areas in search of the Delta’s abundant wildlife. Depending on the season, visitors may encounter elephants, buffalo, giraffes, zebras, antelopes, hippos, crocodiles, and predators such as lions, leopards, and wild dogs. The Delta is also a paradise for bird enthusiasts, with hundreds of bird species inhabiting its wetlands and woodlands. 

Beyond wildlife viewing, the Okavango Delta offers stunning landscapes characterized by winding channels, palm-fringed islands, floodplains, lagoons, and expansive grasslands. Spectacular sunrises and sunsets paint the skies with vibrant colors, creating perfect photographic opportunities. 

This exploration is ideal for travelers seeking a blend of adventure, relaxation, and authentic wilderness experiences. Whether drifting through serene waterways, tracking wildlife on foot, or enjoying exciting game drives, the Okavango Delta Exploration promises an extraordinary safari experience that showcases the beauty and diversity of Botswana’s natural heritage.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
Maun to Moremi Game Reserve 

Your will be met by our representative from Divulge and will pick you up in Maun and a 3 hours drive will lead you to Moremi Game Reserve . You will arrive around lunch time and you will have an afternoon game drive. Moremi is an area that is rich in wildlife such as cats and different types of antelopes. 

Day 02 : 
Okavango Exploration 

After continental breakfast we will set out to experience the famous Moremi Game Reserve. There will be an afternoon boat cruise along the Moanachira channel which will be exploring the meandaring channels of the Okavango. 

Day 03 : 
Moremi Game Reserve to Khwai Concession 

For another wildlife experience a morning drive will lead us to Khwai Concession. On this day we take packed lunch and do a full day trip to allow the back up team to get to khwai and set the camp before we arrive. 

Day 04 : 
Explore the Concession 

We rise early and after the healthy breakfast we set out to explore and experience the abundance of wildlife in this pristine area. Mokoro excursion will be the afternoon activity with a beautiful sundowner drinks after the excursion. After dinner we will have a night drive try to locate nocturnal creatures such as porcupinies, serval cats, spring hares and many more 

Day 05 : 
Khwai to Maun 

After breakfast the tour will lead us to Maun,marking the end of your Okavango Exploration experience,where the guide will drop you where you will be staying or drop you at the airport if you will be flying out.
