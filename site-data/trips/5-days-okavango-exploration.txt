## Overview 
This 5 day Okavango Moremi and Khwai safari is a 5-day, 4-night trip from Maun to Moremi Game Reserve and the Khwai Concession in Botswana’s Okavango region. You spend 2 nights in Moremi and 2 nights in Khwai in tented camps, with game drives, a boat cruise on the Moanachira channel, a mokoro excursion and a night drive. The price starts from USD 3,600 per adult, and it suits wildlife lovers and photographers. 
All meals and drinks, park fees, activities and laundry are included, and our back-up team sets up camp in Khwai ahead of your arrival. 

## Why choose a 5 day Okavango Moremi and Khwai safari? 
Moremi Game Reserve protects the eastern side of the Okavango Delta, where permanent water, floodplains and woodland meet. That mix supports big cats, many types of antelope and a huge range of birds, so game drives here are varied from one hour to the next. 
The Khwai Concession borders Moremi and allows activities the reserve does not, including night drives and mokoro trips. Combining the two gives you day drives, water activities and a chance to see nocturnal animals in one short safari. 
- Wildlife lovers: 2 full areas known for cats, antelope and wild dogs. 
- Photographers: boat, mokoro and vehicle angles on the same trip. 
- Curious travellers: a night drive in Khwai for porcupines, servals and springhares. 

## What to expect on the 5 day Okavango Moremi and Khwai safari 
Day 1 starts with a pick-up in Maun and a drive of about 3 hours to Moremi Game Reserve, arriving around lunchtime for an afternoon game drive. Day 2 is spent exploring Moremi, with an afternoon boat cruise along the Moanachira channel through the Okavango’s winding waterways. 
On day 3 you take a full-day game drive with a packed lunch from Moremi to the Khwai Concession, while the back-up team goes ahead to set up camp. Day 4 is a full day in Khwai: a morning game drive, an afternoon mokoro excursion with sundowner drinks, and a night drive after dinner. 
On day 5 you drive back to Maun after breakfast, and the guide drops you at your hotel or the airport. 

### Is 5 days enough for Moremi and Khwai? 
Five days is enough to see Moremi and Khwai properly, because you get 2 nights in each area and 3 full days of activities. The dry season, roughly May to October, usually brings the best game viewing as animals gather near water. Our best time to visit Botswana guide explains the seasons in detail.

When is the best time for the 5 day Okavango Moremi and Khwai safari? 

The 5 day Okavango Moremi and Khwai safari runs all year, but the dry season from about May to October usually gives the best game viewing, as wildlife gathers near permanent water in Moremi and Khwai. The green season from November to April is quieter, with lush scenery, newborn animals and excellent birding.
