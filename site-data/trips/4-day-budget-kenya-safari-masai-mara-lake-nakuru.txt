## 
This budget Kenya safari covers the Masai Mara and Lake Nakuru in four days without cutting the wildlife. You stay in simple, comfortable tented camps: Enchoro Wildlife Camp near the Mara’s Talek gate and Punda Milia Camp at Lake Naivasha. Game drives run in a 4×4 safari vehicle with a pop-up roof, shared with other travellers to keep the cost down. It suits backpackers, students and first-time visitors who want the Mara on a smaller budget. 
Price: from USD 880 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Two nights near the Masai Mara with full-day game drives 
- Game drive in Lake Nakuru National Park for rhinos and flamingos 
- Simple tented camps instead of costly lodges 
- Shared 4x4 safari vehicle with pop-up roof 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Nairobi to the Masai Mara 

Morning pick-up in Nairobi and drive via the Great Rift Valley viewpoint to the Masai Mara. Afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight at Enchoro Wildlife Camp. 

Day 2 : 
Masai Mara 

Full day of game drives with a picnic lunch, looking for lions, elephants, cheetahs and big herds. Dinner and overnight at Enchoro Wildlife Camp. 

Day 3 : 
Masai Mara to Lake Naivasha 

Morning game drive, then drive to Lake Naivasha. Optional boat ride. Dinner and overnight at Punda Milia Camp. 

Day 4 : 
Lake Nakuru to Nairobi 

Game drive in Lake Nakuru National Park for rhinos, buffaloes and birds, then drive back to Nairobi in the afternoon.

How much does this budget Kenya safari cost? 

It starts from USD 880 per adult, based on low-season rates with a shared vehicle. A private vehicle costs more; ask us for a quote. 

Is a budget safari still good for wildlife? 

Yes. You visit the same parks and do the same game drives as on dearer trips; the savings come from simpler camps and sharing the vehicle. 

When is the best time to go? 

July to October for the migration in the Mara, or January to March for dry weather and lower prices.
