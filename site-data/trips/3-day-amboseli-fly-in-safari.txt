## 
This Amboseli fly-in safari is a short, scenic escape from Nairobi. The flight takes under an hour, and on a clear day Kilimanjaro fills the window as you land. 
Amboseli is famous for large elephant families, many with long tusks, walking across the plains in front of the mountain. 

## Why choose this Amboseli fly-in safari? 
- Quick and easy: skip the four to five hour drive. 
- Kilimanjaro views: best at sunrise and sometimes again at sunset. 
- Elephants: some of the best elephant viewing in Africa. 
- Observation Hill: a short climb for views over the swamps. 

## Where you stay 
Two nights at Ol Tukai Lodge, or similar, in the heart of the park. 

## Best time for a Amboseli fly-in safari 
June to October and January to February are the driest months, with the best chance of clear mountain views. The rainy months bring green grass and fewer visitors. 
Combine it with the Mara on our Masai Mara and Amboseli fly-in safari , or browse all flight packages . Park details are on the Kenya Wildlife Service website.

How long is the flight to Amboseli? 

Under an hour from Wilson Airport. 

How much luggage can I bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. Ask us if you need extra space. 

Will I see Kilimanjaro on the Amboseli fly-in safari? 

Very likely in the early morning. Clouds often cover the mountain later in the day. 

Is Amboseli good for big cats? 

Lions and cheetahs live there, but elephants are the main attraction. 

Can I fly to Amboseli from the coast? 

Yes. See our Diani to Amboseli and Masai Mara fly-in safari. 

Is emergency evacuation cover available? 

Yes. We recommend Flying Doctors evacuation cover for every safari and can arrange it for you. 

What should I wear? 

Neutral colours, a warm layer for early mornings and a hat for the midday sun.
