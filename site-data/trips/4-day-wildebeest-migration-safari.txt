## Overview 
This 4 day Serengeti wildebeest migration safari is a short fly-in trip to the northern Serengeti in Tanzania. You fly from Arusha to Kogatende airstrip and spend 3 nights at Mara Mara Tented Lodge near the Mara River, with a sunrise balloon flight on the itinerary. River crossings are seasonal, usually from about July to October. It costs from USD 3,392 per adult and suits travellers short on time. 
It suits couples, photographers and first-time visitors who want to see the Great Migration without a long road journey, travelling in a modified 4WD Land Cruiser with a photographic roof and staying on full board. 

## Why choose a 4 day Serengeti wildebeest migration safari? 
The northern Serengeti around Kogatende is where the migration herds cross the Mara River in the dry season, with crocodiles waiting in the water and lions and cheetahs following the herds. Flying in puts you at the river on day 1, so almost all of your time goes to game drives rather than travel. 
Three nights at one lodge give you two full days plus an arrival afternoon to wait at the crossing points. Crossings happen on the animals’ schedule, so more time at the river means better chances. 
- Short on time: a fly-in safari that fits the migration into 4 days. 
- Photographers: a Land Cruiser with a photographic roof and full days at the river. 
- Add-on travellers: easy to combine with Zanzibar or a Kilimanjaro trip. 

## What to expect on the 4 day Serengeti wildebeest migration safari 
Day 1 starts with a flight from Arusha to Kogatende airstrip, where your driver-guide meets you for a game drive in the northern Serengeti. You spend the night at Mara Mara Tented Lodge. 
Day 2 is a full day of game drives in Serengeti North along the Mara River, looking for crossings, elephants, giraffes, cheetahs and large lion prides, with lunch at camp or a packed bush lunch. Day 3 begins with a sunrise hot air balloon flight over the Serengeti and a bush breakfast, then another full day exploring the Mara River area. 
On day 4 you have breakfast at camp and a morning game drive on the way to Kogatende airstrip, then fly back to Arusha, with a transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport for your onward flight. 

### When is the best time for a Serengeti river crossing safari? 
The Serengeti river crossings in the north usually happen between about July and October, when the herds move between the Serengeti and Kenya’s Masai Mara. Crossings are never guaranteed, because the herds decide when to cross. Read our Serengeti migration guide and our Great Migration month by month guide to see where the herds usually are.

## Day by Day

Day 01 : 
Arrival in Serengeti North ( Kogatende) 

Upon arrival at the Kogatende Airstrip, you’ll be warmly welcomed by our friendly and experienced driver-guide. Your adventure begins right away with an exhilarating full-day game drive in the northern part of Serengeti National Park, home to the world-famous Willdebeest Great Migration. 
​ 
This region offers a front-row seat to dramatic river crossings (seasonal), large herds of wildebeest and zebras, and incredible predator action. 
It’s also a stronghold for the Big Five, with an impressive lion population exceeding 3,000. Get ready for an unforgettable start to your safari experience. Dinner and overnight at Mara mara Tented Lodge 

Day 02 : 
Serengeti National Park (North Kogatende)- The Ultimate Wildebeest Migration Safari 

Start your day with a hearty breakfast at the camp before heading out for another thrilling full-day game drive in Serengeti North. This region offers some of the best chances to witness the Great Wildebeest Migration Safari as herds of wildebeest and zebras brave the crocodile-infested waters of the Mara River. 
​ 
Keep your eyes peeled for resident wildlife, including elephant herds, giraffes, cheetahs, and the impressive lion prides that can number up to 30 individuals. 
​ 
Enjoy a delicious lunch either back at the camp or as a hot packed bush lunch in the heart of the wilderness. 
As the sun sets over the savannah, return to camp for a freshly prepared dinner and a well-earned night of rest. Dinner and overnight at Mara Tented Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Serengeti National Park- Kogatende Wildebeest Migration Safari 

Rise early for a once-in-a-lifetime experience: a hot air balloon safari over the Serengeti. After landing, enjoy a bush breakfast in the wild,an unforgettable start to your day. 
​ 
Continue your adventure with a full day dedicated to exploring the Mara River area, where the Wildebeest Migration Safari reaches one of its most dramatic moments. 
Watch in awe as wildebeest and zebras risk it all to cross the crocodile-laden waters, a true wildlife spectacle. 
​ 
Return to your camp in the evening for a relaxing dinner under the stars and another peaceful night surrounded by the raw beauty of the Serengeti. Dinner and overnight at Mara Tented Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Morning Game Drive- Departure to Arusha 

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast at the camp, soaking in the final sights of your Wildbeest Migration Safari and sounds of the wild. Afterward, embark on a scenic morning game drive en route to the Kogatende Airstrip, offering one last chance to spot Serengeti’s incredible wildlife. 
As your safari adventure comes to an end, carry with you unforgettable memories of the sweeping plains, diverse wildlife, and the awe-inspiring Mara River crossings. 

Pickup at Arusha airport and drop off Kilimanjaro International Airport.
