## 
This 8 day Botswana photography club safari is planned for camera clubs and photography societies. Members photograph Moremi’s floodplains, cruise the Moanachira Channel, glide through the reeds by mokoro and spend three nights in the Khwai Concession, one of the best areas in Botswana for predators. 

## Why book an 8 day Botswana photography club safari? 
Botswana gives photographers wild scenes with few vehicles. Khwai is a private concession, so guides can drive off-road for a closer angle, and there are chances for night drives and mokoro trips that you cannot do in many national parks. 
Three nights in Khwai and two in Moremi give time to wait for the action, and evenings around the campfire are perfect for image reviews. 

## What does the club itinerary include? 
One night on arrival in the bush, two in Moremi Game Reserve, three in the Khwai Concession and one in the Kaziikini area, with the flight out on day 8. 

### How many photographers ride in each vehicle? 
We keep fewer photographers in each vehicle than on a standard safari, so everyone has a window and room for long lenses. Larger clubs travel in several vehicles that cover different areas.

## Who is the 8 day botswana photography club safari best for? 
The 8 day botswana photography club safari is best for hiking, photography, Rotary, Lions and alumni clubs that want to travel together. We plan vehicles and rooms around your members, so the 8 day botswana photography club safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## How many members can join the 8 day Botswana photography club safari? 
A club of 6 to 16 members works well, travelling in two to four vehicles. Larger clubs can split into two groups on different dates.

Day 1 : 
Start of the safari 

Your 8 day botswana photography club safari begins today. Meet your guide and head into the bush for an afternoon game drive, looking for elephants, buffalo and giraffe. Enjoy sundowners as the sun sets, then dinner around the campfire. 

Day 2 : 
Moremi Game Reserve 

Early morning and afternoon game drives in Moremi when wildlife is most active. A boat trip along the Moanachira Channel can be added. 

Day 3 : 
Moremi Game Reserve 

A second day in Moremi, with time to wait at sightings and photograph the floodplains in the best light. Evening image review around the campfire. 

Day 4 : 
Khwai Concession 

Drive to the Khwai Concession for afternoon game drives in search of predators. 

Day 5 : 
Khwai Concession 

Game drives in Khwai, with a mokoro (dugout canoe) trip through the quiet channels for low-angle photos. 

Day 6 : 
Khwai Concession 

A final full day in Khwai, planned around the best sightings of the trip so far. 

Day 7 : 
Khwai to Kaziikini 

Game drive on the way to the Kaziikini area, then relax around the campfire. 

Day 8 : 
End of the safari 

A last game drive in the Kaziikini area, then return to camp before the transfer to your flight. This is the end of your 8 day botswana photography club safari.

Who is the 8 day Botswana photography club safari designed for? 

The 8 day Botswana photography club safari suits camera clubs and photography societies of all levels. Guides position for light and angles, and evenings are free for image reviews. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 8 day botswana photography club safari. 

What camera gear should members bring? 

A long zoom such as 150-600 mm, a wide-angle lens for landscapes, spare batteries, plenty of memory cards and a dust cover. Light aircraft limit luggage, so pack in soft bags. 

Does the club leader travel free? 

On group bookings the seventh place is free for every six paying travellers. Most clubs give that place to the trip leader or the person who runs the image reviews. 

Can members pay separately? 

Yes. Each member can pay their own share. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the group’s places, and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 8 day botswana photography club safari. 

When is the best time for a photography safari in Botswana? 

June to October is the dry season, when animals gather near water. November to March has dramatic skies, green landscapes and newborn animals. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 8 day botswana photography club safari.
