## Overview 
This 4 day Mount Kenya Sirimon Chogoria trek climbs the gentle Sirimon route via Judmaier Camp (3,300 m) and Shipton’s Camp (4,200 m), reaches Point Lenana (4,985 m) at sunrise on day 3, then descends the Chogoria route to Nairobi. It is a guided camping and banda climb from Nairobi with porters, a cook and full board. Price on request; it suits fit, experienced hikers short on time. 
The trek is graded Expert on the page. It has only one night at 3,300 m before you sleep at 4,200 m, and summit day is 11 to 12 hours, so it is the fast version of this traverse. You need your own sleeping bag and climbing gear, which are not included. 

## Why choose a 4 day Mount Kenya Sirimon Chogoria trek? 
The Sirimon and Chogoria routes are a classic pairing: you go up the gradual, drier northern side of Mount Kenya and come down the steep, scenic eastern side, so you see two faces of the mountain instead of returning the way you came. Four days is the shortest way to do the full traverse. 
- Short on time: a full summit traverse from Nairobi and back in four days. 
- Scenery: Mackinder’s Valley on the way up and the Gorges Valley and Lake Michaelson views on the way down. 
- Summit goals: sunrise on Point Lenana, the trekking summit of Mount Kenya. 

## What to expect on the 4 day Mount Kenya Sirimon Chogoria trek 
Day 1 leaves Nairobi at 7:30 a.m. for a drive of about four hours to Nanyuki for lunch, then an hour to Sirimon Gate (2,440 m). A 9 km walk of 3 to 4 hours climbs through forest into open heathland to Judmaier Camp (3,300 m). Day 2 is 6 to 7 hours and about 17 km: you cross the Ontulili River and Liki North stream, walk up Mackinder’s Valley under the main peaks and sleep at Shipton’s Camp (4,200 m). 
Day 3 starts at 3:00 a.m. with a steep, stony climb past the Lower Hall Tarns to Point Lenana by about 6:30 a.m. for sunrise. You descend 6 km to Mintos Hut for breakfast, then follow the Chogoria route for about 20 km to Mount Meru Bandas (2,950 m), around 32 km and 11 to 12 hours in total. On day 4 you walk down through moorland and giant heather to the forest clearing, take a 4×4 for 21 km to Chogoria town and transfer back to Nairobi. 

### Is 4 days enough to climb Mount Kenya? 
Mount Kenya can be climbed to Point Lenana in 4 days, but it is a tight schedule for acclimatisation, with a 785 m summit climb and a 32 km day straight after a single night at 4,200 m. It works best for hikers who have been at altitude recently. If you want more time to adjust, the 5-Day Mt Kenya Sirimon to Chogoria Traverse adds an acclimatisation day at Shipton’s Camp. ## Day by Day 
Expand all 

This 4-day journey is the most scenic way to experience Mt Kenya. Ascend the gentle Sirimon Route and descend the majestic Chogoria side, traversing Mt Kenya for unparalleled beauty and adventure. 

Day 01 : 
Nairobi - Sirimon Gate - Jumaier Camp (3,300m) 

We depart Nairobi at 7:30 AM, driving approximately four hours to Nanyuki town for lunch. Afterward, we continue for an hour to the Sirimon Gate (2,440m). From the gate, the trek begins, following a track that ascends through the forest. After about three hours, the terrain transitions into open heathland. 
We then veer right, reaching Judmaier Camp (3,300m) in roughly 3.5 hours from the gate. Dinner and overnight stay are provided at the camp. (Lunch, Dinner included) 
- Trekking Time: 3-4 hours 
- Distance: 9 kilometres 
- Ascent: 860m 
- Meal: Lunch & Dinner 

Day 02 : 
Jumaier Camp - Shimpton's Camp (4200m) 

After breakfast at Judmaier Camp, we ascend uphill, taking the left fork at the junction. About an hour later, the track crosses the Ontulili River. The route then contours right through the Moorland, crossing the Liki North stream before continuing uphill. 
We enter the spectacular Mackinders Valley, where you'll be rewarded with panoramic views up the valley towards the main Mount Kenya peaks . The trek concludes at Shipton's Camp (4,200m), where dinner will be served and we'll spend the night, preparing for the summit attempt. (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner included). 
- Trekking Time: 6-7 hours 
- Distance: 17 kilometres 
- Ascent: 900m 
- Meal: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner 

Day 03 : 
Summit Day and Chogoria Descent (Point Lenana to Mount Meru Bandas) 

We begin the ascent pre-dawn at 3:00 AM, tackling a steep, stony quarry that cuts through a cliff band. This tough climb offers a visible path toward the Lower Hall Tarns. After approximately three hours (6 km), we aim to reach Point Lenana (4,985m) on Mount Kenya by 6:30 AM to witness the spectacular sunrise. 
Following the summit experience, we descend (6 km, 2 hours) to Mintos Hut for a well-deserved breakfast. The descent continues along the scenic Chogoria Route for a further 20 km (4–5 hours) down to Mount Meru Bandas (2,950m). We’ll have dinner and spend the night here on Mount Kenya. 
- Trekking Time: 11-12 hours 
- Distance: 32 Kilometres 
- Ascent: 785m 
- Descent: 1,945m 
- Meal: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner 

Day 04 : 
Chogoria Gate Descent and Return to Nairobi 

After breakfast, we begin the final descent. We'll hike a few hours back into the moorland and giant heather (around 3,000m). The route continues downward for about 15 km (approximately 2 hours) to the forest clearance. Here, a 4x4 vehicle will meet us for the 21 km drive down to Chogoria town. From Chogoria, you will connect with the waiting transfer vehicle for the return journey back to Nairobi. (Breakfast and Lunch included). 
- Trekking Time: 2 hours 
- Ascent: 1,055m 
- Distance: 21 Kilometres 
- Descent: 450m 
- Meal: Breakfast, & Lunch

Which route does the 4 day Mount Kenya Sirimon Chogoria trek follow? 

The 4 day Mount Kenya Sirimon Chogoria trek goes up the Sirimon route and down the Chogoria route. You sleep at Judmaier Camp (3,300 m) and Shipton’s Camp (4,200 m), reach Point Lenana at sunrise on day 3, descend past Mintos Hut to Mount Meru Bandas, and walk out to a 4×4 for Chogoria town on day 4. 

How high do you climb on the 4 day Mount Kenya Sirimon Chogoria trek? 

The highest point on the 4 day Mount Kenya Sirimon Chogoria trek is Point Lenana at 4,985 m. You spend only one night at 3,300 m and one at 4,200 m before the summit, so altitude symptoms such as headache are common. Walk slowly, drink plenty of water and tell your guide early if you feel unwell. 

How fit do I need to be for the 4 day Mount Kenya Sirimon Chogoria trek? 

The 4 day Mount Kenya Sirimon Chogoria trek is graded Expert on the page. No climbing skills are needed, but day 2 is 6 to 7 hours and summit day is 11 to 12 hours and about 32 km. You should be used to long hill walks on consecutive days, and recent altitude experience helps a lot on this short schedule. 

When is the best time for the 4 day Mount Kenya Sirimon Chogoria trek? 

The best time for the 4 day Mount Kenya Sirimon Chogoria trek is in the drier months, usually January to February and July to October, when the trails are firmer and the sunrise from Point Lenana is clearer. The rains around April to May and November make the Chogoria forest track muddy, so ask us before booking then.
