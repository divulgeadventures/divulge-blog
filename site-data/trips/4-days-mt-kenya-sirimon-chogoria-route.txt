## Overview 
Conquer Mt Kenya, Africa’s second-highest peak, on this incredible 4-day traverse! This classic package combines the scenic Sirimon ascent with the dramatic, beautiful Chogoria descent, offering a diverse and rewarding trekking experience. The trek begins on the gentler Sirimon Route through forest and moorland to Shipton’s Camp. Day three is the highlight: a pre-dawn summit push to Point Lenana (4,985m) for a breathtaking sunrise, followed by the majestic descent via the Chogoria Route. You’ll pass stunning features like the Gorges Valley, Lake Michaelson, and the Nithi Falls. The package typically includes professional guides, porters, a cook, all park fees, and full-board accommodation in huts/tents. Experience the true majesty of Mt Kenya with this unforgettable adventure! 

### Climbing Highlights 
- Summit Sunrise at Point Lenana (4,985m) 
- Diverse Ecosystems (Sirimon Ascent) 
- Mackinder's Valley Panoramic Views 
- The Majestic Chogoria Descent 
- Gorges Valley & Lake Michaelson 
- Full Mount Kenya Traverse; starting on the gradual northern slopes and descending the dramatic eastern side 

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

This 4-day journey is the most scenic way to experience Mt Kenya. Ascend the gentle Sirimon Route and descend the majestic Chogoria side, traversing Mt Kenya for unparalleled beauty and adventure. 

Day 01 : 
Nairobi - Sirimon Gate - Jumaier Camp (3,300m) 

We depart Nairobi at 7:30 AM, driving approximately four hours to Nanyuki town for lunch. Afterward, we continue for an hour to the Sirimon Gate (2,440m). From the gate, the trek begins, following a track that ascends through the forest. After about three hours, the terrain transitions into open heathland. We then veer right, reaching Judmaier Camp (3,300m) in roughly 3.5 hours from the gate. Dinner and overnight stay are provided at the camp. (Lunch, Dinner included) 
- Trekking Time: 3-4 hours 
- Distance: 9 kilometres 
- Ascent: 860m 
- Meal: Lunch & Dinner 

Day 02 : 
Jumaier Camp - Shimpton's Camp (4200m) 

After breakfast at Judmaier Camp, we ascend uphill, taking the left fork at the junction. About an hour later, the track crosses the Ontulili River. The route then contours right through the Moorland, crossing the Liki North stream before continuing uphill. We enter the spectacular Mackinders Valley, where you'll be rewarded with panoramic views up the valley towards the main Mount Kenya peaks . The trek concludes at Shipton's Camp (4,200m), where dinner will be served and we'll spend the night, preparing for the summit attempt. (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner included). 
- Trekking Time: 6-7 hours 
- Distance: 17 kilometres 
- Ascent: 900m 
- Meal: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner 

Day 03 : 
Summit Day and Chogoria Descent (Point Lenana to Mount Meru Bandas) 

We begin the ascent pre-dawn at 3:00 AM, tackling a steep, stony quarry that cuts through a cliff band. This tough climb offers a visible path toward the Lower Hall Tarns. After approximately three hours (6 km), we aim to reach Point Lenana (4,985m) on Mount Kenya by 6:30 AM to witness the spectacular sunrise. Following the summit experience, we descend (6 km, 2 hours) to Mintos Hut for a well-deserved breakfast. The descent continues along the scenic Chogoria Route for a further 20 km (4–5 hours) down to Mount Meru Bandas (2,950m). We’ll have dinner and spend the night here on Mount Kenya. 
- Trekking Time: 11-12 hours 
- Distance: 32 Kilometres 
- Ascent: 785m 
- Descent: 1,945m 
- Meal: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner 

Day 04 : 
Chogoria Gate Descent and Return to Nairobi 

After breakfast, we begin the final descent. We'll hike a few hours back into the moorland and giant heather (around 3,000m). The route continues downward for about 15 km (approximately 2 hours) to the forest clearance. Here, a 4x4 vehicle will meet us for the 21 km drive down to Chogoria town. From Chogoria, you will connect with the waiting transfer vehicle for the return journey back to Nairobi. (Breakfast and Lunch included). 
- Trekking Time: 2 hours 
- Ascent: 1,055m 
- Distance: 21 Kilometres 
- Descent: 450m 
- Meal: Breakfast, & Lunch
