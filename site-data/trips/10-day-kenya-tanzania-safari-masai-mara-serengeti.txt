## 
This Kenya and Tanzania safari covers both halves of the great Mara-Serengeti ecosystem in one trip. Spend three nights in the Masai Mara, cross into Tanzania for three nights in the Serengeti, then explore Ngorongoro Crater and Tarangire before ending in Arusha. You stay in the reliable Sopa lodges throughout and travel in a private 4×4 with a driver guide in each country. It suits first-time visitors who want the best of East Africa in ten days. 
Price: from USD 4,030 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Three nights in the Masai Mara 
- Three nights in the Serengeti 
- Ngorongoro Crater game drive 
- Tarangire National Park and its elephant herds 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Nairobi to the Masai Mara 

Drive from Nairobi via the Great Rift Valley to the Masai Mara. Afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight at Mara Sopa Lodge. 

Day 2 : 
Masai Mara 

Full day of game drives. Dinner and overnight at Mara Sopa Lodge. 

Day 3 : 
Masai Mara 

Morning and afternoon game drives; optional Maasai village visit. Dinner and overnight at Mara Sopa Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Masai Mara to the Serengeti 

Drive to the Isebania border, meet your Tanzanian guide and continue into the Serengeti. Dinner and overnight at Serengeti Sopa Lodge. 

Day 5 : 
Serengeti National Park 

Full day of game drives in the central Serengeti. Dinner and overnight at Serengeti Sopa Lodge. 

Day 6 : 
Serengeti National Park 

Another full day following the herds and predators. Dinner and overnight at Serengeti Sopa Lodge. 

Day 7 : 
Serengeti to Ngorongoro 

Morning game drive, then drive to the crater rim. Dinner and overnight at Ngorongoro Sopa Lodge. 

Day 8 : 
Ngorongoro Crater 

Descend into the crater for a game drive among rhinos, lions and flamingos. Dinner and overnight at Ngorongoro Sopa Lodge. 

Day 9 : 
Ngorongoro to Tarangire 

Drive to Tarangire National Park for an afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight at Tarangire Sopa Lodge. 

Day 10 : 
Tarangire to Arusha 

Morning game drive, then drive to Arusha or Kilimanjaro airport.
