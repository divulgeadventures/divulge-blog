## Overview 
This 6 day Rwanda safari is our most popular way to see the country’s highlights in under a week. You spend two nights in Akagera National Park for the Big Five and two nights near Volcanoes National Park for gorillas and golden monkeys, with a night in Kigali on arrival. 
It is a well-paced trip in comfortable mid-range lodges, with private 4×4 transport and an English-speaking driver guide throughout. 

## Why choose a 6 day Rwanda safari? 
Six days is the shortest trip that does justice to both of Rwanda’s big experiences. You avoid rushing, you get a full day in Akagera, and you still have time for golden monkeys after your gorilla trek. 
- First-time visitors: the classic Rwanda combination. 
- Couples and friends: a balanced mix of drives and treks. 
- Good value: comfortable lodges without luxury prices. 

## What to expect on your 6 day Rwanda safari 
On arrival day you are met at the airport and taken to your Kigali hotel. Day two takes you east to Akagera for a short game drive, lunch at your lodge and a guided night drive in search of leopards and hyenas. 
Day three is a full day in the park, looking for elephants, lions, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras and rhinos. At 4:30 PM you board a boat on Lake Ihema for a sunset cruise among hippos and crocodiles. 
On day four you drive to Musanze. Day five is your gorilla trek, followed by a cultural afternoon at the Gorilla Guardians Village. On day six you spend an hour with golden monkeys before driving back to Kigali. 

### Where you stay 
Lodges include Akagera Game Lodge or Ruzizi Tented Lodge in Akagera, and Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge or Five Volcanoes Boutique Hotel near the gorillas. We can upgrade to luxury lodges on request. 

## Best time for a 6 day Rwanda safari 
The dry months, June to September and December to February, are best for game viewing and gorilla trails. The wetter months are quieter and greener, and gorilla trekking runs all year. 
Planning your trek? Read our guide to gorilla trekking in Rwanda . For a primate-focused trip, see the Rwanda primate safari . Park facts are on Visit Rwanda’s Volcanoes National Park page .

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is your 6 day Rwanda safari day by day. 

Day 01 : 
Arrival in Kigali 

Upon arrival at Kigali International Airport, you will be met by your Divulge Adventures driver guide and transferred to your hotel. Spend the rest of the day at leisure, perhaps exploring Kigali's cafés and craft shops. Overnight at Park Inn by Radisson Kigali | Hotel Chez Lando 

Day 02 : 
Kigali - Akagera National Park (Game Drive and Night Drive) 

After breakfast, drive east to Akagera National Park, approximately 2.5 hours, with a stop along the way. Enjoy a short game drive on arrival, followed by lunch at your lodge. In the evening, set out on a guided night game drive with spotlights, searching for leopards, hyenas and other nocturnal wildlife. Dinner and overnight at Akagera Game Lodge | Ruzizi Tented Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Akagera Full-Day Safari and Sunset Boat Cruise 

After breakfast, spend the day exploring Akagera on a game drive with a packed lunch, looking for elephants, lions, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras and rhinos. At 4:30 PM, board a boat on Lake Ihema for a sunset cruise past hippos, crocodiles and waterbirds. Dinner and overnight at Akagera Game Lodge | Ruzizi Tented Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Akagera - Musanze 

Enjoy a final morning game drive, then check out and drive to Musanze, approximately 4.5 hours, passing local villages and farms. Check in at your lodge and relax for the evening. Dinner and overnight at Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge | Five Volcanoes Boutique Hotel 

Day 05 : 
Mountain Gorilla Trekking and Gorilla Guardians Village 

After an early breakfast, drive to the Kinigi park headquarters for your briefing. The rangers will assign you a gorilla family based on your fitness. Trek through the rainforest and spend about one hour with the gorillas. In the afternoon, enjoy a cultural programme at the Gorilla Guardians Village. Dinner and overnight at Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge | Five Volcanoes Boutique Hotel 

Day 06 : 
Golden Monkey Trekking - Kigali (Departure) 

After an early breakfast and briefing, hike through the bamboo forest to spend about one hour with the golden monkeys. Return to the lodge and drive back to Kigali, approximately 2.5 hours, for drop-off at your hotel or Kigali International Airport.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

What is the best 6 day Rwanda safari itinerary? 

A popular 6-day plan combines two nights in Akagera for the Big Five with two nights near Volcanoes National Park for gorilla and golden monkey trekking, plus a night in Kigali. 

Can I see the Big Five in Rwanda? 

Yes. Akagera National Park is home to lions, leopards, elephants, buffaloes and rhinos. 

What is included in the gorilla trek? 

The trek includes a ranger briefing, armed ranger guides and one hour with a habituated gorilla family. Porters are optional and paid separately. 

What should I pack for a Rwanda safari? 

Pack light layers, a warm fleece, a rain jacket, hiking boots, a hat, sunscreen, binoculars and a camera. Neutral colours are best on game drives. 

Can I upgrade this 6 day Rwanda safari to luxury lodges? 

Yes. We can switch to lodges such as Magashi Camp in Akagera and Bisate or Virunga Lodge near the gorillas. 

Are meals included? 

Meals are on full board on safari. Lunch and dinner in Kigali are not included, so you can try the city’s restaurants. 

Is a 6 day Rwanda safari enough time? 

Yes, for Akagera and the gorillas. Add two or three days if you also want Nyungwe’s chimpanzees or Lake Kivu.
