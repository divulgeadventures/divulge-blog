## Overview 
The 7-Day Moremi Khwai , and Savuti Special is an immersive Botswana safari that traverses three of Africa’s premier wildlife ecosystems, blending the lush waterways of the Okavango Delta with the rugged plains of Chobe National Park. 
Starting in the water-rich Moremi Game Reserve , travelers experience iconic floodplains via open-vehicle game drives and traditional mokoro (canoe) excursions. 
The journey moves to the predator-dense Khwai Concession for thrilling night safaris and close-up encounters with wild dogs and leopards, before concluding in the dramatic, semi-arid Savuti region, famous for its powerful lion prides and massive elephant herds. 
Typically operated as a mobile tented camping expedition or a luxury fly-in lodge safari, this itinerary delivers a quintessential, high-density wildlife experience across Botswana’s most contrasting terrains.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01- 02 : 
Start of Moremi Khwai and Savuti safari 

Upon arrival you will be picked up by a representative from Divulge Adventures at the airport in Maun ready to begin your Moremi Khwai and Savuti adventure. 
Your professional safari guide will give you time to stop in town and purchase any last-minute essentials before beginning your journey to the world-renowned Moremi Game Reserve. 
Traveling in a specially designed safari game-viewing vehicle, you will pass through Shorobe Village and enjoy excellent wildlife viewing opportunities along the way, with several stops for photography. 
Upon arrival at camp, our team will set up your accommodation while you have time to freshen up and relax before heading out on your first afternoon game drive. 
You will spend 2 nights camping in this spectacular wilderness area, allowing ample time to explore its diverse habitats and abundant wildlife. 
During your stay, you will enjoy morning and afternoon game drives, with time to relax during the hottest part of the day. 
All meals will be freshly prepared by our camp chef and served around the campfire, creating an authentic safari experience. 

Day 03- 04 : 
Khwai concession area 

After an early breakfast, we will break camp and continue our journey to the spectacular Khwai Concession Area 
A wildlife-rich region bordering the famous Moremi Game Reserve. 
The drive offers excellent game-viewing opportunities along the way as we make our way through diverse landscapes. 
Upon arrival, our camp crew will set up the campsite while you enjoy a freshly prepared lunch. 
After some time to relax and freshen up, we will head out for an afternoon game drive to explore this remarkable wilderness area. 
Known for its abundant wildlife, Khwai is one of Botswana's premier safari destinations. 
Offering excellent chances to spot elephants, lions, leopards, wild dogs, buffalo, and a variety of antelope species. 
We will spend two nights camping in the Khwai Concession, allowing ample time to experience the area's incredible wildlife and natural beauty. 
During our stay, we will enjoy both morning and afternoon game drives, with a break during the hottest part of the day to relax at camp. 
All meals will be freshly prepared by our camp chef and enjoyed around the campfire, creating an authentic African safari atmosphere. 
Khwai is one of the few areas in Botswana where additional activities such as guided bush walks and exciting night game drives are permitted. 
Offering unique opportunities to observe nocturnal wildlife and gain a deeper understanding of the bush from an experienced guide's perspective. 

Day 05 -06 : 
Savuti 

After an early breakfast, we break camp and we will begin our journey to the legendary Savuti region of Savuti, one of Botswana's most renowned wildlife areas. 
Along the way, we enjoy a scenic drive through the wilderness with a roadside picnic lunch. 
We arrive in Savuti in the afternoon, where our safari crew will set up camp while you take some time to relax and freshen up. 
Later, we head out on an exciting afternoon game drive to explore this wildlife-rich region. 
Famous for its large elephant herds, lion prides, spotted hyenas, and other predators. 
You will spend two nights camping in Savuti, allowing ample time to experience the area's diverse landscapes and abundant wildlife. 
During your stay, you will enjoy both morning and afternoon game drives, with a break during the hottest part of the day to relax at camp. 

Day 07 : 
Departure ( End of Moremi Khwai and Savuti) 

After breakfast, we will enjoy a final transit game drive as we make our way to the airport for your onward journey. 
This scenic drive offers one last opportunity to experience the beauty of the wilderness and spot wildlife along the way. 
Upon arrival at the airport in either Maun or Kasane, your safari will come to an end as you connect with your onward travel arrangements. 
This will mark the end of your Moremi Khwai and Savuti safari in Botswana.
