## Overview 
This 7 day Moremi Khwai and Savuti safari is a 7-day, 6-night mobile camping trip from Maun through three top northern Botswana wildlife areas. You camp 2 nights in Moremi Game Reserve, 2 nights in the Khwai Concession and 2 nights in Savuti. The dry months, roughly May to October, are best for game viewing. It is priced from USD 4,774 per adult and suits wildlife lovers and photographers. 
Every night is spent in a private bush camp that the crew sets up before you arrive, and all meals are cooked fresh and served around the campfire. 

## Why choose a 7 day Moremi Khwai and Savuti safari? 
Moremi Game Reserve covers the eastern Okavango Delta, where floodplains, lagoons and woodland meet. The Khwai Concession next door allows guided bush walks and night drives, which most of Moremi does not. Savuti, in the south of Chobe National Park, is drier and famous for big elephant herds, lion prides and spotted hyenas. 
Linking all three gives you water-rich delta scenery, a predator-rich community area and a semi-arid wilderness in a single week, with 2 nights in each so you are never rushed. 
- Wildlife lovers: elephants, lions, leopards, wild dogs, buffalo and many antelope species. 
- Photographers: morning and afternoon game drives in three very different landscapes. 
- Adventurous travellers: true bush camping with campfire meals under the stars. 

## What to expect on the 7 day Moremi Khwai and Savuti safari 
Day 1 starts with a pick-up at Maun airport and a short stop in town for last-minute essentials. You drive through Shorobe Village to Moremi Game Reserve, stopping for photos along the way, and head out on an afternoon game drive once camp is ready. Day 2 is a full day of morning and afternoon drives in Moremi. 
On day 3 you break camp and drive to the Khwai Concession, arriving for lunch and an afternoon game drive. Day 4 is spent exploring Khwai, where guided bush walks and night game drives are permitted. On day 5 you travel to Savuti with a picnic lunch on the way, and day 6 brings more game drives in Savuti. 
On day 7 you enjoy a final transit game drive to the airport in Maun or Kasane for your onward flight. 

### Is 7 days enough for Moremi, Khwai and Savuti? 
Seven days is enough for Moremi, Khwai and Savuti because you spend 2 nights in each area, with 2 game drives on every full day. The dry season from about May to October usually gives the best sightings as animals gather near water. Our best time to visit Botswana guide explains each month in detail.

When is the best time for the 7 day Moremi Khwai and Savuti safari? 

The 7 day Moremi Khwai and Savuti safari runs all year, but the dry season from about May to October usually gives the best game viewing, as elephants and predators gather near permanent water. The green season from November to April is quieter, with lush scenery, newborn animals and excellent birding, though some tracks can be muddy.
