## 
This Samburu birding safari is a slower trip for birders and photographers who like to spend real time in each place. It includes four full nights in Samburu and four in the Masai Mara, rather than rushing through. 
Samburu’s red earth, doum palms and the Ewaso Ng’iro River make a beautiful setting for dry-country birds, while the Rift Valley lakes and the Mara add water birds and big cats. 

## Why choose this Samburu birding safari? 
- Four nights in Samburu: vulturine guineafowl, Somali bee-eater and the Samburu Special Five. 
- Nairobi National Park and Mount Kenya: grassland and forest birds early in the trip. 
- Nakuru, Baringo and Naivasha: flamingos, owls, hornbills and fish eagles. 
- Four nights in the Masai Mara: time for big cats and grassland birds. 

## Where you stay 
Boma Hotel in Nairobi, Serena Mountain Lodge, Ashnil Samburu Lodge, Sarova Lion Hill, Lake Baringo Club, Lake Naivasha Country Club, Mara Serena Lodge and Sarova Mara Camp, or similar. 

## Best time for a Samburu birding safari 
Samburu is dry for most of the year and good in every season. July to October adds the migration in the Mara, while November to April brings migrant birds.

Why four nights in Samburu? 

More time lets you find shy northern birds and enjoy the best light along the river without rushing. 

Which lodges are used on the Samburu birding safari? 

Boma Hotel, Serena Mountain Lodge, Ashnil Samburu Lodge, Sarova Lion Hill, Lake Baringo Club, Lake Naivasha Country Club, Mara Serena Lodge and Sarova Mara Camp, or similar. 

Is this trip good for photographers? 

Yes. The slow pace, long stays and pop-up roof vehicle make it ideal for photography. 

Can I end the trip before the Mara? 

Yes. The first 13 days work as a complete trip ending in Nairobi. 

What birds are special in Samburu? 

Vulturine guineafowl, Somali bee-eater, golden-breasted starling and Somali ostrich. 

What is the best time to visit? 

Samburu is good all year. July to October adds the migration in the Mara. 

Will I see big cats? 

Very likely. Samburu has lions and leopards, and the Mara is famous for lions and cheetahs.
