## 
This budget Namibia safari takes you to the country’s most famous sights for a fraction of the lodge price. Camp two nights at Sesriem campsite right by the park gate, so you are first onto the Sossusvlei dunes at sunrise, then continue through the desert to the coast for two nights at Desert Sky Backpackers in Swakopmund, where you can add sandboarding, kayaking or a dolphin cruise. It suits backpackers, students and photographers. 
Price: from USD 640 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Two nights camping at Sesriem, next to the park gate 
- Sunrise on the dunes and a walk into Deadvlei 
- Sesriem Canyon 
- Two nights in Swakopmund on the Atlantic coast 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Windhoek to Sesriem 

Collection in Windhoek and drive south-west to the Namib Desert. Dinner and overnight at Sesriem Campsite. 

Day 2 : 
Sossusvlei and Deadvlei 

Enter the park at first light to climb the dunes and walk into Deadvlei, then visit Sesriem Canyon. Dinner and overnight at Sesriem Campsite. 

Day 3 : 
Sesriem to Swakopmund 

Drive through the Gaub and Kuiseb passes and Walvis Bay to Swakopmund. Overnight at Desert Sky Backpackers. 

Day 4 : 
Swakopmund 

Free day for optional activities. Overnight at Desert Sky Backpackers. 

Day 5 : 
Swakopmund to Windhoek 

Drive back to Windhoek.

How much does this budget Namibia safari cost? 

It starts from USD 640 per adult with a shared vehicle, based on current rates. We confirm the price for your dates when you enquire. 

Do I need my own camping gear? 

No. Tents, mattresses and cooking equipment are provided; bring or hire a sleeping bag. 

When is the best time to go? 

April to October has clear, dry days; desert nights can be cold from June to August.
