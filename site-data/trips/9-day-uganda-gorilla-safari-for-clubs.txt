## 
This 9 day Uganda gorilla safari for clubs is planned for hiking clubs, Rotary and Lions clubs, alumni groups and photography societies. Members track rhinos on foot, see Murchison Falls, trek chimpanzees in Kibale, cruise the Kazinga Channel and spend an hour with a mountain gorilla family in Bwindi. 

## Why book a 9 day Uganda gorilla safari for clubs? 
Uganda suits active clubs. There are walks and treks most days, from rhino tracking at Ziwa to chimpanzees in Kibale and the gorilla trek in Bwindi, with game drives and boat cruises in between. 
Each gorilla family can be visited by up to 8 people a day, so we book permits for several families at once and the club treks in small groups at the same time, then meets again for lunch and the evening. 

## What does the club safari itinerary include? 
One night in Kampala, two in Murchison Falls, two in Kibale, one in Queen Elizabeth and two in Bwindi, before the return to Kampala on day 9. 

### Can the club share talks or reviews in the evenings? 
Yes. Evenings are free for talks, slideshows or a club dinner, and we can ask lodges for a quiet room. A club leader can travel on the group’s free place. 
See our guides to how hard gorilla trekking is and the gorilla trekking packing list . 

## Who is the 9 day uganda gorilla safari for clubs best for? 
The 9 day uganda gorilla safari for clubs is best for hiking, photography, Rotary, Lions and alumni clubs that want to travel together. We plan vehicles and rooms around your members, so the 9 day uganda gorilla safari for clubs runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## How fit do members need to be for the 9 day Uganda gorilla safari for clubs? 
Members should be able to walk 2 to 6 hours on steep, muddy forest trails. Porters can carry bags and help on the slopes, and rangers try to match fitter members with gorilla families that are further away.

Price on request. Group prices depend on your dates, the number of travellers and the rooms you need, so we quote every group personally. Day 1 : 
Arrival in Entebbe 

Your 9 day uganda gorilla safari for clubs begins today. Your club is met at Entebbe International Airport for a briefing and transferred to the hotel. Depending on arrival time, visit the shores of Lake Victoria. Dinner and overnight at Kampala Serena Hotel or similar. 

Day 2 : 
Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary to Murchison Falls 

Drive north to Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary to track white rhinos on foot with rangers. Continue to the Top of Murchison Falls, where the Nile squeezes through a 7-metre gorge. Dinner and overnight at Pakuba Safari Lodge or similar. 

Day 3 : 
Murchison game drive and Nile cruise 

A sunrise game drive across the northern plains, then an afternoon boat cruise to the base of the falls. Overnight at Pakuba Safari Lodge or similar. 

Day 4 : 
Murchison Falls to Kibale 

Drive south through villages and countryside to Kibale National Park. Evening at the lodge. 

Day 5 : 
Chimpanzee trekking and Bigodi Wetland 

Trek with rangers to find chimpanzees in Kibale Forest, then take a guided walk in the community-run Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary. 

Day 6 : 
Kibale to Queen Elizabeth and the Kazinga Channel 

Drive to Queen Elizabeth National Park for an afternoon boat cruise on the Kazinga Channel among elephants, buffalo and hippos. 

Day 7 : 
Kasenyi game drive to Bwindi 

A morning game drive on the Kasenyi Plains, then continue to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park through terraced farmland and forest. 

Day 8 : 
Gorilla trekking and Batwa visit 

Members trek in small groups to different gorilla families and spend one hour with them. In the afternoon, visit the Batwa community. 

Day 9 : 
Return to Kampala 

Drive or fly back to Kampala and Entebbe for your onward journey. This is the end of your 9 day uganda gorilla safari for clubs.

What kind of clubs book the 9 day Uganda gorilla safari for clubs? 

The 9 day Uganda gorilla safari for clubs suits hiking and walking clubs, Rotary and Lions clubs, alumni associations, church groups and photography societies. We adjust the pace and add talks or service visits on request. 

How many members can trek gorillas on the same day? 

Each gorilla family can be visited by up to 8 people a day. Larger clubs trek to several families at the same time, so book early so we can secure enough permits. 

Does the club leader travel free? 

On group bookings the seventh place is free for every six paying travellers. Most clubs give that place to the trip leader. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 9 day uganda gorilla safari for clubs. 

Can members pay separately? 

Yes. Each member can pay their own share. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the group’s places and permits, and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. 

Can the club visit a community project? 

Yes. Rotary and service clubs often add a school or health project visit. Tell us what your club supports and we will suggest a project along the route. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 9 day uganda gorilla safari for clubs.
