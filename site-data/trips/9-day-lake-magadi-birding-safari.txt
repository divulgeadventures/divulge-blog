## 
This Lake Magadi birding safari is built around plovers and other shorebirds. Lake Magadi, south of Nairobi, is one of the best places in East Africa for the chestnut-banded plover, and its shallow soda flats attract flamingos and waders. 
From there the route turns north to the Rift Valley lakes, Lake Baringo, the dry country of Samburu and the forests of Mount Kenya. 

## Why choose this Lake Magadi birding safari? 
- Lake Magadi: chestnut-banded plover, flamingos and migrant sandpipers. 
- Naivasha and Nakuru: fish eagles, pelicans and flamingos. 
- Lake Baringo: white-faced scops owl and Jackson’s hornbill. 
- Samburu: vulturine guineafowl, golden pipit and bee-eaters. 
- Mount Kenya: green ibis, Abbott’s starling and red-fronted parrot. 

## Where you stay 
A Nairobi hotel for two nights, then Lake Naivasha Lodge, Lake Nakuru Lodge, Lake Baringo Club, Samburu Lodge and Naro Moru River Lodge, or similar. 

## Best time for a Lake Magadi birding safari 
September to April is best for migrant waders at Magadi. Magadi is very hot at midday, so we visit early and carry plenty of water. Samburu is good all year.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Nairobi 

Airport pick-up and a short orientation, then a visit to the National Museum bird gallery. Overnight in a Nairobi hotel. 

Day 2 : 
Lake Magadi day trip 

Drive south to Lake Magadi with a picnic lunch. Bird the shore for chestnut-banded plovers, flamingos and sandpipers, then return to Nairobi. 

Day 3 : 
Nairobi to Lake Naivasha 

Drive into the Rift Valley, with a morning bird walk and an afternoon boat ride or a visit to Hell's Gate. Overnight at Lake Naivasha Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Lake Naivasha to Lake Nakuru 

Game and bird drives at Lake Nakuru for flamingos, pelicans and tawny eagles. Overnight at Lake Nakuru Lodge. 

Day 5 : 
Lake Nakuru to Lake Baringo 

Drive north to Lake Baringo Club. Afternoon walk to the cliffs for owls and hornbills. 

Day 6 : 
Lake Baringo to Samburu 

Drive via Nanyuki to Samburu Lodge. Evening game and bird drive. 

Day 7 : 
Full day in Samburu 

Birding and game viewing for vulturine guineafowl, bee-eaters, bustards and Grevy's zebras. 

Day 8 : 
Samburu to Mount Kenya 

Drive south to the slopes of Mount Kenya for forest birding. Overnight at Naro Moru River Lodge. 

Day 9 : 
Mount Kenya to Nairobi 

Return to Nairobi for lunch and a day room before your flight.

Where is Lake Magadi? 

It is about two hours south-west of Nairobi in the southern Rift Valley. It is a soda lake famous for flamingos and the chestnut-banded plover. 

How hot is Lake Magadi? 

Very hot, often above 35 °C at midday. We visit in the morning and bring plenty of water and shade. 

Which lodges are used on the Lake Magadi birding safari? 

A Nairobi hotel, Lake Naivasha Lodge, Lake Nakuru Lodge, Lake Baringo Club, Samburu Lodge and Naro Moru River Lodge, or similar. 

Is Hell's Gate included? 

In the afternoon at Lake Naivasha you can choose a boat ride or a visit to Hell’s Gate National Park. 

How many species can I see? 

Most groups record 300 to 400 species on this route. 

Is this safari good for photographers? 

Yes. The open soda flats and Samburu’s dry country give clear views and good light. 

Can I extend to the Masai Mara? 

Yes. Two or three nights in the Mara can be added after Lake Nakuru.
