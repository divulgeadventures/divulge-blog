## 
This Samburu fly-in safari is a short trip to one of Kenya’s most beautiful and less crowded reserves. Samburu’s red earth, doum palms and the Ewaso Ng’iro River create a very different safari to the Mara. 
The dry north is home to the Samburu Special Five, as well as elephants, lions and leopards along the river. 

## Why choose this Samburu fly-in safari? 
- Samburu Special Five: reticulated giraffe, Grevy’s zebra, Beisa oryx, gerenuk and Somali ostrich. 
- Leopards: Samburu is one of the best places in Kenya to see them. 
- Fewer vehicles: a quieter safari than the southern parks. 
- Culture: an optional visit to a Samburu community. 

## Where you stay 
Two nights at Ashnil Samburu Camp, or similar, on the banks of the Ewaso Ng’iro River. 

## Best time for a Samburu fly-in safari 
Samburu is dry for most of the year and good in every season. The short rains in November and long rains in April and May bring green scenery.

How long is the flight to Samburu? 

About 50 to 70 minutes from Wilson Airport, depending on stops. 

How much luggage can I bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. Ask us if you need extra space. 

Where do the flights leave from in Nairobi? 

From Wilson Airport, not Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. We arrange the transfer from your hotel or from JKIA. 

What are the Samburu Special Five? 

Reticulated giraffe, Grevy’s zebra, Beisa oryx, gerenuk and Somali ostrich. 

Is Samburu hot? 

Yes, days are warm to hot. Mornings and evenings are pleasant for game drives. 

Can I add a community visit? 

Yes, a visit to a Samburu village can be added at extra cost. 

Is emergency evacuation cover available? 

Yes. We recommend Flying Doctors evacuation cover for every safari and can arrange it for you.
