## 
This 7 day Botswana celebration safari is for groups marking a milestone together in one of Africa’s wildest places. You cruise the Chobe River among elephants, fly into the Moremi region of the Okavango and finish in the Khwai Concession, known for predators and walking safaris. 

## Why choose a 7 day Botswana celebration safari? 
Botswana’s camps are small and remote, which makes them feel private for a group. Sunset boat cruises on the Chobe, full days in Moremi and evenings around the fire give time to celebrate together. 
We can arrange a celebration dinner, a cake or a bush sundowner at the camps, and add a visit to Victoria Falls, which is close to Kasane. 

## What does the celebration itinerary include? 
Two nights on the Chobe riverfront, two in the Moremi region at Mankwe Tented Retreat or similar, and two in the Khwai Concession, with a scheduled flight from Kasane to Moremi and a final flight to Maun. 

### How do flights work for a group? 
Light aircraft link the camps and keep travel time short. Luggage is usually limited to about 20 kg in soft bags. For larger groups we can arrange a private charter so everyone flies together. 
Read our Botswana safari guide and our Okavango Delta vs Chobe comparison . 

## Who is the 7 day botswana celebration safari best for? 
The 7 day botswana celebration safari is best for milestone birthdays, anniversaries, reunions, retirements and wedding parties. Tell us the occasion and we will plan the special moments with the lodges, so the 7 day botswana celebration safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## How many guests can join the 7 day Botswana celebration safari? 
Botswana camps often have 8 to 12 tents, so small groups can sometimes book a whole camp. Larger groups split between two nearby camps of the same standard.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Kasane and Chobe River cruise 

Your 7 day botswana celebration safari begins today. Arrive in Kasane and transfer to your lodge on the Chobe riverfront. In the afternoon, take a boat cruise on the Chobe River to see elephants, buffalo and hippos at the water's edge. 

Day 2 : 
Chobe National Park 

A game drive through Chobe National Park, known for its large elephant herds, then another boat cruise for close-up views of hippos, crocodiles and birds. 

Day 3 : 
Flight to the Moremi region 

Transfer to Kasane Airport for a scheduled flight to the Moremi region, then a road transfer to Mankwe Tented Retreat or similar on the edge of the Okavango. 

Day 4 : 
Full day in Moremi Game Reserve 

A full day of game drives through Moremi's floodplains, woodlands and savannah, one of Botswana's richest wildlife areas. 

Day 5 : 
Transfer to the Khwai Concession 

Game drive on the way to the Khwai Concession, known for lions, leopards and wild dogs. Dinner and an evening by the fire. 

Day 6 : 
Khwai wilderness 

Morning game drive, then a walking safari with local guides. In the evening, gather for a celebration dinner. 

Day 7 : 
Flight to Maun 

A final early game drive, then fly to Maun for your onward journey. This is the end of your 7 day botswana celebration safari.

What occasions suit the 7 day Botswana celebration safari? 

The 7 day Botswana celebration safari suits milestone birthdays, anniversaries, reunions, retirements and wedding parties. Small, remote camps make it feel private, and we plan a special dinner around your occasion. 

Can we add Victoria Falls? 

Yes. Victoria Falls is a short drive from Kasane, so many groups add one or two nights there before the safari starts. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 7 day botswana celebration safari. 

Can guests pay separately? 

Yes. Each guest or couple can pay their own share. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the camps and flights, and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. 

Is there a minimum age? 

Some camps and walking safaris have minimum ages, often 6 to 12 years. Tell us the ages of everyone travelling and we will choose suitable camps. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 7 day botswana celebration safari. 

When is the best time for a Botswana celebration safari? 

June to October is the dry season, with the best game viewing and the Okavango floods at their highest in the middle of the year. November to March is green and quieter.
