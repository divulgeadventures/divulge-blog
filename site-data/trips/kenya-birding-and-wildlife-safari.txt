## 
This Kenya birding and wildlife safari covers almost every major habitat in the country in 15 days. You go from the forests of Mount Kenya to the lakes of the Rift Valley, the rainforest of Kakamega, the papyrus of Lake Victoria and the plains of the Masai Mara. 
It is one of the best routes for building a long bird list, often more than 450 species, while still enjoying lions, elephants and rhinos. 

## Why choose this Kenya birding and wildlife safari? 
- Mount Kenya: montane forest with turacos, trogons and green ibises. 
- Bogoria and Baringo: flamingos, greater kudu, owls and hornbills. 
- Kakamega Forest: Kenya’s only tropical rainforest, with great blue turacos and grey parrots. 
- Lake Victoria: papyrus gonolek and other swamp specials at Dunga. 
- Nakuru, Naivasha and the Mara: water birds, rhinos and the big cats. 

## Where you stay 
The route uses Nairobi Safari Club, Serena Mountain Lodge, Naro Moru River Lodge, Lake Baringo Club, Rondo Retreat in Kakamega, Sunset Hotel in Kisumu, Sarova Lion Hill at Nakuru, Lake Naivasha Country Club and Sarova Mara Camp, or similar. 

### A note on the pace 
Some travel days are long, but every drive includes birding stops. The full days at Mount Kenya, Baringo, Kakamega, Naivasha and the Mara give you time to slow down. 

## Best time for this Kenya birding and wildlife safari 
October to April brings Palearctic migrants. June to October is drier and best for game viewing in the Mara. Kakamega is wet all year, so pack a rain jacket. 
Read our Kakamega Forest guide and Lake Bogoria guide , or see shorter birding safaris . Nature Kenya has helpful bird lists for each region.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Nairobi 

Airport pick-up and transfer to Nairobi Safari Club. Afternoon visit to the National Museum bird gallery and a short walk in the botanic garden. 

Day 2 : 
Nairobi to Mount Kenya via Thika 

Drive north with a stop at the Chania and Thika Falls for river and forest birds, then continue to Serena Mountain Lodge in the forest on Mount Kenya's slopes. 

Day 3 : 
Mount Kenya forest 

A full day birding the montane forest for turacos, trogons, green ibis and mountain specialists. Overnight at Naro Moru River Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Mount Kenya to Lake Baringo via Lake Bogoria 

Stop at Nyahururu Falls, then visit Lake Bogoria for flamingos and greater kudu before reaching Lake Baringo Club. 

Day 5 : 
Full day at Lake Baringo 

Bird the cliffs, bush and lake shore, with a chance of owls, hornbills, pygmy falcon and bee-eaters. Optional boat ride. 

Day 6 : 
Lake Baringo to Kakamega Forest 

Drive west via Eldoret to Rondo Retreat in Kakamega Forest. Afternoon walk for forest birds and colobus monkeys. 

Day 7 : 
Full day in Kakamega Forest 

Walk the forest trails with a local guide for great blue turaco, grey parrot, wattle-eyes and barbets. 

Day 8 : 
Kakamega to Lake Victoria 

Drive to Kisumu and walk the papyrus at Dunga for papyrus gonolek and swamp warblers. Overnight at Sunset Hotel. 

Day 9 : 
Lake Victoria to Lake Nakuru 

Drive to Lake Nakuru National Park for flamingos, pelicans and rhinos. Overnight at Sarova Lion Hill Lodge. 

Day 10 : 
Lake Nakuru to Lake Naivasha 

Morning drive at Nakuru, then on to Lake Naivasha Country Club. Optional boat ride in the afternoon. 

Day 11 : 
Full day at Lake Naivasha 

Bird walks in the lodge grounds and a visit to Elsamere for water birds, swifts and raptors. 

Day 12 : 
Lake Naivasha to the Masai Mara 

Drive to the Masai Mara and check in at Sarova Mara Camp. Afternoon game and bird drive. 

Day 13 : 
Masai Mara 

A full day on the plains for bustards, cisticolas, raptors and the big cats. 

Day 14 : 
Masai Mara 

Another full day in the reserve, with time to search for any species still missing from your list. 

Day 15 : 
Masai Mara to Nairobi and departure 

Drive back to Nairobi for lunch and a day room, then transfer to the airport for your flight.

How many species can I expect on this safari? 

Many guests record more than 450 species in 15 days, thanks to the wide range of habitats from highland forest to savannah. 

Which lodges are used? 

Nairobi Safari Club, Serena Mountain Lodge, Naro Moru River Lodge, Lake Baringo Club, Rondo Retreat, Sunset Hotel Kisumu, Sarova Lion Hill, Lake Naivasha Country Club and Sarova Mara Camp, or similar. 

Is Kakamega Forest worth the long drive? 

Yes. It is Kenya’s only tropical rainforest and holds many West and Central African species not found anywhere else in the country. 

Will I see big game too? 

Yes. Lake Nakuru has rhinos and the Masai Mara has lions, elephants, buffaloes and leopards. 

What fitness level is needed? 

Easy to moderate. Forest walks at Kakamega and Mount Kenya are on trails and can be muddy. The rest is by vehicle with short walks. 

Can I shorten the Kenya birding and wildlife safari? 

Yes. We can drop the Lake Victoria section or end after Lake Naivasha. Ask us for a shorter version. 

What should I bring? 

Binoculars, a field guide, a rain jacket, comfortable walking shoes and warm layers for Mount Kenya. See our Kenya packing list for more.
