## Overview 
This 1 day gorilla trekking Rwanda tour takes you from Kigali to Volcanoes National Park and back the same day, with one hour beside a habituated mountain gorilla family. Your USD 1,500 gorilla permit, private 4×4, ranger guides and lunch in Musanze are included. Treks run all year, drier from June to September and December to February, and the price starts from USD 2,066 per adult. 
It suits business travellers, conference delegates and visitors on a short stopover in Kigali who want to see mountain gorillas without adding nights to their trip. Trekkers must be at least 15 years old. 

## Why choose a 1 day gorilla trekking Rwanda tour? 
Volcanoes National Park is about 2.5 hours from Kigali on good tarmac roads, which makes Rwanda one of the few places where a same-day gorilla trek is realistic. In most other gorilla destinations you need at least one night near the park before your trek. 
- Short on time: see the gorillas without adding nights to your stay. 
- Business travellers: fit the trek around meetings or a conference in Kigali. 
- Stopovers: use a long layover for a once-in-a-lifetime wildlife encounter. 

## What to expect on the 1 day gorilla trekking Rwanda tour 
The day starts with a pick-up from your Kigali hotel at about 4:30 AM and a drive of about 2.5 hours north to the park headquarters at Kinigi, arriving before 7:00 AM. There you enjoy a cultural dance welcome and a ranger briefing, and each group of up to eight trekkers is assigned to one gorilla family matched to their fitness. 
The trek leads through farmland, bamboo and montane forest on the slopes of the Virunga volcanoes and takes about 1 to 6 hours in total. Once you find the gorillas, you spend exactly one hour watching the silverback, mothers and youngsters. After lunch at a hotel in Musanze, you drive back to Kigali for drop-off at your hotel or the airport in the late afternoon or early evening. 

### How fit do you need to be for gorilla trekking in a day? 
Gorilla trekking in a day needs no athletic training, but you should be comfortable walking for several hours on steep, muddy ground at altitude. Rangers match groups to families by fitness, walking sticks are provided, and porters can carry your bag. Read how hard gorilla trekking is and our full guide to gorilla trekking in Rwanda before you go.

Not included are international flights, visas, travel insurance, porter fees (about USD 15 to 20 per porter, optional), accommodation before or after the tour, tips (about USD 10 per person per day is suggested), drinks other than water and personal items. Here is how your 1 day gorilla trekking Rwanda tour unfolds. 

Day 01 : 
Kigali - Volcanoes National Park - Kigali 

Your driver guide will pick you up from your Kigali hotel or residence at about 4:30 AM. Drive north for approximately 2.5 hours through the rolling hills of the Land of a Thousand Hills, arriving at the Kinigi park headquarters before 7:00 AM. 
Here you will enjoy a cultural dance welcome and a briefing by the park rangers, who will assign you to a gorilla family based on your fitness. Drive to the trailhead and begin your gorilla trek through farmland, bamboo and montane forest, which can take anywhere from 1 to 6 hours. 
When you find the gorillas, spend one magical hour watching the silverback, mothers and playful youngsters just a few metres away. After the trek, enjoy lunch at a hotel in Musanze, then drive back to Kigali, arriving late afternoon or early evening for drop-off at your hotel or the airport.

Can you do 1 day gorilla trekking in Rwanda from Kigali? 

Yes, 1 day gorilla trekking in Rwanda from Kigali is possible because Volcanoes National Park is about 2.5 hours away by road. You leave at about 4:30 AM, trek the gorillas, have lunch in Musanze and return to Kigali in the late afternoon or early evening. 

What is included in the 1 day gorilla trekking Rwanda tour? 

The 1 day gorilla trekking Rwanda tour includes the USD 1,500 gorilla permit, Kigali hotel pick-up and drop-off, a private 4×4 Land Cruiser with an English-speaking driver guide, ranger guides, lunch in Musanze and drinking water. Flights, visas, insurance, porters, tips and other drinks are extra. 

When is the best time for a one day gorilla trek in Rwanda? 

The best time for a one day gorilla trek in Rwanda is the drier season, roughly June to September and December to February, when trails are firmer. Treks run all year, and the wetter months bring lush forest and fewer visitors. Permits are limited each day, so book early. 

How fit do you need to be for this Rwanda gorilla day trip? 

This Rwanda gorilla day trip suits most reasonably fit travellers, though you hike for about 1 to 6 hours on steep, muddy ground at altitude. Rangers match groups to gorilla families by fitness, and porters can carry your bag. Trekkers must be at least 15 years old.
