## Overview 
This 1 day gorilla trekking Rwanda tour is the quickest way to come face to face with wild mountain gorillas. Volcanoes National Park lies about 2.5 hours’ drive from Kigali, so you can leave before sunrise, spend an unforgettable hour with a habituated gorilla family and be back in the city by evening. 
It is the perfect choice if you are in Kigali for business, a conference or a short stopover, or if you simply want the highlight of Rwanda without the extra nights. Your permit, private 4×4 transport, ranger guides and lunch are all arranged for you. 

## Why choose a 1 day gorilla trekking Rwanda tour? 
Rwanda is one of only three countries where you can see mountain gorillas, and Volcanoes National Park is the easiest of these places to reach. Good tarmac roads run all the way from Kigali to the park headquarters at Kinigi. 
That short transfer is what makes a same-day trip realistic here. In most other gorilla destinations you need at least one night near the park before your trek. 
- Short on time: see the gorillas without adding nights to your trip. 
- Business travellers: fit the trek around meetings in Kigali. 
- Stopovers: make the most of a long layover with a once-in-a-lifetime encounter. 

## What to expect on your 1 day gorilla trekking Rwanda tour 
The day starts with an early pick-up from your Kigali hotel and a scenic drive north through the “Land of a Thousand Hills”. At Kinigi you join a short welcome and a briefing from the park rangers. 
The rangers then assign each group of up to eight trekkers to one gorilla family. Families are matched to fitness levels, so tell your guide if you would prefer a gentler walk. 
The hike leads through farmland and into bamboo and montane forest on the slopes of the Virunga volcanoes. Depending on where the family is feeding, the trek can take anywhere from about one to six hours in total. 
When you reach the gorillas, you spend exactly one hour with them. You watch the silverback, mothers and playful youngsters go about their day just a few metres away. 

### How fit do you need to be? 
You do not need to be an athlete, but you should be comfortable walking for several hours on steep, muddy and uneven ground at altitude. Porters can carry your bag and help you on the trickier sections, and walking sticks are provided at the start. 

## Best time for 1 day gorilla trekking in Rwanda 
Gorilla trekking runs all year. The drier months, roughly June to September and December to February, usually mean firmer trails and easier hiking. 
The wetter months bring muddier paths but lush green forest and fewer visitors. Whenever you travel, permits are limited each day, so book as early as you can. 

## What to pack 
Pack light for your 1 day gorilla trekking Rwanda tour, but come prepared for mud, rain and nettles. 
- Waterproof jacket and long trousers 
- Sturdy, broken-in hiking boots and gaiters 
- Garden or hiking gloves to protect against nettles 
- Long-sleeved shirt in muted colours 
- Camera with spare batteries (no flash allowed) 
- Your passport, which is needed to verify your permit 
For a full guide to permits, seasons and gorilla families, read our complete guide to gorilla trekking in Rwanda . You can also check the official park information on Visit Rwanda’s Volcanoes National Park page .

Here is how your 1 day gorilla trekking Rwanda tour unfolds. 

Day 01 : 
Kigali - Volcanoes National Park - Kigali 

Your driver guide will pick you up from your Kigali hotel or residence at about 4:30 AM. Drive north for approximately 2.5 hours through the rolling hills of the Land of a Thousand Hills, arriving at the Kinigi park headquarters before 7:00 AM. 
Here you will enjoy a cultural dance welcome and a briefing by the park rangers, who will assign you to a gorilla family based on your fitness. Drive to the trailhead and begin your gorilla trek through farmland, bamboo and montane forest, which can take anywhere from 1 to 6 hours. 
When you find the gorillas, spend one magical hour watching the silverback, mothers and playful youngsters just a few metres away. After the trek, enjoy lunch at a hotel in Musanze, then drive back to Kigali, arriving late afternoon or early evening for drop-off at your hotel or the airport.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

Can you do 1 day gorilla trekking in Rwanda from Kigali? 

Yes. Volcanoes National Park is about 2.5 hours’ drive from Kigali, so you can leave at around 4:30 AM, trek the gorillas and return to Kigali the same evening. 

How much is a gorilla permit in Rwanda? 

A gorilla trekking permit in Rwanda costs USD 1,500 per person per trek, and it is included in this package. Permits are limited each day, so book early. Always confirm the current fee before booking. 

How long do you spend with the gorillas? 

Once you reach the gorilla family, you spend exactly one hour with them. The full trek, there and back, can take anywhere from about one to six hours. 

What is the minimum age for gorilla trekking? 

Visitors must be at least 15 years old to trek mountain gorillas in Rwanda. 

How many people are in a gorilla trekking group? 

Each gorilla family can be visited by a group of no more than eight trekkers per day, which keeps the encounter calm for both the gorillas and visitors. 

Is 1 day gorilla trekking in Rwanda tiring? 

A 1 day gorilla trekking Rwanda tour is a long day with an early start and several hours of hiking, but most reasonably fit travellers manage it well. Rangers match groups to families by fitness, and porters can carry your bag. 

What should I wear for gorilla trekking? 

Wear long trousers, a long-sleeved top, sturdy hiking boots, gaiters and gloves, and bring a waterproof jacket. Muted colours are best in the forest.
