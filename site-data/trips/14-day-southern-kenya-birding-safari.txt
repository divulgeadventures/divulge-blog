## 
This southern Kenya birding safari follows the road from Nairobi to the Indian Ocean and back, visiting some of the most interesting birding sites in the country. It combines classic game parks with rare forest birds and coastal waders. 
Highlights include the critically endangered Taita thrush and Taita white-eye, the Sokoke scops owl in Arabuko-Sokoke and crab plovers at Mida Creek. 

## Why choose this southern Kenya birding safari? 
- Amboseli: elephants below Kilimanjaro and swamp birds. 
- Tsavo West: dry-country birds around Kilaguni. 
- Taita Hills: Taita thrush, Taita white-eye and Taita apalis. 
- Arabuko-Sokoke and Mida Creek: Clarke’s weaver, Amani sunbird and crab plovers. 
- Tsavo East and Chyulu Hills: big red elephants and highland forest. 

## Where you stay 
Nairobi Safari Club, Ol Tukai Lodge in Amboseli, Kilaguni Serena Lodge in Tsavo West, a lodge near the Taita Hills, Turtle Bay Beach Club in Watamu, Tsavo Park Hotel and Hunter’s Lodge at Kiboko, or similar. 

## Best time for a southern Kenya birding safari 
September to April brings migrant waders to the coast and migrant songbirds inland. June to October is dry, with excellent game viewing in Amboseli and Tsavo.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Nairobi 

Airport pick-up, a visit to the National Museum bird gallery and a walk in the botanic garden. Overnight at Nairobi Safari Club. 

Day 2 : 
Nairobi to Amboseli 

Drive south to Amboseli with a game drive on arrival. Overnight at Ol Tukai Lodge. 

Day 3 : 
Full day in Amboseli 

Birding with your guide around the swamps and plains, and photos of Kilimanjaro at dawn. 

Day 4 : 
Amboseli to Tsavo West 

Game drive to Tsavo West and lunch at Kilaguni Serena Lodge. Afternoon birding around the lodge waterhole. 

Day 5 : 
Taita Hills 

Visit the forest patches of the Taita Hills for Taita thrush, Taita white-eye and Taita apalis. Overnight at a lodge near the hills. 

Day 6 : 
Taita Hills to Watamu 

Drive to the coast and check in at Turtle Bay Beach Club for five nights. 

Day 7 : 
Arabuko-Sokoke Forest 

A full day for Clarke's weaver, Amani sunbird, Sokoke pipit and the Sokoke scops owl. 

Day 8 : 
Arabuko-Sokoke Forest 

Another forest day for ant-eater thrush, Mombasa woodpecker and eastern nicator. 

Day 9 : 
Mida Creek 

Bird the mangroves and tidal flats for crab plovers, terek sandpipers and mangrove kingfishers. 

Day 10 : 
Sabaki River mouth 

Drive north of Malindi to the Sabaki estuary for terns, gulls, Malindi pipit and waders. 

Day 11 : 
Watamu to Tsavo East 

Drive inland to Tsavo East National Park. Overnight at Tsavo Park Hotel. 

Day 12 : 
Tsavo East to Kibwezi 

Morning game drive in Tsavo East, then on to Kibwezi Forest. Overnight at Hunter's Lodge. 

Day 13 : 
Chyulu Hills 

A day in the forest and grasslands of the Chyulu Hills. Overnight at Hunter's Lodge. 

Day 14 : 
Return to Nairobi 

Drive to Nairobi for lunch and a farewell dinner before your flight.

What are the Taita Hills endemics? 

The Taita thrush, Taita white-eye and Taita apalis live only in the small forest patches of the Taita Hills in southern Kenya. 

Which lodges are used? 

Nairobi Safari Club, Ol Tukai Lodge, Kilaguni Serena Lodge, a Taita Hills lodge, Turtle Bay Beach Club, Tsavo Park Hotel and Hunter’s Lodge, or similar. 

Is the southern Kenya birding safari good for big game? 

Yes. Amboseli and Tsavo have large elephant herds, lions and buffaloes, and Tsavo East is famous for red-dusted elephants. 

How long are the drives? 

Most drives are three to five hours with birding stops. The coast section has short daily trips. 

Can non-birders join? 

Yes. The beach at Watamu, the marine park and the big game parks make it great for mixed groups. 

What is the best time to go? 

September to April for migrants and coastal waders; June to October for dry-season game viewing. 

Is there a farewell dinner? 

Yes. We end with a farewell dinner in Nairobi before your flight.
