## Overview 
The Mount Bisoke hike is one of the most rewarding day adventures in Rwanda. Bisoke rises to 3,711 metres on the border with the Democratic Republic of Congo, and its summit holds a beautiful crater lake about 400 metres across. 
This guided trip leaves Kigali before sunrise and returns the same evening. Your permit, armed ranger guides, private 4×4 transport and a packed lunch are all arranged, so all you need to bring is good boots and energy. 

## Why choose this Mount Bisoke hike? 
Bisoke is the most popular volcano climb in Volcanoes National Park because it packs a lot into one day. You pass through several vegetation zones, from farmland to bamboo and then moss-draped Hagenia and Hypericum forest. 
No technical climbing skills are needed. It is a steady, steep walk that suits anyone with reasonable fitness, and the views over the Virunga chain are a big reward. 
- Short on time: a full volcano adventure without adding nights. 
- Gorilla trekkers: a great extra day before or after your gorilla trek. 
- Hikers: one of the most scenic day climbs in East Africa. 

## What to expect on the Mount Bisoke hike 
After the 7:00 AM briefing at Kinigi park headquarters, you drive to the trailhead and start climbing with your rangers. The trail is steep in places and often muddy, so walking sticks are handed out at the start. 
Most hikers reach the crater rim in about four hours. On a clear day the lake below the rim looks almost unreal, framed by giant lobelias and mist. The descent takes another two to three hours. 
Keep your eyes open on the way. Rangers often point out signs of forest buffaloes and elephants, and the forest is full of birds. 

### How fit do you need to be? 
You should be comfortable walking uphill for several hours at altitude. Go at your own pace, drink plenty of water and consider hiring a porter to carry your bag. Porters are local community members and the fee goes straight to them. 

## Best time for a Mount Bisoke hike 
You can hike Bisoke all year. The drier months, roughly June to September and December to February, give firmer trails and clearer views of the crater lake. In the rainy months, bring good rain gear and expect thick mud. 

## What to pack 
- Waterproof hiking boots and gaiters 
- Rain jacket and a warm fleece for the summit 
- Gardening gloves for grabbing vegetation 
- Snacks, sunscreen and a hat 
- Your passport to check in for the permit

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is what to expect on your Mount Bisoke hike, from the early start in Kigali to the crater lake and back. 

Day 01 : 
Kigali - Volcanoes National Park (Mount Bisoke) - Kigali 

Your driver guide will collect you from your Kigali hotel at 4:30 AM for the approximately 2.5-hour drive north to the Kinigi headquarters of Volcanoes National Park, arriving in time for the 7:00 AM briefing by Rwanda Development Board rangers. 
Start your guided Mount Bisoke hike from the trailhead, climbing steadily through bamboo thickets and then into enchanting Hagenia and Hypericum forest draped in moss and lichen. The ascent takes about 4 hours, and porters are available if you would like help with your daypack. 
At the summit, take in the breathtaking crater lake and, on a clear day, views across the Virunga range into Congo and Uganda. Enjoy your packed lunch before the 2 to 3-hour descent. Meet your driver at the trailhead and drive back to Kigali, arriving around 5:00 PM for drop-off at your hotel.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

How hard is the Mount Bisoke hike? 

It is a moderate to challenging hike. Most people take about 4 hours to reach the summit and 2 to 3 hours to come down, on steep and sometimes muddy trails. Good general fitness is enough; no climbing experience is needed. 

How high is Mount Bisoke? 

Mount Bisoke rises to 3,711 metres (12,175 feet) above sea level. The summit holds a crater lake about 400 metres across. 

Can I hike Mount Bisoke as a day trip from Kigali? 

Yes. You leave Kigali at about 4:30 AM, hike during the day and return to Kigali by early evening. You can also stay overnight in Musanze to make the day more relaxed. 

What should I wear for the Bisoke hike? 

Wear waterproof hiking boots, long trousers, a long-sleeved top, a rain jacket and gardening gloves for grabbing vegetation. Bring warm layers, as it gets cold near the summit. 

Do I need a porter for the Mount Bisoke hike? 

A porter is optional but helpful. They carry your bag and give you a hand on steep, slippery sections, and the fee supports local families. 

Is the crater lake always visible? 

Not always. Mist can cover the summit, especially in the rainy months. Mornings in the dry season usually give the clearest views. 

Can I combine the Mount Bisoke hike with gorilla trekking? 

Yes. Many travellers hike Bisoke the day before or after a gorilla trek, staying in Musanze or Kinigi between the two.
