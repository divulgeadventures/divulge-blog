## Overview 
This is a 5-Day Boteti Tented Safari Lodge Adventure, designed to showcase the breathtaking wilderness of Botswana and offer an authentic African safari experience. 
Set in the beautiful Boteti River region near Makgadikgadi Pans National Park, this safari offers incredible wildlife encounters with elephants, zebras, wildebeests, antelope, and predators gathering along the riverbanks. 
Enjoy exciting game drives, stunning landscapes, unforgettable sunsets, and a comfortable stay in a luxury tented lodge while experiencing Botswana’s raw natural beauty and remarkable wildlife. 

### Highlights 
- Okavango Delta 
- Moremi Game Reserve 
- Botswana Pristine wilderness 
- Boteti Tented Safari 

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
Begining of Boteti Tented Safari Lodge 

Your will be met by our Representative from Divulge Adventures where you will start your safari you will leave for ranger at Maun Airport for a 20-minute ride to Boteti Tented Safari Lodge. 
After check-in, a cool drink and a briefing, relax before a 2-hour mokoro safari on the Boteti River, offering stunning sunset views. 
(If water levels are too low, this will be replaced by a Xobe Village Tour) Encounter diverse wildlife like crocodiles and hippos. 
Your experienced guide will highlight river features. Relax after the safari by a fire with wine and a traditional supper, catering to dietary needs. 
Spend the night in a luxurious safari tent. 

Day 02 : 
Mokoro Safari 

Awake in Boteti Tented Safari Lodge to birdsong, enjoy tea and biscuits, then embark on a full-day mokoro safari in the Okavango Delta. 
Glide through reed waterways with a knowledgeable Bayei guide, learning about the delta's wonders and wildlife. 
Rest on an island for lunch, then explore on foot. Return by 16h00 for drinks on the deck and dinner under the stars 

Day 03 : 
Moremi Safari 

Your full-day Moremi safari runs from 06h00 to 18h00. In an open vehicle, get close to wildlife with a specially adapted experience guided by an educated ranger. 
Encounter diverse species like lions, giraffes, and 500 bird types. Lunch is served stylishly en route, and the day ends with drinks on the deck and dinner under the stars. 

Day 04 : 
Flight over Okavanga Delta 

Wake up to the bush sounds for brunch and coffee. Experience the Okavango Delta from above as you take a flight in a helicopter from the lodge on the banks of the Boteti River. 
Explore the lush habitat, spotting crocodiles, elephants, and more. Enjoy the scenic flight over channels and palm-covered islands. 
There is no better way to appreciate the Okavango Delta's exceptional splendour than by taking a scenic trip in a tiny aircraft. 
Afterwards, unwind by the fire for your final evening in Botswana. 

Day 05 : 
End of Boteti Tented Safari lodge 

After a busy day of activities and travelling, a quieter morning awaits you, allowing you to once again enjoy the lodge and its facilities after a bush breakfast. 
Later in the morning, you transfer back to Maun Airport or your choice of drop-off point if you want to be elsewhere in the town of Maun.
