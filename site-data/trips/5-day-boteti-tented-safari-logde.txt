## Overview 
This 5 day Boteti and Okavango Delta safari is a 5-day, 4-night lodge trip from Maun. You stay all 4 nights at Boteti Tented Safari Lodge on the Boteti River, with a delta mokoro day, a Moremi game drive and a scenic flight. The dry months, roughly May to October, are best for game viewing. It is priced from USD 2,163 per adult and suits couples and first-time Botswana visitors. 
You unpack once, and every day brings a different way to see the delta: by dugout canoe, by open vehicle and from the air. 

## Why choose a 5 day Boteti and Okavango Delta safari? 
The Boteti River flows out of the Okavango system, and its banks draw elephants, zebras, wildebeest, antelope, hippos and crocodiles, along with the predators that follow them. Staying at one tented lodge means no long transfers between camps, and drinks on the deck and dinners under the stars at the end of each day. 
From the lodge you reach the Okavango Delta and Moremi Game Reserve on full-day excursions, so a short trip still covers water, wildlife and a bird’s-eye view of the delta. 
- Couples: a comfortable tented lodge with campfire evenings and sunset views. 
- First-time visitors: mokoro, game drive and scenic flight in just 4 nights. 
- Short on time: starts and ends at Maun Airport. 

## What to expect on the 5 day Boteti and Okavango Delta safari 
Day 1 starts with a meet and greet at Maun Airport and a transfer to Boteti Tented Safari Lodge. After check-in and a briefing you head out on a 2-hour mokoro trip on the Boteti River at sunset, looking for hippos and crocodiles. If the water is too low, a Xobe village tour replaces it. Day 2 is a full-day mokoro safari through the reed channels of the Okavango Delta with a Bayei poler, a picnic lunch on an island and a walk on foot. 
Day 3 is a full-day Moremi safari from 06h00 to 18h00 in an open vehicle, looking for lions, giraffes and some of the reserve’s 500 bird species, with lunch served on the way. On day 4 you take a scenic flight over the Okavango Delta, with its channels and palm islands, then spend your last evening by the fire. On day 5 you enjoy a quiet bush breakfast before the transfer back to Maun Airport or your chosen drop-off in Maun. 

### Is 5 days enough for the Okavango Delta? 
Five days is enough for a first taste of the Okavango Delta because this trip uses three full days for three different experiences: mokoro, game drive and scenic flight. The dry season, roughly May to October, brings the floodwaters and the best game viewing. Our best time to visit Botswana guide explains each month in detail.

Day 01 : 
Begining of Boteti Tented Safari Lodge 

Your will be met by our Representative from Divulge Adventures where you will start your safari you will leave for ranger at Maun Airport for a 20-minute ride to Boteti Tented Safari Lodge. 
After check-in, a cool drink and a briefing, relax before a 2-hour mokoro safari on the Boteti River, offering stunning sunset views. 
(If water levels are too low, this will be replaced by a Xobe Village Tour) Encounter diverse wildlife like crocodiles and hippos. 
Your experienced guide will highlight river features. Relax after the safari by a fire with wine and a traditional supper, catering to dietary needs. 
Spend the night in a luxurious safari tent. 

Day 02 : 
Mokoro Safari 

Awake in Boteti Tented Safari Lodge to birdsong, enjoy tea and biscuits, then embark on a full-day mokoro safari in the Okavango Delta. 
Glide through reed waterways with a knowledgeable Bayei guide, learning about the delta's wonders and wildlife. 
Rest on an island for lunch, then explore on foot. Return by 16h00 for drinks on the deck and dinner under the stars 

Day 03 : 
Moremi Safari 

Your full-day Moremi safari runs from 06h00 to 18h00. In an open vehicle, get close to wildlife with a specially adapted experience guided by an educated ranger. 
Encounter diverse species like lions, giraffes, and 500 bird types. Lunch is served stylishly en route, and the day ends with drinks on the deck and dinner under the stars. 

Day 04 : 
Flight over Okavanga Delta 

Wake up to the bush sounds for brunch and coffee. Experience the Okavango Delta from above as you take a flight in a helicopter from the lodge on the banks of the Boteti River. 
Explore the lush habitat, spotting crocodiles, elephants, and more. Enjoy the scenic flight over channels and palm-covered islands. 
There is no better way to appreciate the Okavango Delta's exceptional splendour than by taking a scenic trip in a tiny aircraft. 
Afterwards, unwind by the fire for your final evening in Botswana. 

Day 05 : 
End of Boteti Tented Safari lodge 

After a busy day of activities and travelling, a quieter morning awaits you, allowing you to once again enjoy the lodge and its facilities after a bush breakfast. 
Later in the morning, you transfer back to Maun Airport or your choice of drop-off point if you want to be elsewhere in the town of Maun.

When is the best time for the 5 day Boteti and Okavango Delta safari? 

The 5 day Boteti and Okavango Delta safari runs all year, but the dry season, roughly May to October, is best for mokoro trips and game viewing because the floodwaters are high and animals gather near water. From November to April the delta is green and quieter, with newborn animals and excellent birding. If river levels are low, the Boteti mokoro trip becomes a village tour.

Where do you stay on the Boteti and Okavango Delta safari? 

On the Boteti and Okavango Delta safari you stay all 4 nights at Boteti Tented Safari Lodge, a tented lodge on the banks of the Boteti River reached by transfer from Maun Airport. You return there each evening after the mokoro trip, the Moremi game drive and the scenic flight, so there is no packing and moving between camps. 

Is the Boteti and Okavango Delta safari suitable for families? 

The Boteti and Okavango Delta safari suits families with older children who can sit through a 12-hour game drive and a full day in a mokoro. Mokoro trips and scenic flights may have minimum ages, so tell us your children’s ages and we will confirm each activity. Only light fitness is needed for the short island walk.
