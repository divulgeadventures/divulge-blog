## Overview 
This 11 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar beach holiday spends 6 nights on safari in northern Tanzania, with Tarangire, 3 nights in the Serengeti and a night at the Ngorongoro Crater, then 4 nights in Zanzibar at Golden Tulip Stone Town. The page gives no fixed season. It costs from USD 3,908 per adult and suits first-time visitors who want safari, culture and beach in one trip. 

## Why choose an 11 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar beach holiday? 
The 11 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar beach holiday gives the Serengeti three full nights, which is more time than most northern circuit trips allow and gives you the best chance of finding the big herds and their predators. Tarangire adds big elephant herds and baobab trees, and the drive to the Serengeti passes the Ngorongoro Crater rim and Olduvai Gorge. 
Zanzibar then balances the early game drives with a guided Stone Town walk and free days by the pool or on the beach. 
- Serengeti time: 3 nights at Tamba Tented Camp with full-day game drives. 
- Four parks and areas: Tarangire, the Serengeti, the Ngorongoro Conservation Area and Olduvai Gorge. 
- Culture: a guided tour of Stone Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, with lunch on a rooftop. 

## What to expect on the 11 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar beach holiday 
Day 1 starts at Kilimanjaro International Airport, where your driver-guide meets you for the transfer to Mount Meru Hotel in Arusha. On day 2 you take packed lunches into Tarangire National Park for a game drive among elephants and baobabs, then stay at Manyara Secret Lodge. 
Day 3 crosses the Ngorongoro Conservation Area with views of the crater and a stop at Olduvai Gorge, then game drives into the Serengeti for a sunset drive. Days 4 and 5 are full days in the Serengeti with picnic lunches. All three Serengeti nights are at Tamba Tented Camp. 
Day 6 is a morning game drive in the Serengeti before you drive to Ang’ata Camps Ngorongoro inside the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. On day 7 you leave at 10:00 for Arusha Airport and fly to Zanzibar, with a 20-minute transfer to Golden Tulip Stone Town. 
Day 8 is a guided Stone Town tour: the market, the 19th-century cathedral on the former slave market site, the Old Fort, Tippu Tip House and the People’s Palace Museum. Days 9 and 10 are free for the pool or the beach, and day 11 ends with a transfer to Abeid Amani Karume International Airport. 

### Is 6 nights on safari enough for Tanzania’s northern circuit? 
Six nights is enough for Tanzania’s northern circuit on this route, because half of them are in the Serengeti, where game viewing is richest. Check our best time to visit Tanzania guide to match your dates to the herds, and our Ngorongoro Crater wildlife guide for what lives in the crater.

Day 01 : 
Arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport -Arusha 

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your driver guide and our representative before being transferred to your hotel in Arusha. 
Depending on your arrival time, you may relax and enjoy the peaceful surroundings of the hotel as you prepare for your Northern Tanzania safari adventure. 
Dinner and overnight at the hotel Mount Meru Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Arusha - Tarangire National Park 

After breakfast, depart with packed lunches for Tarangire National Park, Tanzania’s third-largest national park and home to large herds of elephants and iconic baobab trees. 
The Tarangire River attracts a wide variety of wildlife including lions, leopards, cheetahs, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, and many bird species. 
Enjoy a game drive through the park before proceeding to your lodge for dinner and overnight at Manyara Secret Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Tarangire- Serengeti National Park 

After breakfast, depart for the world-famous Serengeti National Park via the Ngorongoro Conservation Area in Tanzania. 
Along the way, enjoy breathtaking views of the Ngorongoro Crater and visit Olduvai Gorge, known as the cradle of mankind where important archaeological discoveries were made by Dr. Louis and Mary Leakey. 
Continue with game viewing en route as you enter the endless plains of the Serengeti. 
Enjoy an evening sunset game drive before heading to camp.Dinner and overnight at Tamba Tented Camp 

Day 04 : 
Full Day Serengeti National Park 

Early morning game drive to see also nocturnal predators returning from hunting and animals waking up in early morning light followed by full day Serengeti national park in Tanzania tour putting you in a position to see lots of the animals. 
Serengeti is the Name comes from Maasai Language meaning endless plain. 
Therefore is home to many animals including Lions, Elephants, Giraffe, Wildebeest, Zebra, Leopard, Cheetah, warthogs, Hyena plus a lots and lots of bird life. 
In many cases migration is taking place around Serengeti and at that particular time millions of wildebeest and zebra are mingling together followed by predators. 
Dinner and overnight at Tamba Tented Camp Serengeti 

Day 05 : 
Full Day Serengeti National Park 

Spend another exciting day exploring different regions of the Serengeti with picnic lunches provided. 
This additional day allows for more opportunities to witness incredible wildlife encounters and capture unforgettable safari moments across the endless plains. 
Dinner and overnight at Tamba Tented Camp 

Day 06 : 
Serengeti - Ngorongoro Conservation Area 

After breakfast at the lodge set off for a morning game drive. 
After lunch proceed to Ngorongoro game drive in Tanzania en route arriving in time for dinner and overnight at Ang’ata Camps Ngorongoro inside NgoroNgoro conservation Area 

Day 07 : 
Ngorongoro Crater - Zanzibar 

After breakfast at 10:00hrs drive back to Arusha Airport for Zanzibar flight 
On arrival at Zanzibar’s Abeid Amani Karume International Airport, you will be met by our driver who will take you on the short 20-minute transfer to your hotel in Stone Town, the historic quarter of Zanzibar City. 
You have the evening at leisure and your dinner tonight will be provided by your hotel Golden Tulip Stone town 

Day 08 : 
Stone Town Hote- Beach Hotel 

After breakfast at your hotel, meet your expert local guide for a fascinating tour of Stone Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site rich in culture and history. 
Explore the vibrant local market filled with exotic aromas, visit the historic 19th-century cathedral built on the former slave market site, and wander through narrow streets lined with Arab and Indian-style architecture. 
You’ll also see key landmarks such as the Old Fort, Tippu Tip House, and the People’s Palace Museum. 
Enjoy lunch at a rooftop Indian restaurant overlooking the ocean before returning to your hotel for dinner and an evening at Golden Tulip Stone town 

Day 09 : 
Leisure at your Beach Hotel 

Today, you are free to do exactly what you want - and to do as little as you want. 
After all, you can simply relax by your hotel pool or down on the beach, perhaps under a swaying palm tree. 
If a day of total inactivity is really not your thing, you can swim or take advantage of one of the activities on offer via your hotel reception.

When is the best time for the 11 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar beach holiday? 

The 11 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar beach holiday runs all year, and the page gives no fixed season. June to October is dry, with easy game viewing and sunny Zanzibar weather. December to March often brings the herds to the southern and central Serengeti. April and May are the long rains, when some camps close or offer lower rates. Tell us your dates and we will check availability.
