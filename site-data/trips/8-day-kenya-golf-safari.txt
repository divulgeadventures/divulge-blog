## 
This 8 day Kenya golf safari combines four of Kenya’s best-known courses with two days in the Masai Mara. Your group plays Karen Country Club and Muthaiga in Nairobi, the Great Rift Valley Lodge course above Lake Naivasha and the PGA Baobab Course at Vipingo Ridge on the coast, with game drives in between. 

## Why choose an 8 day Kenya golf safari? 
Kenya has some of the best golf in East Africa, and the courses are close to the safari parks. Karen Country Club is laid out on Karen Blixen’s former coffee estate, and Muthaiga has long been home to the Kenya Open. The Great Rift Valley course sits at about 7,000 feet on the Eburru range, with views of Lake Naivasha and Mount Longonot and zebras on the fairways. 
The trip ends at Vipingo Ridge north of Mombasa, where the PGA Baobab Course became the first in Africa with PGA accreditation. Between rounds you spend two days in the Masai Mara looking for lions, elephants and, from July to October, the migration. 

## What does the golf safari itinerary include? 
Two nights in Nairobi, two at the Great Rift Valley Lodge and Golf Resort, two in the Masai Mara and one at Vipingo Ridge, with four rounds of golf, a Lake Naivasha boat ride and a light-aircraft flight from the Mara to the coast. 

### How are tee times arranged? 
We book tee times for the whole group with each club. Muthaiga is a private members’ club, so visitor rounds follow the club’s own rules and are confirmed when we quote. Club hire, caddies and buggies can be arranged on request.

## Who is the 8 day kenya golf safari best for? 
The 8 day kenya golf safari is best for golf clubs, groups of golfing friends and companies running a client golf tour. Tell us your handicaps and preferred tee times and we will book every round, so the 8 day kenya golf safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions .

Yes. Companies use this trip as an incentive or a client golf tour, and we can arrange a scored competition, prizes and a prize-giving dinner. See our page on corporate and incentive safaris for more ideas.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Nairobi 

Your 8 day kenya golf safari begins today. Meet your guide at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport and transfer to your hotel in Karen. Welcome dinner with the group. 

Day 2 : 
Karen Country Club 

Play a morning round at Karen Country Club, laid out on Karen Blixen's former coffee estate. Non-golfers can visit the Giraffe Centre or the elephant orphanage. Free afternoon. 

Day 3 : 
Muthaiga Golf Club to Lake Naivasha 

Play a morning round at Muthaiga Golf Club, then drive about 2 hours to the Great Rift Valley Lodge and Golf Resort on the Eburru range above Lake Naivasha. 

Day 4 : 
Great Rift Valley Lodge golf course 

Play the resort's 18-hole course at about 7,000 feet, with views of Lake Naivasha, Mount Longonot and the Rift Valley escarpments. In the afternoon, take a boat ride on Lake Naivasha among hippos and fish eagles. 

Day 5 : 
Lake Naivasha to the Masai Mara 

Drive about 5 hours to the Masai Mara, arriving for lunch. Afternoon game drive on the plains. 

Day 6 : 
Full day in the Masai Mara 

A full day of game drives with a picnic lunch, looking for lions, cheetahs, elephants and the migration in season. An early balloon flight can be added. 

Day 7 : 
Fly to the coast and Vipingo Ridge 

Morning game drive, then a light-aircraft flight to the coast, usually via Nairobi. Transfer to Vipingo Ridge north of Mombasa and relax by the ocean. 

Day 8 : 
PGA Baobab Course and departure 

Play a final round on the PGA Baobab Course at Vipingo Ridge, then transfer to Moi International Airport in Mombasa for your flight home. This is the end of your 8 day kenya golf safari.

Which courses does the 8 day Kenya golf safari include? 

The 8 day Kenya golf safari includes Karen Country Club and Muthaiga Golf Club in Nairobi, the Great Rift Valley Lodge and Golf Resort above Lake Naivasha, and the PGA Baobab Course at Vipingo Ridge north of Mombasa. We can swap in other courses, such as Windsor or Limuru, on request. 

Do I need a handicap certificate? 

Some clubs ask visitors for a handicap certificate or proof of membership of a golf club. Bring yours, and we will tell each club your handicaps when we book the tee times. 

Can non-golfers join the trip? 

Yes. Non-golfers can visit the Giraffe Centre or the elephant orphanage in Nairobi, take a boat ride or walk at Lake Naivasha and relax by the pool or beach while the group plays. 

Can each golfer pay separately? 

Yes. Each golfer can pay their own share. On group bookings the seventh place is free for every six paying travellers, a deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. 

When is the best time for a golf safari in Kenya? 

Golf is played all year. June to October and January to February are the driest months and best for the Masai Mara, and July to October adds the wildebeest migration.
