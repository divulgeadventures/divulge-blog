## Overview 
This 5 day Sossusvlei and Swakopmund tour is a guided trip from Windhoek with 2 nights at Namib Naukluft Lodge, 1 night in Swakopmund and 1 night at Omaruru Game Lodge. It covers Big Daddy Dune, Deadvlei, Sesriem Canyon, Walvis Bay and a game drive. It runs all year, costs from USD 2,150 per adult and suits travellers who want desert, coast and wildlife. 
The tour travels in a 4×4 Hilux with an English, Danish, Spanish or Italian-speaking driver guide, and all park entry fees and fixed accommodation are included. 

## Why choose a 5 day Sossusvlei and Swakopmund tour? 
The 5 day Sossusvlei and Swakopmund tour gives the Namib Desert two nights instead of one, so you can be in the dunes at sunrise without rushing. You climb Big Daddy, walk across Deadvlei and explore Sesriem Canyon before heading to the coast. 
The last two days add what shorter desert trips miss: a harbour cruise at Walvis Bay with dolphins, pelicans and seals, and a night in the bushveld at Omaruru Game Lodge, where game drives can include elephants when time allows. 
- Active travellers: a climb of Big Daddy Dune and a walk in Sesriem Canyon. 
- Wildlife lovers: marine life at Walvis Bay and a game drive at Omaruru. 
- Photographers: two desert sunrises and the light on Dune 45. 

## What to expect on the 5 day Sossusvlei and Swakopmund tour 
Day 1 starts with a meeting with your Divulge Adventures guide in Windhoek and a drive over the Spreetshoogte Pass, with apple pie in Solitaire, to Namib Naukluft Lodge. Day 2 is a pre-dawn trip into the Namib Desert for Big Daddy Dune, Deadvlei, Dune 45 and Sesriem Canyon, returning to the same lodge. 
On day 3 you drive north through Solitaire and the Gaub and Kuiseb passes to the Atlantic, stopping at the Walvis Bay flamingo lagoon before the night in Swakopmund. Day 4 starts with the Walvis Bay Harbour Cruise, then continues inland to Omaruru Game Lodge for an afternoon game drive if time permits. 
Day 5 may begin with a morning elephant drive at Omaruru, followed by the drive back to Windhoek and a city tour to end the trip. 

### Is 5 days enough for Sossusvlei and Swakopmund? 
Five days is a comfortable length for Sossusvlei and Swakopmund, because the two nights at the desert lodge give you a full day in the dunes. It does not include Etosha, so ask us to add Etosha if big game is your priority. For a shorter version, see our 4 day Sossusvlei and Walvis Bay safari . Our best time to visit Namibia guide covers the seasons.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
Journey to the Desert: Windhoek to Namib Naukluft 

On your arrival you will be met by a Representative from Divulge adventures your Namibia Experience will begin with a scenic drive from Windhoek through the dramatic Spreetshoogte Pass, offering panoramic views of the Namib Desert. 
Stop in the quirky desert town of Solitaire for a slice of legendary apple pie before continuing to your desert retreat at Namib Naukluft Lodge. 
Settle in and enjoy your first Namibia adventure sunset in one of the world’s oldest deserts. 

Day 02 : 
Dunes, Dead Vlei & Desert Landscapes 

Wake before sunrise for a magical excursion into the heart of the Namib Desert. Climb the towering Big Daddy Dune, stroll across the cracked white clay of Deadvlei, and admire the golden glow on Dune 45. 
Later, explore the sculpted depths of Sesriem Canyon, carved by centuries of erosion. Return to the lodge with sand in your shoes and memories in your heart. 

Day 03 : 
Desert to Coast: Namib Naukluft to Swakopmund 

Travel north through more wild desert landscapes. Stop again in Solitaire, then pass through the rugged Ghaub and Kuiseb Passes, descending toward the Atlantic Ocean. 
Along the way, enjoy a scenic pause at Flamingo Lagoon in Walvis Bay, a haven for birdlife. 
Arrive in Swakopmund, a charming seaside town where German colonial architecture meets the sea breeze. 

Day 04 : 
Ocean & Wildlife: Swakopmund to Omaruru Game Lodge 

Begin the day with a Walvis Bay Harbor Cruise, gliding across calm waters while dolphins, pelicans, and seals keep you company. 
Then journey inland to Omaruru Game Lodge, where you may enjoy an afternoon game drive (time permitting). 
As the sun sets over the savanna, relax and reflect on Namibia’s incredible contrasts—from desert to ocean to bushveld. 

Day 05 : 
Final Wildlife Moments: Omaruru to Windhoek 

On your final day, there may be time for a morning elephant drive—a fitting farewell to Namibia adventure majestic wildlife. 
Then it’s a relaxed drive back to Windhoek, where your short but spectacular adventure ends with a Windhoek city tour. The perfect way to soak up the towns culture and history

When is the best time for the 5 day Sossusvlei and Swakopmund tour? 

The 5 day Sossusvlei and Swakopmund tour runs all year, and the trip page does not set a season. May to September is cooler for the Big Daddy climb and Deadvlei, while December to February is hot inland. Swakopmund and Walvis Bay stay mild with morning fog, so bring a warm layer for the harbour cruise.

Is the 5 day Sossusvlei and Swakopmund tour suitable for families?
