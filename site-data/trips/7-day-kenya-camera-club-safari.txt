## 
This 7 day Kenya camera club safari is planned for photography clubs and societies travelling together. Members photograph Samburu’s rare northern species, rhinos at Ol Pejeta, flamingos at Lake Nakuru and big cats in the Masai Mara, from pop-up Land Cruisers fitted with bean bags and charging points. 

## Why book a 7 day Kenya camera club safari? 
Club trips work best when photographers have space and time. We limit the number of members in each vehicle so everyone has a window and room for long lenses, and guides know how to position for light and background. Drives start early and finish late for the best light, with a midday break to edit and back up images. 
The route gives a club a varied portfolio in one week: dry-country species and river scenes in Samburu, rhinos with Mount Kenya behind them, pink shorelines at Nakuru and action on the Mara plains. 

## What does the camera club itinerary include? 
Two nights in Samburu, one at Ol Pejeta, one at Lake Nakuru and two in the Masai Mara. Day 6 is a full day in the Mara with packed meals, so vehicles can stay with a sighting as long as it lasts. A sunrise balloon flight can be added. 

### Can the club share images and hold reviews? 
Yes. Evenings are free for image reviews and talks, and we can ask lodges for a quiet space with a screen. Some clubs bring a trip leader to run sessions, and that leader can travel on the group’s free place. 
For more on the parks, see our guides to Samburu National Reserve and Samburu vs the Masai Mara . 

## Who is the 7 day kenya camera club safari best for? 
The 7 day kenya camera club safari is best for hiking, photography, Rotary, Lions and alumni clubs that want to travel together. We plan vehicles and rooms around your members, so the 7 day kenya camera club safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## How many people can join a 7 day Kenya camera club safari? 
We plan vehicles around your group size, with fewer photographers per vehicle than on a standard safari. A club of 6 to 18 members travelling in two to four vehicles works well, and larger clubs can split into two groups on different dates.

Price on request. Group prices depend on your dates, the number of travellers and the rooms you need, so we quote every group personally. Day 1 : 
Nairobi to Samburu National Reserve 

Your 7 day kenya camera club safari begins today. Early pick-up in Nairobi and drive north past Mount Kenya, arriving in Samburu for lunch. Afternoon game drive along the Ewaso Ng'iro River, where elephants come down to drink in the late light. 

Day 2 : 
Full day in Samburu 

Morning and afternoon drives to photograph the Samburu Special Five: reticulated giraffe, Grevy's zebra, Beisa oryx, gerenuk and Somali ostrich, plus lions, leopards and elephants. Midday break at the lodge to edit and back up images. 

Day 3 : 
Samburu to Ol Pejeta Conservancy 

A sunrise drive in Samburu, then head south to Ol Pejeta. In the afternoon, photograph black and white rhinos on the open plains, with Mount Kenya behind them when the sky is clear. 

Day 4 : 
Ol Pejeta to Lake Nakuru 

Morning drive in Ol Pejeta, then continue to Lake Nakuru National Park. Spend the afternoon on the shoreline photographing flamingos, pelicans and the park's rhinos. 

Day 5 : 
Lake Nakuru to the Masai Mara 

An early start for the drive to the Masai Mara, arriving for lunch. Afternoon game drive on the plains, looking for lions and cheetahs in the golden light. 

Day 6 : 
Full day of big cat photography in the Masai Mara 

A full day in the reserve with packed breakfast and lunch, so the vehicles can stay with a sighting when the action starts. An optional sunrise balloon flight can be added. Evening image review at camp. 

Day 7 : 
Sunrise drive and return to Nairobi 

One last sunrise game drive, then breakfast and the drive back to Nairobi, arriving in the late afternoon. A short flight back can be arranged instead. This is the end of your 7 day kenya camera club safari.

How many photographers ride in each vehicle on the 7 day Kenya camera club safari? 

On the 7 day Kenya camera club safari we keep fewer photographers in each vehicle than on a standard safari so everyone has a window and room for long lenses. We confirm the vehicle plan when you tell us your club’s size. 

Does the club leader travel free? 

On group bookings the seventh place is free for every six paying travellers. Most clubs give that place to the trip leader or the person who organises the image reviews. 

What camera gear should club members bring? 

A long zoom such as 150-600 mm, a mid-range zoom for landscapes and herds, spare batteries, plenty of memory cards and a dust blower. Bean bags are in the vehicles, so tripods are rarely needed on drives. 

Can members pay separately? 

Yes. Each member can pay their own share. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the group’s places, and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 7 day kenya camera club safari. 

When is the best time for a photography safari in Kenya? 

June to October and January to February are the driest months, with good visibility and animals near water. The green season from November to May has dramatic skies, fewer vehicles and newborn animals.
