## 
This Laikipia walking safari swaps the vehicle for your own two feet. With Karisia Walking Safaris you walk through the private and community lands of the Laikipia plateau with Samburu guides, while camels carry the camp from one site to the next. You track elephants, reticulated giraffes, Grevy’s zebras and plenty of smaller life that you would miss from a car, then sleep in a simple fly camp under the stars. It suits fit travellers, repeat visitors and anyone who wants to feel the bush rather than just see it. 
Price: from USD 1,900 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Walk every day with Samburu guides 
- Camels carry the camp from site to site 
- Northern species: Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe and gerenuk 
- Fly camp under the stars, far from other visitors 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Nairobi to Laikipia 

Drive north past Mount Kenya to Laikipia, or take a short flight to Nanyuki. Meet your Samburu guides and the camel team and walk to camp in the late afternoon. Dinner and overnight in the fly camp. 

Day 2 : 
Walking safari 

Set off at dawn and walk through the bush while the camels follow with the camp. Rest through the heat of the day, then walk again in the afternoon. Dinner and overnight in the fly camp. 

Day 3 : 
Walking safari 

A further day on foot, tracking elephants, giraffes and zebras and learning about plants, tracks and Samburu culture. Dinner and overnight in the fly camp. 

Day 4 : 
Return to Nairobi 

Short morning walk, then transfer back to Nanyuki and Nairobi.

How much does this Laikipia walking safari cost? 

It starts from USD 1,900 per adult sharing, based on low-season rates. We confirm the price for your dates when you enquire. 

How far do we walk each day? 

Usually eight to fifteen kilometres, split between a morning and an afternoon walk at an easy pace. A reasonable level of fitness is enough. 

Is a walking safari safe? 

Walks are led by experienced guides who read animal behaviour and keep a safe distance. You follow their briefing at all times.
