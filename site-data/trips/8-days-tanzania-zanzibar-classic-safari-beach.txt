## Overview 
This premium package begins with a thrilling safari experience in Northern Tanzania’s iconic parks like the Serengeti or Ngorongoro Crater, seeking the Big Five and the Great Migration. Following your wildlife encounters, you’ll fly to the tropical paradise of Zanzibar for a relaxing beach stay. Enjoy pristine, white-sand beaches, explore historic Stone Town, and unwind by the Indian Ocean. This Tanzania & Zanzibar Classic Safari & Beach is a perfect combination of exhilarating adventure and serene tropical escape.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Prepare for the journey of a lifetime with our 8-day Tanzania & Zanzibar Classic Safari & Beach package. This meticulously crafted itinerary blends thrilling Big Five safari adventures in Northern Tanzania with a luxurious, relaxing beach escape on the tropical island of Zanzibar. Our Tanzania & Zanzibar Classic Safari & Beach ensures an unforgettable East African experience. 

Day 01 : 
Arrival at Arusha 

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport, your driver-guide will warmly welcome you and transfer you to the Hotel in Arusha which is approximately 1 hour drive. Spend the rest of the day at leisure, settling in, exploring the local surroundings, or relaxing to recover from your flight. Dinner and overnight at Melia Arusha 

Day 02 : 
Arusha to Serengeti National Park 

After an early breakfast, transfer to Arusha Airport for your flight to Serengeti. On arrival, enjoy a game drive en route to your luxury lodge/camp. Later in the afternoon, head out for your first exciting safari experience across the Serengeti plains. Dinner and overnight at: Melia Serengeti 

Day 03 & 04 : 
Two Full Days at Serengeti National Park 

Enjoy a full day in the Serengeti with morning and afternoon game drives. Your guide will track the great herds and predators, ensuring an unforgettable experience of Africa’s most famous ecosystem. Dinner and overnight at: Melia Serengeti Lodge 

Day 05 : 
Serengeti National Park to Zanzibar Beach 

After breakfast, transfer to the airstrip for your flight to Zanzibar. On arrival, meet your representative and transfer to your beach resort. Spend the afternoon at leisure, unwinding on the white-sand beaches. Dinner and overnight at: Melia Zanzibar 

Day 06 & 07 : 
Two Full Days at Zanzibar Beach 

Enjoy two full days at leisure to relax and unwind at your beautiful beachfront hotel . Take in the ocean views, soak up the sun, or simply enjoy the peaceful atmosphere by the beach Melia Zanzibar 

Day 08 : 
Zanzibar Beach - Departure
