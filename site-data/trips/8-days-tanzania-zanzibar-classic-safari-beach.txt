## Overview 
This 8 day Serengeti and Zanzibar safari combines 3 nights at Melia Serengeti Lodge with 3 nights at Melia Zanzibar, after 1 night at Melia Arusha. You fly from Arusha to the Serengeti and on to Zanzibar, so there are no long road transfers. The page gives no fixed season, and the price is on request. It suits couples and families who want one park done well, then the beach. 

## Why choose an 8 day Serengeti and Zanzibar safari? 
The 8 day Serengeti and Zanzibar safari keeps the wildlife part simple: one park, one lodge and three nights, with morning and afternoon game drives across the Serengeti plains in search of the Big Five, big cats and the great herds. Flying in saves the long drive from Arusha. 
The second half moves to the Indian Ocean, with three nights at a beachfront Melia resort on Zanzibar and the days free to swim, rest or add optional excursions such as Stone Town. 
- Short on time: scheduled flights link Arusha, the Serengeti and Zanzibar. 
- One lodge brand: Melia Arusha, Melia Serengeti Lodge and Melia Zanzibar. 
- Balanced pace: three safari nights and three beach nights. 

## What to expect on the 8 day Serengeti and Zanzibar safari 
Day 1 starts at Kilimanjaro International Airport, where your driver-guide meets you for the 1-hour transfer to Arusha and a free afternoon. Dinner and the night are at Melia Arusha. On day 2 you transfer to Arusha Airport for the flight to the Serengeti, game drive on the way to your lodge and head out again in the afternoon. 
Days 3 and 4 are full Serengeti days with morning and afternoon game drives, as your guide tracks the herds and predators. All three Serengeti nights are at Melia Serengeti Lodge with dinner. 
On day 5 you fly from the Serengeti airstrip to Zanzibar and transfer to Melia Zanzibar for an afternoon on the beach. Days 6 and 7 are free at the resort, and day 8 is your departure from Zanzibar. 

### Is 3 nights in the Serengeti enough before Zanzibar? 
Three nights in the Serengeti are enough for most first-time visitors, because the two full days give four long game drives in one of Africa’s richest parks. The Great Migration moves through different parts of the Serengeti during the year, so read our Serengeti migration guide and best time to visit Tanzania before you pick dates, and our Zanzibar beach extension guide for ideas on the island.

When is the best time for the 8 day Serengeti and Zanzibar safari? 

The 8 day Serengeti and Zanzibar safari runs all year, and the page gives no fixed season. June to October is dry, with good game viewing and sunny beach weather, and January to February is also popular. The migration herds move around the Serengeti, so tell us your dates and we will say what to expect.

Is the 8 day Serengeti and Zanzibar safari suitable for families?
