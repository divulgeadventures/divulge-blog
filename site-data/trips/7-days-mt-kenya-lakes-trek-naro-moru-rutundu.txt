## 
This Naro Moru to Rutundu trek is a 7-day lakes journey across Mount Kenya, covering about 60 km. You start at Naro Moru Gate, camp beside Lake Hohnel and below Point John, summit Point Lenana (4,985 m) at dawn, then wander past Lake Michaelson, Lake Ellis and Lake Alice before finishing at Lake Rutundu. It is a non-technical trek for fit walkers who love remote scenery. 

## Why choose the Naro Moru to Rutundu trek? 
Most Mount Kenya treks go up and down the same busy routes. This one spends most of its time on the quiet eastern and northern moorlands, where you can walk all day without meeting another group. Lake Alice is the largest lake on the mountain, and the trail to it crosses some of Mount Kenya’s least-visited country. 
The summit is optional: if you prefer, you can rest by Lake Michaelson or fish (with a Kenya Wildlife Service permit) while others climb. 

## What to expect on the Naro Moru to Rutundu trek 
Days 1 and 2 climb from the Met Station to Lake Hohnel (about 3,950 m) and American Camp below Point John (about 4,450 m). Day 3 is summit day, with Point Lenana at sunrise and a descent to Lake Michaelson. Days 4 to 6 head north past Lake Ellis and the Mugi Hills to Lake Alice and Lake Rutundu, including one long 9-hour day. On day 7 you walk out through the forest to the Rutundu road and drive to Nairobi. 

### When is the best time for this trek? 
January to February and late June to early October bring the driest conditions. The northern moorland can be boggy after rain.

Day 1 : 
Nairobi to Lake Hohnel via Naro Moru Gate 

Drive about 3.5 hours to Naro Moru Gate and on to the Met Station (about 3,050 m). Walk about 4 km (3 to 4 hours) through forest and moorland to camp at Lake Hohnel (about 3,950 m). 

Day 2 : 
Lake Hohnel to American Camp 

A walk of about 4 to 5 hours among giant lobelia and groundsel to American Camp below Point John, at about 4,450 m. 

Day 3 : 
Point Lenana summit, then Lake Michaelson 

Start at 3 a.m. for Point Lenana (4,985 m) via Austrian Hut, then descend to Lake Michaelson (about 3,850 m) in the Gorges Valley. Non-summiters can rest by the lake. 

Day 4 : 
Lake Michaelson to Lake Ellis 

Walk about 4 to 5 hours through the gorges and alpine valleys to Lake Ellis at about 3,300 m. 

Day 5 : 
Lake Ellis to Lake Alice 

The longest day, about 9 to 10 hours and 18 km across the moorland and past the Mugi Hills to Lake Alice, the largest lake on the mountain. 

Day 6 : 
Lake Alice to Lake Rutundu 

A short, easy day of about 2 to 3 hours to Lake Rutundu and a nearby camp. Enjoy a relaxed afternoon and a final dinner on the mountain. 

Day 7 : 
Rutundu to Nairobi 

Walk about 3 to 4 hours down through moorland and hagenia forest to the Rutundu road, then drive back to Nairobi, arriving in the evening.

How long is the Naro Moru to Rutundu trek? 

The Naro Moru to Rutundu trek takes seven days and covers about 60 km. Walking days range from 2 to 10 hours, with the longest stage between Lake Ellis and Lake Alice. 

Do I have to climb Point Lenana? 

No. Point Lenana is optional on the Naro Moru to Rutundu trek. If you prefer, you can rest at Lake Michaelson while others summit, then everyone continues the lakes route together. 

Can I fish on the trek? 

Trout fishing is possible in some of Mount Kenya’s lakes with a fishing permit from the Kenya Wildlife Service. Tell us when you book and we will help arrange the permit. 

What is the best time for the Mount Kenya lakes trek? 

The best months are January to February and late June to early October, when the trails are driest. The northern moorland can be very wet in April, May and November. 

Is this trek good for a group of friends? 

Yes. The quiet lakes route is a great choice for groups and clubs. We plan one guide for every few trekkers and can split the group on summit day. Contact us for a group quote.
