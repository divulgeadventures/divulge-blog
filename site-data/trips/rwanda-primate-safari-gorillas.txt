## Overview 
This 7 days Rwanda primate safari gives you time to enjoy every highlight without rushing. In Nyungwe Forest National Park, you will track chimpanzees at dawn, walk the canopy bridge and pick tea on emerald hillsides. 
Then you will travel north to Volcanoes National Park for your Rwanda primate safari mountain gorilla trek, a cultural afternoon with the Gorilla Guardians, a golden monkey trek and a peaceful canoe trip on the Mukungwa River. With a Kigali city tour and a stop at the King’s Palace in Nyanza, it is a rich mix of wildlife, culture and countryside, ideal for first-time visitors.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is your 7-day Rwanda primate safari day by day. 

Day 01 : 
Arrival in Kigali 

Upon arrival at Kigali International Airport, you will be met by your Divulge Adventures representative for meet, greet and safari briefing about your 7-day Rwanda primate safari. Transfer to your hotel to check in and relax. Dinner and overnight at Park Inn by Radisson Kigali | Hotel Chez Lando 

Day 02 : 
Kigali City Tour - Nyanza - Nyungwe Forest 

After breakfast, enjoy a Kigali city tour including the Kigali Genocide Memorial, marking the start of your Rwanda primate safari. Then drive southwest through terraced hills, stopping at the King's Palace Museum in Nyanza, with lunch en route. Arrive at Nyungwe in the late afternoon. Dinner and overnight at Nyungwe Top View Hill Hotel | Gisakura Guest House 

Day 03 : 
Chimpanzee Trekking and Canopy Walk 

Rise early with a packed breakfast and set out on your chimpanzee trek as part of your Rwanda primate safari, spending one hour with a habituated community. Look out for other primates and forest birds along the way. Return to the lodge for lunch, then walk the canopy walkway in the afternoon for spectacular treetop views. Dinner and overnight at Nyungwe Top View Hill Hotel | Gisakura Guest House 

Day 04 : 
Tea Plantation - Musanze 

After breakfast, join local tea pickers on a plantation for a hands-on experience and great photos. Check out and drive north to Musanze, approximately 7 hours, enjoying views of the countryside with a lunch stop en route. Dinner and overnight at Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge | Kinigi Guest House 

Day 05 : 
Mountain Gorilla Trekking and Gorilla Guardians Village 

After an early breakfast, drive to the Kinigi park headquarters for the 7:00 AM briefing. Trek into the forest and spend about one hour with a mountain gorilla family the best part of your Rwanda primate safari. Enjoy lunch, then visit the Gorilla Guardians Village in the afternoon for traditional dance and village life. Dinner and overnight at Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge | Kinigi Guest House 

Day 06 : 
Golden Monkey Trekking and Mukungwa River Canoeing 

After breakfast, set out on your golden monkey trek and spend about one hour with the troop in the bamboo forest. After lunch, enjoy a relaxing canoe trip on the Mukungwa River near Musanze, gliding past farms, birdlife and village life. Return to the lodge for your last evening near the volcanoes. Dinner and overnight at Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge | Kinigi Guest House 

Day 07 : 
Musanze - Kigali (Departure) 

After breakfast, drive back to Kigali, approximately 2.5 hours, with free time for souvenir shopping before your transfer to Kigali International Airport. This marks the end of your 7-day Rwanda primate safari.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

Is 7 days enough for Rwanda? 

Yes. Seven days covers Kigali, chimpanzees in Nyungwe and gorillas and golden monkeys in Volcanoes National Park at a comfortable pace. 

What is canoeing on the Mukungwa River like? 

It is a gentle, guided paddle on a calm river near Musanze, suitable for beginners, with views of farms, birds and the volcanoes. 

How many primate species can I see on this safari? 

You can expect mountain gorillas, chimpanzees and golden monkeys, plus other species in Nyungwe such as colobus, L’Hoest’s and blue monkeys. 

Can this safari be done in reverse? 

Yes. Divulge Adventures can start in Volcanoes National Park and finish in Nyungwe to suit your flights and permit availability.
