## Overview 
A double gorilla trekking Rwanda safari means two separate treks, on two days, to two different mountain gorilla families. Many travellers say the first hour passes in a blur. The second trek lets you slow down, watch behaviour closely and take better photos. 
This four-day safari includes both gorilla permits, a Kigali city tour, a visit to the Gorilla Guardians Village and private 4×4 transport with your own driver guide. 

## Why choose a double gorilla trekking Rwanda safari? 
Every gorilla family is different. One may be led by a calm silverback with tiny babies, another may be a lively group of youngsters. Two treks give you two very different encounters. 
Volcanoes National Park is only about 2.5 hours from Kigali on good roads, so you spend more time with the gorillas and less time travelling. 
- Photographers: a second chance for the perfect shot. 
- Wildlife lovers: see two families and compare their behaviour. 
- Peace of mind: a backup if weather spoils one trek. 

## What to expect on your double gorilla trekking Rwanda safari 
You arrive in Kigali on day one. On day two you tour the city, including the Kigali Genocide Memorial, then drive north to your lodge near the park, with the Virunga volcanoes on the horizon. 
Day three is your first trek. After the 7:00 AM briefing at Kinigi, rangers assign you a family, and you hike through bamboo and montane forest to spend one hour with them. In the afternoon, the Gorilla Guardians Village shares traditional dance, music and stories. 
On day four you trek again, this time to a different family, before driving back to Kigali for your flight or hotel. 

### How fit do you need to be? 
Treks last from about one to six hours on steep, muddy ground. Moderate fitness is enough for both days, and porters can carry your bag and help on tricky sections. 

## Best time for double gorilla trekking Rwanda trips 
Gorilla trekking runs all year. June to September and December to February bring drier trails. In the November to May low season, Rwanda offers a 30% gorilla permit discount when you also spend two nights in Akagera or Nyungwe, which can make adding a park worthwhile. 
Learn more in our complete guide to gorilla trekking in Rwanda , or add golden monkeys with the gorilla and golden monkey trekking package. Park details are on Visit Rwanda’s Volcanoes National Park page .

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is your 4-day double gorilla trekking safari day by day. 

Day 01 : 
Arrival in Kigali 

Upon arrival at Kigali International Airport, you will be met by your Divulge Adventures representative and assisted through arrivals. After exchanging money if needed, transfer to your hotel to check in and rest after your flight. Dinner and overnight at Park Inn by Radisson Kigali | Hotel Chez Lando 

Day 02 : 
Kigali City Tour - Musanze 

After breakfast, enjoy a short Kigali city tour, including a visit to the Kigali Genocide Memorial and a drive through the city's tidy hills and markets. Then head north to Musanze, approximately 2.5 hours, through spectacular terraced landscapes. Check in at your lodge and enjoy a free afternoon with views of the Virunga volcanoes. Dinner and overnight at Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge | Kinigi Guest House 

Day 03 : 
First Gorilla Trek and Gorilla Guardians Village 

After an early breakfast, drive to the Kinigi park headquarters for the 7:00 AM briefing and your first gorilla family assignment. Trek through bamboo and montane forest and spend one magical hour with the gorillas. Return to the lodge for lunch, then visit the Gorilla Guardians Village in the afternoon for traditional dances, music and stories of community conservation. Dinner and overnight at Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge | Kinigi Guest House 

Day 04 : 
Second Gorilla Trek - Kigali 

Rise early for your second gorilla trek, this time to a different family, which gives you a fresh perspective on gorilla life and new photo opportunities. After your hour with the gorillas, return to the lodge for lunch and check out, then drive back to Kigali for drop-off at your hotel or Kigali International Airport.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

Is it worth doing two gorilla treks in Rwanda? 

Many visitors say yes. A second trek to a different family lets you relax, observe more behaviour and take better photos, since the first hour often passes in a blur. 

Will I see the same gorilla family twice? 

Divulge Adventures requests a different gorilla family for each trek. The final choice is made by the rangers on the day. 

How fit do I need to be for two gorilla treks? 

Moderate fitness is enough. Treks vary from 1 to 6 hours, and porters can help carry your bag on both days. 

Do I need two separate gorilla permits? 

Yes. Each trek requires its own USD 1,500 permit. Both permits are included in this package. 

Can I get a discount on double gorilla trekking in Rwanda? 

From November to May, Rwanda offers a 30% low-season discount on a gorilla permit when you also spend two nights in Akagera or Nyungwe. Each discounted permit needs its own qualifying stay. 

What is the minimum age for gorilla trekking? 

Visitors must be at least 15 years old to trek mountain gorillas in Rwanda. 

How many people are in a gorilla trekking group? 

Each gorilla family is visited by a group of no more than eight trekkers per day, which keeps the encounter calm for the gorillas and for you.
