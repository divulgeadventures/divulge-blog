## Overview 
This 2 day Tsavo West safari from Diani is a private overnight road safari from the Kenya coast. You leave Diani Beach at 5:30 a.m., game drive on arrival, spend 1 night at Salt Lick Safari Lodge on full board and return to Diani by about 7:00 p.m. on day 2. It runs all year and costs from USD 595 per adult, ideal for beach holidaymakers short on time. 
It suits couples, families and first-time visitors staying on the south coast who want a real Kenya game drive without giving up more than one night of their beach holiday. Travel is in a private 4×4 Land Cruiser with an English or bilingual driver guide. 

## Why choose a 2 day Tsavo West safari from Diani? 
Tsavo West National Park is one of the closest big parks to Diani, so a two-day trip gives you three game drives, a lodge night in the bush and the famous red-dust elephants of Tsavo, while you sleep only one night away from the coast. The page lists Mzima Springs, the Shetani Lava Flow and the Ngulia Rhino Sanctuary among the highlights of the Tsavo West area. 
- Beach holidaymakers: a safari add-on that starts and ends at your Diani hotel. 
- Families: one night away, full board, and a private vehicle at your own pace. 
- Elephant lovers: Tsavo’s red-dust elephants and a lodge stay overlooking the wilderness. 

## What to expect on the 2 day Tsavo West safari from Diani 
Day 1 starts with a 5:30 a.m. pickup at your Diani Beach hotel. After a drive of about 4 hours you reach the sanctuary at around 9:30 a.m. and start your first game drive straight away. You arrive at Salt Lick Safari Lodge at about 1:00 p.m. for check-in and lunch, then go out again on a long afternoon game drive from 2:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m. Dinner and the night are at the lodge. 
Day 2 begins with a sunrise game drive from 6:30 to 8:00 a.m., then breakfast at 8:30 a.m. and check-out. An extended game drive runs from 9:00 a.m. to mid-afternoon, with lunch at 2:00 p.m. You leave for the coast at 3:00 p.m. and arrive back in Diani at about 7:00 p.m. 

### Is 2 days enough for Tsavo West? 
Two days is enough for a first taste of Tsavo West: you get three game drives and a night in the bush, which is ideal if you are on a beach holiday. The early start makes the most of the day. If you want time at Mzima Springs, the Shetani Lava Flow and the Ngulia Rhino Sanctuary as well, ask us about adding a night. Our Tsavo West National Park guide explains what each area offers. 

## What is included in this Tsavo safari from Diani? 
The price includes a meet-and-greet and safari briefing, all transport in a private 4WD Land Cruiser, 1 night of accommodation, all park entry and conservation fees, full board meals (breakfast, lunch and dinner), an English or bilingual driver guide, unlimited game drives on the itinerary and unlimited drinking water. 
Not included are travel insurance, visas and international flights, tips (about USD 10 per person per day is suggested, not mandatory), drinks and personal items. See how park fees are set in our Kenya park fees guide and how much to tip in our guide to tipping in Kenya .

Day 01 : 
Diani Beach to Tsavo West National Park 

After breakfast, begins with an early morning departure from Diani Beach at 5:30 a.m., setting the tone for an exciting safari adventure. After a scenic drive, you will arrive at the sanctuary around 9:30 a.m., where your first game drive starts immediately, offering the chance to spot wildlife as you make your way through the reserve. 
By 1:00 p.m., you will reach the iconic Salt Lick Safari Lodge , where you can check in, settle into your room, and enjoy a well-deserved lunch overlooking the surrounding wilderness. 
In the afternoon, from 2:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m., you will head out again for an unhurried and immersive game drive, allowing you to fully appreciate the landscapes, observe animals in their natural habitat, and experience the peaceful atmosphere of the sanctuary as the day slowly comes to an end. 

Day 02 : 
Tsavo National Park to Diani Beach 

The second day starts early with a sunrise game drive from 6:30 to 8:00 a.m., offering a final chance to experience the sanctuary as wildlife becomes active in the cool morning light. After returning to the lodge, breakfast is served at 8:30 a.m., followed by check-out. 
From 9:00 a.m. to mid-afternoon, the journey continues with an extended game drive through several areas of the sanctuary, allowing for continuous and varied wildlife sightings along the way. A lunch break is scheduled at 2:00 p.m. 
The safari concludes with departure for Diani Beach at 3:00 p.m., arriving at the coast around 7:00 p.m. after a memorable safari experience.

When is the best time for the 2 day Tsavo West safari from Diani? 

The 2 day Tsavo West safari from Diani runs all year. The dry months, roughly June to October and January to February, usually give the easiest game viewing because animals gather at water and the bush is thinner. Short rains in November and long rains around April can make tracks muddy, but the park is greener and quieter then.
