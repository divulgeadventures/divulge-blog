## Overview 
Experience an unforgettable 2-day adventure from the coast to the rugged wilderness. Explore Tsavo West National Park, famous for its red-skinned elephants, Mzima Springs, and the Shetani Lava Flow. Enjoy thrilling game drives to spot the Big Five and visit the Ngulia Rhino Sanctuary. This short getaway to Tsavo West National Park offers the perfect blend of wildlife and scenery. 

### Safari Highlights 
- Tsavo West National Park 
- Shetani Lava Flow 
- Ngulia Rhino Sanctuary 
- Saltlick Lodge 
- Red Elephants of Tsavo 
- Mzima Springs 

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
Diani Beach to Tsavo West National Park 

After breakfast, begins with an early morning departure from Diani Beach at 5:30 a.m., setting the tone for an exciting safari adventure. After a scenic drive, you will arrive at the sanctuary around 9:30 a.m., where your first game drive starts immediately, offering the chance to spot wildlife as you make your way through the reserve. By 1:00 p.m., you will reach the iconic Salt Lick Safari Lodge , where you can check in, settle into your room, and enjoy a well-deserved lunch overlooking the surrounding wilderness. In the afternoon, from 2:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m., you will head out again for an unhurried and immersive game drive, allowing you to fully appreciate the landscapes, observe animals in their natural habitat, and experience the peaceful atmosphere of the sanctuary as the day slowly comes to an end. 

Day 02 : 
Tsavo National Park to Diani Beach 

The second day starts early with a sunrise game drive from 6:30 to 8:00 a.m., offering a final chance to experience the sanctuary as wildlife becomes active in the cool morning light. After returning to the lodge, breakfast is served at 8:30 a.m., followed by check-out. From 9:00 a.m. to mid-afternoon, the journey continues with an extended game drive through several areas of the sanctuary, allowing for continuous and varied wildlife sightings along the way. A lunch break is scheduled at 2:00 p.m. The safari concludes with departure for Diani Beach at 3:00 p.m., arriving at the coast around 7:00 p.m. after a memorable safari experience.
