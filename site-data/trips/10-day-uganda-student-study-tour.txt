## 
This 10 day Uganda student study tour is a field trip through Uganda’s rivers, forests and savannah. Students visit the Source of the Nile, track rhinos at Ziwa, see Murchison Falls, follow chimpanzees in Kibale, cruise the Kazinga Channel and, for students aged 15 and over, trek to mountain gorillas in Bwindi. 

## What will students learn on this 10 day Uganda student study tour? 
The route covers conservation and geography in one trip. Ziwa shows how rhinos are being brought back to Uganda, Kibale and Bwindi show how primate tourism funds forest protection, and the Nile, Murchison Falls and the Kazinga Channel bring river systems and wetlands to life. 
Community visits at Bigodi Wetland and with the Batwa people add a social side, so students can discuss how people and parks live side by side. 

## How is the study tour planned? 
Two nights in Kampala, two in Murchison Falls, two in Kibale, one in Queen Elizabeth and two in Bwindi. Students travel in 4×4 vehicles with experienced driver guides, and long drives are broken up with stops. 

### Can younger students join? 
Gorilla trekking is only open to people aged 15 and over, and chimpanzee trekking has a minimum age of 12. For younger groups we replace these with forest walks, the Bigodi wetland and extra game drives. 
Read our guides to chimpanzee trekking in Kibale and gorilla trekking in Bwindi . 

## Who is the 10 day uganda student study tour best for? 
The 10 day uganda student study tour is best for secondary schools, colleges and universities, and for teachers who want a safe trip with clear learning goals. We share a full plan and risk overview with teachers before travel, so the 10 day uganda student study tour runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## Can the 10 day Uganda student study tour include community work? 
Yes. We can add a school visit, tree planting or a conservation talk, or spend more time with a community project. Tell us your learning goals and the students’ ages.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Entebbe and Kampala 

Your 10 day uganda student study tour begins today. Your group is met at Entebbe International Airport for a welcome and safety briefing, then transferred to the hotel. Dinner and overnight at Kampala Serena Hotel or similar. 

Day 2 : 
Mabira Forest and the Source of the Nile 

Drive east to Mabira Forest for zip-lining over the trees and a guided walk to Griffin Falls. Continue to Jinja for lunch and a boat cruise to the Source of the Nile, then return to Kampala. Dinner and overnight at Kampala Serena Hotel or similar. 

Day 3 : 
Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary to Murchison Falls 

Drive north to Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary to track white rhinos on foot with rangers. Continue to the Top of Murchison Falls, where the Nile forces through a 7-metre gorge. Dinner and overnight at Twiga Safari Lodge or similar. 

Day 4 : 
Murchison Falls game drive and Nile cruise 

A sunrise game drive to look for lions, elephants and giraffes, then an afternoon boat cruise to the base of the falls among hippos and crocodiles. Dinner and overnight at Murchison River Lodge or similar. 

Day 5 : 
Murchison Falls to Kibale 

A long drive south through villages and hills to Kibale National Park. Dinner and overnight at Turaco Treetops or similar. 

Day 6 : 
Chimpanzee trekking and Bigodi Wetland 

Trek with rangers to find chimpanzees in Kibale Forest (age 12+), then take a guided walk through the community-run Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary. Dinner and overnight at Turaco Treetops or similar. 

Day 7 : 
Kibale to Queen Elizabeth and the Kazinga Channel 

Drive to Queen Elizabeth National Park for an afternoon boat cruise on the Kazinga Channel among elephants, buffalo and hippos. Dinner and overnight at Ihamba Lakeside Safari Lodge or similar. 

Day 8 : 
Kasenyi game drive to Bwindi 

A morning game drive on the Kasenyi Plains, then continue south to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. Dinner and overnight at Mahogany Springs Lodge or similar. 

Day 9 : 
Gorilla trekking and Batwa visit 

Students aged 15 and over trek to a mountain gorilla family with rangers. In the afternoon, the whole group visits the Batwa community to learn about their forest heritage. Dinner and overnight at Mahogany Springs Lodge or similar. 

Day 10 : 
Bwindi to Entebbe and departure 

Transfer back to Entebbe for your flight home. A domestic flight can replace the long drive. This is the end of your 10 day uganda student study tour.

Who is the 10 day Uganda student study tour designed for? 

The 10 day Uganda student study tour suits secondary school, college and university groups studying biology, geography, environmental science or development. Gorilla trekking needs students aged 15 and over, so we adjust the plan for younger groups. 

Do teachers travel free? 

On group bookings the seventh place is free for every six paying travellers. The free place usually goes to a teacher or group leader, and your quote shows how many your group earns. 

How many students can trek gorillas together? 

Each gorilla family can be visited by up to 8 people a day, so larger groups trek to different families at the same time. Permits must be booked early, so tell us your numbers as soon as possible. 

Can parents pay separately for each student? 

Yes. Each family can pay their share directly. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the group’s places and permits, and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. 

When is the best time for a school trip to Uganda? 

June to August and December to February are the driest months for trekking and fit most school holidays. Uganda can be visited all year, and rain usually falls in short showers.
