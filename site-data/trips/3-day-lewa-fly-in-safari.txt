## 
This Lewa fly-in safari visits Lewa Wildlife Conservancy, a private conservancy north of Mount Kenya and a UNESCO World Heritage Site together with Mount Kenya. It is famous for its black and white rhinos and its success in protecting them. 
Because Lewa is a conservancy, you can enjoy activities not allowed in national parks, such as guided bush walks and night drives. 

## Why choose this Lewa fly-in safari? 
- Rhinos: some of the best chances in Kenya to see both black and white rhinos. 
- Rare northern species: Grevy’s zebra, reticulated giraffe and Beisa oryx. 
- Bush walks: explore on foot with an armed ranger. 
- Night drives: look for aardvarks, bush babies and hunting cats. 

## Where you stay 
Two nights at Lewa Safari Camp, or a similar camp inside the conservancy. 

## Best time for a Lewa fly-in safari 
Lewa is good all year. The dry seasons from June to October and January to March give the easiest game viewing, while the green season brings fewer visitors.

Day 1 : 
Fly from Nairobi to Lewa 

Transfer to Wilson Airport and fly to Lewa Downs airstrip. Short game drive to Lewa Safari Camp, then an afternoon game drive until sunset. 

Day 2 : 
Full day at Lewa 

Early game drive in search of rhinos, a guided bush walk with an armed ranger, and an afternoon drive. Optional night drive after dinner. 

Day 3 : 
Fly back to Nairobi 

Final morning game drive, then fly from Lewa Downs to Wilson Airport and transfer to your hotel or JKIA.

Where is Lewa? 

Lewa Wildlife Conservancy is north of Mount Kenya in Laikipia, about an hour’s flight from Nairobi. 

Where do the flights leave from in Nairobi? 

From Wilson Airport, not Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. We arrange the transfer from your hotel or from JKIA. 

How much luggage can I bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. Ask us if you need extra space. 

Can I walk on safari at Lewa? 

Yes. Guided bush walks with an armed ranger are one of the highlights of a Lewa fly-in safari. 

Are night drives included? 

A night drive is optional and can be added at the camp. 

Can I combine Lewa with Samburu? 

Yes. Samburu is close by and can be added with a short road transfer or flight. 

Is emergency evacuation cover available? 

Yes. We recommend Flying Doctors evacuation cover for every safari and can arrange it for you.
