## 
This 6 day Rwanda family safari works for children of all ages. You see the Big Five in Akagera, take a sunset boat cruise among hippos, learn about gorillas at the Ellen DeGeneres Campus near Volcanoes National Park and finish on the shores of Lake Kivu. 

## Why is this 6 day Rwanda family safari good for children? 
Gorilla trekking is limited to ages 15 and over, so this trip is built around experiences the whole family can share: game drives in Akagera, boat cruises, a cultural village with dancing and drumming, and a night fishing trip on Lake Kivu. 
Drives are short by safari standards, and the hotels have space to relax. Teenagers aged 15 and over can add a gorilla trek on day 5 while younger children stay with a parent. 

## What does the family itinerary include? 
One night in Kigali, one in Akagera, one back in Kigali, one near Volcanoes National Park and one on Lake Kivu, with a boat cruise on the last morning. 

### Can we add gorilla trekking? 
Yes. Family members aged 15 and over can add a gorilla trek. The permit costs USD 1,500 per person, and we add an extra night near the park so younger children have their own activity that day. 
Read our guides to gorilla trekking in Rwanda and Rwanda in December . 

## Who is the 6 day rwanda family safari best for? 
The 6 day rwanda family safari is best for families who want to share a safari, from young children to grandparents. Tell us everyone’s ages and we will match vehicles, rooms and activities to your family, so the 6 day rwanda family safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## Can several families share the 6 day Rwanda family safari? 
Yes. Families can travel in separate vehicles and meet at the hotels, or share one vehicle when the children are close in age. Each family can pay separately.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Kigali 

Your 6 day rwanda family safari begins today. Meet your guide at Kigali International Airport and transfer to your hotel. Dinner and overnight at Park Inn by Radisson Kigali or Hotel Chez Lando. 

Day 2 : 
Kigali to Akagera and sunset boat cruise 

Drive east to Akagera National Park. In the late afternoon, take a sunset boat cruise on Lake Ihema among hippos, crocodiles and water birds. Dinner and overnight at Akagera Game Lodge or Akagera Rhino Lodge. 

Day 3 : 
Akagera game drive and return to Kigali 

A full-day game drive across the giraffe plains and along the lakes, looking for lions, rhinos, elephants and buffalo, then return to Kigali. Dinner and overnight at Park Inn by Radisson Kigali or Hotel Chez Lando. 

Day 4 : 
Kigali city tour to Musanze 

Tour Kigali, then drive to Musanze and visit the Ellen DeGeneres Campus of the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund to learn about gorillas and conservation. Dinner and overnight at Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge or Kinigi Guest House. 

Day 5 : 
Gorilla Guardians Village to Lake Kivu 

Visit the Gorilla Guardians Village for traditional dancing, drumming and village activities, then drive to Lake Kivu. In the evening, join the fishermen for night fishing. Dinner and overnight at Lake Kivu Serena Hotel or Paradis Malahide. 

Day 6 : 
Lake Kivu boat cruise and departure 

A morning boat cruise past Lake Kivu's islands and hot springs, then drive back to Kigali for your flights. This is the end of your 6 day rwanda family safari.

Is the 6 day Rwanda family safari suitable for young children? 

Yes. The 6 day Rwanda family safari has no gorilla trek in the main plan, so children of all ages can take part in every activity. Teenagers aged 15 and over can add a gorilla trek. 

How long are the drives? 

Most drives are 2 to 3 hours, and the longest days have stops along the way. Rwanda’s roads are good, which makes it easier with children than many safari countries. 

Do children get lower rates? 

Many hotels and parks have child rates and family rooms, but the rules vary. We include the right rates for your children’s ages in your quote. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 6 day rwanda family safari. 

Can two families travel together? 

Yes. On group bookings the seventh place is free for every six paying travellers, and each family can pay separately. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 6 day rwanda family safari. 

When is the best time for a family safari in Rwanda? 

June to September and December to February are the driest months and match most school holidays. Rwanda can be visited all year. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 6 day rwanda family safari.
