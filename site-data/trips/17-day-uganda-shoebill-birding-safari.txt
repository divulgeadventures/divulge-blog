## 
This Uganda shoebill birding safari is a full loop of Uganda in 17 days. It starts in the north at Murchison Falls, moves through the forests of the west and ends near Lake Victoria, giving you two of the best shoebill sites in the country. 
Along the way you bird the famous Royal Mile in Budongo, track chimpanzees in Kibale, search Semliki for Congo Basin birds and trek gorillas in Bwindi. 

## Why choose this Uganda shoebill birding safari? 
- Murchison Falls: boat trips to the falls and the Nile delta, a top shoebill site. 
- Budongo’s Royal Mile: one of Uganda’s best forest birding trails. 
- Kibale and Semliki: chimpanzees and rare lowland forest birds. 
- Queen Elizabeth and Bwindi: Kazinga Channel and mountain gorillas. 
- Mabamba and Mabira: a second shoebill chance and a final forest. 

## Where you stay 
Lodges include Lindsay Cottages in Entebbe, Paraa Safari Lodge, Masindi Hotel, Primate Lodge Kibale, Semliki Safari Lodge, Mweya Safari Lodge, Trekker’s Tavern at Ruhija, Mihingo Lodge at Lake Mburo, Cassia Lodge in Kampala and Mabira Rainforest Lodge, or similar. 

### Night drives 
Optional night drives at Murchison and Lake Mburo give you a chance at nightjars, owls and nocturnal mammals. 

## Best time for a Uganda shoebill birding safari 
Shoebills are resident and can be seen all year. The drier months from June to August and December to February are easiest for travel and forest walks. The wetter months bring breeding birds and fewer vehicles. 
Read about Uganda’s top parks in our Uganda national parks guide and plan your trek with our Bwindi gorilla guide . Browse more birding safaris . The Uganda Wildlife Authority manages all the parks on this route.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Entebbe 

Airport pick-up and transfer to Lindsay Cottages or Cassia Lodge. Optional birding at the Entebbe Botanical Gardens. 

Day 2 : 
Entebbe to Murchison Falls 

Drive north through the Luwero area with birding stops and continue into Murchison Falls National Park. Overnight at Paraa Safari Lodge. 

Day 3 : 
Murchison Falls boat trips 

Boat trips to the base of the falls and to the Nile delta for shoebill, rock pratincole and red-throated bee-eaters, plus a game drive. Optional night drive. 

Day 4 : 
Kaniyo Pabidi to Masindi 

Early birding in the Kaniyo Pabidi forest for Puvel's illadopsis, then grassland birding on the way to Masindi Hotel. 

Day 5 : 
Budongo's Royal Mile 

A full day on the Royal Mile and Busingiro trails for forest birds such as Nahan's francolin and white-thighed hornbill. 

Day 6 : 
Masindi to Kibale 

A long drive via Hoima with stops in papyrus and scrub. Check in at Primate Lodge for two nights. 

Day 7 : 
Kibale chimpanzees 

Chimpanzee tracking in the morning and forest birding in the afternoon. 

Day 8 : 
Semliki National Park 

Drive to Semliki for Congo Basin specials such as Nkulengu rail and Congo serpent eagle. Optional night drive. Overnight at Semliki Safari Lodge. 

Day 9 : 
Sempaya to Queen Elizabeth 

Early birding on the Sempaya trail near the hot springs, then drive to Queen Elizabeth National Park. Overnight at Mweya Safari Lodge. 

Day 10 : 
Queen Elizabeth and Kazinga Channel 

A full day of birding and game viewing, with an afternoon boat ride on the Kazinga Channel. 

Day 11 : 
Queen Elizabeth to Ruhija via Ishasha 

Drive south through Ishasha with its tree-climbing lions, then climb to Ruhija in Bwindi. Overnight at Trekker's Tavern. 

Day 12 : 
Gorilla trekking at Ruhija 

Track a habituated gorilla family and spend one hour with them. Birding around Ruhija afterwards. 

Day 13 : 
Ruhija forest birding 

A full day for Albertine Rift endemics such as Rwenzori turaco, regal sunbird and Grauer's warbler. 

Day 14 : 
Bwindi to Lake Mburo 

Drive to Lake Mburo with birding stops. Afternoon boat ride for African finfoot and herons, and an optional night drive. Overnight at Mihingo Lodge. 

Day 15 : 
Lake Mburo to Kampala 

Morning savannah birding and game drive, then on to Kampala. Overnight at Cassia Lodge or Lindsay Cottages. 

Day 16 : 
Mabamba Swamp and Mabira Forest 

An early canoe trip at Mabamba for a second chance at the shoebill, then drive east to Mabira Rainforest Lodge. 

Day 17 : 
Mabira Forest and departure 

Morning birding around the lodge, then transfer to Entebbe Airport.

Where will I look for the shoebill? 

At the Nile delta in Murchison Falls National Park and at Mabamba Swamp near Entebbe, two of the most reliable sites in Uganda. 

Are gorilla and chimpanzee permits included? 

Yes. One gorilla trekking permit and one chimpanzee tracking permit per person are included. 

Which lodges does the Uganda shoebill birding safari use? 

Lindsay Cottages, Paraa Safari Lodge, Masindi Hotel, Primate Lodge, Semliki Safari Lodge, Mweya Safari Lodge, Trekker’s Tavern, Mihingo Lodge, Cassia Lodge and Mabira Rainforest Lodge, or similar. 

What is the Royal Mile? 

It is a wide, flat forest track in Budongo Forest, famous for its birding. Specials include Nahan’s francolin, chocolate-backed kingfisher and many forest flycatchers. 

How long are the drives? 

Some days include long drives, such as Masindi to Kibale. We break them up with birding stops and picnic lunches. 

Can non-birders join? 

Yes. The primates, Murchison Falls and Queen Elizabeth game drives make this a great trip for nature lovers in general. 

Is there a night drive? 

Optional night drives are available at Murchison Falls and Lake Mburo, at an extra cost.
