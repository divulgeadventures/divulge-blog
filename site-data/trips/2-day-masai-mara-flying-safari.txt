## 
This Masai Mara flying safari is made for busy travellers. You fly from Nairobi in the morning, enjoy an afternoon game drive, sleep in the reserve and head back the next day after a sunrise drive. 
It is ideal before or after a business trip, a conference or a beach holiday, when you have only a day or two to spare. 

## Why choose this Masai Mara flying safari? 
- Quick escape: less than an hour in the air from Nairobi. 
- Two game drives: sunset and sunrise, when animals are most active. 
- Big cats: lions, cheetahs and leopards on the open plains. 
- Simple logistics: we handle transfers, flights and park fees. 

## Where you stay 
One night at Mara Serena Safari Lodge, or a similar camp close to your airstrip. 

## Best time for a Masai Mara flying safari 
The Mara is good all year. July to October brings the wildebeest migration and river crossings. January to March is quieter, with green plains and many young animals.

Day 1 : 
Fly from Nairobi to the Masai Mara 

Transfer from your hotel or JKIA to Wilson Airport for the morning flight to the Mara. After lunch at Mara Serena Safari Lodge, head out on an afternoon game drive until sunset.

Early morning game drive, then brunch at the lodge and a transfer to the airstrip for your afternoon flight to Wilson Airport.

Is one night in the Mara worth it? 

Yes, if you are short on time. You get two of the best game drives of the day, at sunset and sunrise. 

Where do the flights leave from in Nairobi? 

From Wilson Airport, not Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. We arrange the transfer from your hotel or from JKIA. 

How much luggage can I bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. Ask us if you need extra space. 

Which airstrip will I land at? 

It depends on your lodge. The main Mara airstrips include Musiara, Ol Kiombo, Keekorok and Serena. 

Can I add a balloon flight? 

Yes, a sunrise balloon flight can replace the morning game drive on day 2, at extra cost. 

Is emergency evacuation cover available? 

Yes. We recommend Flying Doctors evacuation cover for every safari and can arrange it for you. 

Can I extend to more nights? 

Yes. Flights run daily, so extra nights are easy to add.
