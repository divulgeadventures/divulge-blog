## Overview 
This 3 day Okavango Delta mokoro safari is a 3-day, 2-night canoe and walking trip from Maun into the heart of Botswana’s Okavango Delta. You glide by traditional mokoro to a remote island, sleep 2 nights in a bush camp, and spend the days on guided walks, poling lessons and sunset trips on the water. The price starts from USD 715 per adult, and it suits active travellers who want a quiet, back-to-nature Delta adventure. 
The trip runs on full board with camping equipment, bed linen and towels supplied, and a Divulge Adventures representative handles the road transfer from Maun to the mokoro station and back. 

## Why choose a 3 day Okavango Delta mokoro safari? 
The mokoro is a dugout canoe pushed along by a standing poler, so you travel through the Delta’s reed channels and lagoons with no engine noise at all. That silence lets you hear birds, frogs and moving wildlife, and it brings you down to water level for photos you cannot take from a vehicle. 
Island bush camping means you sleep inside the wilderness rather than in a lodge on its edge. Walks with your guide focus on tracks, plants and the smaller details of the ecosystem, which makes this a very different experience from a classic game-drive safari. 
- Nature lovers: silent mokoro trips and guided island walks every day. 
- Short on time: a real Okavango experience in 3 days from Maun. 
- Budget-minded travellers: a bush camp keeps the cost well below fly-in lodges. 

## What to expect on the 3 day Okavango Delta mokoro safari 
Day 1 starts with a morning pick-up in Maun and a road transfer to the mokoro station, where you meet your poler. After about 1 to 2 hours on the water you reach a remote island, set up at the bush camp and head out on a guided nature walk. Dinner is served around the campfire. 
Day 2 begins with an early walk in the cool hours, the best time to track animals. After brunch and a rest, you try seasonal catch-and-release fishing, learn to pole a mokoro yourself, then take an afternoon mokoro excursion with a short walk and a sundowner on the water. 
Day 3 opens with a final early-morning walk and breakfast. You then pack up, glide back through the reeds and lagoons to the mokoro station, and transfer by road to Maun, where the safari ends. 

### When is the best time for a mokoro safari? 
The Okavango Delta’s floodwaters usually peak in the dry winter months, roughly June to August, which is when the channels are fullest for mokoro trips and wildlife gathers near the water. Water levels change each year, so we confirm conditions when you book. Read our best time to visit Botswana guide for a month-by-month view.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
Arrival & Mokoro Transfer into the Delta 

Your adventure begins with a morning pick-up from Maun ready to start your adventure for Okavango Delta Mokoro. You will be picked up by a representative from Divulge Adventures . You will travel by road towards the Okavango Delta, enjoying scenic views along the way as the landscape gradually changes into one of Africa’s most unique ecosystems. 
Upon arrival at the mokoro station, you will meet your experienced poler (guide), who will brief you on the journey ahead. From here, you will glide silently through the waterways in a traditional dugout canoe (mokoro), offering a peaceful and intimate way to explore the Delta. Keep an eye out for birds, frogs, and other wildlife along the channels. 
After approximately 1–2 hours on the water, you will arrive at a remote island where your bush camp will be set up. After settling in, enjoy a guided nature walk, where your guide will introduce you to the smaller details of the Delta ecosystemtracks, plants, and possible wildlife sightings. 
Dinner will be served around the campfire, followed by an evening under the stars. 

Day 02 : 
Walking Safari, Mokoro Training and Sundowner 

Wake up early for a guided morning walk during the cooler hours of the day. This is the best time to track animals and observe the Delta as it comes to life. Your guide will share insights into animal behavior, tracking techniques, and the surrounding environment. 
Return to camp for a well-deserved brunch and relaxation during the heat of the day. 
In the afternoon, enjoy a fishing activity (seasonal and catch-and-release), followed by a hands-on experience where you will learn how to pole a mokoro yourself under the guidance of your poler. This is a fun and interactive way to connect with the traditional way of navigating the Delta. 
Later in the afternoon, head out for a mokoro excursion combined with a short guided walk. As the day comes to an end, enjoy a peaceful sundowner on the water, watching the sunset over the Delta one of the most memorable moments of the safari. 
Return to camp for dinner and storytelling around the fire. 

Day 03 : 
Final Walk and return journey 

Start your final day with an early morning guided walk, taking advantage of the quiet and fresh atmosphere of the Delta. This walk offers another opportunity to track wildlife and appreciate the untouched beauty of the environment. Your guide will deepen your understanding of the ecosystem, from animal tracks to bird calls and plant uses. 
After the walk, return to camp for breakfast and begin packing up. 
You will then board your mokoro for the return journey through the channels back to the mokoro station. Enjoy the last moments of tranquility as you glide through the reeds and open lagoons. 
Upon arrival at the station, your vehicle will be waiting to transfer you back to Maun, marking the end of your unforgettable Okavango Delta mokoro safari experience.

When is the best time for the 3 day Okavango Delta mokoro safari? 

The 3 day Okavango Delta mokoro safari runs best when the floodwaters are high, usually from about June to August, because the channels are full and animals stay close to the water. Water levels vary from year to year, so we check conditions for your dates before confirming the mokoro trip. 

What is included in the 3 day Okavango Delta mokoro safari? 

The 3 day Okavango Delta mokoro safari includes non-resident park fees, all listed activities, 2 nights of bush camp accommodation, camping equipment, bed linen and towels, a professional guide and poler, transport and transfers from Maun, meals, drinks and taxes. International flights, extra nights, tips and personal items are not included. 

Where do you stay on the Okavango Delta mokoro safari? 

On the Okavango Delta mokoro safari you stay 2 nights in a bush camp set up on a remote island inside the Delta, reached by mokoro from the mokoro station. Camping equipment, bed linen and bath towels are provided, and dinners are served around the campfire. There is no lodge on this trip. 

How fit do you need to be for the Okavango mokoro trip? 

The Okavango mokoro trip suits anyone with average fitness who is happy to sit in a low canoe for 1 to 2 hours and walk at a gentle pace on uneven ground. Walks start early to avoid the heat. Families with older children can join, and we advise on minimum ages when you enquire.
