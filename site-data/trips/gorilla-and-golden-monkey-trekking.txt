## Overview 
Meet Rwanda’s two most famous primates in just two days. This 2 days gorilla and golden monkey trekking safari takes you to Volcanoes National Park for an unforgettable hour with a mountain gorilla family, followed the next morning by a lively encounter with the rare golden monkeys of the Virunga bamboo forests. In the afternoon of your gorilla day, you will visit the Gorilla Guardians Village, where local dancers, drummers and storytellers share Rwandan traditions. With a private 4×4, your own driver guide and a comfortable lodge near the park, this short safari delivers Rwanda’s highlights without rushing.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is your 2-day gorilla and golden monkey trekking safari day by day. 

Day 01 : 
Kigali - Volcanoes National Park (Gorilla Trekking & Cultural Visit) 

Your Divulge Adventures driver guide will pick you up at 4:30 AM from your Kigali hotel for the approximately 2.5-hour drive north, watching the sunrise over the volcanoes. Check in at the Kinigi park headquarters for the 7:00 AM briefing about you gorilla and golden monkey trekking and your gorilla family assignment. 
Your trek through bamboo and montane forest can take 1 to 6 hours, and once you find the gorillas you will spend one magical hour watching the silverback and his family. 
After the trek, check in at your lodge for lunch. In the afternoon, visit the Gorilla Guardians Village, a community project where former poachers now champion conservation, to enjoy traditional Intore dances, drumming, and village life. Dinner and overnight at Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge | Kinigi Guest House 

Day 02 : 
Golden Monkey Trekking - Kigali 

After an early breakfast, return to the Volcanoes National Park headquarters for your golden monkey trek. Walk into the bamboo forest with your ranger guide and spend one hour with a troop of these bright-orange, energetic monkeys as they feed and leap between the stems. 
The trek is gentle and perfect for photos. Return to the lodge for an early lunch and check out, then drive back to Kigali, approximately 2.5 hours, for drop-off at your hotel, residence or Kigali International Airport. Marking the endo of your 2-day gorilla and golden monkey trekking in Rwanda.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

Can you do gorilla and golden monkey trekking in 2 days? 

Yes. You trek gorillas on day one and golden monkeys on the morning of day two, both in Volcanoes National Park, and return to Kigali that afternoon. 

Which is harder, gorilla or golden monkey trekking? 

Gorilla trekking is usually harder, with hikes of 1 to 6 hours on steep terrain. Golden monkey treks are shorter and gentler, typically 30 minutes to 1.5 hours. 

What is the Gorilla Guardians Village? 

It is a community cultural centre near Volcanoes National Park, formerly known as Iby’iwacu, where visitors enjoy traditional dances, local crafts and village activities that support conservation. 

What should I pack for gorilla trekking? 

Pack waterproof hiking boots, long trousers, a long-sleeved top, a rain jacket, gardening gloves, a hat and a small daypack with water and snacks.
