## Overview 
A Rwanda golf and gorilla safari is a rare mix: a round on an 18-hole course in Kigali, and a once-in-a-lifetime hour with mountain gorillas in Volcanoes National Park, all in five relaxed days. 
You stay in luxury hotels and lodges, travel in a private 4×4 with your own driver guide, and enjoy time to unwind between the highlights. 

## Why choose a Rwanda golf and gorilla safari? 
Kigali is one of Africa’s cleanest and safest capitals, and Kigali Golf Resort sits right in the city, with rolling fairways and views over the hills. Volcanoes National Park is only about 2.5 hours away, so it is easy to combine both. 
This trip suits golfers who want more than a golf holiday, couples where one partner plays, and business travellers adding a short break. 
- Golf: green fees, rental clubs and a cart included. 
- Gorillas: one gorilla permit per person included. 
- Luxury: top hotels in Kigali and a premium lodge in Musanze. 

## What to expect on your Rwanda golf and gorilla safari 
On day one you are met at Kigali International Airport and taken to your hotel to settle in. Day two is golf day, with a morning round at Kigali Golf Resort and lunch at the clubhouse. 
On day three you visit the Kigali Genocide Memorial, then drive north to Musanze. In the afternoon you may visit the Ellen DeGeneres Campus of the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund to learn about gorilla research. 
Day four is the highlight. You trek through bamboo and montane forest to a habituated gorilla family and spend one unforgettable hour with them. Day five is a relaxed drive back to Kigali for your flight. 

### How fit do you need to be? 
Gorilla treks can take one to six hours on steep, muddy ground. Rangers match groups to families by fitness, and porters can carry your bag and help you along. 

## Best time for golf and gorillas in Rwanda 
Both activities run all year. June to September and December to February are drier, which means firmer fairways and easier trails. Gorilla permits are limited each day, so book early. 
Read our guide to gorilla trekking in Rwanda or see our luxury gorilla trekking Rwanda safari for a trip focused on the lodges. Park information is on Visit Rwanda’s Volcanoes National Park page .

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is your 5-day Rwanda golf and gorilla safari day by day. 

Day 01 : 
Arrival in Kigali 

Upon arrival at Kigali International Airport, you will be welcomed by the Divulge Adventures team and assisted through customs. Meet your private driver guide for a short briefing and transfer to your hotel, where a welcome drink awaits. Spend the afternoon at leisure, perhaps with a spa treatment, before dinner. Dinner and overnight at The Retreat by Heaven | Kigali Marriott Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Golf at Kigali Golf Resort 

After breakfast, transfer to Kigali Golf Resort for a morning round on its well-kept 18-hole course, with rolling fairways and views over the city's hills. Rental clubs and a golf cart are included. Enjoy lunch at the clubhouse, then choose between a second round in the afternoon or a relaxing return to your hotel. Dinner and overnight at The Retreat by Heaven | Kigali Marriott Hotel 

Day 03 : 
Kigali Genocide Memorial - Musanze 

After breakfast, visit the Kigali Genocide Memorial for a moving insight into Rwanda's history and its remarkable recovery. Then drive north to Musanze, approximately 2.5 hours, through the Land of a Thousand Hills. 
Check in at your luxury lodge and enjoy a free afternoon, or visit the Ellen DeGeneres Campus of the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund to learn about gorilla conservation before your trek. Dinner and overnight at The Bishop's House | Virunga Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Mountain Gorilla Trekking 

After an early breakfast, drive to the Kinigi park headquarters for permits and your briefing. Your trek through bamboo and montane forest leads to a habituated mountain gorilla family, where you will spend one unforgettable hour. Return to the lodge for lunch and an afternoon at leisure, perhaps with a massage. Dinner and overnight at The Bishop's House | Virunga Lodge 

Day 05 : 
Musanze - Kigali (Departure) 

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast before the scenic drive back to Kigali, with an optional stop at a craft cooperative for souvenirs. You will be transferred to your hotel or Kigali International Airport for your onward flight.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

Can you play golf in Rwanda? 

Yes. Kigali Golf Resort has a modern 18-hole course in the heart of Kigali, popular with visitors and residents alike. 

Can I combine golf and gorilla trekking in one trip? 

Yes. Kigali’s golf course and Volcanoes National Park are only about 2.5 hours apart, making it easy to combine both in five days. 

Do I need to bring my own golf clubs? 

No. Rental clubs and a golf cart are included, though you are welcome to bring your own clubs. 

What luxury lodges are near Volcanoes National Park? 

Popular luxury choices include Virunga Lodge, Bisate Lodge, The Bishop’s House, Singita Kwitonda and One&Only Gorilla’s Nest. 

Can non-golfers join a Rwanda golf and gorilla safari? 

Yes. While one partner plays, the other can enjoy a spa, a city tour, art galleries or Kigali’s markets and cafés. 

How much is a gorilla permit in Rwanda? 

A gorilla trekking permit costs USD 1,500 per person per trek, and one permit is included in this package. 

Can I play a second round of golf? 

Yes. A second round can be arranged on day two or another free afternoon. Caddie fees and extra rounds are paid separately.
