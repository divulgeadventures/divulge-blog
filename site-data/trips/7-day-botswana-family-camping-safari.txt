## 
This 7 day Botswana family camping safari is an adventure for families with older children. A crew sets up camp in Moremi, the Khwai Concession and Savuti, a camp chef cooks every meal, and you spend the days on game drives looking for elephants, lions, leopards and wild dogs. 

## Why is this 7 day Botswana family camping safari special for children? 
Sleeping in the wild, eating around the campfire and hearing lions at night make this a safari children remember. The crew sets up and packs down the camp, so families can relax between game drives. 
Botswana’s parks are unfenced and remote, so most camping safaris suit children aged around 8 and over. Tell us your children’s ages and we will confirm the right plan. 

## What does the family itinerary include? 
Two nights camping in Moremi Game Reserve, two in the Khwai Concession and two in Savuti, starting in Maun and ending at Maun or Kasane airport. 

### What is the camping like? 
Your crew sets up dome tents with bedding, a dining area and camp toilets and showers. Meals are freshly cooked by the chef and served around the fire.

## Who is the 7 day botswana family camping safari best for? 
The 7 day botswana family camping safari is best for families who want to share a safari, from young children to grandparents. Tell us everyone’s ages and we will match vehicles, rooms and activities to your family, so the 7 day botswana family camping safari runs at the right pace for your group.

For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. ## Can several families share the 7 day Botswana family camping safari? 
Yes. Camping suits groups of families well because the whole camp is yours. Larger groups travel in two vehicles with extra crew, and each family can pay separately.

Day 1 : 
Maun to Moremi Game Reserve 

Your 7 day botswana family camping safari begins today. Meet your guide at Maun airport and stop in town for any last-minute supplies. Drive through Shorobe Village to Moremi Game Reserve, with game viewing on the way. The crew sets up camp while you freshen up, then head out on your first afternoon game drive. 

Day 2 : 
Moremi Game Reserve 

Morning and afternoon game drives through Moremi's varied habitats, with a rest at camp during the hottest part of the day. Meals are cooked by the camp chef and served around the fire. 

Day 3 : 
Moremi to the Khwai Concession 

Break camp after breakfast and drive to the Khwai Concession, with game viewing along the way. After lunch at the new camp, take an afternoon game drive. 

Day 4 : 
Khwai Concession 

Morning and afternoon game drives in Khwai, known for elephants, lions, leopards, wild dogs and buffalo. 

Day 5 : 
Khwai to Savuti 

Drive to Savuti with a picnic lunch on the way. The crew sets up camp while you relax, then head out on an afternoon game drive. 

Day 6 : 
Savuti 

Morning and afternoon game drives in Savuti, famous for its elephant herds, lion prides and hyenas. 

Day 7 : 
Departure 

A final game drive on the way to Maun or Kasane airport for your onward journey. This is the end of your 7 day botswana family camping safari.
