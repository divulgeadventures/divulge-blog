## Overview 
An Akagera night game drive shows you a side of the bush most visitors never see. After dark, leopards, hyenas, servals, genets and bushbabies come out, and a trained park guide finds them with a spotlight. 
This two-day safari from Kigali also includes a morning boat cruise on Lake Ihema, day game drives across the park and a night at Karenge Bush Camp, a simple, seasonal tented camp in the heart of Akagera National Park. 

## Why choose an Akagera night game drive? 
Akagera is one of the few parks in East Africa where night drives are allowed. That makes it special for photographers and anyone who wants more than daytime sightings. 
Staying at a bush camp keeps you close to the action. You fall asleep to the sounds of the wild and wake up ready for the best time of day for lions. 
- Nocturnal wildlife: leopards, hyenas, civets and owls. 
- Water safari: hippos and crocodiles on Lake Ihema. 
- Big Five park: lions, elephants, buffaloes and rhinos by day. 

## What to expect on your Akagera night game drive safari 
You leave Kigali early for the 2.5-hour drive east and head straight to Lake Ihema for a morning boat cruise. Then you game drive north, looking for elephants, buffaloes, giraffes and zebras on the way to Karenge Bush Camp. 
After an early dinner, you set out on the night game drive. It lasts about two hours and is led by a park guide with a spotlight, while you stay safely in the vehicle. 
Day two starts with coffee and a morning game drive, when lions are often on the move. After breakfast you continue game viewing on the way out of the park, arriving in Kigali in the afternoon. 

### About Karenge Bush Camp 
Karenge is a small, seasonal camp, usually open in the dry months. If it is closed on your dates, we book a similar lodge inside or near the park so you still enjoy the night drive. 

## Best time for an Akagera night game drive 
Night drives run all year, but the dry seasons, June to September and December to February, make for easier driving and good sightings around the lakes. Nights can be cool, so bring a warm layer. 
Prefer a shorter trip? Try the Akagera day trip safari from Kigali . Planning gorillas too? Read our guide to gorilla trekking in Rwanda . For park facts, see Visit Rwanda’s Akagera National Park page .

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is your 2-day Akagera bush camp safari day by day. 

Day 01 : 
Kigali - Lake Ihema Boat Cruise - Karenge Bush Camp (Night Game Drive) 

Your driver guide will pick you up early from your Kigali hotel for the approximately 2.5-hour drive east to Akagera National Park. Head straight to Lake Ihema for a morning boat cruise, where hippos snort in the water, crocodiles sun themselves on the banks and fish eagles call overhead. 
Continue north on a game drive, spotting elephants, buffaloes, giraffes and zebras as you make your way to Karenge Bush Camp. Check in, enjoy lunch and relax during the heat of the day. 
As darkness falls, set out on a guided night game drive with spotlights to search for leopards, hyenas, servals, genets and bushbabies, animals you rarely see by day. Return to camp for a bush dinner under the stars. Dinner and overnight at Karenge Bush Camp 

Day 02 : 
Morning Game Drive - Kigali 

Rise early with a hot cup of coffee and set out on a morning game drive, the best time to catch lions on the move and herds heading to the water. Return to camp for a hearty breakfast and check out. 
Continue game viewing on your way to the park gate, then drive back to Kigali, arriving in the afternoon for drop-off in the city or a transfer to Kigali International Airport for an evening flight.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

What can you see on an Akagera night game drive? 

Night drives reveal nocturnal animals such as leopards, hyenas, servals, civets, genets, bushbabies and owls, plus hippos grazing out of the water. 

Is Karenge Bush Camp open all year? 

Karenge Bush Camp is a seasonal camp, usually open in the dry months. If it is closed, Divulge Adventures will book a similar lodge inside or near the park. 

How long is the night game drive in Akagera? 

Night game drives usually last about 2 hours, starting just after sunset, and are led by a park guide with a spotlight. 

Is the Lake Ihema boat cruise better in the morning or evening? 

Both are good. Morning cruises are calm and great for birds, while sunset cruises offer golden light for photos. This itinerary includes a morning cruise. 

Is an Akagera night game drive safe? 

Yes. You stay in the vehicle the whole time, and drives are led by trained park guides who know the animals and the terrain well. 

Can children join the Akagera night game drive? 

Yes, children can usually join. Let us know their ages when you book so we can confirm the current park rules. 

What should I bring for the night drive? 

Bring a warm jacket, a torch for camp, insect repellent and a camera that handles low light well. Binoculars also help.
