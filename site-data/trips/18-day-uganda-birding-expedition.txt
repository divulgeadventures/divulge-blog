## 
This Uganda birding expedition is our longest Ugandan route. In 18 days it visits nearly every major birding site in the country, ending with a cruise on the Nile at Murchison Falls. 
The pace allows two nights at most stops, so you have time to search properly for difficult forest birds. 

## Why choose this Uganda birding expedition? 
- Entebbe and Mabamba: turacos, then the shoebill by boat. 
- Lake Mburo: African finfoot and white-backed night heron. 
- Buhoma and Ruhija: Albertine Rift endemics in two parts of Bwindi. 
- Queen Elizabeth: Kasenyi plains and the Kazinga Channel. 
- Semliki, Budongo and Murchison: Congo Basin birds, the Royal Mile and the Nile. 

## Where you stay 
Lodges include an Entebbe hotel, a hotel in Mbarara, Buhoma Tented Camp, a lodge at Ruhija, Mweya Safari Lodge, Semliki Safari Lodge, Mountains of the Moon Hotel, Masindi Hotel and Paraa Safari Lodge, or similar. 

## Best time for a Uganda birding expedition 
Birding is good all year. The drier months are easiest for forest walking, while the wet months bring breeding activity and fewer visitors. 
Read our Uganda national parks guide and browse all birding safaris . Gorilla trekking can be added in Bwindi; see our Bwindi gorilla guide . Park permits come from the Uganda Wildlife Authority .

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Entebbe 

Airport pick-up and transfer to your hotel. 

Day 2 : 
Entebbe Botanical Gardens 

A relaxed day of birding in the botanical gardens for great blue and Ross's turacos and hornbills. 

Day 3 : 
Mabamba to Mbarara 

Boat trip in Mabamba for the shoebill and lesser jacana, then drive to Mbarara. 

Day 4 : 
Lake Mburo National Park 

Boat trip and park tracks for African finfoot, white-backed night heron and rufous-bellied heron. 

Day 5 : 
Mbarara to Bwindi 

Drive through the Kabale highlands to Buhoma Tented Camp, birding along the way. 

Day 6 : 
Buhoma forest 

A full day on the forest trails for alethes, cuckoo-shrikes and negrofinches. 

Day 7 : 
Buhoma to Ruhija 

Bird the Neck and the river on the way to Ruhija. 

Day 8 : 
Ruhija 

A full day for handsome francolin, Grauer's warbler and Shelley's crimsonwing. 

Day 9 : 
Ruhija to Queen Elizabeth via Ishasha 

Drive north via Ishasha's pools to Mweya Safari Lodge. 

Day 10 : 
Kasenyi and Kazinga Channel 

Early game drive on the Kasenyi plains and an afternoon boat trip. 

Day 11 : 
Queen Elizabeth to Semliki 

Drive to Semliki for humid forest birding. Overnight at Semliki Safari Lodge. 

Day 12 : 
Semliki National Park 

A full day for white-bellied kingfisher, red-billed dwarf hornbill and forest robins. 

Day 13 : 
Semliki to Fort Portal 

Morning birding in Semliki, then a short drive to Mountains of the Moon Hotel. 

Day 14 : 
Fort Portal to Masindi 

Drive north with stops in forest patches. Overnight at Masindi Hotel. 

Day 15 : 
Budongo's Royal Mile 

A pre-dawn start for the Royal Mile, with chocolate-backed kingfisher and malimbes. 

Day 16 : 
Masindi to Murchison Falls 

Drive over the escarpment and cross the Nile by ferry to Paraa Safari Lodge. 

Day 17 : 
Nile cruise and game drive 

Boat cruise towards the delta for goliath heron and northern carmine bee-eater, then an afternoon game drive. 

Day 18 : 
Murchison to Entebbe 

Drive back via Kampala to Entebbe for your flight.

How long is the Uganda birding expedition? 

It is 18 days, from arrival in Entebbe to departure from Entebbe. 

Is gorilla trekking included? 

Not by default, but a gorilla trek can easily be added in Buhoma or Ruhija. 

Which lodges are used? 

An Entebbe hotel, a Mbarara hotel, Buhoma Tented Camp, a Ruhija lodge, Mweya Safari Lodge, Semliki Safari Lodge, Mountains of the Moon Hotel, Masindi Hotel and Paraa Safari Lodge, or similar. 

How many species can I expect? 

Experienced birders often pass 500 species on this route. 

Is there a lot of driving? 

Yes, it is a long loop, but most stops have two nights and every drive includes birding. 

What is special about the Royal Mile? 

It is a wide forest track in Budongo with excellent views of forest birds such as chocolate-backed kingfisher. 

Can I fly back from Murchison? 

Yes. A scheduled flight from Murchison to Entebbe can replace the final drive.
