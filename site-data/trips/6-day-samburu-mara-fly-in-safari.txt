## 
This Samburu and Masai Mara fly-in safari shows you two very different sides of Kenya. Samburu is home to animals you will not see in the south, while the Mara is famous for big cats and the migration. 
The two reserves are far apart by road, so flying makes this route easy and comfortable. 

## Why choose this Samburu and Masai Mara fly-in safari? 
- Samburu Special Five: reticulated giraffe, Grevy’s zebra, Beisa oryx, gerenuk and Somali ostrich. 
- Leopards: both reserves are excellent for leopards. 
- Three nights in the Mara: two full days with bush lunches. 
- Time saved: flights replace two full days of driving. 

## Where you stay 
Two nights at Ashnil Samburu Camp and three nights at Mara Serena Safari Lodge, or similar. 

## Best time for a Samburu and Masai Mara fly-in safari 
Samburu is good all year. July to October adds the migration in the Mara.

Day 1 : 
Fly from Nairobi to Samburu 

Morning flight from Wilson Airport to Samburu. Afternoon game drive along the Ewaso Ng'iro River. Overnight at Ashnil Samburu Camp. 

Day 2 : 
Full day in Samburu 

Morning and afternoon game drives for the Special Five and leopards. Optional community visit. 

Day 3 : 
Fly from Samburu to the Masai Mara 

Morning game drive, then fly to the Mara via Nairobi. Afternoon game drive. Overnight at Mara Serena Safari Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Full day in the Masai Mara 

Morning and afternoon game drives with a bush lunch. Optional balloon flight or Maasai visit. 

Day 5 : 
Second full day in the Mara 

Explore new areas of the reserve, with another bush lunch. 

Day 6 : 
Fly back to Nairobi 

Early game drive, then fly to Wilson Airport and transfer to your hotel or JKIA.

How do I fly from Samburu to the Masai Mara? 

By scheduled light aircraft, usually connecting at Wilson Airport. The journey takes about one and a half to two hours. 

How much luggage can I bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. Ask us if you need extra space. 

Which lodges are used on the Samburu and Masai Mara fly-in safari? 

Ashnil Samburu Camp and Mara Serena Safari Lodge, or similar. 

Is Samburu hot? 

Yes, days are warm to hot. Mornings and evenings are pleasant. 

Can I add Amboseli? 

Yes. Two or three nights in Amboseli can be added with another short flight. 

Is this safari good for photographers? 

Very good. Samburu’s red earth and the Mara’s plains give varied, beautiful backgrounds. 

Is emergency evacuation cover available? 

Yes. We recommend Flying Doctors evacuation cover for every safari and can arrange it for you.
