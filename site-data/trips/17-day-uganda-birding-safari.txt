## 
This Uganda birding safari is designed for birders who want Uganda’s full range in one trip. It starts with the shoebill at Mabamba, heads north to Murchison Falls and Budongo, then sweeps through the western forests to Bwindi and back via Lake Mburo. 
Uganda packs more than 1,000 bird species into a small country. This route visits savannah, papyrus, lowland rainforest and montane forest, which is why lists of 450 species or more are common. 

## Why choose this Uganda birding safari? 
- Mabamba Swamp: a boat trip for the shoebill. 
- Murchison Falls: standard-winged nightjar, rock pratincole and a night drive. 
- Budongo and the Royal Mile: Puvel’s illadopsis, Nahan’s francolin and Ituri batis. 
- Kibale and Semliki: chimpanzees, pittas and Congo Basin specials. 
- Bwindi and Ruhija: African green broadbill, regal sunbird and an optional gorilla trek. 

## Where you stay 
Resort Beach Hotel in Entebbe, Paraa Safari Lodge, Masindi Hotel, Ndali Lodge, Semliki Safari Lodge, Mountains of the Moon Hotel, Mweya Safari Lodge, Gorilla Forest Camp, a lodge at Ruhija and Mantana Tented Camp, or similar. 

## Best time for a Uganda birding safari 
You can bird Uganda all year. June to August and December to February are drier, which makes forest trails easier. The wet months bring breeding plumage and fewer visitors. 
Read our Uganda national parks guide and Bwindi gorilla trekking guide , or browse all Uganda safaris . The Uganda Wildlife Authority issues permits for every park on this route.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Entebbe 

Airport pick-up and optional birding in the Entebbe Botanical Gardens for turacos and palm-nut vultures. Overnight at Resort Beach Hotel. 

Day 2 : 
Mabamba Swamp 

A boat trip through Mabamba's papyrus channels for the shoebill, papyrus gonolek and goliath heron. Return to Entebbe. 

Day 3 : 
Entebbe to Murchison Falls 

Drive north with birding stops. Evening night drive for nightjars. Overnight at Paraa Safari Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Murchison Falls National Park 

A full day in the savannah and woodland for ground hornbills, secretary birds, bustards and eagles. 

Day 5 : 
Budongo Forest 

Bird the Kaniyo Pabidi section of Budongo for Puvel's illadopsis, crested guineafowl and white-thighed hornbill. Overnight at Masindi Hotel. 

Day 6 : 
The Royal Mile 

A full day on the Royal Mile for Nahan's francolin, kingfishers and forest flycatchers. Overnight at Masindi Hotel. 

Day 7 : 
Masindi to Kibale 

Drive south along the Rift escarpment to Ndali Lodge near Kibale. 

Day 8 : 
Kibale chimpanzees and birds 

Chimpanzee tracking and forest birding, with an optional night walk. 

Day 9 : 
Kibale to Semliki 

Drive to Semliki for Congo Basin forest birds and the hot springs. Overnight at Semliki Safari Lodge. 

Day 10 : 
Semliki to Fort Portal 

Early birding on the Kirumia trail, then drive to Mountains of the Moon Hotel in Fort Portal. 

Day 11 : 
Queen Elizabeth National Park 

Game drive in the Kasenyi plains and a launch trip on the Kazinga Channel. Overnight at Mweya Safari Lodge. 

Day 12 : 
Ishasha to Bwindi 

Drive through Ishasha to Buhoma and start your forest birding. Overnight at Gorilla Forest Camp. 

Day 13 : 
Bwindi forest or gorilla trek 

Forest birding on the Buhoma trails, or an optional gorilla trek. 

Day 14 : 
The Neck to Ruhija 

Bird the Neck for twinspots and bush-shrikes on the way up to Ruhija. 

Day 15 : 
Ruhija high forest 

Bamboo and montane forest birding for crimsonwings, regal sunbird and Lagden's bush-shrike. 

Day 16 : 
Ruhija to Lake Mburo 

Drive to Lake Mburo for savannah and wetland birds. Overnight at Mantana Tented Camp. 

Day 17 : 
Lake Mburo to Entebbe 

Morning birding at Lake Mburo, then drive to Entebbe via the equator for your flight.

How many birds can I see on a Uganda birding safari? 

On this 17-day route, many birders record 450 to 550 species. 

Is gorilla trekking included? 

Gorilla trekking is optional on day 13 and the permit can be added. Chimpanzee tracking in Kibale is included. 

Which lodges are used? 

Resort Beach Hotel, Paraa Safari Lodge, Masindi Hotel, Ndali Lodge, Semliki Safari Lodge, Mountains of the Moon Hotel, Mweya Safari Lodge, Gorilla Forest Camp, a Ruhija lodge and Mantana Tented Camp, or similar. 

Where is the best place to see a shoebill? 

Mabamba Swamp near Entebbe is one of the most reliable sites, and Murchison Falls is another good chance. 

How fit do I need to be? 

Moderate fitness is enough for most trails. Optional gorilla trekking and some Ruhija trails are steeper. 

Is Uganda good for photographers? 

Yes. Turacos, kingfishers and the shoebill make great subjects, and the boat trips give low angles. 

Can I shorten this Uganda birding safari? 

Yes. We can drop the north and start from Kibale for a 12 to 13 day version.
