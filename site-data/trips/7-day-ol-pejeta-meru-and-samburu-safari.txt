## Overview 
This 7 day Ol Pejeta Meru and Samburu safari is a private road safari from Nairobi through northern Kenya. You spend 2 nights in Ol Pejeta Conservancy, 2 nights in Meru National Park and 2 nights in Samburu Game Reserve, looking for rhinos, the Big Five and the Special Five. It runs all year round, costs from USD 2,915 per adult and suits repeat visitors, photographers and anyone who wants quieter parks. 
You travel in a 4×4 Land Cruiser with an English or bilingual driver guide and stay in 3 and 4-star camps and lodges on full board, with unlimited game drives on the itinerary. 

## Why choose a 7 day Ol Pejeta Meru and Samburu safari? 
Northern Kenya offers wildlife you will not see in the Masai Mara. Ol Pejeta Conservancy is a sanctuary for black and white rhinos, with elephants, lions and views of Mount Kenya. Meru National Park, where Elsa the lioness of Born Free was released, is green, quiet and rich in birdlife, with more than 400 species. 
Samburu Game Reserve is home to the Special Five: Grevy’s zebra, reticulated giraffe, Beisa oryx, Somali ostrich and gerenuk, found mainly in Kenya’s dry north. Read our Ol Pejeta Conservancy guide and our Samburu Game Reserve guide before you go. 
- Rhino lovers: black and white rhinos at Ol Pejeta, plus the conservancy’s northern white rhinos. 
- Repeat visitors: three parks away from the busy southern circuit. 
- Photographers: rare northern species and two full game-drive days in each area. 

## What to expect on the 7 day Ol Pejeta Meru and Samburu safari 
Day 1 starts with a meet-and-greet and briefing in Nairobi, then a drive of about 4 hours to Ol Pejeta Conservancy and an afternoon game drive. Day 2 is a full day in Ol Pejeta, with rhinos, elephants and the Big Five, the conservancy’s research centre and the Sweetwaters Chimpanzee Sanctuary. Both nights are at Maisha Sweetwaters Camp or Sweetwaters Serena Camp. 
On day 3 you drive about 4 hours to Meru National Park for an evening game drive. Day 4 is a full day in Meru, visiting Elsa’s Kopje for the views and looking for elephants, lions, leopards and the park’s birdlife. Both nights are at Rhino River Camp or Ikweta Safari Camp. 
Day 5 is a drive of about 4 hours to Samburu Game Reserve with an evening game drive. Day 6 is a full day in Samburu for the Special Five, lions, leopards, elephants and the crocodiles and hippos of the Ewaso Ng’iro River, staying both nights at Ashnil Samburu Camp or Samburu Sopa Lodge. On day 7 you drive about 6 hours back to Nairobi, with an en-route game drive, for your hotel or flight. 

### When is the best time for this northern Kenya safari? 
The Ol Pejeta, Meru and Samburu safari runs all year round. Game viewing in northern Kenya is usually easiest in the dry months, roughly June to October and January to February, when animals gather along the Ewaso Ng’iro River and around water. The green season brings lush scenery, birdlife and fewer vehicles. See what affects the price in our Kenya safari cost guide .

The price includes a meet-and-greet and safari briefing, all transport in a 4WD Land Cruiser, 6 nights of accommodation (2 nights each in Ol Pejeta, Meru and Samburu), all park entry and conservation fees, full board meals, an English or bilingual driver guide, unlimited game drives on the itinerary and unlimited bottled drinking water. 
Not included are travel insurance, visas and international flights, tips (about USD 10 per person per day is suggested, not mandatory), alcoholic and soft drinks, and personal items such as phone calls and mosquito repellent. ## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
Nairobi - Ol Pejeta Conservancy 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by Divulge Adventures representative for meet, greet and safari briefing. Afterwards, you will depart Nairobi for Ol Pejeta conservancy, approximately 4 hours drive. Arriving at around lunchtime for check-in and lunch, followed by afternoon siesta. Later in the evening set out for afternoon game drive at Ol Pejeta Conservancy. 
Being a sanctuary for both black and white rhinos, Ol Pejeta hosts a large number of rhinos among other wildlife such as elephants, buffaloes, lions, 
leopards, cheetahs among others. Afterwards, return to the camp for dinner and overnight at Maisha Sweetwaters Camp / Sweetwaters Serena Camp 

Day 02 : 
Full Day at Ol Pejeta Conservancy 

After breakfast, set out for a full-day game drive into the home of large herds of elephants, rhinos and Mt Kenya view. A full-day game drive at Olpejeta Conservancy offers an unforgettable wildlife experience. You’ll explore vast savannahs teeming with wildlife, including the Big Five. 
Visit Baraka, the blind black rhino, and learn about conservation efforts at the research center. A highlight is seeing the last two remaining northern white rhinos. You’ll also stop by the Chimpanzee Sanctuary, where rescued chimps are rehabilitated. The trip provides insight into wildlife protection, making it both adventurous and educational. 
Later in the evening return back to the camp for dinner, and overnight 
at Maisha Sweetwaters Camp / Sweetwaters Serena Camp 

Day 03 : 
Ol Pejeta Conservacny - Meru National Park 

After a steamy hot delicious breakfast depart Ol Pejeta Conservancy and proceed to Meru National Park, which is approximately 4 hours away. Upon arrival, check in at the camp for lunch followed by afternoon siesta. In the evening set out for an evening game drive at Meru National Park, the home for Elsa the Lioness and bird watchers paradise. 
An afternoon game drive at Meru National Park offers a serene experience with spectacular wildlife sightings. As the sun sets, spot elephants, lions, and 
leopards against a beautiful landscape. The tranquil atmosphere makes it a perfect time for photography and exploration. Later, return back to the camp for dinner and overnight at Rhino River 
Camp | Ikweta Safari Camp 

Day 04 : 
Full Day at Meru National Park 

After an early and steamy breakfast, set out for Meru National Park full day game drive. A full-day game drive at Meru National Park takes you through the stunning landscapes where Elsa the Lioness, of Born Free fame, roamed. 
Visit Elsa’s Kopje for breathtaking views, and explore habitats home to the “Special Five” – gerenuk, Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe, Somali ostrich, and Beisa oryx. Discover scenic waterfalls and enjoy the park’s rich birdlife, with over 400 species making it a birdwatcher’s paradise. The diverse wildlife, scenic beauty, and connection to Elsa’s legacy make it 
an unforgettable experience. 
Dinner and overnight at Rhino River 
Camp | Ikweta Safari Camp 

Day 05 : 
Meru National Park - Samburu Game Reserve 

After delicious breakfast at the camp, checkout and depart Meru National Park proceeding to Samburu Game Reserve, which is approximately 4 hours away. Upon arrival, checking at the camp followed by lunch and afternoon relaxing moments. In the evening, set out for an evening game drive into the famous Samburu Game Reserve. 
An evening game drive at Samburu Reserve offers the chance to see the Big Four—lions, leopards, elephants, and buffalos—along with the Special Five, including Gravy’s zebra and reticulated giraffe. The golden sunset enhances the wildlife experience. Afterwards, return to the camp for dinner and overnight at Ashnil Samburu Camp | Samburu Sopa Lodge 

Day 06 : 
Full Day at Samburu Game Reserve 

After breakfast at your camp, set out for a full day game drive at Samburu National Game Reserve. A full-day game drive at Samburu National Game Reserve immerses you in Kenya’s rich wildlife. 
Home to the Big Four—lions, leopards, elephants, and buffalos—the reserve also boasts the Special Five: Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe, Beisa oryx, Somali ostrich, and gerenuk. Other wildlife includes cheetahs, common zebras, warthogs, and birdlife. Explore the dramatic landscapes of acacia woodlands and riverine forests, while spotting unique species like crocodiles and hippos along the Ewaso Ng'iro River. 
Afterwards, return to the camp for dinner and overnight at Ashnil Samburu Camp | Samburu Sopa Lodge 

Day 07 : 
Samburu Game Reserve - Nairobi 

After breakfast checkout at the camp, and embark on an enroute game drive to Nairobi approximately 6 hours away. Upon arrival, dorp off at your Nairobi hotel or at the airport for oubound flight. This marks the end of your 7 Day special five to Ol Pejeta Conservancy, Meru National park and Samburu Game Reserve.
