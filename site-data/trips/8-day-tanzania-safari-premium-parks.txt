## Overview 
This 8 day Tarangire Manyara Serengeti and Ngorongoro safari is a private 7-night northern Tanzania safari from Kilimanjaro International Airport. You spend a night in Arusha, game drive in Tarangire and Lake Manyara, stay 3 nights in the Serengeti and tour the Ngorongoro Crater before flying home. It runs all year round and costs from USD 3,395 per adult. 
It suits first-time visitors, couples and families who want all four classic northern parks in one trip, travelling in a private 4×4 Land Cruiser with a pop-up roof, a private English-speaking driver-guide and full board in mid-range lodges and tented camps. 

## Why choose an 8 day Tarangire Manyara Serengeti and Ngorongoro safari? 
Tarangire, Lake Manyara, the Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Crater each give a different safari, and this route visits all four in eight days. Tarangire is a baobab-rich park known for massive elephant herds and good predator sightings, while Lake Manyara is famous for tree-climbing lions, elephants, soda lake flamingos and groundwater forest below the Rift Valley wall. 
The Serengeti then gets 3 nights at one camp, with two full days on the plains looking for the wildebeest herds and big cats. The safari ends with a full crater tour, where black rhinos, lion prides, elephants and hyenas live in one natural bowl. 
- Private travellers: your own vehicle and driver-guide, with unlimited game drives. 
- First-timers: all four headline parks of the northern circuit in one trip. 
- Photographers: a pop-up roof and 3 nights in the Serengeti. 

## What to expect on the 8 day Tarangire Manyara Serengeti and Ngorongoro safari 
Day 1 starts at Kilimanjaro International Airport, where your guide meets you and drives you about 1 hour to Arusha for a rest, a safari briefing and the night at Mt. Meru Hotel. On day 2 you drive about 2.5 to 3 hours to Tarangire National Park for a full day of game drives with a picnic lunch, then about 1 hour to the Lake Manyara escarpment for the night at Ngorongoro Coffee Lodge. 
Day 3 is a game drive in Lake Manyara National Park, followed by an afternoon transfer of about 1 to 1.5 hours to the Karatu area and a second night at Ngorongoro Coffee Lodge. On day 4 you drive about 5 hours from Karatu to the Serengeti through the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, with scenic stops, and check in at Tamba Tented Camp. 
Days 5 and 6 are full days in Serengeti National Park, with sunrise and sunset game drives and both nights at Tamba Tented Camp. On day 7 you drive about 3 to 4 hours to the Ngorongoro Crater, descend for a crater tour with a picnic lunch on the floor and return to Ngorongoro Coffee Lodge. Day 8 is the drive of about 4 to 5 hours to Kilimanjaro Airport, which suits evening flights. 

### Is 8 days enough for Tarangire, Manyara, the Serengeti and Ngorongoro? 
Eight days are enough for Tarangire, Lake Manyara, the Serengeti and Ngorongoro because each park gets its own game drive and the Serengeti gets 3 nights. The long days are day 4 (about 5 hours to the Serengeti) and day 8 (4 to 5 hours to the airport). Read our Tarangire National Park guide and our Serengeti vs Ngorongoro guide to see what each park offers. For a mid-range route with Lake Eyasi instead of Lake Manyara, see the 8 day midrange Tanzania safari .

## Day by Day

Day 01 : 
Arrival at Arusha 

Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport. As you walk ou of the airport, your personal Divulge Adventures guide, greets you with a warm smile and welcome "Karibu Tanzania" and transfers you to Arusha (~1 hour drive) for relaxation and safari briefing. Overnight at Mt. Meru Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Arusha - Tarangire National Park 

After your delicious morning breakfast, you will exit Arush and drive to Tarangire National Park (~2.5–3 hours). Full-day game drives in this baobab-rich park, renowned for massive elephant herds and excellent predator sightings. Picnic lunch. Late afternoon transfer to the Lake Manyara escarpment area (~1 hour). Dinner and Overnight at Ngorongoro Coffee Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Tarangire National Park - Lake Manyara National Park 

After your delicious morning breakfast, you will exit Tarangire National Park and drive to Lake Manyara Game Drive a magical destination with the famous for tree-climbing lions, large elephant troops, soda lake flamingos, and varied habitats (groundwater forest, rift valley views). Afternoon transfer to Karatu area (~1–1.5 hours). Dinner and Overnight at Ngorongoro Coffee Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Lake Manyara National park - Serengeti National Park 

After your steamy hot breakfast, you will exit Lake Manyara National Park and drive from Karatu to Serengeti (~5 hours), transiting Ngorongoro Conservation Area with scenic stops and possible wildlife viewing en route. Three full days of extensive game drives across the endless plains, focusing on central/southern regions for calving season wildebeest herds and big cat action. Dinner and Overnight at Tamba Tented Camp 

Day 05 & 06 : 
Two Full Days at Serengeti National Park 

Experience the ultimate Tanzania Safari with two immersive days exploring the vast Serengeti plains. Witness the legendary Great Migration and track the "Big Five" across golden savannas. Your Tanzania Safari includes sunrise game drives to spot elusive predators and sunset excursions near iconic acacia trees. Spend your nights under the stars, surrounded by the incredible sounds of Africa’s most famous wilderness sanctuary. Dinner and Overnight at Tamba Tented Camp 

Day 07 : 
Serengeti National Park - Ngorongoro Crater 

Morning after breakfast exit Serengeti National Park with a packed lunch and head to Ngorongoro crater (~3–4 hours). Upon arrival, descent into the crater for a full-day tour (picnic lunch on the floor). Exceptional density of wildlife: black rhinos, lion prides, elephants, hyenas, and more in this natural wonder. Ascend in the evening, dinner and overnight at Ngorongoro Coffee Lodge 

Day 08 : 
Ngorongoro Crater - Arusha 

After your breakfast, depending on your flight time you will be transferred to Kilimanjaro Airport (~4–5 hours) for your outbound flight (suitable for evening departures; morning flights may require adjustment).
