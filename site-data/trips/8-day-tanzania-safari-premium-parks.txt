## Overview 
Experience an unforgettable 8-day Tanzania Safari exploring the iconic Tarangire, Lake Manyara, and Ngorongoro Crater. Witness the Great Migration’s vast herds in the Serengeti. This premium Tanzania Safari package offers unrivaled wildlife encounters and breathtaking landscapes across Africa’s premier parks. 

### Safari Highlights 
- Arusha National Park 
- Tarangire National Park 
- Lake Manyara National Park 
- Ngorongoro Crater 
- Serengeti National Park 

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
Arrival at Arusha 

Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport. As you walk ou of the airport, your personal Divulge Adventures guide, greets you with a warm smile and welcome "Karibu Tanzania" and transfers you to Arusha (~1 hour drive) for relaxation and safari briefing. Overnight at Mt. Meru Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Arusha - Tarangire National Park 

After your delicious morning breakfast, you will exit Arush and drive to Tarangire National Park (~2.5–3 hours). Full-day game drives in this baobab-rich park, renowned for massive elephant herds and excellent predator sightings. Picnic lunch. Late afternoon transfer to the Lake Manyara escarpment area (~1 hour). Dinner and Overnight at Ngorongoro Coffee Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Tarangire National Park - Lake Manyara National Park 

After your delicious morning breakfast, you will exit Tarangire National Park and drive to Lake Manyara Game Drive a magical destination with the famous for tree-climbing lions, large elephant troops, soda lake flamingos, and varied habitats (groundwater forest, rift valley views). Afternoon transfer to Karatu area (~1–1.5 hours). Dinner and Overnight at Ngorongoro Coffee Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Lake Manyara National park - Serengeti National Park 

After your steamy hot breakfast, you will exit Lake Manyara National Park and drive from Karatu to Serengeti (~5 hours), transiting Ngorongoro Conservation Area with scenic stops and possible wildlife viewing en route. Three full days of extensive game drives across the endless plains, focusing on central/southern regions for calving season wildebeest herds and big cat action. Dinner and Overnight at Tamba Tented Camp 

Day 05 & 06 : 
Two Full Days at Serengeti National Park 

Experience the ultimate Tanzania Safari with two immersive days exploring the vast Serengeti plains. Witness the legendary Great Migration and track the "Big Five" across golden savannas. Your Tanzania Safari includes sunrise game drives to spot elusive predators and sunset excursions near iconic acacia trees. Spend your nights under the stars, surrounded by the incredible sounds of Africa’s most famous wilderness sanctuary. Dinner and Overnight at Tamba Tented Camp 

Day 07 : 
Serengeti National Park - Ngorongoro Crater 

Morning after breakfast exit Serengeti National Park with a packed lunch and head to Ngorongoro crater (~3–4 hours). Upon arrival, descent into the crater for a full-day tour (picnic lunch on the floor). Exceptional density of wildlife: black rhinos, lion prides, elephants, hyenas, and more in this natural wonder. Ascend in the evening, dinner and overnight at Ngorongoro Coffee Lodge 

Day 08 : 
Ngorongoro Crater - Arusha 

After your breakfast, depending on your flight time you will be transferred to Kilimanjaro Airport (~4–5 hours) for your outbound flight (suitable for evening departures; morning flights may require adjustment). 
This itinerary is flexible – we can adjust for flight times, add a hot-air balloon ride, cultural visits (e.g., Maasai village),
