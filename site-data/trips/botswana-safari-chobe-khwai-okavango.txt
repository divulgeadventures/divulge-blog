## Overview 
This 7 day Chobe Moremi and Khwai safari is a fly-in and drive Botswana safari from Kasane to Maun. You spend 2 nights on the Chobe Riverfront, 2 nights near Moremi Game Reserve in the Okavango region and 2 nights in the Khwai Concession, on full board. The dry season, roughly May to October, is usually best for game viewing. The price starts from USD 4,870 per adult, and it suits couples and wildlife lovers. 
It combines boat cruises on the Chobe River with game drives in Moremi and Khwai, scheduled domestic flights within Botswana and stays at Chobe Safari Lodge, Mankwe Tented Retreat and Hideaways Mogotlho Safari Lodge. 

## Why choose a 7 day Chobe Moremi and Khwai safari? 
Chobe National Park is known for its large elephant herds, and the Chobe Riverfront lets you see them from a boat as well as a vehicle, with hippos, crocodiles and birdlife close by. Moremi Game Reserve protects part of the Okavango Delta, with floodplains, woodland and lions, leopards, wild dogs and buffalo. 
The Khwai Concession borders Moremi and is known for predator sightings, including African wild dogs, so the 7 day Chobe Moremi and Khwai safari covers three of northern Botswana’s best wildlife areas in one week. 
- Elephant lovers: two Chobe River cruises and a Chobe game drive. 
- Predator seekers: two full days in Moremi and Khwai. 
- Time-poor travellers: a scheduled flight saves a long drive into the Okavango region. 

## What to expect on the 7 day Chobe Moremi and Khwai safari 
Day 1 starts in Kasane with a transfer to Chobe Safari Lodge on the Chobe Riverfront and a sunset boat cruise among elephants, hippos and crocodiles. Day 2 brings a morning game drive in Chobe National Park, looking for lions, spotted hyenas, leopards, giraffes and zebras, and another river cruise in the afternoon. 
On day 3 you take a scheduled flight from Kasane to an airstrip near Moremi Game Reserve and a road transfer to Mankwe Tented Retreat. Day 4 is a full-day game drive in Moremi Game Reserve, through floodplains and woodland in the Okavango Delta ecosystem. 
Day 5 is a road transfer to the Khwai Concession and Hideaways Mogotlho Safari Lodge, with an evening game drive. Day 6 is spent in Khwai with morning and evening drives and an optional guided walking safari. On day 7, after breakfast and perhaps an early activity, you fly from the airstrip to Maun, where the safari ends. 

### When is the best time for a Chobe, Moremi and Khwai safari? 
The dry season, roughly May to October, is usually the best time for this safari, because animals gather near the Chobe River and the permanent water of the Okavango Delta. The green season from November to April brings fewer vehicles, newborn animals and good birding. See the months in detail in our best time to visit Botswana guide .

## Day by Day

Day 01 : 
Arrival in Kasane – Experience Chobe Riverfront Safari 

Upon arrival in Kasane, you will be warmly welcomed and transferred to Chobe Safari Lodge, beautifully located along the banks of the famous Chobe Riverfront. 
After check-in and lunch, take time to relax before beginning your Botswana Safari adventure with an unforgettable sunset boat cruise along the Chobe River. 
This scenic cruise offers incredible opportunities to witness large elephant herds gathering at the riverbanks, hippos submerged in the water, crocodiles basking along the shores, and spectacular African sunsets. 

Day 02 : 
Chobe National Park Wildlife Experience 

Begin your morning with an exciting game drive through Chobe National Park, one of Africa’s finest wildlife destinations known for its massive elephant population and abundant predators. 
Explore the park’s rich landscapes while searching for lions, spotted hyenas, leopards, giraffes, zebras, and antelope species. 
Later in the afternoon, enjoy another relaxing river safari cruise, allowing close-up views of hippos, crocodiles, and exceptional bird species. 
Chobe is internationally recognized as one of Southern Africa’s best wildlife-viewing destinations. 

Day 03 : 
Flight to Moremi Region – Gateway to the Okavango Wildernes 

After breakfast, transfer to Kasane Airport for a scheduled scenic flight to the wilderness region near Moremi Game Reserve. 
Upon arrival at the airstrip, enjoy a scenic road transfer to Mankwe Tented Retreat, a luxury tented retreat located near one of Botswana’s richest Safari ecosystems. 
The area is known for its beautiful landscapes of floodplains, woodland forests, and open savannah, creating ideal habitats for a wide range of wildlife species. 

Day 04 : 
Full Day Moremi Game Reserve Safari Experience 

Begin your day early as the African sunrise paints the vast wilderness of Moremi Game Reserve in golden hues. 
After breakfast, set out for a thrilling full-day game drive through one of Botswana’s most spectacular wildlife conservation areas, renowned for its rich biodiversity and breathtaking delta landscapes. 
The reserve offers exceptional opportunities to encounter Africa’s iconic wildlife, including large herds of elephants roaming freely, powerful lions resting in the grasslands, elusive leopards hidden within the woodlands, African wild dogs, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras, and numerous antelope species. 
As you journey deeper into the reserve, experience the beauty of the unique Okavango Delta ecosystem.Throughout the safari, highly experienced guides will share fascinating insights into animal behavior, local ecosystems, and Botswana’s ongoing conservation efforts, making the experience both adventurous and educational. 
Return to camp in the evening after an unforgettable day surrounded by some of Africa’s most pristine wilderness and extraordinary wildlife encounters. 

Day 05 : 
Transfer to Khwai Concession – Enter Botswana’s Predator Territory 

After an early breakfast, depart from the Moremi region and embark on a scenic road transfer toward the breathtaking Khwai Concession, a private wildlife concession renowned for its untouched wilderness, remarkable biodiversity, and some of Botswana’s best predator sightings. The journey itself offers spectacular views of changing landscapes, from open grasslands and acacia woodlands to seasonal floodplains that support an abundance of wildlife along the route. 
Upon arrival, check in at Hideaways Mogotlho Safari Lodge, a beautifully designed eco-friendly safari lodge set in complete harmony with nature, offering an authentic yet comfortable bush experience overlooking the Khwai River ecosystem. 
After settling in and enjoying lunch, spend the afternoon relaxing while taking in the peaceful surroundings before heading out later for an evening game drive. 
Explore the diverse floodplains and open bush landscapes as experienced guides track wildlife in search of elephants, buffaloes, zebras, giraffes, hippos, and predators such as lions, leopards, and hyenas that thrive within this remarkable wilderness area. 
End the day with spectacular sunset views over the African plains before returning to camp for dinner and overnight surrounded by the sounds of the wild. 

Day 06 : 
Khwai Wilderness Wildlife Viewing Experience 

Begin another exciting day in the heart of Botswana’s untamed wilderness as you set out to explore the spectacular landscapes of the Khwai Concession, one of the country’s most rewarding wildlife destinations known for its rich ecosystems and incredible predator activity. 
Start the morning with an early game drive as the bush comes alive with wildlife activity, offering excellent opportunities to encounter large herds of elephants, buffaloes grazing across the floodplains, zebras, giraffes, and antelope species roaming freely through the open savannah. 
The Khwai region is particularly famous for its high concentration of predators, giving guests the chance to witness thrilling sightings of lions, elusive leopards, spotted hyenas, and the rare endangered African wild dog, one of the continent’s most remarkable predators. 
During the day, guests may also participate in an optional guided walking safari, where experienced local guides provide fascinating insight into animal tracking techniques, smaller wildlife species, native vegetation, and the delicate balance of Botswana’s wilderness ecosystem. 
As the day winds down, embark on a final evening safari drive and witness the breathtaking beauty of the African sunset stretching across the Khwai floodplains, creating the perfect setting for photography and quiet appreciation of nature. 
Return to camp for dinner and relaxation, ending another unforgettable day surrounded by the sounds and beauty of Botswana’s extraordinary wilderness. 

Day 07 : 
Departure Flight to Maun 

Enjoy your final morning in Botswana with a relaxed breakfast and one last opportunity to appreciate the peaceful wilderness surroundings. 
Depending on flight schedules, guests may enjoy a final early morning activity before transferring to the airstrip for a scheduled domestic flight to Maun, where the safari officially concludes. 
Depart with unforgettable memories of your Botswana’s Safari stunning landscapes, world-class wildlife encounters, and authentic African safari experiences.
