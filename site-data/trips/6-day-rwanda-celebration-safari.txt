## 
This 6 day Rwanda celebration safari is for groups marking a special moment together, such as a milestone birthday, an anniversary, a reunion or a wedding party. You see the Big Five in Akagera, trek to mountain gorillas and golden monkeys in Volcanoes National Park, and stay in some of Rwanda’s finest lodges. 

## Why choose a 6 day Rwanda celebration safari? 
Few experiences mark a milestone like sitting with a gorilla family. This trip pairs that hour with two nights at Magashi Camp in Akagera, where boat cruises and game drives offer time to relax together, and two nights at Bisate Lodge near the volcanoes. 
We can arrange a celebration dinner, a cake, decorations or a surprise for the guest of honour at either lodge. 

## What does the celebration itinerary include? 
One night in Kigali, two at Magashi Camp in Akagera and two at Bisate Lodge in Volcanoes National Park. The last day includes golden monkey trekking before the drive back to Kigali for your flights. 

### Can guests who don’t want to trek still come? 
Yes. Guests under 15 or who prefer not to trek can visit the Ellen DeGeneres Campus of the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund, the Gorilla Guardians Village or a nature walk while the others trek. 
Read our guides to gorilla trekking in Rwanda and golden monkey trekking . 

## Who is the 6 day rwanda celebration safari best for? 
The 6 day rwanda celebration safari is best for milestone birthdays, anniversaries, reunions, retirements and wedding parties. Tell us the occasion and we will plan the special moments with the lodges, so the 6 day rwanda celebration safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## How many guests can join the 6 day Rwanda celebration safari? 
Luxury camps in Rwanda are small, so we plan rooms early and can split a large group between two lodges of the same standard. Gorilla families take up to 8 visitors each, so bigger groups trek to different families at the same time.

Price on request. Group prices depend on your dates, the number of travellers and the rooms you need, so we quote every group personally. Day 1 : 
Arrival in Kigali 

Your 6 day rwanda celebration safari begins today. Meet your guide at Kigali International Airport and transfer to your hotel. Dinner and overnight at Kigali Marriott Hotel or similar. 

Day 2 : 
Kigali to Akagera National Park 

Drive east to Akagera National Park for game drives and a boat cruise on Lake Rwanyakazinga. Dinner and overnight at Magashi Camp. 

Day 3 : 
Akagera safari day 

A full day of game drives and boat cruises in search of lions, rhinos, elephants and buffalo, with time to relax at camp. Dinner and overnight at Magashi Camp. 

Day 4 : 
Akagera to Volcanoes National Park 

Drive about 5 hours to Volcanoes National Park, then visit the Ellen DeGeneres Campus of the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund. Dinner and overnight at Bisate Lodge. 

Day 5 : 
Mountain gorilla trekking and Gorilla Guardians Village 

Trek with rangers to a mountain gorilla family and spend one hour with them. In the afternoon, visit the Gorilla Guardians Village for traditional dances. Dinner and overnight at Bisate Lodge. 

Day 6 : 
Golden monkey trekking and departure 

Trek to find golden monkeys, then drive back to Kigali International Airport for your flights. This is the end of your 6 day rwanda celebration safari.

What occasions suit the 6 day Rwanda celebration safari? 

The 6 day Rwanda celebration safari suits milestone birthdays, anniversaries, family reunions, retirements and wedding parties. We plan one or two special moments around your occasion at the lodges. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 6 day rwanda celebration safari. 

Is the gorilla permit included? 

Yes. The USD 1,500 Rwanda gorilla permit and the golden monkey permit are included for each guest who treks. Permits must be booked early for the whole group. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 6 day rwanda celebration safari. 

Can guests pay separately? 

Yes. Each guest or couple can pay their own share. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the rooms and permits, and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. 

What is the minimum age for gorilla trekking? 

Gorilla trekking is open to people aged 15 and over. Younger guests can join the rest of the trip and enjoy other activities while the group treks. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 6 day rwanda celebration safari. 

When is the best time for a Rwanda celebration safari? 

June to September and December to February are the driest months for trekking. Gorillas can be seen all year, and the green seasons are quieter. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 6 day rwanda celebration safari.
