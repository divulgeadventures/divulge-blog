## 
This Uganda honeymoon safari mixes gentle game viewing, island-dotted lake scenery and the thrill of meeting mountain gorillas together. Start at Mihingo Lodge in Lake Mburo National Park, spend two unhurried nights at Birdnest at Bunyonyi Resort on Lake Bunyonyi, then trek for gorillas from Buhoma Lodge in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest. A flight from Kihihi takes you back to Entebbe. It suits couples who want a romantic, less-travelled honeymoon with a real adventure at its heart. 
Price: from USD 3,630 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Gorilla trekking permit in Bwindi included 
- Two relaxed nights on Lake Bunyonyi 
- Zebras and impalas in Lake Mburo National Park 
- Flight from Kihihi back to Entebbe 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Entebbe to Lake Mburo 

Pick-up in Entebbe and drive west, crossing the equator, to Lake Mburo National Park. Evening game drive. Dinner and overnight at Mihingo Lodge. 

Day 2 : 
Lake Mburo to Lake Bunyonyi 

Morning boat trip or walk, then drive to the terraced hills of Kigezi and Lake Bunyonyi. Dinner and overnight at Birdnest at Bunyonyi Resort. 

Day 3 : 
Lake Bunyonyi 

Free day for a canoe trip between the islands, swimming or simply relaxing. Dinner and overnight at Birdnest at Bunyonyi Resort. 

Day 4 : 
Lake Bunyonyi to Bwindi 

Drive to the Buhoma sector of Bwindi Impenetrable Forest. Afternoon community walk. Dinner and overnight at Buhoma Lodge. 

Day 5 : 
Gorilla trekking 

Trek with rangers to spend one hour with a habituated mountain gorilla family. Dinner and overnight at Buhoma Lodge. 

Day 6 : 
Fly to Entebbe 

Transfer to Kihihi airstrip for the scheduled flight back to Entebbe.

How much does this Uganda honeymoon safari cost? 

It starts from USD 3,630 per adult sharing, including the gorilla permit, based on low-season rates. We confirm the price for your dates when you enquire. 

Is gorilla trekking safe for couples? 

Yes. Treks are led by armed Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers and trackers, and groups are limited to eight visitors per gorilla family. 

When is the best time to go? 

June to September and December to February are drier and easier for trekking.
