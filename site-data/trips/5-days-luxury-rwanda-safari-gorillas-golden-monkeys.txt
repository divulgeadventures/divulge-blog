## Overview 
This 5 days luxury Rwanda safari is designed for travellers who want the gorilla experience with the highest level of comfort. You stay in top Kigali hotels and at an ultra-luxury lodge on the edge of Volcanoes National Park, such as Singita Kwitonda. 
The trip includes a mountain gorilla trek, a golden monkey trek, a visit to the Kigali Genocide Memorial and private 4×4 transport, with the option of helicopter transfers. 

## Why choose a 5 days luxury Rwanda safari? 
Five days is the sweet spot for a luxury gorilla trip. You have time for two primate treks, time to enjoy your suite and spa, and no rushed early drives on arrival or departure days. 
Rwanda’s luxury lodges are among the finest in Africa, with private plunge pools, fireplaces, views of the volcanoes and strong conservation and community programmes. 
- Honeymooners: romantic suites and private experiences. 
- Discerning travellers: seamless VIP service from arrival. 
- Primate lovers: gorillas and rare golden monkeys. 

## What to expect on your 5 days luxury Rwanda safari 
You receive a VIP welcome at Kigali International Airport and spend day one relaxing at your hotel. On day two you visit the Kigali Genocide Memorial, enjoy lunch at a top restaurant and travel to Volcanoes National Park by road or by optional helicopter. 
Day three is your gorilla trek. Rangers assign your group of up to eight to a habituated family, and you spend one magical hour in their company before returning to the lodge. 
On day four you trek golden monkeys in the bamboo forest. These playful, orange-backed monkeys live only in the Albertine Rift. You then return to Kigali for a final night, and day five is free until your flight. 

### How fit do you need to be? 
Gorilla treks can take one to six hours. Golden monkey treks are usually shorter and gentler. Porters are available on both, and your guide will set a comfortable pace. 

## Best time for a luxury Rwanda safari 
The drier months, June to September and December to February, are most popular. Luxury lodges fill up early, so book six to twelve months ahead for the best suites and permit dates. 
Read our guide to golden monkey trekking in Rwanda and gorilla trekking in Rwanda . For a different lodge style, see our luxury gorilla trekking Rwanda safari . Park details are on Visit Rwanda’s Volcanoes National Park page .

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is your 5-day luxury Rwanda safari day by day. 

Day 01 : 
Arrival in Kigali 

Upon arrival at Kigali International Airport, you will receive a VIP welcome and fast-track assistance from the Divulge Adventures team. Transfer to your hotel and spend the rest of the day enjoying its pool, spa and restaurants. Dinner and overnight at The Retreat by Heaven | Kigali Marriott Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Kigali Genocide Memorial - Volcanoes National Park 

After breakfast, visit the Kigali Genocide Memorial and enjoy lunch at one of Kigali's top restaurants. Then drive north to Volcanoes National Park, approximately 2.5 hours, or take an optional 30-minute helicopter flight at extra cost. Check in at your lodge, where your private suite looks out over the volcanoes. 
In the afternoon, you may visit the Ellen DeGeneres Campus of the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund. Dinner and overnight at Singita Kwitonda Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Mountain Gorilla Trekking 

After an early breakfast, transfer to the Kinigi park headquarters for your briefing and gorilla family assignment. Trek through bamboo and montane forest and spend one magical hour with a mountain gorilla family. Return to the lodge for lunch and rest. In the afternoon, an optional visit to the Gorilla Guardians Village offers traditional dances and a glimpse of local life. Dinner and overnight at Singita Kwitonda Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Golden Monkey Trekking - Kigali 

After breakfast, set out on a golden monkey trek, spending one hour with these vibrant monkeys in the bamboo forest. Return to the lodge for lunch and check out, then drive back to Kigali, approximately 2.5 hours, or fly by helicopter at extra cost. Check in for your final night. Dinner and overnight at The Retreat by Heaven | Kigali Marriott Hotel 

Day 05 : 
Departure 

Enjoy a leisurely breakfast and check out. If time allows, browse Kigali's craft and design shops before your transfer to Kigali International Airport for your onward flight.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

What is Singita Kwitonda Lodge? 

Singita Kwitonda is an ultra-luxury lodge on the edge of Volcanoes National Park, with private suites, plunge pools and a strong focus on conservation and community. 

Can I take a helicopter to Volcanoes National Park? 

Yes. A private helicopter transfer from Kigali takes about 30 minutes and can be added at extra cost. 

How many days do you need for a luxury gorilla safari in Rwanda? 

Four to five days is ideal for a gorilla trek, a golden monkey trek and time to enjoy your lodge. Add Akagera or Nyungwe for a longer trip. 

Is a 5-day luxury safari suitable for a honeymoon? 

Yes. The combination of a romantic lodge, private guiding and a gorilla encounter makes it a favourite with honeymooners. 

What makes a 5 days luxury Rwanda safari different from a standard trip? 

You stay in top lodges with suites and spas, enjoy VIP service and private guiding, and can add helicopter transfers. The gorilla trek itself is the same for every visitor. 

How long is a golden monkey trek? 

Golden monkey treks usually take a few hours in total, including one hour with the monkeys. They are generally easier than gorilla treks. 

What is the minimum age for gorilla trekking? 

Visitors must be at least 15 years old to trek mountain gorillas in Rwanda.
