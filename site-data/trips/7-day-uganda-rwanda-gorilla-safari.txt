## 
This Uganda and Rwanda gorilla safari gives you two different gorilla encounters in one trip. Start in Kigali, track golden monkeys and mountain gorillas in Rwanda’s Volcanoes National Park, then cross the Cyanika border into Uganda for a second gorilla trek in the Rushaga sector of Bwindi Impenetrable Forest. Finish with a night on Lake Bunyonyi before returning to Kigali. Seeing two gorilla families in two countries, in bamboo slopes and in dense rainforest, makes this one of the best primate trips in Africa. 
Price: from USD 4,580 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Two gorilla treks: Volcanoes National Park and Bwindi 
- Golden monkey tracking in the Virunga bamboo forest 
- Lake Bunyonyi, one of Africa's deepest lakes 
- Starts and ends in Kigali 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Kigali 

Meet at Kigali airport and city tour including the Kigali Genocide Memorial. Dinner and overnight at Hotel des Mille Collines. 

Day 2 : 
Golden monkeys in Volcanoes 

Early drive to Kinigi and golden monkey tracking in the bamboo zone. Afternoon at leisure. Dinner and overnight at Mountain Gorilla View Lodge. 

Day 3 : 
Gorilla trekking in Rwanda 

Trek with rangers in Volcanoes National Park to spend one hour with a mountain gorilla family. Dinner and overnight at Mountain Gorilla View Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Cross into Uganda 

Drive to the Cyanika border and on to the Rushaga sector of Bwindi Impenetrable Forest. Dinner and overnight at Gorilla Safari Lodge. 

Day 5 : 
Gorilla trekking in Uganda 

A second gorilla trek, this time in the dense rainforest of Bwindi. Dinner and overnight at Gorilla Safari Lodge. 

Day 6 : 
Bwindi to Lake Bunyonyi 

Short drive to Lake Bunyonyi for a relaxed afternoon canoe trip between the islands. Dinner and overnight at Arcadia Cottages. 

Day 7 : 
Lake Bunyonyi to Kigali 

Drive back to Kigali via the Katuna border for your departure flight.

How much does this Uganda and Rwanda gorilla safari cost? 

It starts from USD 4,580 per adult sharing, including both gorilla permits, based on low-season rates. We confirm the price and permit availability for your dates when you enquire. 

Which visa do we need? 

The East Africa Tourist Visa covers Rwanda, Uganda and Kenya for 90 days and costs USD 100. Apply online before travel. 

Why trek gorillas twice? 

Each family behaves differently, and the habitats differ: bamboo and volcano slopes in Rwanda, thick rainforest in Bwindi. Many guests say the second trek is when they truly take it in.
