## Overview 
Experience Ngorongoro & Serengeti Safari that begins with an exclusive, up-close animal interaction at Serval Wildlife. 

Before heading into Tarangire National Park to witness massive elephant herds and iconic baobab trees. 
The adventure continues through the lush groundwater forests of Lake Manyara National Park, home to tree-climbing lions, followed by a dramatic descent into the wildlife-dense Ngorongoro Crater to spot endangered black rhinos. 
Travelers then journey into the Serengeti National Park, spending multiple days tracking the massive herds of the Great Wildebeest Migration alongside active apex predators. 
The itinerary dynamically shifts location based on the season to capture peak migration highlights—such as the Southern calving season or the Northern river crossings—before concluding with a scenic charter flight back to Arusha for your departure. 

### Highlights 
- The Great Migration 
- Ngorongoro Crater Floor 
- Tarangire’s Giants 
- Serengeti Predator Action 
- Manyara’s Tree-Climbing Lions 
- Tanzania Wildebeest Migration Safari 

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
BEGIN YOUR GREAT MIGRATION SAFARI 

As you walk out of Kilimanjaro International Airport ready to begin your Tanzania Wildebeest Migration Safari your personal Divulge Adventures guide greets you with a big smile: 'Karibu Tanzania - welcome to Tanzania!' 
The guide takes you straight to your hotel approximately 2 hours’ drive, where you can relax and take in the African atmosphere. 
Afterwards, you will head to Serval Wildlife, where you will get a chance to interact with wildlife up close to even feeding and patting them with your own hands. 
Afterwards, you will be taken to your hotel for hot lunch, followed by afternoon relaxing. Later, dinner and overnight at Serval Wildlife Villas 

Day 02 : 
SERVAL WILDLIFE to TARANGIRE NATIONAL PARK 

After your morning breakfast, you will check out and proceed to Tarangire National Park, approximately 3 hours’ drive to begin your Tanzania Wildebeest Migration Safari. 
Enjoy the Tarangire diverse landscapes, marvel at massive elephant herds and ancient baobab trees. Enjoy a scenic picnic lunch overlooking the Tarangire River, a vital wildlife lifeline. 
Afterwards, exit Tarangire National Park and head to your hotel. Dinner and Overnight and Manyara Secrete Lodge | Escarpment Luxury Lodge Manyara 

Day 03 : 
TARANGIRE NATIONAL PARK to MTO WA MBU (LAKE MANYARA PARK) 

After your delicious breakfast,begin your Tanzania Wildebeest Migration Safari with a drive and proceed to Mto wa Mbu, approximately, 2 hours’ drive. 
Upon arrival, you will enjoy cycling in/near Mto wa Mbu area, get to visit the community plains . 

At the Maasai village visit: you will get to learn traditions, see dances, homes and interactive cultural activities for all ages. 
You will return back to the lodge for hot lunch and afternoon relaxing. 

Later in the evening, set out for a Canoe Ride the deepest lake in Africa. 
Afterwards, you will head back to the lodge to prepare for your night game drive at Lake Manyara National Park. Dinner and overnight at Lake Manyara Kilimamoja Lodge. 

Day 04 : 
LAKE MANYARA NATIONAL PARK to SERENGETI NATIONAL PARK 

After your steamy hot breakfast,begin your Ngorongoro & Serengeti Safari with a drive and you will exit Lake Manyara National Park and drive from Karatu to Serengeti approximately 5 hours, drive, transiting through Ngorongoro Conservation Area with scenic stops and possible wildlife viewing en route. 
Afterwards, you will head to Serengeti arriving by around lunchtime, check-in for lunch followed by afternoon siesta. 

Later in the afternoon set out for afternoon game drive at the great plains of Serengeti National Park. 
Then return to the camp for dinner and overnight at Mbali Mbali Soroi Serengeti Lodge | Gran Melia Serengeti Lodge 

Day 05 & 06 : 
TWO FULL DAYS ON A TANZANIA WILDEBEEST MIGRATION SAFARI 

Experience the ultimate Ngorongoro & Serengeti Safari with two immersive days exploring the vast Serengeti plains. 

Your two-day Serengeti expedition plunges you into the heart of the Great Migration, where millions of wildebeest and zebras paint the plains in a living mosaic of movement. 
Your safari game drive will focus on the herd’s current location, whether you’re witnessing the desperate, dust-filled river crossings in the north or the vulnerable calving season in the south. 
Between the adrenaline of the hunt and sightings of the Big Five, you’ll experience the raw, unscripted beauty of the African savannah, culminating in an unforgettable journey through one of nature’s last great spectacles. Dinner and overnight at Mbali Mbali Soroi Serengeti Lodge | Melia Serengeti Lodge 

Day 07 : 
SERENGETI NATIONAL PARK to NGORONGORO CRATER 

Morning after steamy hot breakfast and having had an experience of Ngorongoro & Serengeti Safari exit Serengeti National Park with a packed lunch and head to Ngorongoro crater approximately 4 hours’ drive. 
You will begin by enjoying a scenic picnic lunch near a hippo pool, surrounded by the sights and sounds of the wild. 
Afterward, your afternoon game drive at Ngorongoro & Serengeti Safari traverses this self-contained paradise, home to the densest concentration of lions and the rare black rhino. 
Navigating through golden grasslands and lush forests, you’ll witness a remarkable sanctuary where wildlife thrives in stunning abundance. Later, ascend in the evening, dinner and overnight at Ngorongoro Oldeani Mountain Lodge | Melia Ngorongoro Crater 

Day 08 : 
NGORONGORO CRATER to ARUSHA (DEPARTURE) 

After your breakfast, depending on your flight time you will be transferred to Kilimanjaro Airport approximately 4 hours’ drive for your outbound flight, Marking the end of your Ngorongoro & Serengeti Safari
