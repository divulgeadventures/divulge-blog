## Overview 
This nine day Tanzania honeymoon itinerary offers a seamless blend of thrilling wildlife safaris and romantic beach relaxation. 
Tailored perfectly for both jungle enthusiasts and beach lovers. Your journey begins with a gentle introduction to Arusha, featuring a scenic tour of a Kilimanjaro coffee plantation and romantic volcano views. You will then venture into Tanzania’s premier wildlife destinations. 
Starting with the ancient baobab landscapes and massive elephant herds of Tarangire National Park, followed by two days of deep immersion in the iconic Serengeti National Park to track big cats and witness the endless savanna plains. 
After descending into the spectacular, wildlife-rich Ngorongoro Crater, you will trade the safari dust for paradise with a flight to Zanzibar. 
The final leg of your honeymoon is dedicated to ultimate coastal relaxation in Kendwa, filled with crystal-clear waters, snorkeling at Mnemba Atoll, a sunset dhow cruise, a fragrant spice tour, and intimate beachside dining before your departure back. 

### Highlights 
- Kilimanjaro Coffee Tour 
- Tarangire Elephants 
- Serengeti Endless Plains 
- Ngorongoro Crater Floor 
- Honeymoon Perks

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
Arrival in Arusha & Kilimanjaro Introduction 

Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) at 7:25 AM ready to experience your Tanzania honeymoon. Private transfer (1 hour) to Arusha. 
Light breakfast and check-in later in the afternoonPrivate guided tour to a Kilimanjaro coffee plantation near Moshi (1-hour drive), where you'll stroll lush foothills, sip fresh brews, and catch stunning volcano views perfect for your jungle lover without a full climb. 
In the evening you will experience Romantic sundowner at a viewpoint overlooking Mount Kilimanjaro. Dinner and overnigt stay at Arusha Green Side Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Arusha to Tarangire National Park 

Your Tanzania honeymoon experience will begin in the Morning with a transfer (2.5 hours) to Tarangire, Tanzania's "elephant kingdom." 
You will have a Full afternoon game drive to Spot elephants, lions, giraffes, and over 500 bird species amid iconic baobab trees. Your guide can arrange a private picnic for two. 
Later in the evening you will Relax with a bush dinner under the stars. Dinner and overnight stay at Sangaiwe Tented Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Tarangire to Serengeti National Park 

You will have a morning drive (4-5 hours) for your Tanzania honeymoon experience to central Serengeti, with en-route stops for photos. 
Later in the afternoon game drive: (zebras, wildebeest, predators in action)—tailored for your wildlife enthusiast. 
At sunset you will have an optional hot-air balloon ride for aerial romance. Dinner and overnight stay at Moyo Tented Camp 

Day 04 : 
Full Day in Serengeti 

All-day exploration for your Tanzania honeymoon Morning and afternoon drives tracking big cats, cheetahs, and hippo pools. Add a bush walk (guided, safe) for your jungle fan. 
At midday you will have a Private shaded lunch in the bush. Later in the Evening you will have stargazing with a Maasai storyteller for cultural flair. Dinner and overnight stay at Moyo Tented Camp 

Day 05 : 
Serengeti to Ngorongoro Crater 

Your Tanzania honeymoon will begin with an early Morning game drive in Serengeti, then transfer (3 hours) to Ngorongoro Conservation Area. 
Later in the Afternoon Enter the crater floor for a game drive—home to the Big Five (lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, rhino) in a lush, volcanic bowl. Picnic lunch by a soda lake. 
Later in the evening Unwind with crater rim views and a couples' massage. Dinner and overnight stay at Baghayo Garden Suites Lodge 

Day 06 : 
Ngorongoro to Zanzibar 

In the morning you will have an Optional early crater drive or relaxation. Then later transfer (2 hours) to Arusha Airport. 
You will have an afternoon flight (1 hour) to Zanzibar and a Private transfer to your resort. 
Later in the evening you will have a Sunset dhow cruise. Dinner and overnight stay at Zanzibar Bay Resort 

Day 07 : 
Zanzibar Beaches & Water Adventures 

In the morning you will experience Snorkeling trip to Mnemba Atoll (colorful reefs, turtles—gear included). 
Later in the afternoon you will go to Lounge on white-sand beaches or try stand-up paddleboarding/kayaking. 
Later in the evening you will have Private beach dinner with Zanzibari spices and live music. Dinner and overnight stay at Zanzibar Bay Resort 

Day 08 : 
Zanzibar Exploration 

In the morning you will have a guided spice tour (1 hour from resort) wander clove plantations, taste exotic fruits, and learn about Swahili flavors (great for couples' photos). 
In the afternoon you will experience Boat excursion to nearby Changuu Island (Prison Island) for giant tortoises and more snorkeling. 
In the evening you have Free time for spa treatments or beach walks; optional couples' cooking class. Dinner and overnight stay at Zanzibar Bay Resort 

Day 09 : 
Departure from Zanzibar 

Morning transfer to Zanzibar Airport (ZNZ) for your flight back.
