## Overview 
This 9 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar honeymoon combines 5 nights on safari with 3 nights on the beach. You stay 1 night in Arusha, 1 in Tarangire, 2 in the Serengeti and 1 near the Ngorongoro Crater, then fly to Zanzibar. The page gives no fixed season, and it costs from USD 3,242 per adult, with mid-range lodges on a double-occupancy basis. 
It suits honeymooners and couples who want the classic northern parks followed by beach time, snorkelling and a sunset dhow cruise, with private transfers, an English-speaking guide and an internal flight to Zanzibar included. 

## Why choose a 9 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar honeymoon? 
The 9 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar honeymoon gives you the two halves most couples want from Tanzania without changing guides or arranging flights yourself. The safari covers Tarangire’s elephants and baobabs, two nights in the Serengeti for big cats and the open plains, and an afternoon on the Ngorongoro Crater floor. 
The Zanzibar part then slows the pace, with three nights at Zanzibar Bay Resort, a snorkelling trip to Mnemba Atoll, a spice tour and a boat trip to Changuu (Prison) Island for giant tortoises. 
- Honeymooners: a sundowner overlooking Mount Kilimanjaro, a bush dinner and a private beach dinner on the itinerary. 
- Wildlife lovers: Tarangire, the Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Crater in five days. 
- Beach lovers: three nights in Zanzibar with time for snorkelling, paddleboarding or the spa. 

## What to expect on the 9 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar honeymoon 
Day 1 starts at Kilimanjaro International Airport with a 1-hour private transfer to Arusha, then a guided tour of a coffee plantation near Moshi (about 1 hour away) and an evening sundowner with views of Mount Kilimanjaro. The night is at Arusha Green Side Hotel. On day 2 you transfer about 2.5 hours to Tarangire for an afternoon game drive and a bush dinner, staying at Sangaiwe Tented Lodge. 
Day 3 is a 4 to 5 hour drive to the central Serengeti with an afternoon game drive, and day 4 is a full day of Serengeti game drives with a bush lunch. Both nights are at Moyo Tented Camp. On day 5 you have an early game drive, transfer about 3 hours to the Ngorongoro Conservation Area and descend to the crater floor in the afternoon, staying at Baghayo Garden Suites Lodge. 
On day 6 you transfer about 2 hours to Arusha Airport for a 1-hour flight to Zanzibar and a sunset dhow cruise. Day 7 brings snorkelling at Mnemba Atoll and a private beach dinner, and day 8 a spice tour and a boat trip to Changuu (Prison) Island. All three Zanzibar nights are at Zanzibar Bay Resort. Day 9 is your transfer to Zanzibar Airport. 

### Is 9 days enough for a Tanzania safari and Zanzibar honeymoon? 
Nine days are enough for a Tanzania safari and Zanzibar honeymoon if you want the highlights of both, because the safari covers three parks in five days and the beach gets three full nights. Days 3 and 5 involve the longest drives. Read our Tanzania honeymoon guide and Zanzibar beach extension guide , and compare camps in our Serengeti camps guide .

The Tanzania and Zanzibar honeymoon price includes all ground transfers, park fees, game drives, the internal flight to Zanzibar, most meals, mid-range accommodation on a double-occupancy basis, an English-speaking guide, bottled water and the Zanzibar health insurance.

## Day by Day

Day 01 : 
Arrival in Arusha & Kilimanjaro Introduction 

Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) at 7:25 AM ready to experience your Tanzania honeymoon. Private transfer (1 hour) to Arusha. 
Light breakfast and check-in later in the afternoonPrivate guided tour to a Kilimanjaro coffee plantation near Moshi (1-hour drive), where you'll stroll lush foothills, sip fresh brews, and catch stunning volcano views perfect for your jungle lover without a full climb. 
In the evening you will experience Romantic sundowner at a viewpoint overlooking Mount Kilimanjaro. Dinner and overnigt stay at Arusha Green Side Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Arusha to Tarangire National Park 

Your Tanzania honeymoon experience will begin in the Morning with a transfer (2.5 hours) to Tarangire, Tanzania's "elephant kingdom." 
You will have a Full afternoon game drive to Spot elephants, lions, giraffes, and over 500 bird species amid iconic baobab trees. Your guide can arrange a private picnic for two. 
Later in the evening you will Relax with a bush dinner under the stars. Dinner and overnight stay at Sangaiwe Tented Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Tarangire to Serengeti National Park 

You will have a morning drive (4-5 hours) for your Tanzania honeymoon experience to central Serengeti, with en-route stops for photos. 
Later in the afternoon game drive: (zebras, wildebeest, predators in action)—tailored for your wildlife enthusiast. 
At sunset you will have an optional hot-air balloon ride for aerial romance. Dinner and overnight stay at Moyo Tented Camp 

Day 04 : 
Full Day in Serengeti 

All-day exploration for your Tanzania honeymoon Morning and afternoon drives tracking big cats, cheetahs, and hippo pools. Add a bush walk (guided, safe) for your jungle fan. 
At midday you will have a Private shaded lunch in the bush. Later in the Evening you will have stargazing with a Maasai storyteller for cultural flair. Dinner and overnight stay at Moyo Tented Camp 

Day 05 : 
Serengeti to Ngorongoro Crater 

Your Tanzania honeymoon will begin with an early Morning game drive in Serengeti, then transfer (3 hours) to Ngorongoro Conservation Area. 
Later in the Afternoon Enter the crater floor for a game drive—home to the Big Five (lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, rhino) in a lush, volcanic bowl. Picnic lunch by a soda lake. 
Later in the evening Unwind with crater rim views and a couples' massage. Dinner and overnight stay at Baghayo Garden Suites Lodge 

Day 06 : 
Ngorongoro to Zanzibar 

In the morning you will have an Optional early crater drive or relaxation. Then later transfer (2 hours) to Arusha Airport. 
You will have an afternoon flight (1 hour) to Zanzibar and a Private transfer to your resort. 
Later in the evening you will have a Sunset dhow cruise. Dinner and overnight stay at Zanzibar Bay Resort 

Day 07 : 
Zanzibar Beaches & Water Adventures 

In the morning you will experience Snorkeling trip to Mnemba Atoll (colorful reefs, turtles—gear included). 
Later in the afternoon you will go to Lounge on white-sand beaches or try stand-up paddleboarding/kayaking. 
Later in the evening you will have Private beach dinner with Zanzibari spices and live music. Dinner and overnight stay at Zanzibar Bay Resort 

Day 08 : 
Zanzibar Exploration 

In the morning you will have a guided spice tour (1 hour from resort) wander clove plantations, taste exotic fruits, and learn about Swahili flavors (great for couples' photos). 
In the afternoon you will experience Boat excursion to nearby Changuu Island (Prison Island) for giant tortoises and more snorkeling. 
In the evening you have Free time for spa treatments or beach walks; optional couples' cooking class. Dinner and overnight stay at Zanzibar Bay Resort 

Day 09 : 
Departure from Zanzibar 

Morning transfer to Zanzibar Airport (ZNZ) for your flight back.

When is the best time for the 9 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar honeymoon? 

The 9 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar honeymoon runs all year, and the page gives no fixed season. June to October is dry, sunny and good for game viewing and the beach, and January to February is also popular. Tell us your wedding date and we will check weather and lodge availability for those weeks. 

What is included in the 9 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar honeymoon? 

This honeymoon includes all ground transfers, park fees, game drives, the internal flight to Zanzibar, most meals, mid-range accommodation for two, an English-speaking guide, bottled water and Zanzibar health insurance. International flights, tips, premium drinks and optional activities such as the Serengeti balloon ride are extra. 

Where do you stay on this Tanzania safari and Zanzibar honeymoon? 

On this honeymoon you stay at Arusha Green Side Hotel on night 1, Sangaiwe Tented Lodge in Tarangire, 2 nights at Moyo Tented Camp in the Serengeti, Baghayo Garden Suites Lodge near the Ngorongoro Crater and 3 nights at Zanzibar Bay Resort. We confirm every property in your written quote. 

Is the 9 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar honeymoon very tiring? 

No, the 9 day Tanzania safari and Zanzibar honeymoon is a moderate pace for most couples. The longest days are the 4 to 5 hour drive to the Serengeti and the transfer from the crater to Arusha Airport for your flight, and the three Zanzibar days are relaxed, with snorkelling and boat trips you can skip.
