## 
This Kenya turaco birding safari is a grand 19-day tour built around Kenya’s most colourful birds. Turacos are the stars, from Hartlaub’s turaco in the highlands to great blue and Ross’s turacos in the west. 
The route also covers Ol Pejeta, Samburu, five Rift Valley lakes, Saiwa Swamp, Kakamega, Lake Victoria, the Masai Mara and Amboseli. 

## Why choose this Kenya turaco birding safari? 
- Turacos: Hartlaub’s, great blue, Ross’s and black-billed turacos. 
- Saiwa Swamp: Kenya’s smallest national park, with crowned cranes and Ludher’s bush-shrike. 
- Kakamega: grey parrot and Turner’s eremomela. 
- Rift Valley lakes: Nakuru, Bogoria, Baringo and Naivasha with Hell’s Gate. 
- Masai Mara and Amboseli: big cats and elephants to finish. 

## Where you stay 
Nairobi Safari Club, Sweetwaters Tented Camp, Sarova Shaba Lodge, Naro Moru River Lodge, Lake Nakuru Lodge, Lake Bogoria Hotel, Lake Baringo Club, a Kitale hotel, Kakamega Golf Hotel, Sunset Hotel, Lake Naivasha Country Club, Mara Sopa Lodge and Ol Tukai Lodge, or similar. 

## Best time for a Kenya turaco birding safari 
Turacos are resident and seen all year. October to March brings migrants, while June to September is drier with excellent game viewing. 
Read our Kakamega Forest guide and Lake Bogoria guide , or browse all birding safaris . Nature Kenya works to protect Saiwa Swamp and Kakamega.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Nairobi 

Airport pick-up, a short city orientation and an introduction to Kenya's birds at the National Museum. Overnight at Nairobi Safari Club. 

Day 2 : 
Nairobi to Ol Pejeta 

Drive to Ol Pejeta and visit the Sweetwaters chimpanzee sanctuary. Overnight at Sweetwaters Tented Camp. 

Day 3 : 
Ol Pejeta to Samburu 

Morning game drive at Ol Pejeta, then north to Sarova Shaba Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Samburu and Shaba 

A full day of birding and game viewing in the dry north. 

Day 5 : 
Buffalo Springs to Naro Moru 

Morning in Buffalo Springs, a stop at Meru Forest, then Naro Moru River Lodge. 

Day 6 : 
Mount Kenya to Lake Nakuru 

Mount Kenya forest for Mackinder's eagle-owl, then drive to Lake Nakuru Lodge. 

Day 7 : 
Lake Nakuru to Lake Bogoria 

Morning at Nakuru, then on to Lake Bogoria Hotel for the hot springs. 

Day 8 : 
Lake Bogoria to Lake Baringo 

Bird walk and a boat ride at Lake Baringo. Overnight at Lake Baringo Club. 

Day 9 : 
Lake Baringo to Saiwa Swamp 

Drive west to Saiwa Swamp for an afternoon walk. Overnight in Kitale. 

Day 10 : 
Saiwa Swamp to Kakamega 

Morning at Saiwa, then a full afternoon in Kakamega Forest. Overnight at Kakamega Golf Hotel. 

Day 11 : 
Kakamega to Lake Victoria 

Morning forest walk, then lakeside birding in Kisumu. Overnight at Sunset Hotel. 

Day 12 : 
Kisumu to Lake Naivasha 

Drive to Lake Naivasha with a boat ride and a walk on Crescent Island. Overnight at Lake Naivasha Country Club. 

Day 13 : 
Lake Naivasha and Hell's Gate 

Shoreline birding in the morning and Hell's Gate in the afternoon. 

Day 14 : 
Lake Naivasha to the Masai Mara 

Drive to the Mara with an afternoon game drive. Overnight at Mara Sopa Lodge. 

Day 15 : 
Masai Mara 

Birding and game drives with a picnic lunch on the plains. 

Day 16 : 
Masai Mara 

Another full day in the reserve. 

Day 17 : 
Masai Mara to Amboseli 

Drive via Nairobi to Amboseli. Overnight at Ol Tukai Lodge. 

Day 18 : 
Amboseli National Park 

A full day of birding and Kilimanjaro photography. 

Day 19 : 
Amboseli to Nairobi 

Drive to Nairobi for lunch and a farewell dinner, then your flight.

Which turacos can I see? 

Hartlaub’s turaco in the highlands, plus great blue, Ross’s and black-billed turacos in western Kenya. 

Where is Saiwa Swamp? 

Near Kitale in western Kenya. It is a small wetland park famous for sitatunga antelope and swamp birds. 

Which lodges are used on the Kenya turaco birding safari? 

Nairobi Safari Club, Sweetwaters Tented Camp, Sarova Shaba Lodge, Naro Moru River Lodge, Lake Nakuru Lodge, Lake Bogoria Hotel, Lake Baringo Club, a Kitale hotel, Kakamega Golf Hotel, Sunset Hotel, Lake Naivasha Country Club, Mara Sopa Lodge and Ol Tukai Lodge, or similar. 

How long is the trip? 

19 days from arrival in Nairobi to departure from Nairobi. 

Is there a chimpanzee visit? 

Yes. Day 2 includes the Sweetwaters chimpanzee sanctuary at Ol Pejeta. 

How many species can I expect? 

Many guests record more than 500 species on this route. 

Can I shorten it? 

Yes. We can end after the Masai Mara for a 16-day version.
