## Overview 
This 10 day Uganda primate and wildlife safari is a private road trip from Entebbe with 2 nights in Kampala, 2 in Murchison Falls, 2 in Kibale, 1 in Queen Elizabeth and 2 in Bwindi, adding a Source of the Nile cruise, Ziwa rhino trekking, chimpanzee and gorilla treks. The drier months, usually June to September and December to February, are best, and the price starts from USD 6,070 per adult. 
It suits travellers with time to see Uganda properly: couples, wildlife photographers and active families with teenagers who want the Nile, the savannah and both great apes in one trip, travelling in an extended Land Cruiser with a pop-up roof on full board. 

## Why choose a 10 day Uganda primate and wildlife safari? 
The 10 days cover five of Uganda’s highlights in one loop: the Source of the Nile at Jinja, white rhinos at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary, the Nile squeezing through the gorge at Murchison Falls, chimpanzees in Kibale and mountain gorillas in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. 
Two nights in Murchison Falls give you a morning game drive and an afternoon Nile boat cruise to the base of the falls, and the route south passes through the Ishasha sector of Queen Elizabeth, known for its tree-climbing lions, so you see far more than the primate parks. 
- Primate lovers: a chimpanzee trek in Kibale and a gorilla trek in Bwindi. 
- Big game fans: rhino trekking at Ziwa and game drives in Murchison Falls and Queen Elizabeth. 
- Active travellers: zip-lining in Mabira Forest and a walk to Griffin Falls. 

## What to expect on the 10 day Uganda primate and wildlife safari 
Day 1 starts with a welcome at Entebbe International Airport and a transfer to Kampala Serena Hotel. On day 2 you leave at about 7:00am for Mabira Forest zip-lining and a nature walk to Griffin Falls, lunch at Jinja Sailing Club and an afternoon boat cruise to the Source of the Nile and the Speke Monument, returning to Kampala Serena Hotel. 
Day 3 is the drive north, with rhino trekking at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary and a stop at the Top of Murchison Falls, staying at Twiga Safari Lodge. Day 4 has a sunrise game drive and an afternoon Nile boat cruise to the base of the falls, with the night at Murchison River Lodge. On day 5 you drive to Kibale National Park, staying 2 nights at Turaco TreeTops Lodge. 
Day 6 is chimpanzee trekking in Kibale (2 to 4 hours) and a Bigodi Wetland walk. On day 7 you drive to Queen Elizabeth National Park for a Kazinga Channel boat cruise, staying at Ihamba Lakeside Safari Lodge. Day 8 starts with a game drive on the Kasenyi Plains, then you drive south through Ishasha to Bwindi, staying 2 nights at Mahogany Springs Lodge. Day 9 is the gorilla trek (2 to 6 hours) and a Batwa cultural visit, and on day 10 you drive back to Entebbe via the Uganda Equator. 

### Is 10 days enough for a Uganda primate and wildlife safari? 
Ten days is enough to see Uganda’s main highlights by road without rushing, because each primate trek and the Murchison Falls game drive gets a full day. The longest drives are on days 3, 5 and 10, so expect some long road days. See how the parks compare in our best national parks in Uganda guide .

Day 01 : 
Welcome to Uganda The Pearl of Africa Primate and Wildlife Safari 

Upon arrival at Entebbe International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by our company representative or your professional safari driver-guide from Divulge adventures who will brief you about your upcoming Primate and Wildlife Safari adventure. You will then be transferred to Kampala, Uganda’s vibrant capital city, for relaxation after your flight. 
You will have an opportunity to see the views of the bustling city, local markets, and surrounding hills after checking in to your hotel. Dinner and overnight stay at Kampala Serena Hotel. 

Day 02 : 
Transfer to Jinja for Primate and Wildlife Safari 

After an early breakfast at around 7:00am, begin your journey eastwards to Jinja, Uganda’s adventure capital and home to the source of the River Nile. Your first stop will be at Mabira Forest for an exciting zip-lining adventure through the tropical rainforest canopy. 
Enjoy breathtaking aerial views while gliding above ancient trees and listening to sounds of birds and primates hidden within the forest. After the zip-lining experience, proceed on a guided nature walk to the beautiful Griffin Falls where you will learn about medicinal plants, birdlife, butterflies, and local ecosystems. 
Continue to Jinja for lunch at the famous Jinja Sailing Club overlooking the Nile River. In the afternoon at about 2:00pm, enjoy a relaxing boat cruise to the legendary Source of the Nile. During the cruise, visit the eastern and western points of the Nile source and the historic John Speke Monument, where explorer John Hanning Speke identified the source of the Nile. 
You will also have an opportunity to shop for locally made African crafts and souvenirs before returning to Kampala in the evening for dinner and overnight stay at Kampala Serena Hotel. . 

Day 03 : 
Transfer to Murchison Falls National Park 

Wake up early for breakfast and depart Kampala at approximately 7:00am for a scenic journey for primate and Wildlife Safari to Murchison Falls National Park, Uganda’s largest and oldest national park. Along the way, stop at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary for an exciting rhino trekking experience. 
Accompanied by trained rangers, walk through the sanctuary to observe the endangered white rhinos in their natural habitat. After lunch at the sanctuary restaurant, continue your journey northwards through beautiful countryside and local villages. 
Before reaching the lodge, visit the spectacular Top of Murchison Falls where the mighty River Nile forcefully squeezes through a narrow 7-meter gorge before plunging dramatically below. The thunderous roar and misty surroundings create one of Africa’s most breathtaking natural wonders. 
Dinner and overnight stay at Twiga Safari Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Morning Game Drive 

Have an early morning breakfast before heading out for an unforgettable sunrise primate and wildlife safari drive across the savannah plains of Murchison Falls National Park. During the game drive, look out for wildlife including lions, elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, leopards, hyenas, warthogs, Uganda kobs, waterbucks, bushbucks, oribis, topis, and numerous bird species. 
Morning hours are the best time to spot predators before they retreat from the heat. Return to the lodge for relaxation and lunch. 
In the afternoon, enjoy a scenic boat cruise along the River Nile towards the base of Murchison Falls. This cruise offers excellent opportunities to see large schools of hippos, Nile crocodiles basking along the riverbanks, elephants drinking at the shoreline, and countless bird species including the African fish eagle and kingfishers. 
As the boat approaches the falls, witness the dramatic power of the Nile as it crashes through the narrow gorge. Dinner and overnight stay at Murchison River Lodge . 

Day 05 : 
Transfer to Kibale National Park 

After breakfast, enjoy another brief stop at the top of the falls for photography and panoramic views before departing for Kibale National Park, known as the primate capital of the world. The journey takes you through scenic landscapes, tea plantations, traditional villages, and rolling hills of western Uganda. 
Enjoy lunch en route before continuing to Kibale Forest where you will check into your lodge surrounded by lush tropical rainforest. Dinner and overnight stay at Turaco treetops 

Day 06 : 
Chimpanzee Trekking & Bigodi Wetland Nature Walk 

After breakfast, head to the park headquarters for a briefing from Uganda Wildlife Authority guides about chimpanzee trekking rules and safety guidelines. Enter the dense rainforest in search of habituated chimpanzees. The trek may last between 2–4 hours depending on the movement of the chimpanzees. 
Along the way, encounter other primates such as vervet monkeys, black-and-white colobus monkeys, olive baboons, red-tailed monkeys, and grey-cheeked mangabeys. Once located, spend one magical hour observing the chimpanzees as they feed, groom, communicate, and swing through the forest canopy. Return to the lodge for lunch and relaxation. 
Later in the afternoon, proceed for a guided nature walk in Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary, a community-based ecotourism project rich in biodiversity. The wetland is home to over 138 bird species and several monkey species. This experience also supports local community conservation initiatives. Dinner and overnight stay at Turaco treetops. 

Day 07 : 
Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park & Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise 

After a relaxed breakfast, depart for Queen Elizabeth National Park, Uganda’s most popular savannah park famous for diverse ecosystems and abundant wildlife. Arrive in time for lunch at your lodge before preparing for an afternoon boat cruise on the famous Kazinga Channel. The Kazinga Channel is renowned for having one of the highest concentrations of hippos in Africa. 
During the cruise, spot elephants, buffaloes, crocodiles, water birds, and numerous animals gathering along the shoreline to drink water. This is one of the best wildlife viewing experiences in Uganda and offers excellent photography opportunities. 
Dinner and overnight stay at Ihamba Lakeside Safari lodge . 

Day 08 : 
Morning Game Drive & Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park 

Begin the day with an early morning game drive through the Kasenyi Plains and Queen’s Mile areas where chances of spotting wildlife are highest. Look out for lions, elephants, buffaloes, Uganda kobs, hyenas, waterbucks, warthogs, and numerous bird species as the park awakens. 
After the game drive, continue your journey southwards through the Ishasha sector, famous for its rare tree-climbing lions which are often seen resting in fig trees. Proceed to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, home to nearly half of the world’s remaining mountain gorillas. Dinner and overnight stay at Mahogany Springs Lodge 

Day 09 : 
Gorilla Trekking & Batwa Cultural Experience 

After an early breakfast, report to the park headquarters for a detailed briefing by Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers about gorilla trekking rules and conservation efforts. You will then venture deep into the rainforest in search of a habituated gorilla family. The trek can take between 2–6 hours depending on the movement of the gorillas. 
Once found, spend one unforgettable hour observing these gentle giants as they interact, feed, and care for their young in their natural habitat. This is one of the most extraordinary wildlife encounters in the world.After the trek, enjoy a cultural experience with the indigenous Batwa community where you will learn about their traditional forest lifestyle, music, dances, and survival skills. 
Dinner and overnight stay at Mahogany Springs Lodge . 

Day 10 : 
Transfer Back to Entebbe Airport 

Wake up to a relaxed morning breakfast before checking out and beginning your return journey to Entebbe. Drive through the scenic landscapes of southwestern Uganda with an en-route lunch stop in Mbarara. You will also make a memorable stop at the Uganda Equator for photographs, souvenir shopping, and experiments demonstrating the equator line. 
Continue to Entebbe International Airport for your departure flight back home or to your hotel marking the end of your unforgettable Uganda safari experience.

When is the best time for the 10 day Uganda primate and wildlife safari? 

The best time for this 10 day Uganda primate and wildlife safari is usually the drier months, June to September and December to February, when forest trails are firmer and game viewing in Murchison Falls and Queen Elizabeth is easier. The safari runs all year, but April, May, October and November are wetter, so treks can be muddier.

Is the 10 day Uganda primate and wildlife safari suitable for families? 

The 10 day Uganda primate and wildlife safari suits families with teenagers, because Uganda sets a minimum age of 15 for gorilla trekking and 12 for chimpanzee trekking. Zip-lining, rhino trekking and the treks need reasonable fitness, and several drives are long. Tell us your group’s ages and we can suggest swaps for younger children.
