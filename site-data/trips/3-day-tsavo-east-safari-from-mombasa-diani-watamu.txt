## 
This Tsavo East safari is the easiest way to add real wildlife to a beach holiday on the Kenya coast. We collect you from your hotel in Mombasa, Diani, Watamu or Malindi and drive to Tsavo East National Park, one of Kenya’s largest parks, famous for elephants coloured red by its dust, big lion prides and wide open plains. You stay two nights at Voi Wildlife Lodge, whose waterhole draws elephants right past the rooms, and explore Aruba Dam, Mudanda Rock and Lugard Falls on the Galana River before returning to the beach. 
Price: from USD 930 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Pick-up from any beach hotel on the south or north coast 
- Red elephants, lions and big herds on the Tsavo plains 
- Aruba Dam, Mudanda Rock and Lugard Falls 
- Two nights at Voi Wildlife Lodge with a floodlit waterhole 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Coast to Tsavo East 

Early pick-up from your hotel and drive inland to the Buchuma or Bachuma gate of Tsavo East National Park. Game drive to the lodge. Afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight at Voi Wildlife Lodge. 

Day 2 : 
Tsavo East National Park 

Full day of game drives with a picnic lunch, visiting Aruba Dam, Mudanda Rock and the Galana River at Lugard Falls, looking for elephants, lions, gerenuk and fringe-eared oryx. Dinner and overnight at Voi Wildlife Lodge. 

Day 3 : 
Tsavo East to the coast 

Morning game drive, then drive back to your hotel on the coast, arriving in the afternoon.

How much does this Tsavo East safari from Mombasa cost? 

It starts from USD 930 per adult sharing with two people in a private vehicle, based on low-season rates. We confirm the price for your dates and pick-up point when you enquire. 

How far is Tsavo East from the coast? 

About three hours by road from Mombasa and Diani, and about three and a half hours from Watamu and Malindi. 

Can we do it in one or two days? 

Yes, we also run one and two day trips, but three days gives you a full day in the park without long drives on both ends.
