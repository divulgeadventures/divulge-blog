## Overview 
This 4 day Naivasha Nakuru and Masai Mara safari is a short private road safari from Nairobi: Hell’s Gate and a Lake Naivasha boat ride on day 1, 1 night at Lake Nakuru and 2 nights in the Masai Mara. The price starts from USD 1,627 per adult, and the Great Migration is usually in the Mara from July to September. It suits travellers with limited time who want variety. 
The safari travels by 4×4 Land Cruiser with a driver guide, on full board, staying at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge and PrideInn Mara Camp, and ends with a Giraffe Centre visit in Nairobi before your flight. 

## Why choose a 4 day Naivasha Nakuru and Masai Mara safari? 
The 4 day Naivasha Nakuru and Masai Mara safari packs three very different experiences into a long weekend. You cycle and walk among zebras and giraffes in Hell’s Gate, cruise Lake Naivasha to Crescent Island, look for rhinos at Lake Nakuru and then spend a full day in the Masai Mara looking for the Big Five. 
Because it starts and ends in Nairobi with an airport meet-and-greet and drop-off, the trip fits easily before or after a business trip, a beach holiday or a longer East Africa itinerary. 
- Short stays: three parks and a lake in four days. 
- Active travellers: cycling in Hell’s Gate and a walk on Crescent Island. 
- Wildlife lovers: rhinos at Lake Nakuru and a full day in the Masai Mara. 

## What to expect on the 4 day Naivasha Nakuru and Masai Mara safari 
Day 1 is the busiest day. After a meet-and-greet at the airport, you drive about 2 hours to Lake Naivasha, stopping at a Great Rift Valley viewpoint. You cycle and walk the gorges of Hell’s Gate National Park, take a boat to Crescent Island for a nature walk, then continue to Lake Nakuru National Park for an evening game drive and a night at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge. 
Day 2 begins with an early morning game drive in Lake Nakuru, then you drive to the Masai Mara, arriving for lunch, with an afternoon visit to a Maasai village. Day 3 is a full day of game drives in the Masai Mara National Reserve with a picnic lunch on the plains, staying 2 nights at PrideInn Mara Camp. 
On day 4 you drive about 5 hours back to Nairobi, visit the Giraffe Centre to feed the giraffes and are dropped at the airport for your flight. 

### Is 4 days enough for Naivasha, Nakuru and the Masai Mara? 
Four days is enough for a good first taste of the Rift Valley lakes and the Masai Mara, but it is a fast pace, with a long first day and about 5 hours on the road on the last day. If you can add time, our 9 day Hells Gate to Masai Mara safari follows a similar route more slowly. Read our best time to visit the Masai Mara guide to choose your dates.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Cycle and hike through Hell's Gate, spot rhinos in Nakuru, and end with the ultimate wildlife viewing experience on your thrilling Mara Safari. This journey perfectly sets the stage for your unforgettable Masai Mara Safari adventure. 

Day 01 : 
Nairobi - Hell's Gate - Lake Naivasha - Lake Nakuru Park 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by Divulge Adventures representative for meet, greet and safari briefing. Afterward, you will depart Nairobi for Lake Naivasha, which is approximately 2 hours away. There will be a stop to view the great rift valley scenery as you proceed to Lake Naivasha. 
Upon arrival at Naivasha, you will proceed directly to Hell's Gate National Park for cycling amidst the dramatic cliffs and walk in the famous gorges. Afterward, enjoy a serene boat ride to Crescent Island for a nature walk among the wildlife. 
Following this, you will exit for Lake Nakuru National Park, where you'll enjoy a thrilling evening game drive before settling in for dinner and overnight at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge 

Day 02 : 
Lake Nakuru National Park - Masai Mara National Reserve 

After an early breakfast and a final morning game drive at Nakuru National Park , you will depart for the spectacular Masai Mara for amazing Masai Mara Safari, arriving for lunch. After check-in and a relaxing afternoon, you will head out for an enriching visit to a traditional Maasai village . 
Here, you will immerse yourself in the vibrant culture, witnessing warrior dances and learning about their ancient customs. This cultural encounter is a perfect complement to your authentic Mara Safari experience, before returning for dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp . 

Day 03 : 
Full Day at Masai Mara National Safari 

The highlight of your trip is a full day dedicated to the Masai Mara Safari experience within the spectacular Masai Mara National Reserve . This extensive game viewing allows for thorough exploration, focusing on locating the Big Five, especially the majestic big cats in action. 
You will witness the incredible density of wildlife, including elephants, zebras, giraffes, and hippos, potentially seeing crocodiles feeding at the Mara River. A delightful picnic lunch will be served amid the plains. For an unforgettable perspective, consider an optional hot air balloon safari for a magnificent aerial view. Capture these magical moments during your ultimate Masai Safari. Dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp . 

Day 04 : 
Masai Mara National Reserve - Nairobi - Departure 

After breakfast at your camp, you will checkout and exit Masai Mara Nairobi arriving in the afternoon, approximately 5 hours’ drive. Afterwards, exit Masai Mara Nairobi arriving in the afternoon and proceed to giraffe center visit where you get a chance to feed giraffes with your own hands and later drop off at the airport for your departure flight.

When is the best time for the 4 day Naivasha Nakuru and Masai Mara safari? 

The 4 day Naivasha Nakuru and Masai Mara safari runs all year as a private trip. For the Great Migration in the Masai Mara, travel from July to September, when the herds are usually there. Outside those months the Mara still has lions, elephants and other resident wildlife, and Lake Nakuru and Hell’s Gate are good all year. 

What is included in the 4 day Naivasha Nakuru and Masai Mara safari? 

This 4 day safari includes a 4WD Land Cruiser with a driver guide, 3 nights of accommodation on full board, all park and conservation fees, unlimited game drives on the itinerary, drinking water, the Lake Naivasha boat ride and a Maasai village visit. Flights, visas, insurance, tips, drinks and the balloon safari are extra. 

Where do you stay on the 4 day Naivasha Nakuru and Masai Mara safari? 

On this 4 day safari you stay 1 night at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge and 2 nights at PrideInn Mara Camp in the Masai Mara, on full board. There is no night in Nairobi, because day 4 ends with a drop-off at the airport. We confirm each lodge in your quote. 

Is the 4 day Naivasha Nakuru and Masai Mara safari suitable for children? 

Yes, children can join the 4 day Naivasha Nakuru and Masai Mara safari, and the Hell’s Gate cycling, Giraffe Centre and Maasai village are family favourites. The pace is fast, with a long first day and about 5 hours of driving on day 4, so tell us your children’s ages and we will plan rest stops.
