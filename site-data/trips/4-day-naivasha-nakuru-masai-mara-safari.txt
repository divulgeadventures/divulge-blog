## Overview 
This thrilling 4-day Hell’s Gate, Naivasha, Nakuru & Masai Mara Safari begins with cycling and hiking through Hell’s Gate National Park and a relaxing boat trip on Lake Naivasha. Next, we journey to Lake Nakuru National Park, a renowned sanctuary famous for its rhinos and diverse birdlife. 
The grand finale is an immersive, two-day journey into Kenya’s flagship reserve. You will experience unlimited game drives searching for the Big Five in this comprehensive Hell’s Gate, Naivasha, Nakuru & Masai Mara Safari package. Book this action-packed Hell’s Gate, Naivasha, Nakuru & Masai Mara Safari for an unforgettable Kenyan experience! 

### Safari Highlights 
- Lake Naivasha 
- Hells Gate National Park 
- Boat ride at Lake Naivasha 
- Nature walk at Crescent Island 
- Lake Nakuru National Park 
- Masai Mara National Reserve 
- Masai Village Visit 
- The Rift Valley View Point 

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Cycle and hike through Hell's Gate, spot rhinos in Nakuru, and end with the ultimate wildlife viewing experience on your thrilling Mara Safari. This journey perfectly sets the stage for your unforgettable Masai Mara Safari adventure. 

Day 01 : 
Nairobi - Hell's Gate - Lake Naivasha - Lake Nakuru Park 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by Divulge Adventures representative for meet, greet and safari briefing. Afterward, you will depart Nairobi for Lake Naivasha, which is approximately 2 hours away. There will be a stop to view the great rift valley scenery as you proceed to Lake Naivasha. Upon arrival at Naivasha, you will proceed directly to Hell's Gate National Park for cycling amidst the dramatic cliffs and walk in the famous gorges. Afterward, enjoy a serene boat ride to Crescent Island for a nature walk among the wildlife. Following this, you will exit for Lake Nakuru National Park, where you'll enjoy a thrilling evening game drive before settling in for dinner and overnight at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge 

Day 02 : 
Lake Nakuru National Park - Masai Mara National Reserve 

After an early breakfast and a final morning game drive at Nakuru National Park , you will depart for the spectacular Masai Mara for amazing Masai Mara Safari, arriving for lunch. After check-in and a relaxing afternoon, you will head out for an enriching visit to a traditional Maasai village . Here, you will immerse yourself in the vibrant culture, witnessing warrior dances and learning about their ancient customs. This cultural encounter is a perfect complement to your authentic Mara Safari experience, before returning for dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp . 

Day 03 : 
Full Day at Masai Mara National Safari 

The highlight of your trip is a full day dedicated to the Masai Mara Safari experience within the spectacular Masai Mara National Reserve . This extensive game viewing allows for thorough exploration, focusing on locating the Big Five, especially the majestic big cats in action. 
You will witness the incredible density of wildlife, including elephants, zebras, giraffes, and hippos, potentially seeing crocodiles feeding at the Mara River. A delightful picnic lunch will be served amid the plains. For an unforgettable perspective, consider an optional hot air balloon safari for a magnificent aerial view. Capture these magical moments during your ultimate Masai Safari. Dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp . 

Day 04 : 
Masai Mara National Reserve - Nairobi - Departure 

After breakfast at your camp, you will checkout and exit Masai Mara Nairobi arriving in the afternoon, approximately 5 hours’ drive. Afterwards, exit Masai Mara Nairobi arriving in the afternoon and proceed to giraffe center visit where you get a chance to feed giraffes with your own hands and later drop off at the airport for your departure flight.
