## 
This Kenya birding tour is a complete circuit of the country’s best birding regions. In two weeks you visit papyrus swamps, soda lakes, rainforest, alpine moorland and semi-desert, so your bird list grows every single day. 
The route is especially good in June and July, when the weather is dry, roads are easy and many resident birds are active. It works well at other times of year too. 

## Why choose this Kenya birding tour? 
- Nairobi National Park: Hartlaub’s bustard and white-winged widowbird in sight of the city. 
- Kinangop Plateau: Sharpe’s longclaw, an endemic of the highland grasslands. 
- Lake Baringo: Heuglin’s courser, Jackson’s hornbill and nightjars. 
- Kakamega Forest: three nights for great blue turaco, trogons and broadbills. 
- Mount Kenya and Samburu: moorland birds, then vulturine guineafowl and Somali specials. 

## Where you stay 
We use comfortable lodges close to the birding sites: a Nairobi hotel, Lake Naivasha Country Club, Soi Lodge at Baringo, Rondo Retreat at Kakamega, a tented camp in Kisumu, Lake Nakuru Lodge, Naro Moru River Lodge, Samburu Sopa Lodge and Serena Mountain Lodge, or similar. 

## Best time for a Kenya birding tour 
Birding is good all year. June to September is dry and comfortable. October to April brings migrant birds from Europe and Asia, and the short rains in November make the country green. 
Read our Kakamega Forest guide , see the shorter 5 day Kenya birding safari , or browse all birding safaris . Nature Kenya keeps useful lists of Kenya’s Important Bird Areas.

Day 1 : 
Nairobi National Park 

Pick-up at your hotel for a full day in Nairobi National Park, with lunch at the park restaurant. Look for Hartlaub's bustard, Shelley's francolin and northern pied babbler. Overnight in Nairobi. 

Day 2 : 
Limuru, Kinangop Plateau and Lake Naivasha 

Bird Limuru pond and Gatamaiyu Forest, then search the Kinangop grasslands for Sharpe's longclaw. Continue to Lake Naivasha Country Club. 

Day 3 : 
Lake Naivasha to Lake Baringo 

Morning birding at Naivasha, with an optional boat trip, then drive north to Soi Lodge at Lake Baringo. 

Day 4 : 
Lake Baringo 

A full day at Baringo's lake shore and cliffs for Heuglin's courser, Jackson's hornbill and owls. 

Day 5 : 
Kerio Valley to Kakamega 

Drive west through the Kerio Valley with birding stops and arrive at Rondo Retreat in Kakamega Forest. 

Day 6 : 
Kakamega Forest 

A full day on the forest trails for great blue turaco, bar-tailed trogon and African broadbill, plus colobus monkeys. 

Day 7 : 
Kakamega Forest 

Another day in the forest for flufftails, barbets and the many West African species found here. 

Day 8 : 
Kakamega to Lake Victoria 

Drive to Kisumu and bird the papyrus for papyrus gonolek, papyrus canary and blue-headed coucal. Overnight in a tented camp. 

Day 9 : 
Lake Victoria to Lake Nakuru 

Cross the Rift Valley to Lake Nakuru National Park for flamingos, pelicans and white rhinos. Overnight at Lake Nakuru Lodge. 

Day 10 : 
Lake Nakuru to Mount Kenya 

Morning birding at Nakuru, then drive to Naro Moru River Lodge. Evening search for Mackinder's eagle-owl. 

Day 11 : 
Mount Kenya to Samburu 

Drive up Mount Kenya to around 3,000 m for highland birds, then head north to Samburu Sopa Lodge. 

Day 12 : 
Samburu National Reserve 

A full day in Samburu for vulturine guineafowl, Somali bee-eater and Grevy's zebras. 

Day 13 : 
Samburu to Mount Kenya forest 

Drive back south with birding stops and stay at Serena Mountain Lodge in the forest. 

Day 14 : 
Thika and Nairobi 

Early birding from the lodge, then stops at Wajee and Thika for Hinde's babbler before returning to Nairobi.

How many birds does the Kenya birding tour cover? 

Most groups record 450 to 550 species in 14 days, thanks to the wide range of habitats. 

Which lodges are used? 

A Nairobi hotel, Lake Naivasha Country Club, Soi Lodge, Rondo Retreat, a Kisumu tented camp, Lake Nakuru Lodge, Naro Moru River Lodge, Samburu Sopa Lodge and Serena Mountain Lodge, or similar. 

Do I need to be fit? 

Moderate fitness helps. Forest walks at Kakamega and the drive up Mount Kenya to about 3,000 m are the most demanding parts. 

Is the Naivasha boat trip included? 

It is optional and can be added. It is a great way to see pelicans and water birds. 

Can I join if I am not an expert birder? 

Yes. Your guide helps everyone, and the mix of birds and big game makes it enjoyable for all. 

What is the best month for this tour? 

June and July are dry and pleasant. October to April adds migrant birds. 

Is Samburu safe? 

Yes. Samburu National Reserve is a popular, well-managed reserve and we travel with experienced guides.
