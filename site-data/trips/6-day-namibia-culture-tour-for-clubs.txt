## 
This 6 day Namibia culture tour for clubs is planned for photography clubs, travel societies, alumni and Rotary groups who want more than game drives. Members meet the San, Damara and Himba people, see rock art thousands of years old at Twyfelfontein and track desert-adapted elephants in Damaraland. 

## Why book a 6 day Namibia culture tour for clubs? 
The route travels through some of Namibia’s most remote and least-visited regions. Each day has a strong theme for talks and photos: San traditions in the Erongo Mountains, Damara culture, ancient engravings, desert elephants and the Himba of Kaokoland. 
Small vehicles and guides who know the communities mean the club can ask questions and take portraits respectfully, with time each evening to share images or stories. 

## What does the club itinerary include? 
One night in the Erongo Mountains, two in Damaraland around Twyfelfontein, and two in Kaokoland, with the return to Windhoek on day 6. 

### Is this tour good for photography clubs? 
Yes. Rugged mountains, ancient rock engravings, desert elephants and portraits of the Himba make it one of Namibia’s best photography routes. We plan around early and late light.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Namibia 

Your 6 day namibia culture tour for clubs begins today. Meet our representative on arrival for a briefing on the route through Damaraland and Kaokoland, regions known for dramatic scenery and communities living alongside wildlife. 

Day 2 : 
Windhoek to the Erongo Mountains 

Drive to the Erongo Mountains. After lunch, visit a San living museum to learn about hunting, tracking, fire-making and bush food, and see rock paintings over 2,000 years old. The visit ends with San singing and dancing. 

Day 3 : 
Erongo Mountains to Twyfelfontein 

Drive to Damaraland and visit the Damara Living Museum to learn about the traditional culture of the Damara people. 

Day 4 : 
Desert elephants and Twyfelfontein engravings 

Track desert-adapted elephants along the dry riverbeds of Damaraland, then visit the San rock engravings at Twyfelfontein, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. 

Day 5 : 
Damaraland to Kaokoland 

Drive north through villages and concessions to Kaokoland, with chances to see wildlife on the way. Meet the OvaHimba people, then take a sundowner drive. 

Day 6 : 
Kaokoland to Windhoek 

Drive back to Windhoek for your onward journey. This is the end of your 6 day namibia culture tour for clubs.
