## 
This 8 day Tanzania student study tour takes school and university groups through Tarangire, the Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Crater, then to Lake Eyasi to spend a day with the Hadzabe, one of Africa’s last hunter-gatherer communities. It links ecology, geography and culture in one field trip. 

## What will students learn on this 8 day Tanzania student study tour? 
The parks show the full Serengeti ecosystem: Tarangire’s dry-season elephant herds and baobabs, the migration on the Serengeti plains and the Ngorongoro Crater, a volcanic caldera with its own closed wildlife population and black rhinos. 
The day with the Hadzabe at Lake Eyasi opens discussion about how people have lived from the land for thousands of years and how that way of life is changing. Teachers can set field journals, species counts or photo projects around each stop. 

## How is the study tour planned? 
One night in Arusha on arrival, one in Tarangire, two in the Serengeti, two around the Ngorongoro Crater and a final night back in Arusha after Lake Eyasi. Students travel in 4×4 Land Cruisers with roof hatches and professional guides. 

### Is the tour safe for students? 
Yes. Groups stay at established lodges, travel only with licensed guides and have a safety briefing on arrival. We share a full risk overview with teachers before travel.

Yes. We can add a school visit, a conservation talk or a short community project, or extend the trip to Zanzibar to study Swahili history and coastal ecology. Tell us your learning goals and the students’ ages.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Arusha 

Your 8 day tanzania student study tour begins today. Your group is met at Kilimanjaro International Airport for a welcome and safety briefing, then transferred to the hotel in Arusha to rest. 

Day 2 : 
Arusha to Tarangire National Park 

Drive to Tarangire, known for its large elephant herds and ancient baobab trees. Watch how elephants use the baobabs for water and how wildlife gathers along the Tarangire River. Dinner and overnight at Mamadunia Safari Lodge or similar. 

Day 3 : 
Tarangire to the Serengeti 

Drive to the Serengeti, arriving in time for a half-day game drive across the plains, home to the largest land migration on Earth. Dinner and overnight at Moyo Tented Camp or similar. 

Day 4 : 
Full day in the Serengeti 

A full day of game drives along the migration routes, with your guide explaining how grazers, predators and rainfall shape the ecosystem. Dinner and overnight at Moyo Tented Camp or similar. 

Day 5 : 
Serengeti to the Ngorongoro highlands 

A sunrise game drive when wildlife is most active, then brunch and the drive to the Ngorongoro highlands. Overnight near the crater rim at Country Lodge or similar. 

Day 6 : 
Ngorongoro Crater 

Descend to the crater floor, a UNESCO World Heritage Site with lions, zebras, buffalo and black rhinos in a closed volcanic caldera. Return to the lodge in the late afternoon. 

Day 7 : 
Lake Eyasi and the Hadzabe 

An early start to spend time with the Hadzabe, joining the men on a hunt or the women foraging. Visit Lake Eyasi, then return to Arusha. Dinner and overnight at Tulia Hotel and Spa or similar. 

Day 8 : 
Departure 

After breakfast, transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport for your flight home, or extend the trip to Zanzibar. This is the end of your 8 day tanzania student study tour.

Who is the 8 day Tanzania student study tour designed for? 

The 8 day Tanzania student study tour suits secondary school, college and university groups studying biology, geography, environmental science, anthropology or tourism. We adjust the pace and briefings to the students’ ages and subjects.

Is the Hadzabe visit respectful? 

Yes. Visits are run with local guides who arrange them with the community. Students learn about hunting and foraging, and the visit fee supports the community. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 8 day tanzania student study tour. 

Can parents pay separately for each student? 

Yes. Each family can pay their share directly. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the group’s places and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 8 day tanzania student study tour. 

When is the best time for a school trip to Tanzania? 

June to October is dry and suits northern-hemisphere summer holidays. January to February brings the calving season in the southern Serengeti. We work around your term dates. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 8 day tanzania student study tour.
