## Overview 
This 8 day midrange Tanzania safari is a 7-night northern Tanzania lodge safari from Kilimanjaro International Airport. You game drive in Tarangire, spend 2 nights in the Serengeti, tour the Ngorongoro Crater and spend a morning with the Hadzabe at Lake Eyasi, staying in mid-range lodges on full board. The page gives no fixed season, and it costs from USD 2,725 per adult. 
It suits first-time visitors, couples and families who want the classic northern parks plus a cultural day, at a mid-range budget, travelling in a modified 4WD Land Cruiser with a photographic roof hatch and a professional English-speaking guide. 

## Why choose an 8 day midrange Tanzania safari? 
The 8 day midrange Tanzania safari combines three very different wildlife areas with a cultural visit that few northern circuit trips include. Tarangire is known for ancient baobab trees and massive elephant herds that gather along the Tarangire River, especially in the dry season. The Serengeti gets 2 nights at Moyo Tented Camp, with a full day on the migration trails. 
The Ngorongoro Crater is a UNESCO World Heritage Site with lions, zebras, buffalo and the rare black rhino in one enclosed bowl. The trip then ends at Lake Eyasi with the Hadzabe, one of Africa’s last hunter-gatherer communities, before a final night in Arusha. 
- Value seekers: mid-range lodges with full board and park fees included. 
- Culture lovers: a morning with the Hadzabe and a visit to Lake Eyasi. 
- First-timers: Tarangire, the Serengeti and the crater in one trip. 

## What to expect on the 8 day midrange Tanzania safari 
Day 1 starts with a welcome at Kilimanjaro International Airport and a transfer to Green Mountain Hotel in Arusha. On day 2 you drive to Tarangire National Park for game drives among baobabs, elephants, zebras and wildebeest, with the night at Mamadunia Safari Lodge. Day 3 is the long drive to Serengeti National Park, with a half-day game drive on the way into the park and the night at Moyo Tented Camp. 
Day 4 is a full day of game drives in the Serengeti, following the migration when the herds are there, with a second night at Moyo Tented Camp. On day 5 you have a sunrise game drive and brunch at camp, then drive towards the Ngorongoro Crater for the night at Country Lodge Karatu. 
Day 6 is the Ngorongoro Crater, with time on the crater rim and a descent to the floor by safari vehicle, then a late afternoon drive to Lake Eyasi Safari Lodge. On day 7 you rise early to join the Hadzabe in their daily hunting or foraging, visit Lake Eyasi and return to Arusha for the night at Tulia Hotel and Spa. Day 8 is your transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport, or the start of a Zanzibar beach extension. 

### Is 8 days enough for the Serengeti, Ngorongoro and Lake Eyasi? 
Eight days are enough for the Serengeti, Ngorongoro and Lake Eyasi if you are happy with 2 Serengeti nights, because Tarangire and the crater each get their own day and Lake Eyasi adds a cultural morning. Day 3 is the longest travel day. Read our Tarangire National Park guide and our Ngorongoro Crater lodges guide , and check the best time to visit Tanzania for your dates. For 3 Serengeti nights and Lake Manyara, see the 8 day Tarangire Manyara Serengeti and Ngorongoro safari .

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
Arrival in Tanzania 

Upon your arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport, your friendly guide will meet you with a big welcome!” From there, you’ll be picked up from the Airport to your hotel, where you can unwind and immerse yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of Africa. Next day marks the beginning of your safari adventure! Diner and overnight at Green Mountain Hotel Arusha 

Day 02 : 
Arusha- Tarangire National Park 

Your adventure of your Tanzania Safari begins with a visit to Tarangire National Park, famous for its massive elephant herds and ancient baobab trees. As you explore, you’ll see elephants using the baobab trunks as water sources and witness wildlife flocking to the Tarangire River, especially during the dry season. 
Be prepared to see large gatherings of elephants, zebras, wildebeest, and more in this picturesque, rugged landscape. Dinner and overnight at Mamadunia Safari Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Tarangire National Park-Serengeti National Park 

Around day time you will arrive at Serengeti National Park. Enjoy an exhilarating half day game drive as you drive to the park’s breathtaking landscapes. Known for its incredible wildlife spectacle, the Serengeti is known for its migration of over 1.5 million wildebeest and countless zebras. 
Witness the raw beauty of nature as these animals bravely cross crocodile-infested waters, giving birth to over 8,000 calves each day. Dinner and overnight at Moyo Tented Camp Serengeti 

Day 04 : 
Serengeti National Park 

Following another delicious African breakfast, dive deeper into the wilderness with a full day of game driving along the Great Migration trails. This is mostly when you visit during migration time. Your knowledgeable driver-guide will share captivating insights into this extraordinary phenomenon, solidifying Tanzania’s reputation as Africa’s best safari destination. 
This UNESCO World Heritage site is alive with the sounds of over six million hooves echoing across the plains, as zebras and Thomson’s gazelles join the throng in search of fresh pastures. Moyo Tented Camp Serengeti 

Day 05 : 
Serengeti National Park-Ngorongoro Crater 

Wake up early to experience the Tanzania Safari with a sunrise game drive, catching the Serengeti’s wildlife at its most active time. Afterward, return to camp for brunch before heading to Ngorongoro Crater. 
This UNESCO World Heritage Site is home to nearly all of East Africa’s wildlife, including the elusive black rhino, you will be able to spot the rhino here. Spend the night near the crater, preparing for tomorrow’s descent. Dinner and overnight at Country Lodge Karatu 

Day 06 : 
Ngorongoro Crater 

Travel to the worldwide known Ngorongoro Crater, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Africa’s greatest wildlife havens. Descend into the crater, home to a rich variety of animals including lions, zebras, buffalo, and the rare black rhino. 
With its stunning scenery and dense concentration of wildlife, the Ngorongoro Crater offers an exceptional Tanzania Safari experience, you will have time to check the crater outside and inside with a safari vehicle Later afternoon drive to the Lodge. Diner and overnight at Lake Eyasi Safari Lodge 

Day 07 : 
Lake Eyasi and The Hadzabe Tribe 

Rise early for an extraordinary day in your Tanzania Safari with the Hadzabe tribe, one of Africa’s last true hunter gatherer communities. Immerse yourself in their daily activities, whether it’s joining the men in hunting or the women in foraging. After this incredible cultural experience, visit the tranquil Lake Eyasi before returning to Arusha city. Dinner and overnight at Tulia Hotel and SPA 

Day 08 : 
Departure 

After breakfast and some leisure time at the lodge, your guide will escort you to Kilimanjaro International Airport markinghte end if your Tanzania Safari for your journey home. If you’re not ready to leave yet, consider extending your adventure with a trip to the pristine beaches of Zanzibar Islands for a relaxing island escape. Please contact us to prepare for you!

The 8 day midrange Tanzania safari runs all year, and the trip page gives no fixed season.
