## 
This Kenya fly-in safari is our most complete fly-in route. It visits Samburu in the north, Ol Pejeta’s rhino sanctuary, the Masai Mara and Amboseli, with two nights in each. 
Flights link the parks, with one short road transfer from Samburu to Ol Pejeta, so you spend your time with the animals. 

## Why choose this Kenya fly-in safari? 
- Samburu: the Special Five and riverside leopards. 
- Ol Pejeta: black and white rhinos in one of Kenya’s top rhino sanctuaries. 
- Masai Mara: big cats and the migration in season. 
- Amboseli: elephants with Kilimanjaro behind them. 

## Where you stay 
Ashnil Samburu Camp, Sweetwaters Serena Camp at Ol Pejeta, Mara Serena Safari Lodge and Ol Tukai Lodge, or similar. 

## Best time for a Kenya fly-in safari 
June to October is dry and excellent, with the migration in the Mara from July. January to March is also very good, with fewer visitors.

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Fly from Nairobi to Samburu 

Morning flight from Wilson Airport to Samburu. Afternoon game drive. Overnight at Ashnil Samburu Camp. 

Day 2 : 
Full day in Samburu 

Morning and afternoon game drives along the Ewaso Ng'iro River. 

Day 3 : 
Samburu to Ol Pejeta 

Road transfer of two to three hours to Ol Pejeta Conservancy, with an afternoon game drive. Overnight at Sweetwaters Serena Camp. 

Day 4 : 
Ol Pejeta Conservancy 

Visit the rhino sanctuary and look for lions and elephants. Optional chimpanzee sanctuary visit. 

Day 5 : 
Fly from Ol Pejeta to the Masai Mara 

Fly to the Mara via Nairobi and enjoy an afternoon game drive. Overnight at Mara Serena Safari Lodge. 

Day 6 : 
Full day in the Masai Mara 

Game drives with a bush lunch. Optional balloon flight or Maasai visit. 

Day 7 : 
Fly from the Masai Mara to Amboseli 

Morning game drive, then fly to Amboseli via Nairobi. Overnight at Ol Tukai Lodge. 

Day 8 : 
Full day in Amboseli 

Sunrise drive for Kilimanjaro, Observation Hill and an afternoon drive. 

Day 9 : 
Fly back to Nairobi 

Early game drive and a flight to Wilson Airport, or a connecting flight to the coast.

How do I travel between the parks? 

By scheduled light aircraft, plus a two to three hour road transfer from Samburu to Ol Pejeta. 

How much luggage can I bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. Ask us if you need extra space. 

Which lodges are used on the Kenya fly-in safari? 

Ashnil Samburu Camp, Sweetwaters Serena Camp, Mara Serena Safari Lodge and Ol Tukai Lodge, or similar. 

Can I start from the coast? 

Yes. We can fly you from Mombasa or Diani to Nairobi to connect with the first flight, and back at the end. 

Is the chimpanzee sanctuary included? 

It is optional at Ol Pejeta and can be added. 

Is this safari good for families? 

Yes. Short flights and two nights in each place make it relaxed for children. 

Is emergency evacuation cover available? 

Yes. We recommend Flying Doctors evacuation cover for every safari and can arrange it for you.
