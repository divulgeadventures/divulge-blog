## 
This fly-in safari from Diani combines Kenya’s two most famous parks with a beach holiday. You fly from Ukunda airstrip to Amboseli for two nights, on to the Masai Mara for two nights, then back to the coast. 
It is the perfect way to add the elephants of Kilimanjaro and the big cats of the Mara to a stay in Diani or Mombasa. 

## Why choose this fly-in safari from Diani? 
- Beach and bush: start and finish at your coast hotel. 
- Amboseli: elephant herds below Mount Kilimanjaro. 
- Masai Mara: lions, cheetahs and the migration in season. 
- All by air: no long road journeys. 

## Where you stay 
Two nights at Ol Tukai Lodge in Amboseli and two nights at Mara Serena Safari Lodge, or similar. 

## Best time for a fly-in safari from Diani 
June to October is dry, with clear skies in Amboseli and the migration in the Mara. January to March is also excellent and quieter. 
See also our Masai Mara and Amboseli fly-in safari from Nairobi, or browse all flight packages . Learn more at Magical Kenya .

Day 1 : 
Fly from Diani to Amboseli 

Transfer from your hotel to Ukunda airstrip and fly to Amboseli. Afternoon game drive. Overnight at Ol Tukai Lodge. 

Day 2 : 
Full day in Amboseli 

Sunrise game drive for Kilimanjaro views and elephant herds, with a second drive in the afternoon. 

Day 3 : 
Fly from Amboseli to the Masai Mara 

Morning game drive, then fly to the Mara via Nairobi. Afternoon game drive. Overnight at Mara Serena Safari Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Full day in the Masai Mara 

Game drives with a picnic lunch. Optional balloon flight or Maasai visit. 

Day 5 : 
Fly back to Diani 

Early game drive, then fly back to the coast and transfer to your hotel.

Where does the fly-in safari from Diani start? 

From Ukunda airstrip near Diani. We can also start from Mombasa airport. 

How do I get from Amboseli to the Mara? 

By light aircraft, usually connecting through Nairobi’s Wilson Airport. 

How much luggage can I bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. Ask us if you need extra space. 

Can I leave luggage at my beach hotel? 

Yes. Most hotels will store large bags while you are on safari. 

Which lodges are used? 

Ol Tukai Lodge in Amboseli and Mara Serena Safari Lodge in the Masai Mara, or similar. 

Can I start from Watamu or Malindi? 

Yes. We can arrange flights from Malindi or a transfer to Mombasa. 

Is emergency evacuation cover available? 

Yes. We recommend Flying Doctors evacuation cover for every safari and can arrange it for you.
