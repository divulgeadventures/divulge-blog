## 
This Masai Mara and Amboseli fly-in safari starts on the plains of the Mara, famous for lions, cheetahs and the migration, then flies you to Amboseli for elephants below Kilimanjaro. 
Flying saves around ten hours on the road, so you spend your time on game drives instead. 

## Why choose this Masai Mara and Amboseli fly-in safari? 
- Two iconic parks: open plains in the Mara and mountain views in Amboseli. 
- Big Five chances: lions, elephants, buffaloes, leopards and sometimes rhinos. 
- Bush lunch in the Mara: a full day out on the plains. 
- Three nights in Amboseli: plenty of chances for a clear Kilimanjaro. 

## Where you stay 
Two nights at Mara Serena Safari Lodge and three nights at Ol Tukai Lodge, or similar. 

## Best time for a Masai Mara and Amboseli fly-in safari 
July to October is ideal for the migration in the Mara and clear days in Amboseli. January to March is also excellent, with fewer visitors. 
See the single-park Amboseli fly-in safari , our Kenya safaris or all flight packages . For travel information, see Magical Kenya .

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Fly from Nairobi to the Masai Mara 

Morning flight from Wilson Airport to the Mara. Afternoon game drive. Overnight at Mara Serena Safari Lodge. 

Day 2 : 
Full day in the Masai Mara 

Morning and afternoon game drives with a bush lunch. Optional balloon flight or Maasai visit. 

Day 3 : 
Fly from the Masai Mara to Amboseli 

Morning game drive, then fly to Amboseli via Nairobi. Afternoon game drive and check-in at Ol Tukai Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Full day in Amboseli 

Sunrise game drive for Kilimanjaro, a climb up Observation Hill and an afternoon drive. 

Day 5 : 
Amboseli swamps and plains 

Game drives across the swamps, the dry lakebed and the plains. Optional Maasai cultural visit. 

Day 6 : 
Fly back to Nairobi 

Early game drive and a flight back to Wilson Airport.

How do I get from the Masai Mara to Amboseli? 

By scheduled light aircraft, usually connecting through Wilson Airport. The whole journey takes about two hours. 

How much luggage can I bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. Ask us if you need extra space. 

Which lodges are used on the Masai Mara and Amboseli fly-in safari? 

Mara Serena Safari Lodge and Ol Tukai Lodge, or similar. 

Is this a good honeymoon safari? 

Yes. Short flights, great lodges and two iconic parks make it a popular honeymoon choice. 

Can I add a beach stay? 

Yes. You can fly from Amboseli or Nairobi to Diani or Zanzibar. 

Will I see the migration? 

If you travel between July and October, there is a good chance of seeing the herds in the Mara. 

Is emergency evacuation cover available? 

Yes. We recommend Flying Doctors evacuation cover for every safari and can arrange it for you.
