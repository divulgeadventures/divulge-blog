## 
This 8 day Tanzania incentive safari rewards high performers with the whole northern circuit: Tarangire, Lake Manyara, three nights in the Serengeti and a day in the Ngorongoro Crater. Comfortable lodges, shared activities such as cycling and canoeing, and a Serengeti sundowner keep the group together. 

## Why choose an 8 day Tanzania incentive safari? 
It mixes game drives with team experiences that feel different from the office: cycling and a canoe ride at Mto wa Mbu, a night game drive at Lake Manyara and a sundowner on the Serengeti plains. Three nights in the Serengeti give time to follow the migration without rushing. 
We can add an awards dinner, a private bush breakfast, branded welcome packs or a meeting session at a lodge. Tell us what your programme needs. 

## What does the incentive safari itinerary include? 
One night at Serval Wildlife, one near Tarangire, one at Lake Manyara, three in the Serengeti and one at the Ngorongoro Crater. Drives are broken up with stops, and light-aircraft flights can replace the longest drives. 

### How large can the group be? 
We plan Land Cruisers and lodges around your numbers. Larger groups can split across two lodges of the same standard and meet for shared dinners and activities. 
See our Tanzania northern circuit guide and our best Serengeti camps . 

## Who is the 8 day tanzania incentive safari best for? 
The 8 day tanzania incentive safari is best for companies rewarding top sales teams, dealers, partners or long-serving staff. We plan the programme with your HR or events team, so the 8 day tanzania incentive safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## Can the 8 day Tanzania incentive safari include flights? 
Yes. Many incentive groups fly from Arusha to the Serengeti to save a day’s driving, or end with a few days in Zanzibar. We can quote both versions so you can compare.

Day 1 : 
Kilimanjaro Airport to Serval Wildlife 

Your 8 day tanzania incentive safari begins today. Meet your guides at Kilimanjaro International Airport and transfer about 2 hours to Serval Wildlife for an up-close wildlife experience. Dinner and overnight at Serval Wildlife Villas. 

Day 2 : 
Serval Wildlife to Tarangire 

Drive about 3 hours to Tarangire for elephants and baobab trees, with a picnic lunch above the Tarangire River. Dinner and overnight at Manyara Secret Lodge or Escarpment Luxury Lodge Manyara. 

Day 3 : 
Mto wa Mbu and Lake Manyara 

Cycle around Mto wa Mbu and visit a Maasai village, then take an evening canoe ride and a night game drive in Lake Manyara National Park. Dinner and overnight at Lake Manyara Kilimamoja Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Lake Manyara to the Serengeti 

Drive about 5 hours through the Ngorongoro Conservation Area to the Serengeti for an afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight at Mbali Mbali Soroi Serengeti Lodge or Melia Serengeti Lodge. 

Day 5 : 
Full day in the Serengeti 

A full day following the migration herds with your guide. Dinner and overnight at Mbali Mbali Soroi Serengeti Lodge or Melia Serengeti Lodge. 

Day 6 : 
Serengeti game drives and sundowner 

Another full day in the Serengeti, ending with a group sundowner on the plains. Dinner and overnight at Mbali Mbali Soroi Serengeti Lodge or Melia Serengeti Lodge. 

Day 7 : 
Serengeti to the Ngorongoro Crater 

Drive about 4 hours to the Ngorongoro Crater for a picnic by a hippo pool and an afternoon game drive on the crater floor. Dinner and overnight at Ngorongoro Oldeani Mountain Lodge or Melia Ngorongoro Crater. 

Day 8 : 
Ngorongoro to Kilimanjaro Airport 

Drive about 4 hours back to Kilimanjaro International Airport for your flight home. This is the end of your 8 day tanzania incentive safari.

What is included in the 8 day Tanzania incentive safari? 

The 8 day Tanzania incentive safari includes a 4×4 Land Cruiser with a guide, 7 nights in lodges on full board, all park fees, cycling, a Maasai village visit, a canoe ride, a night game drive and a Serengeti sundowner. Flights and private events can be added. 

Can we hold an awards dinner on safari? 

Yes. Most lodges can host a private dinner or awards evening, and some can set up a bush dinner. Tell us your programme early so we can confirm it with the lodge. 

Can we fly instead of driving? 

Yes. Light aircraft fly between Arusha, the Serengeti and other airstrips. Flights save time but add cost and limit luggage to about 15 kg in soft bags. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 8 day tanzania incentive safari. 

How far ahead should we book? 

Book 6 to 12 months ahead for June to October, when Serengeti lodges fill early. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the rooms and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. 

Can the safari be combined with a conference in Arusha? 

Yes. Arusha has conference hotels, and we can plan the safari before or after your event, with transfers for the whole group. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 8 day tanzania incentive safari.
