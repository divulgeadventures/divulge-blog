## Overview 
Experience Kenya’s most iconic wildlife spectacle with our 9 Day Hells Gate to Masai Mara Safari. Journey through legendary parks including Lake Naivasha, Lake Nakuru, and the Masai Mara, where millions of wildebeests thunder across the plains. Encounter Africa’s Big Five, dramatic predator action, and breathtaking landscapes. This Wildebeest Migration Safari guarantees unforgettable game drives, cultural encounters, and front-row views of nature’s greatest migration phenomenon—perfect for travelers seeking an extraordinary African adventure. 

### Safari Highlights 
- Hells Gate National Park 
- Lake Naivasha 
- Crescent Island Sanctuary 
- Lake Nakuru National Park 
- Masai Mara National Reserve 

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

The 9 Days Best of Kenya Wildebeest Migration Safari invites you to explore Kenya’s most iconic wildlife destinations, culminating in the spectacular Mara crossings. Enjoy diverse landscapes, rich wildlife encounters, and unforgettable adventures as you follow nature’s greatest show. This Safari promises front-row views of thrilling animal interactions and breathtaking scenery throughout your journey. 

Day 01 : 
Arrival - Lake Naivasha 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by a Divulge Adventures representative for a meet, greet, and safari briefing. Afterward, you will depart Nairobi for Lake Naivasha, which is approximately 2 hours away. There will be a stop to view the Great Rift Valley scenery as you proceed to Lake Naivasha. You will check in for lunch, followed by afternoon relaxation. Later, set out for a boat safari at Lake Naivasha and a nature safari walk at Crescent Island . A boat ride at Lake Naivasha offers a peaceful escape with scenic views, diverse birdlife, and glimpses of hippos. Glide across calm waters, enjoy fresh air, and experience the beauty of nature in this serene Kenyan destination. Later, return to your hotel for dinner and overnight at Lake Naivasha Sopa Resort 

Day 02 : 
Hell's Gate National Park 

After your delicious breakfast, you will proceed to Hell’s Gate National Park, a remarkable destination famed for its dramatic landscapes and thrilling outdoor activities. Unlike many other parks, Hell’s Gate offers a unique opportunity to explore nature on foot or by bicycle, allowing you to ride alongside grazing wildlife such as zebras, gazelles, and warthogs without the confines of a safari vehicle. The park is renowned for its towering cliffs, rugged rock formations, and volcanic scenery that create an awe-inspiring backdrop throughout your adventure. You will also enjoy a guided walk into the stunning gorges, where narrow passageways, steaming vents, and sculpted sandstone walls reveal the area’s incredible geological history. Hell’s Gate promises a refreshing, active, and immersive safari experience like no other. Afterwards, return to the lodge for dinner and overnight at Lake Naivasha Sopa Resort 

Day 03 : 
Lake Naivasha - Lake Nakuru National Park 

After your delicious breakfast, you will check out and proceed to Lake Nakuru National Park, arriving by lunchtime approximately. Check in for lunch followed by afternoon relaxing. Later in the afternoon, we set out for an afternoon game drive at Lake Nakuru National Park. As the sun begins to set, watch for sightings of the Big Four—lion, leopard, rhino, and buffalo—as well as cheetahs, giraffes, and grazing antelope. The golden hour offers perfect photography opportunities and a magical atmosphere. After an exhilarating safari experience, return to the lodge to relax and enjoy a delicious dinner at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Lake Nakuru National Park - Masai Mara National Reserve 

After a steamy, hot, delicious breakfast, set exit Lake Nakuru National Park for the Masai Mara Reserve, approximately a 5-hour drive . Check in at the camp by lunchtime. Later in the afternoon, head for an afternoon game drive. The cooling air stirs wildlife into action—lions begin to hunt, elephants gather near waterholes, and herds of wildebeest move across the plains. The sky transforms into shades of orange and purple, creating a breathtaking backdrop for photography. It’s the perfect time to witness the Mara’s raw beauty and vibrant life come alive at dusk. Later, dinner and overnight at Mara Leisure Camp 

Day 05, 06, 07 : 
Three Full Days Wildebeest Migration Safari Tracking at Masai Mara 

Enjoy three full days of wildebeest migration safari tracking at the world-renowned Maasai Mara National Reserve, giving you plenty of time to explore its stunning landscapes and abundant wildlife. Spot big cats hunting, elephants, buffaloes, zebras, giraffes, wildebeests, hippos, and crocodiles preying on wildebeests crossing the Mara River. Capture lion prides, impalas, topi, gazelles, and diverse bird species on camera. Picnic lunches are served in the vast plains, with an optional balloon safari offering breathtaking aerial views. This unforgettable experience is a key highlight of your Kenya Wildebeest Migration Safari, providing intimate encounters with the epic Kenya Wildebeest Migration Safari. Dinner and overnight at Mara Leisure Camp 

Day 08 : 
Masai mara National Reserve to Nairobi 

Waking up to a melodious bird chirp, you will have a mouthwatering breakfast and check out from the hotel, and head to Nairobi. Arriving in the afternoon, you will spend the evening relaxing, shopping, or having a special dinner as per your own plans. Overnight stay at Argyle Grand Hotel 

Day 09 : 
Departure 

After your breakfast, you will check out of the Hotel. Afterwards, you will be dropped off at the airport for your Outbound Flight.
