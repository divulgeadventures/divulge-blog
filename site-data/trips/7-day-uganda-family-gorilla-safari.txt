## 
This 7 day Uganda family gorilla safari is planned for parents travelling with teenagers. Together you trek chimpanzees in Kibale, cruise the Kazinga Channel in Queen Elizabeth National Park and spend an hour with a mountain gorilla family in Bwindi, with time for village and wetland walks in between. 

## Who is the 7 day Uganda family gorilla safari for? 
Gorilla trekking is only open to people aged 15 and over, and chimpanzee trekking to those aged 12 and over, so this trip suits families with older children. Younger siblings can join the trip and do the Bigodi Wetland walk, the boat cruise and game drives while the others trek. 
Grandparents and extended family often join. Porters can carry bags and help on steep slopes, and rangers try to give less active walkers a gorilla family that is closer. 

## What does the family itinerary include? 
One night in Entebbe, two in Kibale, one in Queen Elizabeth and two in Bwindi, with the return to Entebbe on day 7. 

### Which lodges are used? 
Turaco Treetops Lodge in Kibale, a lodge in Queen Elizabeth, and The Haven Lodge and Gorilla Safari Lodge in Buhoma, or similar. We confirm family rooms and child rates in your quote. 
Read our guides to how hard gorilla trekking is and chimpanzee trekking in Kibale . 

## Who is the 7 day uganda family gorilla safari best for? 
The 7 day uganda family gorilla safari is best for families who want to share a safari, from young children to grandparents. Tell us everyone’s ages and we will match vehicles, rooms and activities to your family, so the 7 day uganda family gorilla safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## Can several families share the 7 day Uganda family gorilla safari? 
Yes. Families often travel together in separate vehicles and meet at the lodges, and each family can pay separately. Tell us the ages of everyone travelling and we will check permits and plan around them.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Entebbe 

Your 7 day uganda family gorilla safari begins today. Meet your driver guide at Entebbe International Airport for a safari briefing, then transfer to your hotel. Welcome dinner and overnight in Entebbe. 

Day 2 : 
Entebbe to Kibale and Bigodi Wetland 

Drive to Kibale National Park. In the afternoon, take a guided walk through the community-run Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary to see monkeys and birds and learn about local coffee and crafts. Dinner and overnight at Turaco Treetops Lodge or similar. 

Day 3 : 
Chimpanzee trekking in Kibale 

Family members aged 12 and over trek with rangers to find chimpanzees and spend an hour watching them feed and play. The afternoon is free at the lodge. Dinner and overnight at Turaco Treetops Lodge or similar. 

Day 4 : 
Kibale to Queen Elizabeth and the Kazinga Channel 

Drive two to three hours past crater lakes to Queen Elizabeth National Park for an afternoon boat cruise on the Kazinga Channel among elephants, buffalo and hippos. 

Day 5 : 
Game drive and transfer to Bwindi 

A morning game drive when wildlife is most active, then continue south to Bwindi. Dinner and overnight at The Haven Lodge in Buhoma or similar. 

Day 6 : 
Gorilla trekking and Batwa visit 

Family members aged 15 and over trek to a mountain gorilla family and spend one hour with them. In the afternoon, everyone visits the Batwa people to learn about their life in the forest. Dinner and overnight at Gorilla Safari Lodge or similar. 

Day 7 : 
Return to Entebbe and departure 

Drive or fly back to Kampala and Entebbe for your onward journey. This is the end of your 7 day uganda family gorilla safari.

What is the minimum age for the 7 day Uganda family gorilla safari? 

On the 7 day Uganda family gorilla safari, gorilla trekking is open to people aged 15 and over and chimpanzee trekking to those aged 12 and over. Younger children can join the rest of the trip and stay with a parent or a guide during the treks. 

How hard is the gorilla trek for families? 

Treks take 1 to 6 hours on steep, muddy trails, depending on where the gorillas are. Porters can carry bags and help you up the slopes, and rangers try to match less active walkers with a closer family. 

Can two families travel together? 

Yes. Families can share a vehicle or use separate vehicles that meet at the lodges. On group bookings the seventh place is free for every six paying travellers. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 7 day uganda family gorilla safari. 

When is the best time for a family gorilla safari? 

June to August and December to February are the driest months for trekking and match most school holidays. Gorillas can be seen all year. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 7 day uganda family gorilla safari. 

How far ahead should we book? 

Book 6 to 12 months ahead, especially for school holidays, so we can secure gorilla permits for the whole family on the same day. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 7 day uganda family gorilla safari.
