## Overview 
This 3 day Nyungwe and Lake Kivu tour is a private trip from Kigali with 1 night at Nyungwe Forest National Park and 1 night at Kibuye on Lake Kivu. It includes the canopy walk, a chimpanzee trek, a tea plantation visit and a Lake Kivu boat trip, on full board. The drier months are June to September and December to February, and the price starts from USD 1,520 per adult. 
It suits travellers who want rainforest adventure and lake relaxation in a short break, including couples, friends and visitors adding a primate trip to time in Kigali. 

## Why choose a 3 day Nyungwe and Lake Kivu tour? 
Nyungwe Forest National Park is a large montane rainforest in southwest Rwanda and the country’s main place to track wild chimpanzees. Pairing it with Lake Kivu turns a long drive into a balanced short break, with a forest trek one morning and a swim off an island the next. 
- Primate lovers: one hour with a habituated chimpanzee community, permit included. 
- Adventure seekers: the suspended canopy walk high above the Nyungwe forest. 
- Relaxation: sunset at Kibuye and a boat trip to Napoleon and Amahoro islands. 

## What to expect on the 3 day Nyungwe and Lake Kivu tour 
Day 1 starts with pick-up in Kigali and a drive of about 5 to 6 hours to Nyungwe Forest National Park, past tea estates and terraced hills. In the afternoon you walk the canopy walkway at Uwinka, then spend the night at Nyungwe Top View Hill Hotel or Gisakura Guest House. 
Day 2 begins before dawn with a packed breakfast and a chimpanzee trek with a ranger guide, followed by a tea plantation visit and tasting. You then drive about 3 hours north to Kibuye on Lake Kivu for sunset, staying at Cormoran Lodge or Rwiza Village. On day 3 a morning boat trip visits Napoleon Island, home to a large fruit bat colony, and Amahoro Island for a swim, before lunch and a drive of about 3 hours back to Kigali. 

### When is the best time for a Nyungwe and Lake Kivu trip? 
Nyungwe chimpanzee trekking runs all year, but the drier months of June to September and December to February usually bring easier forest trails. Lake Kivu is pleasant for swimming all year. See what the dry season is like in our Rwanda in January guide .

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is how your 3-day Nyungwe and Lake Kivu tour unfolds. 

Day 01 : 
Kigali - Nyungwe Forest National Park (Canopy Walk) 

After pick-up from your Kigali hotel or the airport, you will begin your Nyungwe and Lake Kivu tour by setting off to Nyungwe Forest National Park, approximately 5 to 6 hours away, passing tea estates and terraced hills with a lunch stop en route. 
In the afternoon, head to the Uwinka area for the canopy walk, a suspended bridge high above the forest that gives you incredible views of the rainforest canopy, tree ferns and birdlife. Check in at your lodge for a relaxing evening surrounded by forest sounds. Dinner and overnight at Nyungwe Top View Hill Hotel | Gisakura Guest House 

Day 02 : 
Chimpanzee Trekking - Tea Plantation - Lake Kivu (Kibuye) 

Rise before dawn with a packed breakfast and set out on your chimpanzee trek, following your ranger guide through the forest to find a habituated community. Spend one hour watching the chimps feed, call and move through the trees. 
Afterwards, visit a local tea plantation to learn how tea is picked and processed, and enjoy a tasting of fresh Rwandan tea. In the afternoon, drive north to Kibuye on the shores of Lake Kivu, approximately 3 hours, arriving in time for sunset over the lake. Dinner and overnight at Cormoran Lodge | Rwiza Village 

Day 03 : 
Lake Kivu Boat Trip - Kigali 

After breakfast, board a boat for a morning cruise along Lake Kivu's shoreline, visiting islands such as Napoleon Island, home to a large colony of fruit bats, and Amahoro Island, a lovely spot for a swim. Return to the shore for lunch and then drive back to Kigali, approximately 3 hours, for drop-off at your hotel or Kigali International Airport. 
Marking the end of your Nyungwe and Lake Kivu tour in Rwanda

## FAQs

When is the best time for the 3 day Nyungwe and Lake Kivu tour? 

The best time for the 3 day Nyungwe and Lake Kivu tour is the drier season, June to September and December to February, when Nyungwe’s forest trails are easier. Chimpanzee trekking runs all year, and Lake Kivu is pleasant for a swim in every season. 

What is included in the 3 day Nyungwe and Lake Kivu tour? 

The 3 day Nyungwe and Lake Kivu tour includes a private 4×4 with a driver guide, 2 nights on full board, Nyungwe park fees, the chimpanzee permit, the canopy walk, a tea plantation visit, the Lake Kivu boat trip and drinking water. Flights, visas, insurance, porters, tips and drinks are extra. 

Where do you stay on this Nyungwe and Lake Kivu trip? 

On this Nyungwe and Lake Kivu trip you stay 1 night at Nyungwe Top View Hill Hotel or Gisakura Guest House near the forest, and 1 night at Cormoran Lodge or Rwiza Village in Kibuye on Lake Kivu. We confirm each lodge in your quote, based on availability for your dates. 

Can you swim in Lake Kivu on this tour? 

Yes, you can swim in Lake Kivu on this tour. The lake is free of bilharzia and crocodiles, so swimming is safe at hotel beaches and islands such as Amahoro Island, which the boat trip visits on day 3 before you drive back to Kigali.
