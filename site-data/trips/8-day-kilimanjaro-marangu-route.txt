## 
This 8 day Kilimanjaro Marangu route is a 6-day climb to Uhuru Peak (5,895 m) on Kilimanjaro’s classic hut route, starting and ending at Kilimanjaro International Airport. You sleep in dormitory huts at Mandara, Horombo and Kibo, spend an extra day at Horombo to acclimatise, and return the same way. Hotel nights in Moshi are included before and after. 
It suits first-time high-altitude trekkers who prefer a bed in a hut to a tent. 

## Why choose the Marangu route? 
Marangu is Kilimanjaro’s oldest route and the only one with huts. The gradient is steady and the path is well made, so it feels gentler than the camping routes. The extra day at Horombo makes a real difference, because the standard 5-day Marangu climb has a lower summit success rate. 

## What to expect on the 8 day Kilimanjaro Marangu route 
Day 2 walks through rainforest to Mandara Hut (about 2,720 m), with an optional visit to Maundi Crater. Day 3 crosses the moorland to Horombo Hut (about 3,720 m), and day 4 is the acclimatisation day with a hike towards Zebra Rocks or the Mawenzi saddle. Day 5 crosses the alpine desert to Kibo Hut (about 4,700 m). Summit night begins around midnight on day 6, via Gilman’s Point to Uhuru Peak, before descending to Horombo. Day 7 returns to Marangu Gate. 

### When is the best time to climb? 
January to mid-March and June to October are the driest months. See our best time to climb Kilimanjaro and Kilimanjaro routes guides.

Day 1 : 
Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport 

Transfer to your hotel in Moshi for a briefing and gear check with your guide. 

Day 2 : 
Marangu Gate to Mandara Hut 

Drive to Marangu Gate (about 1,860 m) and walk about 8 km (3 to 4 hours) through rainforest to Mandara Hut at about 2,720 m. Optional short walk to Maundi Crater. 

Day 3 : 
Mandara Hut to Horombo Hut 

Walk about 12 km (5 to 6 hours) across the moorland, with views of Kibo and Mawenzi, to Horombo Hut at about 3,720 m. 

Day 4 : 
Acclimatisation day at Horombo 

Hike towards Zebra Rocks or the Mawenzi saddle to gain height, then return to Horombo Hut to sleep lower. 

Day 5 : 
Horombo Hut to Kibo Hut 

Cross the alpine desert of the saddle, about 10 km (5 to 6 hours), to Kibo Hut at about 4,700 m. Early dinner and rest. 

Day 6 : 
Summit night: Uhuru Peak, then Horombo 

Leave around midnight, reach Gilman's Point on the crater rim and continue to Uhuru Peak (5,895 m) at dawn. Descend to Horombo Hut, 11 to 15 hours in total. 

Day 7 : 
Horombo Hut to Marangu Gate and Moshi 

Descend through Mandara to Marangu Gate, collect your summit certificate and transfer to Moshi. 

Day 8 : 
Departure 

Transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport, or continue on safari.

Is the 8 day Kilimanjaro Marangu route good for beginners? 

Yes. the 8 day Kilimanjaro Marangu route is a good choice for first-time high-altitude trekkers. The path is steady, you sleep in huts, and the extra night at Horombo improves your chances of reaching the summit. 

Why add an acclimatisation day on Marangu? 

The standard 5-day Marangu climb gains height quickly, which lowers summit success. An extra day at Horombo (about 3,720 m), with a short hike higher, gives your body time to adjust before Kibo Hut and summit night. 

What are the huts like? 

The Marangu huts are simple shared dormitories with bunk beds and mattresses, plus a dining hall. You still need a warm sleeping bag. Toilets are basic but there are flush toilets at some huts. 

When is the best time to climb the Marangu route? 

The best months are January to mid-March and June to October. Marangu is also a good choice in the rainy months because you sleep under a roof. 

Can a group climb the Marangu route together? 

Yes. Marangu is popular with groups, schools and charity climbs. We book huts early and add guides so the group can climb at different speeds on summit night.
