## Golden monkey Trekking and bisoke hike. 
This golden monkey and Bisoke hike tour is a fantastic Volcanoes National Park adventure without the cost of a gorilla permit. You will start with a Kigali city tour and a visit to the Kigali Genocide Memorial, then head north to Musanze. 
Day two is all about the Mount Bisoke hike , climbing through bamboo and moss-covered forest to a stunning crater lake on the summit. On your final morning, you will trek to a troop of playful golden monkeys before returning to Kigali. 

## How much does the golden monkey and Bisoke hike cost? 

Price on request. The cost depends on your travel dates, group size, the season and the lodges you choose, so we prepare every quote personally rather than showing one fixed price.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

3-day golden monkey and Bisoke hike tour day by day. 

Day 01 : 
Kigali City Tour - Musanze 

You will be picked up from your Kigali hotel or the airport by your Divulge Adventures driver guide for your golden monkey and Bisoke hike tour briefing. Begin with a Kigali city tour, including a moving visit to the Kigali Genocide Memorial, which tells the story of the 1994 genocide and Rwanda's journey of reconciliation. 
Continue through the city's clean, hilly streets and markets before driving north to Musanze, approximately 2.5 hours, through stunning terraced landscapes. Check in at your lodge and enjoy a free evening with views of the volcanoes. Dinner and overnight at Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge | Kinigi Guest House 

Day 02 : 
Mount Bisoke Hike 

After an early breakfast, drive to the Kinigi park headquarters for the 7:00 AM briefing, then continue to the trailhead. Your guided Mount Bisoke hike climbs through bamboo and then Hagenia and Hypericum forest draped in moss, with chances to spot birds and signs of forest elephants and buffaloes. 
After about 4 hours, reach the summit and its beautiful crater lake, where you will enjoy a picnic lunch and, on clear days, views across the Virunga range inside Volcanoes National Park . Descend in 2 to 3 hours and return to the lodge for a hot shower and a well-earned rest. Dinner and overnight at Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge | Kinigi Guest House 

Day 03 : 
Golden Monkey Trekking - Kigali 

After an early breakfast, return to the park headquarters for your golden monkey trek. Hike into the bamboo zone with your ranger guide and spend one hour with these bright-orange monkeys as they feed and leap through the stems. 
Return to the lodge for an early lunch and check out, then drive back to Kigali, approximately 2.5 hours, for drop-off at your hotel or Kigali International Airport. Marking your 3-day Golden Monkey and Bisoke hike at the Volcanoes National Park.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

Can I visit Volcanoes National Park without a gorilla permit? 

Yes. You can hike Mount Bisoke, trek golden monkeys, visit Dian Fossey’s grave or explore the caves and cultural villages, all for much less than a gorilla permit. 

How long does the Bisoke hike take? 

Most hikers take about 4 hours up and 2 to 3 hours down, so plan for a full day. 

Is this tour suitable for beginners? 

The golden monkey trek is easy, but the Bisoke hike is steep and muddy in places. Beginners with reasonable fitness can manage it with a porter and a slow pace. 

Can I add gorilla trekking to this tour? 

Yes. Divulge Adventures can add a gorilla trek on an extra day, subject to permit availability.
