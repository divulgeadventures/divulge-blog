## Overview 
This full day corporate team building at Burudani Adventure Park is a guided company day out in Limuru, outside Nairobi, running from 08:30 arrival to 18:00 departure. It includes round transport, morning tea, a facilitated team building session, a Zumba class, a buffet lunch, photography and certificates. High ropes, ziplining and sky cycling cost extra, and the price is on request. It suits corporate teams and staff retreats. 

## Why choose a full day corporate team building at Burudani Adventure Park? 
Burudani Adventure Park gives a team a full day of structured activities without an overnight stay, so it is easy to fit into a working week. The programme mixes a facilitated problem-solving session with a fitness class, a long lunch for networking and an afternoon of optional adventure activities. 
The day is built around clear team goals: better communication, trust, creativity and morale. A facilitator runs the sessions and a photographer records the day, and every participant receives a certificate of participation. 
- HR and team leads: a facilitated programme with a recap of key takeaways at the end. 
- Mixed-fitness groups: the core activities are ground-based, and the high ropes are optional. 
- Nairobi offices: round transport is included, so nobody needs to drive. 

## What to expect on the full day corporate team building at Burudani Adventure Park 
The group arrives at Burudani Adventure Park in Limuru at 08:30 and starts with morning tea, pastries and fresh fruit until 10:00. From 10:00 to 13:00 a facilitator leads a strategic problem-solving session focused on communication and trust, followed by a Zumba fitness class. 
A buffet lunch of local and international dishes runs from 13:00 to 14:30. From 14:30 to 16:30 the team chooses optional activities such as board games, archery, ziplining, high ropes levels 1 to 3 and sky cycling, while the professional photographer captures the day. 
From 16:30 to 17:30 there are icebreakers and a team building recap, with evening tea. Final refreshments are served before departure between 17:30 and 18:00. 

### Do the high ropes and zipline cost extra? 
The high ropes and zipline are priced separately from the core package. The page lists high ropes levels 1, 2 and 3, ziplining and sky cycling, plus combined options such as high ropes level 1 with the zipline, as excluded from the package cost. Tell us which activities your group wants and we will add them to your quote. Read our Burudani Adventure Park team building guide for more on the park. 

## What is included in the Burudani team building day? 
The package includes round transport, lunch, morning and evening tea, team building props, Zumba and icebreaker activities, facilitation, photography and a certificate of participation for each person. 
Not included are the adventure activities: high ropes levels 1 to 3, ziplining, sky cycling and the combined high ropes and zipline options. Comparing venues? See our team building venues near Nairobi guide and our Nkasiri Adventure Park team building guide .

What is included in the full day corporate team building at Burudani Adventure Park? 

The full day corporate team building at Burudani Adventure Park includes round transport, morning and evening tea, a buffet lunch, team building props, Zumba and icebreaker activities, facilitation, photography and a certificate of participation. High ropes, ziplining and sky cycling are optional extras that we price separately in your quote. 

Where is Burudani Adventure Park and how long is the day? 

Burudani Adventure Park is in Limuru, outside Nairobi. The team building day runs from 08:30 arrival to departure between 17:30 and 18:00, with tea, a facilitated session, Zumba, lunch, optional activities and a recap. Round transport is included, so your team travels together from the office. 

Is the Burudani team building day suitable for all fitness levels? 

Yes, the Burudani team building day suits most fitness levels because the core programme is ground-based: a problem-solving session, Zumba, icebreakers and board games. The high ropes, zipline and sky cycling are optional, so people can choose how active they want to be. Tell us about any special needs and we will plan around them. 

Can we add high ropes and ziplining to the Burudani package? 

Yes, you can add high ropes levels 1 to 3, ziplining, sky cycling or a combined high ropes and zipline option to the Burudani package. These activities are not in the base cost. Tell us which ones your team wants and how many people will take part, and we will include them in your quote.
