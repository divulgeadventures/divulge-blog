## Overview 
Elevate your team’s spirit with a Full Day Corporate Team Building package at Burudani Adventure Park. This immersive experience features high-energy challenges and collaborative activities designed to boost communication, leadership, and morale. Invest in your team’s success with an unforgettable day at Burudani Adventure Park. 

### Team Buidling Highlights 
- Improve productivity 
- Increase communication 
- Foster creativity 
- Develop relationships 
- Enhance engagement 
- Enhance team work & unity 
- Build trust & boost morale 

## Day Corporate Team Building Program 
Expand all 

Unlock your team's potential at Burudani Adventure Park. Our curated program blends exciting challenges and strategic games for maximum team synergy. Strengthen bonds and collaboration with a dynamic day. 

08:30 Am : 
Arrival 

Arrival at Burudani Adventure Park, in Limuru. 

8:40 Am – 10:00 Am : 
Morning Tea & Snacks 

Enjoy a refreshing Morning Tea break with assorted pastries and fresh fruits. This quick refuel ensures energy levels remain high for optimal session focus. 

10:00 Am – 1:00 Pm : 
Team Building & Zumba Fusion 

Combine strategic problem-solving in a team-building session to enhance communication and trust at Burudani Adventure Park. Follow up with an exhilarating Zumba fitness class. This dynamic duo boosts team morale, reduces stress, and promotes healthy synergy for a productive, energized workforce. 

1:00 Pm – 2:30 Pm : 
Buffet Lunch Break 

Indulge in a delicious, varied Buffet Lunch featuring local and international cuisine. This relaxing break allows networking and refuels participants for the afternoon's activities at Burudani Adventure Park. 

2:30 Pm – 4:30 Pm : 
Optional Activities & Photography 

Choose from fun Optional Ground Activities like board games, ziplining, high ropes level 1, 2, and 3, archery, and sky cycling for light engagement. Conclude with a professional Photography Session to capture key moments and achievements, providing lasting memories of your team's successful day. 

4:30 Pm – 5:30 Pm : 
Recap and Evening Tea 

Finalize the day with icebreakers. Conclude with a Team Building Recap summarizing key takeaways, followed by an Optional Evening Tea for final relaxed networking. 

5:30 Pm – 6:00 Pm : 
Refreshments & Departure 

Enjoy final Refreshment moments before preparing for departure. We ensure a smooth and safe exit for all participants, leaving them energized and motivated after a productive team-building day.
