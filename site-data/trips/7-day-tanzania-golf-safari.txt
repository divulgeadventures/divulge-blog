## 
This 7 day Tanzania golf safari pairs two of Tanzania’s most scenic courses with the Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Crater. Your group plays the Kilimanjaro Golf and Wildlife Estate between Mount Kilimanjaro and Mount Meru, then flies to the Serengeti to play the new 18-hole Serengeti Golf Course at Fort Ikoma before game drives and a day in the crater. 

## Why choose a 7 day Tanzania golf safari? 
Few places let you play a round in the morning and go on a game drive in the afternoon. The Serengeti Golf Course, opened by Tanzania National Parks in October 2026, is an 18-hole, par-72 course on the edge of the park, where elephants, giraffes and zebras are often seen around the fairways. 
The trip starts at the Kilimanjaro Golf and Wildlife Estate, an 18-hole championship course about 25 km from Arusha, so you can play on the first full day without a long transfer. 

## What does the golf safari itinerary include? 
Two nights at the Kilimanjaro Golf and Wildlife Estate, three nights near Fort Ikoma in the western Serengeti, and one night on the Ngorongoro Crater rim, with a scheduled flight from Arusha to the Serengeti.

## Who is the 7 day tanzania golf safari best for? 
The 7 day tanzania golf safari is best for golf clubs, groups of golfing friends and companies running a client golf tour. Tell us your handicaps and preferred tee times and we will book every round, so the 7 day tanzania golf safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## Can the 7 day Tanzania golf safari include Zanzibar? 
Yes. Many golf groups add a few days on the beach in Zanzibar after the crater. We can also add a scored competition and a prize-giving dinner for a club or corporate golf tour.

Day 1 : 
Arrival at Kilimanjaro Airport 

Your 7 day tanzania golf safari begins today. Meet your guide at Kilimanjaro International Airport and transfer to the Kilimanjaro Golf and Wildlife Estate, set between Mount Kilimanjaro and Mount Meru. Welcome dinner with the group. 

Day 2 : 
Kilimanjaro Golf and Wildlife Estate 

Play the estate's 18-hole championship course. Non-golfers can choose horse riding or a cultural tour. Free afternoon. 

Day 3 : 
Fly to the Serengeti 

After breakfast, fly from Arusha to the western Serengeti. Afternoon game drive, then check in near Fort Ikoma. 

Day 4 : 
Serengeti Golf Course 

Play the 18-hole Serengeti Golf Course at Fort Ikoma in the cooler morning hours, with wildlife often seen around the fairways. Afternoon game drive. 

Day 5 : 
Full day in the Serengeti 

A full day of game drives looking for lions, cheetahs, elephants and the migration herds, depending on the season. A second round can be played instead of the morning drive. 

Day 6 : 
Serengeti to the Ngorongoro Crater 

Drive through central Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, with game viewing on the way, to a lodge on the crater rim. 

Day 7 : 
Ngorongoro Crater and departure 

Descend into the crater for a morning game drive among lions, buffalo and black rhinos, then drive to Arusha or Kilimanjaro Airport for your flight home. This is the end of your 7 day tanzania golf safari.

Which courses does the 7 day Tanzania golf safari include? 

The 7 day Tanzania golf safari includes the 18-hole Kilimanjaro Golf and Wildlife Estate near Arusha and the 18-hole, par-72 Serengeti Golf Course at Fort Ikoma. A round at the Arusha Gymkhana Club can be added on day 3. 

Is the Serengeti Golf Course inside the national park? 

No. The course is at Fort Ikoma, just outside the Serengeti National Park boundary in a wildlife area that borders the park. Animals move freely around the course, and players must not disturb them. 

Can non-golfers join the trip? 

Yes. Non-golfers can take cultural tours or horse riding on the Kilimanjaro estate and extra game drives in the Serengeti while the group plays. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 7 day tanzania golf safari. 

Can each golfer pay separately? 

Yes. Each golfer can pay their own share. On group bookings the seventh place is free for every six paying travellers, a deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. 

When is the best time for a golf safari in Tanzania? 

June to October is dry and good for both golf and game viewing. The migration herds pass through the western Serengeti around May to July, close to the Fort Ikoma course.
