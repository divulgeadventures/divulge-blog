## 
This Kenya bush and beach safari drives from Nairobi through Tsavo West and Tsavo East to the Indian Ocean at Watamu, so the safari and the beach flow into each other with no backtracking. See Mzima Springs and the rhino sanctuary at Ngulia in Tsavo West, the red elephants of Tsavo East, then relax for three nights at Turtle Bay Beach Club in Watamu. From there you can snorkel in Watamu Marine National Park, kayak in Mida Creek, walk through the Gede Ruins and visit Malindi before flying back to Nairobi. It suits families and couples, and is popular with Italian travellers who know this coast well. 
Price: from USD 1,970 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Mzima Springs and Ngulia Rhino Sanctuary in Tsavo West 
- Red elephants of Tsavo East 
- Three nights on Watamu beach at Turtle Bay Beach Club 
- Snorkelling in Watamu Marine National Park, Mida Creek and Gede Ruins 
- Short flight from Malindi back to Nairobi 

##

Day 1 : 
Nairobi to Tsavo West 

Drive south-east from Nairobi to Tsavo West National Park. Afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight at Kilaguni Serena Safari Lodge. 

Day 2 : 
Tsavo West National Park 

Visit Mzima Springs, where hippos and crocodiles can be seen through clear water, the Shetani lava flow and the Ngulia Rhino Sanctuary. Dinner and overnight at Kilaguni Serena Safari Lodge. 

Day 3 : 
Tsavo West to Tsavo East 

Drive to Tsavo East with game viewing on the way. Afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight at Voi Wildlife Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Tsavo East to Watamu 

Morning game drive, then exit at Sala gate and drive along the Galana River to Watamu on the coast. Dinner and overnight at Turtle Bay Beach Club. 

Day 5 : 
Watamu Marine National Park 

Glass-bottom boat and snorkelling trip in Watamu Marine National Park. Afternoon on the beach. Dinner and overnight at Turtle Bay Beach Club. 

Day 6 : 
Mida Creek, Gede and Malindi 

Free day, or an optional trip to the Gede Ruins, a canoe or boardwalk visit to Mida Creek and an afternoon in Malindi town. Dinner and overnight at Turtle Bay Beach Club. 

Day 7 : 
Fly to Nairobi 

Transfer to Malindi airport for the flight to Nairobi and your onward connection.

How much does this Tsavo and Watamu safari cost? 

It starts from USD 1,970 per adult sharing, based on low-season rates. Prices rise over Christmas, Easter and in August; we confirm the price for your dates when you enquire. 

Can we stay longer on the beach? 

Yes. Add as many beach nights as you like, or swap Turtle Bay for a boutique hotel or villa in Watamu or Malindi. 

When is the best time for the Kenya coast? 

December to March and July to October are dry and sunny; April to June is the long rains, when some beach hotels close.
