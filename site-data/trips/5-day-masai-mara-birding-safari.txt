## 
This Masai Mara birding safari is perfect when you have only five days. It visits Lake Naivasha and Lake Nakuru for water birds, then spends two nights in the Masai Mara for grassland birds and big game. 
The Mara has more than 450 recorded bird species. Its open plains are great for bustards, larks, widowbirds and raptors, while the river forests add turacos and kingfishers. 

## Why choose this Masai Mara birding safari? 
- Lake Naivasha: fish eagles, ospreys, crakes and jacanas. 
- Lake Nakuru: flamingos, pelicans, tawny eagles and wood hoopoes. 
- Masai Mara grasslands: Jackson’s widowbird, Denham’s bustard and magpie shrike. 
- Big game: lions, elephants and, in season, the wildebeest migration. 

## Where you stay 
Lake Naivasha Lodge, Lake Nakuru Lodge and Mara Sopa Lodge, or similar. On the last day you have a day room in Nairobi before your flight. 

## Best time for a Masai Mara birding safari 
November to April brings migrants such as white storks and Caspian plovers to the Mara. July to October is the migration season, with huge herds and very active predators.

Day 1 : 
Nairobi to Lake Naivasha 

Drive into the Rift Valley with viewpoint stops, then bird walks and a visit to Elsamere. Overnight at Lake Naivasha Lodge.

Lake Naivasha to Lake Nakuru 

Game and bird drives in Lake Nakuru National Park and a walk on the lake shore. Overnight at Lake Nakuru Lodge.

Drive across the Rift Valley to the Mara. Afternoon game drive and sunset photography. Overnight at Mara Sopa Lodge.

A full day on the grasslands and in the woodland for bustards, widowbirds, raptors and big cats.

Masai Mara to Nairobi

Is five days enough for a Masai Mara birding safari? 

Yes for a taste of Kenya’s birds. You will see water birds at two lakes and grassland birds in the Mara. Add days for more species. 

Which lodges are used? 

Lake Naivasha Lodge, Lake Nakuru Lodge and Mara Sopa Lodge, or similar. 

Will I see the Big Five? 

Lions, elephants and buffaloes are common. Rhinos are seen at Nakuru and leopards sometimes in the Mara. 

What birds are special in the Mara? 

Jackson’s widowbird, Denham’s bustard, rufous-bellied heron and many raptors. In the wet season, corncrakes and Caspian plovers visit. 

Can I fly back from the Mara? 

Yes. A short flight from the Mara to Nairobi can replace the drive on the last day. 

Is this trip suitable for families? 

Yes. The short drives and mix of birds and big game suit families with older children. 

What should I pack? 

Binoculars, a light jacket for early mornings, sun protection and a camera. See our Kenya safari packing list.
