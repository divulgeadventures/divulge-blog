## 
This Masai Mara fly-in safari is the most popular short safari in Kenya. A light aircraft takes you from Wilson Airport to the reserve in about 45 minutes, instead of a five to six hour drive. 
Two nights give you a full day in the Mara, with time for a picnic lunch on the plains and an optional sunrise balloon flight. 

## Why choose this Masai Mara fly-in safari? 
- More time on safari: you can be on a game drive the same day you leave Nairobi. 
- Views from the air: the Rift Valley and the Mara plains from the plane window. 
- Big cats: lions, cheetahs and leopards in one of Africa’s best reserves. 
- Full day out: a picnic lunch and no rush to get back. 

## Where you stay 
Mara Serena Safari Lodge, or similar, close to the Serena airstrip and the Mara River. 

## Best time for a Masai Mara fly-in safari 
The Mara is good all year. July to October brings the wildebeest migration and river crossings. January to March is quieter, with green plains and many young animals.

Day 1 : 
Fly from Nairobi to the Masai Mara 

Transfer to Wilson Airport for your morning flight. Your guide meets you at the airstrip for a game drive on the way to Mara Serena Safari Lodge, followed by an afternoon game drive. 

Day 2 : 
Full day in the Masai Mara 

A full day of game viewing with a picnic lunch, or morning and afternoon drives with a rest at the lodge. Optional balloon flight at sunrise. 

Day 3 : 
Morning game drive and fly back to Nairobi 

One last early game drive, then breakfast and a transfer to the airstrip for your flight to Wilson Airport.

How long is the flight to the Masai Mara? 

About 45 minutes to an hour from Wilson Airport, depending on stops at other airstrips. 

How much luggage can I bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. Ask us if you need extra space. 

Where do the flights leave from in Nairobi? 

From Wilson Airport, not Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. We arrange the transfer from your hotel or from JKIA. 

Is the Masai Mara fly-in safari good for families? 

Yes. Short flights are much easier for children than long drives. 

Can I add a balloon flight? 

Yes. A sunrise balloon flight can be added at an extra cost. 

Is emergency evacuation cover available? 

Yes. We recommend Flying Doctors evacuation cover for every safari and can arrange it for you. 

Can I extend my stay? 

Yes. Extra nights can be added easily, as flights run daily.
