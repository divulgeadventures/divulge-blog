## Overview 
This 7 day Solio Naivasha and Masai Mara safari is a private road safari from Nairobi. You spend 2 nights at Solio Game Reserve for black and white rhinos, 1 night at Lake Naivasha with a boat ride and Crescent Island walk, and 3 nights in the Masai Mara Triangle. The page lists the best season as all year round, and the price is on request. It suits rhino lovers, photographers and repeat visitors. 
The trip travels in a 4×4 Land Cruiser with a professional driver guide, on full board, staying at Rhino Watch Lodge, Lake Naivasha Resort and Mara Serena Safari Lodge, in accommodation rated 4 to 5 stars on the trip page. 

## Why choose a 7 day Solio Naivasha and Masai Mara safari? 
Solio Game Reserve is a private rhino reserve north of Nairobi, and it is one of the best places in Kenya to see both black and white rhinos on open plains. Few safari routes include it, so game drives there feel quiet compared with the busiest parks. Read more in our Solio Game Reserve guide . 
The Masai Mara Triangle is the western corner of the Masai Mara National Reserve, run by the Mara Conservancy and known for big male lions, leopards and the Mara River. Three nights there, with two full days, give you time for big cats and, in season, the migration herds. 
- Rhino seekers: two nights and a full day at Solio Game Reserve. 
- Active travellers: cycling in Hell’s Gate National Park and a walking safari on Crescent Island. 
- Big cat fans: two full days in the Masai Mara Triangle. 

## What to expect on the 7 day Solio Naivasha and Masai Mara safari 
Day 1 starts with a meet-and-greet and safari briefing in Nairobi, then a drive of about 4 hours to Solio Game Reserve, an afternoon siesta and an evening game drive, with the night at Rhino Watch Lodge. Day 2 is a full day of game drives in Solio with a picnic lunch, looking for black and white rhinos, lions, buffalo, giraffes and zebras. 
On day 3 you drive about 4 hours to Lake Naivasha, stopping at Thomson’s Falls in Nyahururu, then take an evening boat ride and a guided walk on Crescent Island among giraffes, zebras and antelopes, staying at Lake Naivasha Resort. Day 4 begins with cycling and a gorge walk in Hell’s Gate National Park, then the drive to the Masai Mara with lunch on the way and an evening game drive in the Mara Triangle. 
Days 5 and 6 are full days in the Masai Mara with picnic lunches on the plains, staying at Mara Serena Safari Lodge, with an optional hot air balloon flight. On day 7 you visit a Maasai village, then drive about 5 hours back to Nairobi for your flight. 

### Is 7 days enough for Solio, Naivasha and the Masai Mara? 
Seven days is enough for this route because each stop gets at least one full day or a busy activity day: a full day in Solio, a boat ride, Crescent Island and Hell’s Gate around Lake Naivasha, and two full days in the Mara. If you want to time the Mara for the Great Migration, check our Great Migration month by month guide before choosing dates.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

This 7 Days Best of Solio Game Reserve, Naivasha & Masai Mara Safari begins with an immersive exploration of the remarkable Solio Game Reserve, home to Kenya’s most successful rhino sanctuary. Enjoy exceptional wildlife viewing and stunning scenery as you discover the beauty of Solio Game Reserve before continuing to Lake Naivasha and the iconic Masai Mara for unforgettable game drives and breathtaking landscapes. 

Day 01 : 
Nairobi - Solio Game Reserve 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by Divulge Adventures representative for meet, greet and safari briefing. Afterward, you will depart Nairobi for Solio Game Reserve to the north of Nairobi, approximately 4 hours drive. Arriving at around lunchtime for check-in and lunch, followed by afternoon siesta. 
Later in the evening, set out for evening game drive at the ranch. An evening game drive at Solio Game Reserve offers a breathtaking safari experience, featuring close encounters with black and white rhinos, lions, leopards, buffalo, and giraffes. As the sun sets over the vast plains, the golden light creates a magical atmosphere. 
With fewer crowds, abundant wildlife, and a scenic sundowner stop, Solio’s exclusive setting makes for an unforgettable African adventure. Afterwards, return to the camp / lodge for dinner and overnight at Rhino Watch Lodge . 

Day 02 : 
Full Day at Solio Game Reserve 

After breakfast, set out for a full day game drive at Solio Game Reserve. The ranch offers an immersive safari experience in one of Kenya’s premier rhino conservancies. The drive takes you through breathtaking landscapes, where you’ll encounter black and white rhinos, lions, leopards, buffalo, giraffes, and zebras. 
Mornings provide the best wildlife activity, while midday offers a chance to enjoy a picnic in the wild. Afternoon drives reveal more game as temperatures cool, ending with a stunning sunset. With exclusive access and diverse wildlife, a full-day safari at Solio Game Reserve promises an unforgettable adventure in a serene, uncrowded setting. 
Dinner and overnight at Rhino Watch Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Solio Game Reserve - Lake Naivasha 

After a delightful morning breakfast, checkout and proceed to Lake Naivasha approximately 4 hours drive. Embark on a scenic drive to Lake Naivasha, making a stop at the stunning Thompson Falls along the way, a stunning 74-meter waterfall in Nyahururu, Kenya, surrounded by lush greenery and offering breathtaking scenic views. 
Continue the journey, arriving at Lake Naivasha just in time for lunch and a relaxing afternoon siesta. In the evening, enjoy a serene boat ride on the lake, followed by a guided walk on the renowned Crescent Island, a private wildlife sanctuary known for its walking safaris among giraffes, zebras, pythons and antelopes. 
Originally a peninsula, it became an island after rising lake levels. It gained fame as a filming location for *Out of Africa* (1985), enhancing its historical significance. As the day winds down, return to the resort for a delightful dinner and an overnight stay, surrounded by the tranquil beauty of Naivasha. 
Later return to the lodge for dinner and overnight at Lake Naivasha Resort 

Day 04 : 
Lake Naivasha - Masai Mara Triangle 

After a steamy hot delicious breakfast set out for Hells Gate National Park for cycling and take a walk at the gorges before exiting Lake Naivasha for Masai Mara Reserve. You will have an enroute lunch as you head to Masai Mara arriving late in the afternoon. You will check in and have relaxing moments. 
In the evening, you will have and evening game drive at the Mara Triangle Reserve mainly known for large male lions and leopards before returning to the lodge for dinner and at Mara Serena Safari Lodge 

Day 05 & 06 : 
Two Full Days at Masai Mara Triangle 

After breakfast, you will have a full day at Masai Mara National Reserve for game viewing during your stay. This allows you a chance to thoroughly explore this wonderful reserve while you're here. 
Stay on the lookout for the big cats with magical scenes when hunting, elephants, buffaloes, zebras, wildebeests, giraffes, hippos and dangerous crocodiles feeding on the wildebeests as they cross the Mara River. Elephants, giraffes, lion prides, thousands of impalas, topi, grants and Thomson's gazelles, and different bird species could all be seen. 
So, make sure to carry your phone or camera to capture these moments. You will have picnic lunch served in the vast plains of Maasai Mara National Reserve. In addition, optional balloon safari over the Masai Mara which offers you a magnificent aerial view of the park and viewing the wildlife and meandering Mara River from above. 
Dinner and overnight at Mara Serena Safari Lodge 

Day 07 : 
Masai Mara Triagle - Nairobi - Departure 

After breakfast at your camp, you will check out and head for Masai Village Visit; Immerse yourself in the rich culture of the Masai people by visiting a traditional village. Learn about their customs, traditions, and way of life, witness their intricate beadwork and warrior dances, and perhaps even purchase a handcrafted souvenir to support their community. 
Afterwards, exit Masai Mara for Nairobi approximately 5 hours’ drive. Upon arrival, drop off at the airport for your outbound flight.

When is the best time for the 7 day Solio Naivasha and Masai Mara safari? 

The 7 day Solio Naivasha and Masai Mara safari runs all year round, as the trip page shows. Rhinos live in Solio year-round. If you want the Great Migration herds in the Masai Mara Triangle, July to October is usually best, while the drier months of January and February are also good for game drives.
