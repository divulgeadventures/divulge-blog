## Overview 
Experience an unforgettable wildlife safari with our 7 Day best of Solio Game Reserve, Lake Naivasha and Masai Mara Safari. Explore the renowned Solio Game Reserve, famous for its thriving populations of black and white rhinos and stunning savannah landscapes. Continue to Lake Naivasha for serene boat rides and rich birdlife before heading to the iconic Masai Mara for dramatic big cat encounters and breathtaking scenery. This adventure through Solio Game Reserve and Kenya’s top destinations guarantees exceptional game viewing, comfort, and lifelong safari memories. 

### Safari Highlights 
- Solio Game Reserve 
- Lake Naivasha 
- Masai Mara National Reserve 

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

This 7 Days Best of Solio Game Reserve, Naivasha & Masai Mara Safari begins with an immersive exploration of the remarkable Solio Game Reserve, home to Kenya’s most successful rhino sanctuary. Enjoy exceptional wildlife viewing and stunning scenery as you discover the beauty of Solio Game Reserve before continuing to Lake Naivasha and the iconic Masai Mara for unforgettable game drives and breathtaking landscapes. 

Day 01 : 
Nairobi - Solio Game Reserve 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by Divulge Adventures representative for meet, greet and safari briefing. Afterward, you will depart Nairobi for Solio Game Reserve to the north of Nairobi, approximately 4 hours drive. Arriving at around lunchtime for check-in and lunch, followed by afternoon siesta. Later in the evening, set out for evening game drive at the ranch. An evening game drive at Solio Game Reserve offers a breathtaking safari experience, featuring close encounters with black and white rhinos, lions, leopards, buffalo, and giraffes. As the sun sets over the vast plains, the golden light creates a magical atmosphere. With fewer crowds, abundant wildlife, and a scenic sundowner stop, Solio’s exclusive setting makes for an unforgettable African adventure. Afterwards, return to the camp / lodge for dinner and overnight at Rhino Watch Lodge . 

Day 02 : 
Full Day at Solio Game Reserve 

After breakfast, set out for a full day game drive at Solio Game Reserve. The ranch offers an immersive safari experience in one of Kenya’s premier rhino conservancies. The drive takes you through breathtaking landscapes, where you’ll encounter black and white rhinos, lions, leopards, buffalo, giraffes, and zebras. Mornings provide the best wildlife activity, while midday offers a chance to enjoy a picnic in the wild. Afternoon drives reveal more game as temperatures cool, ending with a stunning sunset. With exclusive access and diverse wildlife, a full-day safari at Solio Game Reserve promises an unforgettable adventure in a serene, uncrowded setting. Dinner and overnight at Rhino Watch Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Solio Game Reserve - Lake Naivasha 

After a delightful morning breakfast, checkout and proceed to Lake Naivasha approximately 4 hours drive. Embark on a scenic drive to Lake Naivasha, making a stop at the stunning Thompson Falls along the way, a stunning 74-meter waterfall in Nyahururu, Kenya, surrounded by lush greenery and offering breathtaking scenic views. Continue the journey, arriving at Lake Naivasha just in time for lunch and a relaxing afternoon siesta. In the evening, enjoy a serene boat ride on the lake, followed by a guided walk on the renowned Crescent Island, a private wildlife sanctuary known for its walking safaris among giraffes, zebras, pythons and antelopes. Originally a peninsula, it became an island after rising lake levels. It gained fame as a filming location for *Out of Africa* (1985), enhancing its historical significance. As the day winds down, return to the resort for a delightful dinner and an overnight stay, surrounded by the tranquil beauty of Naivasha. Later return to the lodge for dinner and overnight at Lake Naivasha Resort 

Day 04 : 
Lake Naivasha - Masai Mara Triangle 

After a steamy hot delicious breakfast set out for Hells Gate National Park for cycling and take a walk at the gorges before exiting Lake Naivasha for Masai Mara Reserve. You will have an enroute lunch as you head to Masai Mara arriving late in the afternoon. You will check in and have relaxing moments. In the evening, you will have and evening game drive at the Mara Triangle Reserve mainly known for large male lions and leopards before returning to the lodge for dinner and at Mara Serena Safari Lodge 

Day 05 & 06 : 
Two Full Days at Masai Mara Triangle 

After breakfast, you will have a full day at Masai Mara National Reserve for game viewing during your stay. This allows you a chance to thoroughly explore this wonderful reserve while you're here. Stay on the lookout for the big cats with magical scenes when hunting, elephants, buffaloes, zebras, wildebeests, giraffes, hippos and dangerous crocodiles feeding on the wildebeests as they cross the Mara River. Elephants, giraffes, lion prides, thousands of impalas, topi, grants and Thomson's gazelles, and different bird species could all be seen. So, make sure to carry your phone or camera to capture these moments. You will have picnic lunch served in the vast plains of Maasai Mara National Reserve. In addition, optional balloon safari over the Masai Mara which offers you a magnificent aerial view of the park and viewing the wildlife and meandering Mara River from above. Dinner and overnight at Mara Serena Safari Lodge 

Day 07 : 
Masai Mara Triagle - Nairobi - Departure 

After breakfast at your camp, you will check out and head for Masai Village Visit; Immerse yourself in the rich culture of the Masai people by visiting a traditional village. Learn about their customs, traditions, and way of life, witness their intricate beadwork and warrior dances, and perhaps even purchase a handcrafted souvenir to support their community. Afterwards, exit Masai Mara for Nairobi approximately 5 hours’ drive. Upon arrival, drop off at the airport for your outbound flight.
