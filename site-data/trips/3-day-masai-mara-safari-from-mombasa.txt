## 
This Masai Mara safari from Mombasa is a favourite with guests staying in Mombasa, Diani, Kilifi or Watamu. You fly to the Mara, spend two nights in the reserve and fly back to the coast. 
A full day in the middle gives you time for a picnic lunch on the plains and, in season, the chance of a river crossing. 

## Why choose this Masai Mara safari from Mombasa? 
- From the coast: flights from Mombasa or Ukunda (Diani). 
- A full day in the Mara: with a picnic lunch on the plains. 
- Migration season: river crossings from July to October. 
- Easy logistics: hotel transfers and flights arranged for you. 

## Where you stay 
Two nights at Mara Serena Safari Lodge, or similar. 

## Best time for a Masai Mara safari from Mombasa 
The Mara is good all year. July to October brings the wildebeest migration and river crossings. January to March is quieter, with green plains and many young animals. 
Short on time? See the 2 day Masai Mara safari from Diani . Browse all flight packages and read the Masai Mara entrance fee guide . See also Magical Kenya .

Day 1 : 
Fly from the coast to the Masai Mara 

Transfer from your hotel to Mombasa airport or Ukunda airstrip for the flight to the Mara. Lunch at Mara Serena Safari Lodge and an afternoon game drive. 

Day 2 : 
Full day in the Masai Mara 

A full day of game drives with a picnic lunch. Optional balloon flight at sunrise. 

Day 3 : 
Fly back to the coast 

Early morning game drive, then fly back to Mombasa or Diani and transfer to your hotel.

### Cost Includes 
- Scheduled light-aircraft flights between the coast (Ukunda/Diani or Mombasa) and the safari airstrips 
- Transfers between your beach hotel and the airport 
- Airstrip meet and greet and game drives in a 4x4 safari vehicle with a professional guide 
- Accommodation as per the itinerary (or similar), full board 
- All park and reserve entry fees 
- Drinking water during game drives 

### Cost Excludes 
- International flights, visa and travel insurance 
- Excess baggage over the light-aircraft limit (usually 15 kg in soft bags) 
- Optional activities such as balloon flights and village visits 
- Drinks, laundry and items of a personal nature 
- Tips for guides and lodge staff

Can I join from Watamu or Kilifi? 

Yes. We can arrange a transfer to Mombasa or a flight from Malindi, depending on the schedule. 

How long is the flight from Mombasa to the Mara? 

Usually around two hours, sometimes with a short stop. 

How much luggage can I bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. Ask us if you need extra space. 

Which lodge is used on the Masai Mara safari from Mombasa? 

Mara Serena Safari Lodge, or a similar lodge close to the airstrip. 

Can I add a balloon flight? 

Yes, at extra cost, on the morning of day 2. 

Can I continue to Amboseli? 

Yes. See our Diani to Amboseli and Masai Mara fly-in safari. 

Is emergency evacuation cover available? 

Yes. We recommend Flying Doctors evacuation cover for every safari and can arrange it for you.
