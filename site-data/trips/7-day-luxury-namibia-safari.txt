## 
This luxury Namibia safari takes you through the country’s three signature landscapes, staying two nights in each. Climb the red dunes and walk into Deadvlei from Kulala Desert Lodge inside the private Kulala reserve next to Sossusvlei. Look for desert-adapted elephants and ancient rock engravings in Damaraland from Mowani Mountain Camp. Finish with game drives around the waterholes of Etosha National Park from Onguma The Fort. A private guided vehicle covers the long distances in comfort, making this a strong choice for couples and photographers who want Namibia’s highlights with fine lodges. 
Price: from USD 3,270 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Sunrise at Sossusvlei and Deadvlei with early access from the Kulala area 
- Desert-adapted elephants and Twyfelfontein rock engravings in Damaraland 
- Game drives in Etosha National Park 
- Two nights each at Kulala Desert Lodge, Mowani Mountain Camp and Onguma The Fort 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Windhoek to Sossusvlei 

Collection in Windhoek and drive south-west over the Khomas Highlands to the Namib Desert. Dinner and overnight at Kulala Desert Lodge. 

Day 2 : 
Sossusvlei and Deadvlei 

Early start for sunrise on the dunes at Sossusvlei, then a walk into Deadvlei among its dead camel-thorn trees. Afternoon visit to Sesriem Canyon. Dinner and overnight at Kulala Desert Lodge. 

Day 3 : 
Namib Desert to Damaraland 

Long scenic drive north through Solitaire and the Gaub and Kuiseb passes into Damaraland. Dinner and overnight at Mowani Mountain Camp. 

Day 4 : 
Damaraland 

Search for desert-adapted elephants along the dry riverbeds and visit the rock engravings at Twyfelfontein, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Dinner and overnight at Mowani Mountain Camp. 

Day 5 : 
Damaraland to Etosha 

Drive to Etosha National Park with a game drive through the park to its eastern side. Dinner and overnight at Onguma The Fort. 

Day 6 : 
Etosha National Park 

Full day of game drives between Etosha's waterholes for elephants, lions, rhinos and large herds of plains game. Dinner and overnight at Onguma The Fort. 

Day 7 : 
Etosha to Windhoek 

Drive back to Windhoek for your onward flight or hotel.

How much does this luxury Namibia safari cost? 

It starts from USD 3,270 per adult sharing, based on low-season rates at the listed lodges. We confirm the exact price for your dates when you enquire. 

Is there a lot of driving? 

Yes, Namibia is large and some days include five to seven hours on the road. The scenery is part of the trip, and stops are built in along the way. 

When is the best time to visit Namibia? 

May to October is dry and best for Etosha game viewing; the desert can be visited all year.
