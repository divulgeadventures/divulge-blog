## 
This Rwanda honeymoon safari is short, smooth and unforgettable. After a night in Kigali, drive to Volcanoes National Park for two nights at Mountain Gorilla View Lodge and a morning with a mountain gorilla family. Then relax on the shores of Lake Kivu at the Lake Kivu Serena Hotel before returning to Kigali. Distances are short and the roads are good, so it fits easily into a week’s leave or combines with a beach extension. 
Price: from USD 3,400 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Gorilla trekking permit in Volcanoes National Park included 
- Two nights at Mountain Gorilla View Lodge 
- A night on the shores of Lake Kivu 
- Short, easy drives on good roads 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Kigali 

Meet at Kigali airport; afternoon city tour including the Kigali Genocide Memorial. Dinner and overnight at Hotel des Mille Collines. 

Day 2 : 
Kigali to Volcanoes National Park 

Drive about two and a half hours to Musanze at the foot of the Virunga volcanoes. Afternoon at leisure. Dinner and overnight at Mountain Gorilla View Lodge. 

Day 3 : 
Gorilla trekking 

Briefing at Kinigi park headquarters, then trek to spend one hour with a habituated mountain gorilla family. Dinner and overnight at Mountain Gorilla View Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Volcanoes to Lake Kivu 

Drive to Gisenyi on Lake Kivu. Afternoon boat trip or time on the lake shore. Dinner and overnight at Lake Kivu Serena Hotel. 

Day 5 : 
Lake Kivu to Kigali 

Drive back to Kigali for your departure flight.

How much does this Rwanda honeymoon safari cost? 

It starts from USD 3,400 per adult sharing, including the USD 1,500 gorilla permit, based on low-season rates. We confirm the price for your dates when you enquire. 

How far ahead should we book? 

Gorilla permits are limited, so book three to six months ahead, especially for June to September. 

Can we add a beach? 

Yes. Rwanda combines well with a few nights in Zanzibar or on the Kenyan coast.
