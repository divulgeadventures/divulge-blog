## Overview 
This 5 day Southern Skeleton Coast tour is a guided trip from Windhoek based in Swakopmund, with a final night at Spitzkoppe. It covers the Living Desert Tour and Welwitschia Drive in Dorob National Park, a Walvis Bay catamaran cruise, 4×4 dune driving at Sandwich Harbour and the Cape Cross seal colony. It runs all year, costs from USD 4,200 per adult and suits active travellers who want coast, desert and marine life. 
The tour travels in an air-conditioned vehicle with a professional English-speaking guide, and breakfast, park entrance fees, the listed activities and airport transfers are included. 

## Why choose a 5 day Southern Skeleton Coast tour? 
The 5 day Southern Skeleton Coast tour is built around the place where the Namib dunes meet the Atlantic. Most Namibia itineraries give the coast a single night; this one gives it three full days, so you can do the Living Desert Tour, the Walvis Bay cruise and Sandwich Harbour without rushing. 
The trip also reaches beyond Swakopmund: north to the Cape Cross seal colony, where up to 200,000 Cape fur seals gather, and inland to the granite peaks of Spitzkoppe, the “Matterhorn of Africa”, for San rock paintings and a sunrise hike. 
- Wildlife lovers: dolphins, seals and possible whales on the cruise, and desert-adapted creatures in the dunes. 
- Adventure seekers: heavy 4×4 dune driving at Sandwich Harbour and a guided hike on Groot Spitzkoppe. 
- Photographers: pink lakes, shipwrecks on the coast road and Spitzkoppe at first light. 

## What to expect on the 5 day Southern Skeleton Coast tour 
Day 1 starts with a pick-up from the airport or your Windhoek accommodation and the drive west to the coast for a city tour of Swakopmund’s history since the late 1800s, with an optional township tour of Mondesa and DRC. Day 2 is the Living Desert Tour by 4×4 in the dunes of Dorob National Park, then the Welwitschia Drive past lichen fields, the moon landscape and Welwitschia plants over 2,000 years old. 
Day 3 begins at the Walvis Bay Waterfront with a catamaran cruise for bottlenose and Benguela dolphins, Cape fur seals, pelicans and seasonal whales, followed after lunch by 4×4 dune driving at Sandwich Harbour, a Ramsar wetland with pink lakes. 
Day 4 heads north past a shipwreck to the Cape Cross Seal Reserve, then inland to the Spitzkoppe mountains for San rock paintings. Day 5 starts with an early guided hike on Groot Spitzkoppe before the drive back to Windhoek. 

### Is the Groot Spitzkoppe hike difficult? 
The Groot Spitzkoppe hike is the most demanding part of the tour. The peak rises to 1,728 m and the page describes the route as technical, so an experienced local guide leads the way and the summit is only for fit, confident scramblers. Everyone else can enjoy shorter walks around the base. Our Namibia hiking safari guide and stargazing in Namibia guide cover the area.

## Day by Day

Day 01 : 
Begining of Namibian Southern Skeleton Coast Tour 

You will be met by a guide from Divulge Adventure who will pick you up either from the airport or accommodation in Windhoek. The guide will drive westwards to the Namibian Southern Skeleton Coast. 
You will embark on a city tour that enlightens you on the early days in the Namibian Southern Skeleton Coast dating back to the late 1800s, with plenty of anecdotes. 
Township tours are optional but highly recommended as one visits the townships of Mondesa and DRC. 
You get to visit the market, traditional elders and kindergarten. Meet friendly local people, have tea and traditional food in a private home. 

Day 02 : 
Dorob National Park 

After breakfast, you will embark on an exciting Living Namibian Southern Skeleton Coast Desert Tour, a guided 4x4 drive into the dunes. Explore the incredible landscape and its sneaky creatures that have adapted to living in a harsh environment. 
For these little creatures who either camouflage, use their agility, or fake deaths, they help serve food on the table or somehow avoid being someone’s meal. 
You will return around lunchtime, and after a rest, you will embark on the Welwitschia Drive in the late afternoon. This drive takes you through several beacons at points of interest, culminating at one of Namibia's oldest plants, Welwitschia mirabilis (over 2,000 years old). 
The other stops include lichen fields, drought-resistant bushes (dollar and ink bushes), tracks of ox wagons made decades ago but still visible, the "moon landscape", Old South African Camp (WW1), and a dolorite dyke among others. 
The moon landscape is a rolling, barren area of rocky desert formed by valleys around the course of the Swakop River. 

Day 03 : 
Catamaran and Sandwich Harbour Tour 

After breakfast, you will be taken to Walvis Bay Waterfront. You will embark on a boat cruise with possible sightings of humpback whales, Southern right whales, minke and even killer whales, as well as leatherback turtles. 
Bottlenose and the endemic Benguela (heavysides) dolphins are present all year round. You might be entertained by Cape fur seals and pelicans jumping on the boat for a free ride and food. 
After lunch on the boat, you will go for heavy 4x4 sand dune driving at Sandwich Harbour, where the dunes meet the sea. This is a large saltwater lagoon with extensive tidal mudflats and a band of reed-lined pools fed by freshwater springs. 
This forms a Ramsar Site (Wetlands of International Importance), an important bird refuge site for over 30 species. 
Besides mammals like black-backed jackal, oryx, brown hyaena and springbok, you get to see the breathtaking scenery, including pink lakes. 

Day 04 : 
Cape Cross Seal Reserve and Spitzkoppe 

After breakfast, you will head northwards past a shipwreck to the largest seal colony in the world, the Cape Cross. 
There can be up to 200,000 seals at a given time, making an unexpected noise that can be described as a harsh discordant mixture of sounds resembling an entire farmyard of animals at full volume. 
You will leave the coastal environment and make our way to the Spitzkoppe Mountains. These mountains resemble the famous Swiss mountain, hence named the "Matterhorn of Africa". We will visit some San (Bushman) rock paintings in the afternoon 

Day 05 : 
Spitzkoppe to Windhoek 

You will embark on a guided hike up the Groot Spitzkoppe very early in the morning. The highest point is 1,728m and towers 600m above the surrounding plains. 
It demands a technical climb, so an experienced local guide will accompany us. After breakfast, you will make our way to Windhoek, marking the end of our Namibian Southern Skeleton coastal experience.

When is the best time for the 5 day Southern Skeleton Coast tour? 

The 5 day Southern Skeleton Coast tour runs all year, and the trip page does not set a season. The coast stays mild with morning fog, so bring a warm layer for the Walvis Bay cruise. Humpback and southern right whales are most likely from about July to November, and May to September is cooler for the Spitzkoppe hike.

Is the Southern Skeleton Coast tour suitable for families? 

Yes, families can join the Southern Skeleton Coast tour. Children usually love the seals and dolphins on the catamaran, the Living Desert Tour and the Cape Cross colony. The Sandwich Harbour dune drive is bumpy and the Groot Spitzkoppe summit is a technical climb, so tell us your children’s ages and we will adjust the plan.
