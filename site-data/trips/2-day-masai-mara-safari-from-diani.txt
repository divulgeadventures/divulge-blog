## 
This Masai Mara safari from Diani is designed for guests staying on Kenya’s south coast. You fly from Ukunda airstrip near Diani, or from Mombasa, straight to the Mara, then return to the beach the next day. 
It is the easiest way to add a real safari to a beach holiday without giving up much beach time. 

## Why choose this Masai Mara safari from Diani? 
- From the beach: pick-up from your hotel in Diani or Mombasa. 
- Two game drives: sunset and sunrise, the best times of day. 
- Big cats: lions, cheetahs and leopards on the plains. 
- Back the next day: only one night away from the coast. 

## Where you stay 
One night at Mara Serena Safari Lodge, or a similar camp near your airstrip. 

## Best time for a Masai Mara safari from Diani 
The Mara is good all year. July to October brings the wildebeest migration and river crossings. January to March is quieter, with green plains and many young animals.

Day 1 : 
Fly from Diani or Mombasa to the Masai Mara 

Transfer from your beach hotel to Ukunda airstrip or Mombasa airport for the morning flight to the Mara. Lunch at Mara Serena Safari Lodge and an afternoon game drive until sunset. 

Day 2 : 
Sunrise game drive and fly back to the coast 

Early game drive, brunch at the lodge, then fly back to the coast and transfer to your hotel.

Where do the flights leave from on the coast? 

From Ukunda airstrip near Diani or from Mombasa airport, depending on the day’s schedule. 

How long is the flight from Diani to the Mara? 

Usually around two hours, sometimes with a short stop on the way. 

How much luggage can I bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. Ask us if you need extra space. 

Can I leave my main luggage at the hotel? 

Yes. Most beach hotels will store your large bags while you are on safari. 

Is one night enough? 

It is a taste of safari. For more time, choose our three day version. 

Can I add a balloon flight? 

Yes, at extra cost, on the morning of day 2. 

Is emergency evacuation cover available? 

Yes. We recommend Flying Doctors evacuation cover for every safari and can arrange it for you.
