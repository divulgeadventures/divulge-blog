## 
This 9 day Tanzania celebration safari is made for groups marking a milestone together, such as a big birthday, an anniversary, a reunion or a wedding party. It combines bush dinners and game drives in Tarangire, the Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Crater with three nights on the beach in Zanzibar. 

## Why choose a 9 day Tanzania celebration safari? 
Every day has a moment to share: a sundowner facing Kilimanjaro, a bush dinner under the stars in Tarangire, a private lunch in the Serengeti and a sunset dhow cruise in Zanzibar. We can add a cake, decorations, a photographer or a surprise for the guest of honour. 
The safari ends with three nights on the beach, so the group can relax, snorkel and celebrate before flying home. 

## What does the celebration safari itinerary include? 
One night in Arusha, one in Tarangire, two in the central Serengeti, one at the Ngorongoro Crater and three in Zanzibar. A short flight from Arusha to Zanzibar saves a long drive. 

### Can you plan a surprise or special dinner? 
Yes. Tell us the occasion when you enquire and we will arrange it with the lodges, from a private dinner set-up to a celebration cake or a short ceremony on the beach. 
For more ideas, read our Tanzania honeymoon guide and our notes on adding Zanzibar to a safari . 

## Who is the 9 day tanzania celebration safari best for? 
The 9 day tanzania celebration safari is best for milestone birthdays, anniversaries, reunions, retirements and wedding parties. Tell us the occasion and we will plan the special moments with the lodges, so the 9 day tanzania celebration safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## Can a large group share this 9 day Tanzania celebration safari? 
Yes. Larger groups travel in several Land Cruisers and stay in lodges with enough rooms, or split between two camps of the same standard. Guests who can only join for part of the trip can fly straight to Zanzibar for the beach days.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Arusha 

Your 9 day tanzania celebration safari begins today. Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport and transfer to Arusha. In the afternoon, visit a coffee farm on the foothills near Moshi, then gather for a welcome sundowner with views of Kilimanjaro. Dinner and overnight at Arusha Green Side Hotel or similar. 

Day 2 : 
Arusha to Tarangire National Park 

Drive about 2.5 hours to Tarangire for an afternoon game drive among elephants, baobabs and big cats. In the evening, the group enjoys a bush dinner under the stars. Overnight at Sangaiwe Tented Lodge or similar. 

Day 3 : 
Tarangire to the Serengeti 

Drive to the central Serengeti with photo stops along the way. Afternoon game drive to look for lions, cheetahs and the migration herds. Overnight at Moyo Tented Camp or similar. 

Day 4 : 
Full day in the Serengeti 

Morning and afternoon game drives with a private lunch in the bush. An early balloon flight can be added. In the evening, enjoy stargazing with a Maasai storyteller. Overnight at Moyo Tented Camp or similar. 

Day 5 : 
Serengeti to the Ngorongoro Crater 

An early game drive, then drive about 3 hours to the Ngorongoro Crater for an afternoon on the crater floor, home to black rhinos, lions and elephants. Picnic lunch by a soda lake. Overnight at Baghayo Garden Suites Lodge or similar. 

Day 6 : 
Ngorongoro to Zanzibar 

Transfer to Arusha Airport for the flight to Zanzibar. In the evening, the group sails on a sunset dhow cruise. Overnight at Zanzibar Bay Resort or similar. 

Day 7 : 
Zanzibar beach day 

Snorkel at Mnemba Atoll in the morning, then spend the afternoon on the beach or kayaking. The evening is free for a celebration dinner on the beach. Overnight at Zanzibar Bay Resort or similar. 

Day 8 : 
Zanzibar spice tour and Prison Island 

Take a guided spice tour, then a boat trip to Changuu (Prison) Island to see the giant tortoises. Free evening for the spa or a beach walk. Overnight at Zanzibar Bay Resort or similar. 

Day 9 : 
Departure from Zanzibar 

Transfer to Zanzibar Airport for your flight home. This is the end of your 9 day tanzania celebration safari.

What occasions suit the 9 day Tanzania celebration safari? 

The 9 day Tanzania celebration safari suits milestone birthdays, wedding anniversaries, family reunions, retirement trips and wedding parties. We plan one or two special moments around your occasion, such as a private dinner, a cake or a beach ceremony. 

Can guests join for only part of the safari? 

Yes. Some guests join only the safari or only the Zanzibar beach days. We arrange separate transfers and flights so everyone meets up at the right place. Tell us your dates and group size and we will confirm this in the quote for your 9 day tanzania celebration safari. 

Is a hot air balloon flight included? 

No. A sunrise balloon flight over the Serengeti can be added on day 3 or 4 at an extra cost. It is one of the most popular ways to celebrate a big birthday or anniversary. 

Can each guest pay separately? 

Yes. Each guest or couple can pay their own share. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the group’s rooms and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. 

When is the best time for a celebration safari in Tanzania? 

June to October is dry and good for game viewing across the northern parks. January to February is the calving season in the southern Serengeti, and Zanzibar is warm and sunny in both periods.
