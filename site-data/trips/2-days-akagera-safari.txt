## Overview 
This 2 days Akagera safari is the easiest way to see the wild savannah side of Rwanda. Akagera National Park lies about 2.5 hours east of Kigali and is now home to the Big Five, after lions and rhinos were brought back by African Parks and the Rwanda Development Board. 
Day one ends with a sunset boat cruise on Lake Ihema. Day two is a full day of game drives across plains, woodland and lakeshore, in a private 4×4 with your own driver guide. 

## Why choose a 2 days Akagera safari? 
Akagera is very different from the rainforests and volcanoes most visitors know Rwanda for. Here you find open grassland, acacia woodland, papyrus swamps and a chain of lakes, all in one compact park. 
Two days is long enough to enjoy the lakes and the plains without rushing. It works well on its own, or as the wildlife part of a longer gorilla trip. 
- Big Five: lions, leopards, elephants, buffaloes and rhinos. 
- Water wildlife: hippos and crocodiles on the boat cruise. 
- Birding: more than 480 bird species recorded in the park. 

## What to expect on your 2 days Akagera safari 
After pick-up in Kigali, you drive east through rolling countryside and check in at your lodge for lunch. In the late afternoon you board a boat on Lake Ihema. Hippos grunt in the shallows, crocodiles bask on the banks and fish eagles call overhead as the sun goes down. 
Day two starts early, when animals are most active. You head north through the park looking for elephants, giraffes, zebras, topi and buffaloes, with a good chance of lions. A picnic lunch is served at a scenic spot before the drive back to Kigali. 

### Where you stay 
You spend one night at a comfortable lodge in or near the park, on full board. Ask us about upgrades if you would like a tented camp or lakeside room. 

## Best time for an Akagera safari 
The dry seasons, from June to September and December to February, are best for game viewing because animals gather near the lakes. The green season brings lush scenery, baby animals and excellent birding.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is your 2 days Akagera safari day by day. 

Day 01 : 
Kigali - Akagera National Park (Sunset Boat Cruise) 

Upon pick-up at your Kigali hotel or the airport, you will be met by your Divulge Adventures driver guide for a safari briefing. Depart for Akagera National Park in the Kayonza district, approximately 2.5 hours away, with photo stops along the way to take in Rwanda's countryside. Arrive at the lodge in time for check-in and lunch. 
In the late afternoon, board a boat on Lake Ihema for a sunset cruise. Glide past pods of hippos, basking crocodiles and waterbirds such as African fish eagles, kingfishers and herons as the sky turns gold. Return to the lodge for a relaxing evening. Dinner and overnight at Akagera Game Lodge | Akagera Rhino Lodge 

Day 02 : 
Akagera Full-Day Game Drive - Kigali 

After an early and steamy breakfast, set out on a full-day game drive across Akagera's plains, woodlands and lakeshores. Look out for elephants, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras, topi, elands and impalas, and keep your eyes peeled for lions and black rhinos. Birders will love the variety, with over 480 species recorded in the park. 
Enjoy a picnic lunch at a scenic spot inside the park before a final afternoon drive towards the exit gate. Drive back to Kigali, arriving in the evening for drop-off at your hotel, residence or Kigali International Airport.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

Is 2 days enough for Akagera National Park? 

Two days lets you enjoy a sunset boat cruise and a full-day game drive, which covers the park’s main highlights. Add a third day if you want to explore the remote northern plains. 

What animals can you see on a Lake Ihema boat cruise? 

Expect hippos, Nile crocodiles, African fish eagles, kingfishers, cormorants and herons, plus elephants and buffaloes drinking along the shore. 

Does Akagera have the Big Five? 

Yes. Akagera is Rwanda’s only Big Five park, with lions, leopards, elephants, buffaloes and black and white rhinos. 

How far is Akagera from Kigali? 

Akagera National Park is about 110 km east of Kigali, roughly a 2.5-hour drive. 

Can I add a night game drive to my 2 days Akagera safari? 

Yes. A guided night drive can be added at extra cost. It is a great way to look for leopards, hyenas, bushbabies and other nocturnal animals. 

Is an Akagera safari good for children? 

Yes. There is no minimum age for game drives or boat cruises, which makes Akagera a great family safari. 

What should I pack for Akagera? 

Pack light, neutral-coloured clothing, a warm layer for early mornings, a hat, sunscreen, insect repellent, binoculars and a camera with a zoom lens.
