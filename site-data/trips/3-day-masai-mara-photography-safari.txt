## 
This Masai Mara photography safari is built around one thing: being in the right place when the light is good. You leave camp before sunrise, stay out through the soft evening glow, and spend the harsh midday hours resting, editing and backing up your cards. 
The Masai Mara is one of the best places in Africa to photograph lions, cheetahs and leopards. Open grassland, scattered acacias and big skies give clean backgrounds, and the cats here are used to vehicles. 

## Why choose this Masai Mara photography safari? 
On a normal group safari you share the vehicle and the schedule. Here the vehicle is private, so you decide how long to wait at a sighting. If a cheetah is about to hunt, we stay. If the light is flat, we move on. 
- Private pop-up roof 4×4: room for long lenses and a clear view on every side. 
- Bean bags and charging: steady support for big lenses and power between drives. 
- Light-led timing: early starts and late returns, with packed breakfasts when needed. 
- A guide who reads behaviour: positioning for the sun, the wind and the animal’s direction. 

## What you can photograph 
Lions are the stars, and prides often rest in the open in the early morning. Cheetahs hunt in daylight, which makes them perfect subjects. Leopards are harder to find but are seen along the riverine forest. 
Between the cats you will have elephants, giraffes, topi on termite mounds, hyenas and huge skies at sunset. From July to October the wildebeest migration adds river crossings, covered in our Mara River crossing safari . 

### Optional balloon flight 
A sunrise balloon flight gives you aerial images of herds and rivers that you cannot get from the ground. Read our Masai Mara balloon safari guide before you decide, and book early in high season. 

## Best time for a Masai Mara photography safari 
The Mara is good all year. July to October brings the migration and the most action, but also the most vehicles. January to March is quieter, with green grass and plenty of young animals. The long rains in April and May can make roads muddy, but the light after a storm is beautiful. 

## Camera tips for the Mara 
- Bring a 100-400 mm or 150-600 mm zoom for wildlife and a wide lens for landscapes. 
- Use a fast shutter speed (1/2000 s or more) for running cheetahs and birds in flight. 
- Pack at least two batteries and plenty of memory cards. Dust is everywhere, so bring a blower.

Day 1 : 
Nairobi to the Masai Mara 

Morning pick-up in Nairobi and drive through the Great Rift Valley to the Masai Mara, arriving in time for lunch. After a short rest, head out for your first game drive in the warm late-afternoon light, looking for lions and cheetahs. Return to camp after sunset for dinner. 

Day 2 : 
Full day of photography in the Masai Mara 

Leave camp before sunrise to catch the cats while they are still active. Stay out with a packed breakfast, then return to camp for the midday heat to rest and back up your images. The afternoon drive runs until the park closes, giving you sunset silhouettes on the plains. An optional balloon flight can be added at dawn. 

Day 3 : 
Sunrise game drive and return to Nairobi 

One last sunrise drive for golden light on the grasslands. Return to camp for breakfast, check out and drive back to Nairobi, arriving in the late afternoon. You can also take a short flight back to save time.

How long is the Masai Mara photography safari? 

It is 3 days and 2 nights. You drive or fly from Nairobi on day 1, spend a full day in the reserve on day 2 and return after a sunrise game drive on day 3. We can add more nights if you want more time with the cats. 

Is the vehicle private? 

Yes. The pop-up roof 4×4 is only for you and your group, so you decide how long to stay at each sighting and when to move on. 

What lens should I bring to the Masai Mara? 

A 100-400 mm or 150-600 mm zoom covers most wildlife. Add a wide-angle lens for landscapes and skies. A second camera body saves time changing lenses in the dust. 

Can I fly to the Masai Mara instead of driving? 

Yes. Daily flights leave Nairobi for airstrips in the reserve and take about 45 minutes. The drive takes 5 to 6 hours. Flying gives you more time for photography. 

When is the best time to photograph the Masai Mara? 

July to October is best for the migration and river crossings. January to March is quieter, with green landscapes and young animals. Big cats are seen all year. 

Is the balloon flight included? 

No, the balloon flight is optional and paid separately. We can book it for you when you confirm your safari. 

Can beginners join this photography safari? 

Yes. Your guide helps with positioning and timing, and you can ask for simple camera tips during the drives. Phones and compact cameras are welcome too.
