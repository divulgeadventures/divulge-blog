## Overview 
This 4 day Kidepo Valley National Park safari is a private road trip from Entebbe or Kampala to Uganda’s remote northeast. You spend 1 night at Cassia Lodge, then 2 nights near Kidepo Valley National Park with a sunrise game drive and a Karamojong cultural visit. The dry months, roughly September to March, are usually best. Prices start from USD 1,789 per adult, for travellers who want an uncrowded park. 
It uses a pop-up roof extended Land Cruiser with an English-speaking driver guide, park fees, a ranger on game drives and full board, with a choice of budget, mid-range or luxury lodges. 

## Why choose a 4 day Kidepo Valley National Park safari? 
Kidepo Valley National Park lies in the far northeast of Uganda, near the South Sudan border, and is one of the country’s most remote and least visited parks. Its open savannah, seasonal rivers and rocky outcrops are ringed by mountains, and the park has more than 470 recorded bird species, as well as lions, elephants and large buffalo herds. 
The 4 day Kidepo Valley National Park safari also includes time with the Karamojong, a pastoralist people of the region, so you see both the wildlife and the culture of northern Uganda in one short trip. 
- Repeat Africa visitors: a wild park with few other vehicles. 
- Birders: more than 470 recorded species in the park. 
- Culture lovers: a guided visit to a Karamojong community. 

## What to expect on the 4 day Kidepo Valley National Park safari 
Day 1 starts with a welcome at Entebbe International Airport and a transfer to your hotel, about 10 minutes in Entebbe or 45 to 60 minutes to Kampala, with dinner and the night at Cassia Lodge. 
Day 2 is a long road day north. You leave at about 5:00 am, stop at Karuma Falls for photos, and continue through Gulu and Kitgum with lunch on the way, reaching Adere Safari Lodge in the late afternoon. 
Day 3 begins with a sunrise game drive in Kidepo Valley National Park, when predators are most active, followed by breakfast and lunch at the lodge. In the afternoon you visit a Karamojong community to see manyattas (traditional homesteads), dances, songs and cattle keeping, then stay the night at Kidepo Savannah Lodge. On day 4 you drive back south with lunch en route and are dropped at your hotel in Kampala or Entebbe, or at Entebbe International Airport. 

### Is 4 days enough for Kidepo Valley National Park? 
Four days is enough for one full day in Kidepo Valley National Park, but two of the days are long drives, so the 4 day Kidepo Valley National Park safari suits travellers who do not mind time on the road. If you want more game drives in the Narus Valley, ask us to add a night. Compare Kidepo with Uganda’s other parks in our best national parks to visit in Uganda guide .

Day 01 : 
Arrival at Uganda the Pearl of Africa. 

Upon arrival at Entebbe International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by a professional safari representative from Divulge Adventure Safaris who will assist you with your transfer to your hotel in either Kampala or Entebbe depending on your preference and arrival time. The drive takes approximately 10 minutes in Entebbe or about 45–60 minutes to Kampala 
After check-in, you will have time to relax from your jetlag. In the evening, you will enjoy dinner at your hotel and settle into your first night in Uganda in preparation for your safari adventure the following day. Dinner and overnight at Cassia Lodge . 

Day 02 : 
Entebbe/ Kampala- Kidepo Valley National Park 

After an early breakfast at around 5: 00 am you will be picked up by your safari driver-guide and begin your scenic journey to Kidepo Valley National Park.The drive takes you through beautiful countryside landscapes, local villages, rolling hills, and vast plains of northern Uganda. 
Along the way, you will pass through the famous Karuma Falls where you will make a brief stop to admire the powerful waterfalls and take memorable photographs. 
Continue the journey through Gulu and Kitgum while enjoying changing scenery and learning about the culture and history of northern Uganda from your guide.Lunch will be served en route before proceeding to the park. As you approach Kidepo, the landscape transforms into endless savannah plains surrounded by distant mountain ranges, offering breathtaking views and opportunities to spot wildlife even before entering the park. 
Arrive at Adere Safari Lodge i n the late afternoon, check in, relax, and enjoy dinner as you prepare for the next day’s safari adventures 

Day 03 : 
Full day at Kidepo Valley National Park 

Your day begins with an early sunrise game drive in the magnificent wilderness of Kidepo Valley National Park, widely regarded as one of Africa’s best safari destinations because of its untouched beauty and incredible concentration of wildlife.The morning hours offer the best opportunities to spot predators and other wildlife while they are most active. 
Kidepo is also a paradise for bird lovers with over 470 bird species recorded in the park.Enjoy the spectacular scenery featuring open savannah plains, seasonal rivers, rocky outcrops, and panoramic mountain views stretching across the Uganda–South Sudan border. 
After the game drive, return to the lodge for a full breakfast and relaxation. Lunch will be served at the lodge before heading out in the afternoon for a cultural experience with the local Karamojong community. 
During the cultural visit, you will have the opportunity to learn about traditional Karamojong lifestyles, visit local manyattas (traditional homesteads), experience traditional dances and songs, Learn about cattle keeping practices, Interact with local elders and community members, discover their unique customs, food, dress code, and traditions.This cultural interaction provides a deeper understanding of one of East Africa’s most fascinating indigenous communities. 
Return to the lodge in the evening for dinner, relaxation, and overnight stay at Kidepo Savannah Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Kidepo Valley National Park - Entebbe / Kampala 

After an early breakfast, check out of the lodge and begin your return journey to Kampala. 
Enjoy another scenic drive through northern Uganda with lunch served en route. You may make brief stops along the journey for refreshments, stretching, and photography depending on time and preference. 
Arrive in Kampala or Entebbe in the late afternoon or evening where you will either be dropped at your hotel or transferred to Entebbe International Airport for your onward flight.

When is the best time for the 4 day Kidepo Valley National Park safari? 

The 4 day Kidepo Valley National Park safari runs all year, but the dry season, roughly September to March, is usually best for game viewing because animals gather near the remaining water. The wetter months bring green landscapes and good birding, but some tracks in and around the park can be muddier.
