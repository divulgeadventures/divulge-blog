## Overview 
Experience the ultimate wilderness escape at Kidepo Valley National Park, Africa’s most remote and breathtaking savanna destination. This curated adventure offers immersive game drives through the Narus Valley, where lions, elephants, and massive buffalo herds roam against a backdrop of jagged mountains. Explore the rugged Kidepo Valley to spot unique species like cheetahs and ostriches, and visit the Kanangorok Hot Springs near the Sudanese border. Your journey is enriched by authentic cultural encounters with the Karamojong people, providing deep insight into ancient pastoralist traditions. Whether arriving by scenic flight or adventurous road trip, our Kidepo package includes expert guiding and stays at stunning lodges overlooking the plains. Book now for an unforgettable encounter with the raw, untouched heart of the African continent.

Day 01 : 
Arrival at Uganda the Pearl of Africa. 

Upon arrival at Entebbe International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by a professional safari representative from Divulge Adventure Safaris who will assist you with your transfer to your hotel in either Kampala or Entebbe depending on your preference and arrival time. The drive takes approximately 10 minutes in Entebbe or about 45–60 minutes to Kampala 
After check-in, you will have time to relax from your jetlag. In the evening, you will enjoy dinner at your hotel and settle into your first night in Uganda in preparation for your safari adventure the following day. Dinner and overnight at Cassia Lodge . 

Day 02 : 
Entebbe/ Kampala- Kidepo Valley National Park 

After an early breakfast at around 5: 00 am you will be picked up by your safari driver-guide and begin your scenic journey to Kidepo Valley National Park.The drive takes you through beautiful countryside landscapes, local villages, rolling hills, and vast plains of northern Uganda. Along the way, you will pass through the famous Karuma Falls where you will make a brief stop to admire the powerful waterfalls and take memorable photographs. 
Continue the journey through Gulu and Kitgum while enjoying changing scenery and learning about the culture and history of northern Uganda from your guide.Lunch will be served en route before proceeding to the park. As you approach Kidepo, the landscape transforms into endless savannah plains surrounded by distant mountain ranges, offering breathtaking views and opportunities to spot wildlife even before entering the park. 
Arrive at Adere Safari Lodge i n the late afternoon, check in, relax, and enjoy dinner as you prepare for the next day’s safari adventures 

Day 03 : 
Full day at Kidepo Valley National Park 

Your day begins with an early sunrise game drive in the magnificent wilderness of Kidepo Valley National Park, widely regarded as one of Africa’s best safari destinations because of its untouched beauty and incredible concentration of wildlife.The morning hours offer the best opportunities to spot predators and other wildlife while they are most active. 
Kidepo is also a paradise for bird lovers with over 470 bird species recorded in the park.Enjoy the spectacular scenery featuring open savannah plains, seasonal rivers, rocky outcrops, and panoramic mountain views stretching across the Uganda–South Sudan border. 
After the game drive, return to the lodge for a full breakfast and relaxation. Lunch will be served at the lodge before heading out in the afternoon for a cultural experience with the local Karamojong community. 
During the cultural visit, you will have the opportunity to learn about traditional Karamojong lifestyles, visit local manyattas (traditional homesteads), experience traditional dances and songs, Learn about cattle keeping practices, Interact with local elders and community members, discover their unique customs, food, dress code, and traditions.This cultural interaction provides a deeper understanding of one of East Africa’s most fascinating indigenous communities. 
Return to the lodge in the evening for dinner, relaxation, and overnight stay at Kidepo Savannah Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Kidepo Valley National Park - Entebbe / Kampala 

After an early breakfast, check out of the lodge and begin your return journey to Kampala. 
Enjoy another scenic drive through northern Uganda with lunch served en route. You may make brief stops along the journey for refreshments, stretching, and photography depending on time and preference. 
Arrive in Kampala or Entebbe in the late afternoon or evening where you will either be dropped at your hotel or transferred to Entebbe International Airport for your onward flight.
