## 
This 10 day Mount Kenya trek and safari starts with a three-day climb up the Sirimon route to Point Lenana (4,985 m) and down to the Chogoria side. You then spend two nights in Samburu National Reserve, a night at Ol Pejeta Conservancy, a night at Lake Naivasha and two nights in the Masai Mara before returning to Nairobi. 
It is ideal for fit travellers, families with teenagers and groups who want the mountain and a full safari in one trip. 

## Why choose the 10 day Mount Kenya trek and safari? 
Samburu, Ol Pejeta and the Mara show three very different Kenyas: the dry north with Grevy’s zebras and reticulated giraffes, the rhino sanctuary of the Laikipia plateau, and the open savannah of the Mara. Mount Kenya sits right between them, so the trek fits naturally at the start. 

## What to expect on the 10 day Mount Kenya trek and safari 
Day 1 takes you from Nanyuki to Old Moses Camp, day 2 to Shipton’s Camp, and day 3 is the long summit day: Point Lenana at sunrise and a descent to the bandas near Chogoria Gate. On day 4 you drive to Samburu for an afternoon game drive, day 5 is a full day there, and day 6 moves to Ol Pejeta. Days 7 to 9 include Lake Naivasha and Hell’s Gate, a boat ride and the Masai Mara, and day 10 returns to Nairobi. 

### When is the best time? 
January to February and late June to early October are best for the climb, and July to October adds the Great Migration in the Masai Mara. 
See our guides to Samburu National Reserve and Samburu vs the Masai Mara .

Day 1 : 
Nairobi to Old Moses Camp via Sirimon Gate 

Drive to Nanyuki for lunch, then walk about 9 km (3 to 4 hours) from Sirimon Gate to Old Moses Camp at about 3,300 m. 

Day 2 : 
Old Moses Camp to Shipton's Camp 

Cross the Ontulili River and follow Mackinder's Valley to Shipton's Camp (about 4,200 m), around 6 to 7 hours. 

Day 3 : 
Point Lenana and down to Chogoria 

Start at 3 a.m. for sunrise on Point Lenana (4,985 m), then descend past Mintos Hut to the bandas near Chogoria Gate, an 11 to 12 hour day. 

Day 4 : 
Chogoria to Samburu National Reserve 

Short walk down the forest road, then drive north to Samburu National Reserve for an afternoon game drive along the Ewaso Nyiro River. 

Day 5 : 
Full day in Samburu 

Morning and afternoon game drives looking for Samburu's special species, elephants and big cats. 

Day 6 : 
Samburu to Ol Pejeta Conservancy 

Drive south via Nanyuki to Ol Pejeta, with a game drive on arrival and an evening drive in search of rhinos. 

Day 7 : 
Ol Pejeta to Lake Naivasha 

Drive along the edge of the Aberdares into the Rift Valley. In the afternoon, cycle in Hell's Gate National Park. 

Day 8 : 
Lake Naivasha to the Masai Mara 

Morning boat ride on Lake Naivasha, then drive via Narok to the Masai Mara for an afternoon game drive. 

Day 9 : 
Full day in the Masai Mara 

A full day of game viewing with a bush picnic lunch. 

Day 10 : 
Masai Mara to Nairobi 

Early game drive, breakfast and the drive back to Nairobi.

How fit do I need to be for the 10 day Mount Kenya trek and safari? 

For the 10 day Mount Kenya trek and safari you should be able to walk 6 to 7 hours on consecutive days, plus one summit day of 11 to 12 hours. No climbing skills are needed. Six to eight weeks of hill walking is good preparation. 

Can teenagers join this trip? 

Yes. Fit teenagers often enjoy this trip. Altitude affects everyone differently, so our guides keep a slow pace and watch for symptoms. Tell us their ages when you enquire. 

When is the best time for this trek and safari? 

The best time is January to February or late June to early October, when Mount Kenya is driest. July to October also brings the Great Migration to the Masai Mara. 

What lodges are used on the safari? 

You stay in comfortable lodges and tented camps on full board in Samburu, Ol Pejeta, Lake Naivasha and the Masai Mara. We confirm the exact properties in your quote to suit your budget. 

Can this be arranged for a group or company? 

Yes. We plan this trip for groups, clubs and companies, adding guides and safari vehicles as needed. Contact us with your group size and dates.
