## Overview 
This 8 day family Tanzania and Zanzibar safari flies you to 3 nights at Serengeti Serena, then 3 beach nights at Zanzibar Beach Resort, after 1 night at Arusha Serena Hotel. Domestic flights, park fees and a private guide and vehicle are included. The page gives no fixed season, and it costs from USD 4,120 per adult and USD 2,060 per child, so it suits families. 

## Why choose an 8 day family Tanzania and Zanzibar safari? 
The 8 day family Tanzania and Zanzibar safari is built around short travel days. Flights link Arusha, the Serengeti and Zanzibar, so children spend their time on game drives and the beach rather than on the road, and the private vehicle lets you stop or return to the lodge when they are tired. 
The Serengeti days bring morning and afternoon game drives in search of the Big Five, predators and the great herds, and the Zanzibar days are free for swimming, rest and optional water sports. 
- Families: a child price of USD 2,060 and a private guide and vehicle. 
- Easy logistics: domestic flights, airport transfers and park fees included. 
- Two halves: 3 Serengeti nights and 3 beach nights. 

## What to expect on the 8 day family Tanzania and Zanzibar safari 
Day 1 starts at Kilimanjaro International Airport, where your driver-guide meets you for the 1-hour transfer to Arusha. Dinner and the night are at Arusha Serena Hotel. On day 2 you fly about 1 hour from Arusha Airport to Seronera Airstrip, game drive to your lodge and head out again in the afternoon. 
Days 3 and 4 are full Serengeti days with morning and afternoon game drives. All three Serengeti nights are at Serengeti Serena with dinner. On day 5 you fly about 2 hours to Zanzibar and transfer to Zanzibar Beach Resort. 
Days 6 and 7 are free at the resort for swimming, optional water sports and excursions. Day 8 is your transfer to Zanzibar Airport for your flight home. 

### Is a Serengeti and Zanzibar safari good for children? 
A Serengeti and Zanzibar safari works well for children because the flights keep travel short and the beach days balance the early game drives. Read our family safari planning guide for health and safety tips, our best time to visit Tanzania guide for school-holiday dates, and our Zanzibar beach extension guide for things to do on the island.

- Included: all accommodation and meals as per the itinerary, half board in Zanzibar, a private guide and safari vehicle, unlimited game drives, park entry fees, in-car binoculars, airport transfers, domestic flights, emergency evacuation insurance and Zanzibar health insurance. 
- Not included: international flights, the hot air balloon safari, personal insurance, souvenirs, alcoholic drinks and extra meals, tips, the Tanzania visa, the online payment surcharge and optional Zanzibar activities.

Day 01 : 
Arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport 

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your professional driver-guide and transferred to your hotel in Arusha which is approximately 1 hour drive ready to start your family Tanzania and Zanzibar Safari. 
Depending on your arrival time, you may relax at the hotel and later leave and explore the city to enjoy the beautiful surroundings, or you will take time to recover from your international flight before the start of your safari adventure. Dinner and overnight stay at Arusha Serena Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Arusha to Serengeti National Park 

After an early breakfast, you will be transfered to Arusha Airport for your scheduled flight to Serengeti National Park which is aproximately 1 hour ready to start your Tanzania and Zanzibar Safari . Upon arrival at Seronera Airstrip, enjoy a game drive en route to your luxury lodge. 
In the afternoon, head out for your first exciting safari experience across the endless plains of the Serengeti, home to abundant wildlife and breathtaking landscapes. Dinner and overnight stay at Serengeti Serena 

Day 03 & 04 : 
Full Day Safari in Serengeti 

You will begin your Tanzania and Zanzibar safari by Spending a full day exploring the spectacular Serengeti ecosystem with morning and afternoon game drives. Your experienced guide will track wildlife movements, including predators and the great herds, giving you the best possible safari experience in one of Africa’s most iconic national parks. Dinner and overnigt stay at Serengeti Serena 

Day 05 : 
Serengeti to Zanzibar 

After breakfast, transfer to the airstrip for your scheduled flight to Zanzibar the flight is approximately 2 hours. Upon arrival, meet your representative and transfer to your beachfront resort to have a family Tanzania and Zanzibar Safari. Spend the rest of the day relaxing on the white sandy beaches and enjoying the tranquil atmosphere of the island. Dinner and overnight stay at Zanzibar Beach Resort 

Day 06 & 07 : 
Relaxation in Zanzibar 

Enjoy leisurely days at your beachfront resort for yor family Tanzania and Zanzibar Safari. You may relax by the ocean, swim in the turquoise waters, enjoy optional water sports and excursions, or simply unwind while taking in the beautiful tropical surroundings. Dinner and overnight stay at Zanzibar Beach Resort 

Day 08 : 
Departure 

After breakfast, check out from your hotel and transfer to Zanzibar Airport for your onward international flight back home, marking the end of your unforgettable Tanzania and Zanzibar Safari beach holiday.
