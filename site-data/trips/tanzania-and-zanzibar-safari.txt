## Overview 
Experience this 8 day Family Tanzania and Zanzibar Safari the perfect blend of adventure and relaxation on this unforgettable beach holiday. 
Your journey begins with a warm welcome at Kilimanjaro International Airport followed by an overnight stay in Arusha, the gateway to Tanzania’s northern safari circuit. 
From there, fly to the world-famous Serengeti National Park for three nights of thrilling wildlife encounters, where you will enjoy extensive game drives across the endless plains in search of the Big Five, predators, and the spectacular wildlife that make the Serengeti one of Africa’s most iconic safari destinations. 
After an immersive safari experience, continue to the exotic island of Zanzibar for a relaxing beach escape along the Indian Ocean. 
Spend your days unwinding on pristine white-sand beaches, enjoying the tranquil tropical atmosphere, and taking in the breathtaking ocean views from your luxury beachfront resort. 
This itinerary perfectly combines the excitement of an African safari with the serenity of a tropical island getaway, creating a truly memorable East African experience.

- Tanzania and Zanzibar Safari

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
Arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport 

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your professional driver-guide and transferred to your hotel in Arusha which is approximately 1 hour drive ready to start your family Tanzania and Zanzibar Safari. Depending on your arrival time, you may relax at the hotel and later leave and explore the city to enjoy the beautiful surroundings, or you will take time to recover from your international flight before the start of your safari adventure. Dinner and overnight stay at Arusha Serena Hotel 

Day 02 : 
Arusha to Serengeti National Park 

After an early breakfast, you will be transfered to Arusha Airport for your scheduled flight to Serengeti National Park which is aproximately 1 hour ready to start your Tanzania and Zanzibar Safari . Upon arrival at Seronera Airstrip, enjoy a game drive en route to your luxury lodge. In the afternoon, head out for your first exciting safari experience across the endless plains of the Serengeti, home to abundant wildlife and breathtaking landscapes. Dinner and overnight stay at Serengeti Serena 

Day 03 & 04 : 
Full Day Safari in Serengeti 

You will begin your Tanzania and Zanzibar safari by Spending a full day exploring the spectacular Serengeti ecosystem with morning and afternoon game drives. Your experienced guide will track wildlife movements, including predators and the great herds, giving you the best possible safari experience in one of Africa’s most iconic national parks. Dinner and overnigt stay at Serengeti Serena 

Day 05 : 
Serengeti to Zanzibar 

After breakfast, transfer to the airstrip for your scheduled flight to Zanzibar the flight is approximately 2 hours. Upon arrival, meet your representative and transfer to your beachfront resort to have a family Tanzania and Zanzibar Safari. Spend the rest of the day relaxing on the white sandy beaches and enjoying the tranquil atmosphere of the island. Dinner and overnight stay at Zanzibar Beach Resort 

Day 06 & 07 : 
Relaxation in Zanzibar 

Enjoy leisurely days at your beachfront resort for yor family Tanzania and Zanzibar Safari. You may relax by the ocean, swim in the turquoise waters, enjoy optional water sports and excursions, or simply unwind while taking in the beautiful tropical surroundings. Dinner and overnight stay at Zanzibar Beach Resort 

Day 08 : 
Departure 

After breakfast, check out from your hotel and transfer to Zanzibar Airport for your onward international flight back home, marking the end of your unforgettable Tanzania and Zanzibar Safari beach holiday.
