## Overview 
This 1 day Akagera safari from Kigali is a private full-day game drive in Akagera National Park, Rwanda’s only savannah and Big Five park, about 2.5 hours east of the city. You leave Kigali at around 5:30 AM and return in the evening, with a picnic lunch and park fees included. The best months are June to September and December to February, and the price starts from USD 310 per adult. 
It suits travellers with one free day in Kigali, business visitors and anyone adding a wildlife day before or after a gorilla trek in Volcanoes National Park, travelling in a private 4×4 Land Cruiser with a pop-up roof. 

## Why choose a 1 day Akagera safari from Kigali? 
Akagera National Park is the only place in Rwanda to see lions, elephants, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras and the reintroduced black rhinos, set among rolling plains, acacia woodland and papyrus-fringed lakes. Because the park is close to Kigali, one long day gives you a full game drive without a night away from your hotel. 
- Short on time: a full safari day with hotel or airport pick-up and drop-off in Kigali. 
- Gorilla trekkers: savannah wildlife to round off a primate trip in Rwanda. 
- Birders: over 480 bird species, including grey crowned cranes and African fish eagles. 

## What to expect on the 1 day Akagera safari from Kigali 
The day starts with pick-up from your Kigali hotel at around 5:30 AM and a drive of about 2.5 hours east to Akagera National Park. After a short briefing at the park reception, you head south to the giraffe plains, then follow the lake roads towards Birengero, where elephants come to drink and hippos wallow in the shallows. 
The game drive looks for lions resting in the shade, buffalo herds, zebras, topi and impalas, and birds such as lilac-breasted rollers. A picnic lunch is served at a scenic spot inside the park before a final afternoon drive. You exit late in the afternoon and arrive back in Kigali in the evening for drop-off at your hotel or the airport. There is no overnight stay on this trip. 

### Can you see the Big Five in Akagera in one day? 
Akagera is home to lions, leopards, elephants, buffaloes and rhinos. Most visitors on a day trip see elephants, buffaloes and plenty of plains game, and lions and rhinos are seen regularly, but no sighting is guaranteed. The dry seasons from June to September and December to February are best because animals gather near the lakes. See our guide to Rwanda in December for what a dry-season visit is like.

Can you do a 1 day Akagera safari from Kigali? 

Yes, a 1 day Akagera safari from Kigali is very doable because the park is about 2.5 hours east of the city. Your driver guide picks you up at around 5:30 AM, you spend the whole day on a game drive with a picnic lunch, and you are back at your Kigali hotel or the airport in the evening. 

What is the best time for the 1 day Akagera safari from Kigali? 

The best time for this 1 day Akagera safari from Kigali is the dry seasons, June to September and December to February, when animals gather near the lakes and the park tracks are easier to drive. Akagera National Park is open all year, so the day trip runs in every month.

Is the Akagera day trip from Kigali suitable for children and families? 

Yes, the Akagera day trip from Kigali suits families because it is a private safari with no walking, and the pace can be adjusted. It is a long day, with a 5:30 AM start and about 2.5 hours of driving each way, so tell us your children’s ages and we can plan rest stops.
