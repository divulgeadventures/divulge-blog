## 
This Kenya highlands birding safari is for birders who love forest birds. It includes two nights at Castle Forest on Mount Kenya, a night at The Ark in the Aberdares and three nights in Kakamega, plus the dry north, the Rift Valley and the Masai Mara. 
It is a great choice from January to March, when the weather is mostly dry and migrants are still present. 

## Why choose this Kenya highlands birding safari? 
- Castle Forest: African wood owl, Hartlaub’s turaco and green ibis. 
- The Aberdares: crowned eagle, Abyssinian crimsonwing and night-time mammals at The Ark. 
- Samburu: three nights for Somali specials and vulturine guineafowl. 
- Rift Valley lakes: Naivasha, Nakuru and Baringo. 
- Kakamega and the Mara: rainforest birds and big cats. 

## Where you stay 
Boulevard Hotel in Nairobi, Castle Forest Lodge, Samburu Sopa Lodge, Naro Moru River Lodge, The Ark, Elsamere at Lake Naivasha, Flamingo Hill Tented Camp at Nakuru, Soi Lodge, Rondo Retreat, Mara West Camp and Lake Naivasha Resort, or similar. 

## Best time for a Kenya highlands birding safari 
January to March is ideal, with drier weather and migrants still around. The highlands can be cold at night, so pack warm layers.

Buffalo Springs to Naro Moru 

Morning at Buffalo Springs, a stop in Meru Forest, then Naro Moru River Lodge.

Mount Kenya to the Aberdares 

Drive into the Aberdares for forest birds and an evening at The Ark. 

Day 9 : 
Aberdares to Lake Naivasha 

Drive down the escarpment to Elsamere on Lake Naivasha, with an optional boat ride. 

Day 10 : 
Lake Nakuru 

Flamingos, pelicans and rhinos. Overnight at Flamingo Hill Tented Camp. 

Day 11 : 
Lake Nakuru to Lake Baringo 

Morning at Nakuru, then on to Soi Lodge. 

Day 12 : 
Lake Baringo 

Semi-desert birding and a boat ride for Heuglin's courser and Hemprich's hornbill. 

Day 13 : 
Kerio Valley to Kakamega 

Drive west via the Kerio Valley to Rondo Retreat. 

Day 14 : 
Kakamega Forest 

Rainforest birding for great blue turaco and casqued hornbills.

Kakamega Forest 

More forest birding and primates. 

Day 16 :

Papyrus birding at Lake Victoria, then drive to Mara West Camp. 

Day 17 : 
Masai Mara 

A full day of game drives and birding.

Morning game drive, then drive to Lake Naivasha Resort. 

Day 19 : 
Lake Naivasha to Nairobi 

Boat trip on the lake and stops at Kinangop, Gatamaiyu and Limuru, then transfer to the airport.

What makes this a highlands safari? 

It spends more time than usual in Kenya’s highland forests on Mount Kenya, the Aberdares and Kakamega, where many forest specials live. 

Which lodges are used on the Kenya highlands birding safari? 

Boulevard Hotel, Castle Forest Lodge, Samburu Sopa Lodge, Naro Moru River Lodge, The Ark, Elsamere, Flamingo Hill Tented Camp, Soi Lodge, Rondo Retreat, Mara West Camp and Lake Naivasha Resort, or similar. 

How cold does it get? 

Nights in the highlands can drop to around 5 to 10 °C. Bring a warm fleece and jacket. 

What is The Ark? 

A lodge in the Aberdare forest overlooking a floodlit waterhole, where elephants, buffaloes and sometimes leopards visit at night. 

How many species can I expect? 

Many birders record 500 to 600 species in 19 days. 

Is this trip suitable for older travellers? 

Yes. Most birding is from the vehicle or on easy trails, though the days are long. 

Can I add Amboseli? 

Yes. Two nights in Amboseli can be added at the end.
