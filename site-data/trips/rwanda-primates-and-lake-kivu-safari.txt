## Overview 
This Rwanda primates and Lake Kivu safari is a gentle, well-rounded journey. It starts with a full day in Kigali, continues to Nyungwe for chimpanzees and the canopy walk, slows down on Lake Kivu, and ends with mountain gorillas and golden monkeys. 
Unlike our more active eight-day trip, there is no volcano hike. Instead you get extra time for culture, food and the lake, which makes this itinerary ideal for travellers who like a relaxed pace. 

## Why choose a Rwanda primates and Lake Kivu safari? 
Rwanda’s three great primate experiences sit in different corners of the country. Linking them through Lake Kivu turns long drives into a scenic loop, with a beach break exactly when you need it. 
- Culture lovers: a full day in Kigali and the King’s Palace. 
- Primate fans: chimps, gorillas and golden monkeys. 
- Slow travellers: sunset cruises and lakeside evenings. 

## What to expect on your Rwanda primates and Lake Kivu safari 
Day two is dedicated to Kigali: the Genocide Memorial, Kimironko Market and the colourful Nyamirambo neighbourhood. On day three you drive to Nyungwe via the King’s Palace Museum in Nyanza. 
Day four is your chimpanzee trek and the canopy walkway. On day five you try tea picking and continue to Kibuye for a sunset boat cruise among Lake Kivu’s islands. Day six includes a coffee tour and the drive north to Musanze, with a stop at the Ellen DeGeneres Campus. 
Day seven is gorilla day, followed by the Gorilla Guardians Village. On day eight you trek golden monkeys and return to Kigali. 

### How fit do you need to be? 
Average fitness is enough. Primate treks can be steep and muddy, but porters are available and your guide will set a comfortable pace. 

## Best time for this safari 
June to September and December to February are drier and great for trekking. The green season brings fewer visitors and lush forest, and Nyungwe is beautiful in any month. 
Read our guide to gorilla trekking in Rwanda , or see the shorter Nyungwe and Lake Kivu tour . Forest information is on Visit Rwanda’s Nyungwe National Park page .

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is your 8-day Rwanda primates and Lake Kivu safari day by day. 

Day 01 : 
Arrival in Kigali 

Upon arrival at Kigali International Airport, you will be met by your Divulge Adventures driver guide, with time to exchange money at the airport. Transfer to your hotel to check in and rest. Dinner and overnight at Park Inn by Radisson Kigali | Hotel Chez Lando 

Day 02 : 
Kigali City Tour 

After breakfast, spend the day discovering Kigali. Visit the Kigali Genocide Memorial, browse the crafts and fresh produce at Kimironko Market, and enjoy lunch at a local restaurant. If time allows, explore Nyamirambo, Kigali's most colourful neighbourhood, known for its street life, tailors and cafés. Dinner and overnight at Park Inn by Radisson Kigali | Hotel Chez Lando 

Day 03 : 
Kigali - Nyanza - Nyungwe 

After breakfast, drive southwest through rolling hills to Nyanza for a visit to the King's Palace Museum. Continue to Nyungwe with a lunch stop en route, arriving in the late afternoon to check in. Dinner and overnight at Nyungwe Top View Hill Hotel | Gisakura Guest House 

Day 04 : 
Chimpanzee Trekking and Canopy Walk 

After an early breakfast, set out on a guided chimpanzee trek and spend one hour with a habituated community. Later, walk the canopy walkway about 70 metres above the forest floor for spectacular views of the rainforest. Dinner and overnight at Nyungwe Top View Hill Hotel | Gisakura Guest House 

Day 05 : 
Tea Plantation - Lake Kivu (Kibuye) Sunset Cruise 

After breakfast, visit a tea plantation to try tea picking and learn how the leaves are processed. Then drive to Kibuye on Lake Kivu, approximately 3 hours. In the late afternoon, enjoy a sunset boat cruise among the lake's islands. Dinner and overnight at Cormoran Lodge | Rwiza Village 

Day 06 : 
Coffee Experience - Musanze (Ellen DeGeneres Campus) 

After breakfast, visit a local coffee cooperative for a crop-to-cup tour and tasting. Continue north to Musanze and stop at the Ellen DeGeneres Campus of the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund to prepare for tomorrow's trek. Check in at your lodge near Volcanoes National Park. Dinner and overnight at Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge | Kinigi Guest House 

Day 07 : 
Mountain Gorilla Trekking and Gorilla Guardians Village 

After breakfast, drive to the Kinigi park headquarters for your briefing and gorilla family assignment. Trek into the forest and spend about one hour with the gorillas. Return to the lodge for lunch, then visit the Gorilla Guardians Village in the afternoon for traditional dances and village life. Dinner and overnight at Da Vinci Gorilla Lodge | Kinigi Guest House 

Day 08 : 
Golden Monkey Trekking - Kigali (Departure) 

After an early breakfast and briefing, trek through the bamboo forest to spend about one hour with the golden monkeys. Return to the lodge and drive back to Kigali for drop-off at your hotel or Kigali International Airport.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

What is there to do in Kigali for a day? 

Popular activities include the Kigali Genocide Memorial, Kimironko Market, the Nyamirambo neighbourhood, art galleries such as Inema Arts Centre, and Kigali’s cafés and craft shops. 

Is Lake Kivu worth visiting on a Rwanda safari? 

Yes. Lake Kivu adds relaxing beaches, island boat trips and coffee experiences, a lovely break between primate treks. 

What order should I visit Nyungwe, Lake Kivu and Volcanoes National Park? 

Travelling Kigali, Nyungwe, Lake Kivu and then Volcanoes makes a natural loop, with the gorilla trek as the grand finale. 

Can this itinerary include luxury lodges? 

Yes. Divulge Adventures can upgrade every night to luxury options such as One&Only Nyungwe House and Bisate Lodge. 

How is this Rwanda primates and Lake Kivu safari different from the 8-day Bisoke trip? 

This version replaces the Mount Bisoke hike with a full day in Kigali and a sunset cruise, so it is more relaxed. 

How long do you spend with the gorillas and chimps? 

You spend one hour with each once you find them. The full trek, there and back, can take from one to several hours. 

Is this safari suitable for older travellers? 

Yes, for anyone comfortable walking a few hours on uneven ground. Porters and walking sticks make the treks easier.
