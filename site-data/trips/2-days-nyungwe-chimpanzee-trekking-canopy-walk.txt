## Overview 
Nyungwe chimpanzee trekking is Rwanda’s best primate experience after the gorillas. Nyungwe Forest National Park in the southwest is one of the oldest montane rainforests in Africa, and it is home to 13 primate species, including habituated chimpanzee communities. 
On this two-day trip you walk the canopy walkway on day one, track chimpanzees at dawn on day two and visit the King’s Palace Museum in Nyanza on the way back to Kigali. 

## Why choose Nyungwe chimpanzee trekking? 
Chimpanzees are our closest living relatives, and watching them in the wild is loud, fast and thrilling. You hear their pant-hoots echo through the valley long before you see them. 
Nyungwe adds more than chimps. The forest has waterfalls, tea-covered hills, colourful birds such as the great blue turaco, and other primates like L’Hoest’s monkeys and Angola colobus. 
- Primate lovers: chimps plus several monkey species. 
- Adventurers: the canopy walk hangs high above a forested valley. 
- Culture: a stop at the royal palace of Rwanda’s former kings. 

## What to expect on your Nyungwe chimpanzee trekking tour 
Day one is a scenic drive from Kigali through tea estates and terraced hills, about 5 to 6 hours with a lunch stop. In the afternoon you walk to the Uwinka area for the canopy walk, a suspended walkway of roughly 160 metres that gives a bird’s-eye view of the forest. 
Day two starts very early with a packed breakfast. Chimps are most active at dawn, so you set out with a ranger guide before sunrise. Once you find the community, you spend one hour with them as they feed, call and swing through the trees. 
After the trek, you drive back towards Kigali and stop at the King’s Palace Museum in Nyanza to see a traditional royal hut and the long-horned Inyambo cattle. 

### How fit do you need to be? 
Chimps move quickly, so treks can be steep and fast, lasting from one to several hours. Average fitness is enough, but good boots and a steady pace help. 

## Best time for chimpanzee trekking in Nyungwe 
The dry seasons, June to September and December to February, usually bring the best sightings because chimps feed lower and closer to the trails. Nyungwe is a rainforest, so pack a rain jacket in any month.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Here is how your 2-day Nyungwe chimpanzee trekking tour runs. 

Day 01 : 
Kigali - Nyungwe Forest National Park (Canopy Walk) 

After breakfast, you will be picked up from your Kigali hotel by your Divulge Adventures driver guide and drive southwest towards Nyungwe Forest National Park, approximately 5 to 6 hours away. The road winds through tea-covered hills and terraced farms, with a lunch stop en route. In the afternoon, head to the Uwinka visitor centre for the canopy walk. 
This suspended walkway stretches about 160 metres and hangs some 70 metres above the forest floor, giving you a bird's-eye view of the rainforest, its orchids, tree ferns and colourful birds. Later, check in and relax as the forest comes alive with evening sounds. Dinner and overnight at Nyungwe Top View Hill Hotel | Gisakura Guest House 

Day 02 : 
Chimpanzee Trekking - Nyanza King's Palace - Kigali 

Wake up very early for a packed breakfast and drive to the starting point of your chimpanzee trek, ideally before dawn when the chimps are most active. Follow your ranger guide through the forest, listening for their loud pant-hoots, and spend one hour watching them feed, play and swing through the canopy. 
Look out for other primates such as L'Hoest's monkeys and Angola colobus, and birds like the great blue turaco. Return to the lodge for a late breakfast and check out. On the drive back to Kigali, stop at the King's Palace Museum in Nyanza to explore a traditional royal hut and meet the famous long-horned Inyambo cattle. 
Arrive in Kigali in the evening for drop-off at your hotel or the airport.

## FAQs 
Expand all 

Where is the best place for chimpanzee trekking in Rwanda? 

Nyungwe Forest National Park in southwest Rwanda is the main chimpanzee trekking destination. Smaller groups can also be seen in the Gishwati-Mukura forest. 

How long is the Nyungwe canopy walk? 

The canopy walkway is roughly 150 to 160 metres long and hangs high above a forested valley. The walk, including the trail to and from it, takes around 2 to 3 hours. 

Are chimpanzee sightings guaranteed in Nyungwe? 

Sightings are not guaranteed because chimps move fast and far, but success rates are good, especially in the dry season. Starting early gives you the best chance. 

What is the minimum age for chimpanzee trekking in Rwanda? 

The usual minimum age for chimpanzee trekking is 12 years, and we confirm the current rule when you book. There is no strict age limit for the canopy walk, though young children should be supervised closely. 

How long do you spend with the chimpanzees? 

Once you find the chimpanzee community, you spend one hour with them, just like on a gorilla trek. 

How far is Nyungwe from Kigali? 

Nyungwe is about 5 to 6 hours’ drive from Kigali, through beautiful hills and tea plantations. 

What should I wear for Nyungwe chimpanzee trekking? 

Wear waterproof boots, long trousers, a long-sleeved top and a rain jacket. Gloves help on steep sections, and neutral colours are best in the forest.
