## 
This 10 day Kenya student study tour is designed for school and university groups who want to learn in the field. Students see conservation at work in Nairobi, study elephants in Amboseli, cycle through the volcanic gorge at Hell’s Gate, and finish with three nights in the Masai Mara, where predators and the migration bring ecology lessons to life. 

## What will students learn on this 10 day Kenya student study tour? 
The route covers several subjects in one trip. The Sheldrick elephant orphanage and the Giraffe Centre show how rescue and breeding programmes work. Amboseli explains how swamps fed by Kilimanjaro’s snowmelt support large elephant herds. Hell’s Gate and Lake Naivasha show Rift Valley geology, geothermal power and freshwater ecosystems, and the Maasai village visit opens a discussion about culture, land and tourism. 
Your guides brief the group each day, and teachers can set tasks, journals or species counts around the game drives. We can also arrange a talk with a ranger or a conservation organisation on request. 

## How is the study tour itinerary planned? 
Two nights in Nairobi, two in Amboseli, one at Lake Naivasha, one at Lake Nakuru and three in the Masai Mara. Drives are broken up with stops, and there is a free evening on day 2 to rest after the flight. 

### Is the tour safe for students? 
Students travel in 4×4 vehicles with experienced driver guides, stay at established lodges with security, and have a briefing on arrival. Cycling at Hell’s Gate is supervised, and we share a full risk overview with teachers before travel. 
For more planning help, read our guide to a Kenya school trip and our notes on Lake Naivasha lodges . 

## Who is the 10 day kenya student study tour best for? 
The 10 day kenya student study tour is best for secondary schools, colleges and universities, and for teachers who want a safe trip with clear learning goals. We share a full plan and risk overview with teachers before travel, so the 10 day kenya student study tour runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## Can the 10 day Kenya student study tour include community work? 
Yes. We can add a school visit, tree planting or a short community project, or replace a game drive with a conservation talk. Tell us your learning goals and the students’ ages, and we will shape the itinerary around your curriculum.

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Nairobi 

Your 10 day kenya student study tour begins today. Your group is met at the airport for a welcome and safety briefing, then transferred to the hotel to rest. Dinner and overnight at Argyle Grand Hotel or similar. 

Day 2 : 
Nairobi National Park, elephant orphanage and Giraffe Centre 

Morning game drive in Nairobi National Park, where lions, rhinos and giraffes live within sight of the city skyline. Visit the Sheldrick elephant orphanage to learn how rescued calves are raised for release, then feed endangered Rothschild giraffes at the Giraffe Centre. Free evening at the hotel. Dinner and overnight at Argyle Grand Hotel or similar. 

Day 3 : 
Nairobi to Amboseli National Park 

Drive about 4 hours to Amboseli, arriving for lunch and a rest. Afternoon game drive among elephant herds with Kilimanjaro in the background. Dinner and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Full day in Amboseli 

A full day exploring Amboseli's swamps and plains, with time to observe elephant family behaviour. Climb Observation Hill for a 360-degree view of the park and its habitats. Lunch during the game drive. Dinner and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge. 

Day 5 : 
Amboseli to Lake Naivasha 

Drive about 5 hours to Lake Naivasha, stopping at a Great Rift Valley viewpoint. In the afternoon, take a boat ride to see hippos and fish eagles, then walk among plains animals on Crescent Island. Dinner and overnight at Lake Naivasha Resort. 

Day 6 : 
Hell's Gate to Lake Nakuru 

Cycle and walk in Hell's Gate National Park, past towering cliffs, Fischer's Tower and steam vents from the geothermal field, alongside zebras and giraffes. Then drive about 2 hours to Lake Nakuru for an afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge. 

Day 7 : 
Lake Nakuru to the Masai Mara 

Drive about 5 hours to the Masai Mara, arriving for lunch. In the afternoon, visit a Maasai village to learn about customs, beadwork and how communities live alongside wildlife. Dinner and overnight at PrideInn Mara Camp. 

Day 8 : 
Full day in the Masai Mara 

A full day of game drives looking for lions, cheetahs, elephants and, from July to October, the wildebeest migration. Lunch is a picnic on the plains. Dinner and overnight at PrideInn Mara Camp. 

Day 9 : 
Second full day in the Masai Mara 

Another day in the reserve, with time for a different area such as the Mara River, where crocodiles and hippos gather. Teachers can use the day for field tasks or species counts. Dinner and overnight at PrideInn Mara Camp. 

Day 10 : 
Masai Mara to Nairobi and departure 

After breakfast, drive about 5 hours back to Nairobi and transfer to the airport for your flight home. This is the end of your 10 day kenya student study tour.

Who is the 10 day Kenya student study tour designed for? 

The 10 day Kenya student study tour suits secondary school, college and university groups studying biology, geography, environmental science or tourism. We adjust the pace, activities and briefings to the students’ ages and the subjects teachers want to cover. 

Do teachers travel free on a student study tour? 

On group bookings the seventh place is free for every six paying travellers, and that free place usually goes to a teacher or group leader. We show exactly how many free places your group earns in the quote. 

Is Hell's Gate cycling safe for students? 

Cycling at Hell’s Gate is on flat park tracks with a guide, and bicycles and helmets are provided. Students who prefer not to cycle can ride in the vehicle and join the gorge walk instead. 

Can parents pay separately for each student? 

Yes. Each family can pay their share directly. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the group’s places and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full rules are in our terms and conditions. 

When is the best time for a school trip to Kenya? 

July to August suits northern-hemisphere summer holidays and overlaps with the Great Migration in the Masai Mara. December to February is also dry. We can work around your term dates.
