## 
This Kenya Tanzania safari fits two countries into one week by crossing at the Namanga border. Start with two nights in Amboseli for elephants against the backdrop of Kilimanjaro, then continue to Tarangire, Lake Manyara and Ngorongoro Crater, finishing in Arusha. It is a practical route for travellers with limited time who want Kenya’s elephants and Tanzania’s crater in one trip. 
Price: from USD 2,540 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Two nights in Amboseli with views of Kilimanjaro 
- Tarangire and Lake Manyara game drives 
- Ngorongoro Crater game drive 
- Short Namanga border crossing 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Nairobi to Amboseli 

Drive from Nairobi to Amboseli National Park. Afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge. 

Day 2 : 
Amboseli National Park 

Full day of game drives among the elephant herds and swamps below Kilimanjaro. Dinner and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge. 

Day 3 : 
Amboseli to Tarangire 

Drive to the Namanga border, meet your Tanzanian guide and continue to Tarangire. Dinner and overnight at Tarangire Sopa Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Tarangire to Lake Manyara 

Morning game drive in Tarangire, then an afternoon game drive at Lake Manyara. Dinner and overnight at Kitela Lodge in Karatu. 

Day 5 : 
Ngorongoro Crater 

Descend into the crater for a game drive. Dinner and overnight at Kitela Lodge. 

Day 6 : 
Karatu to Arusha 

Morning visit to a coffee farm or local market in Karatu, then drive to Arusha. Overnight at Mount Meru Hotel. 

Day 7 : 
Departure from Arusha 

Transfer to Kilimanjaro airport or take the shuttle back to Nairobi.

Why cross at Namanga? 

Namanga is the main border between Nairobi and Arusha and the quickest link between Amboseli and Tanzania’s northern parks. 

Can we end in Nairobi instead? 

Yes. A shuttle or short flight links Arusha with Nairobi.
