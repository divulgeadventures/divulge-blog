## Overview 
Dive into the ultimate East African adventure with our 7 Days Wildebeest Migration Safari Sopa Lodge Circuit. This carefully crafted journey takes you through iconic parks where the dramatic movement of millions of wildebeests unfolds across vast savannahs. Enjoy comfortable stays at renowned Sopa Lodges, offering exceptional views and warm hospitality. From thrilling game drives to breathtaking landscapes, this Wildebeest Migration Safari guarantees unforgettable wildlife encounters, stunning scenery, and immersive moments in nature’s greatest migration spectacle. Perfect for travelers seeking comfort, adventure, and extraordinary memories. 

### Safari Highlights 
- Amboseli National Park 
- Lake Naivasha 
- Lake Elementaita 
- Lake Nakuru National Park 
- Masai Mara National Reserve 

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Enjoy 7 Days Wildebeest Migration Safari Sopa Lodge Circuit immerses you in the heart of East Africa’s most dramatic wildlife movement. Explore legendary parks while enjoying the comfort of Sopa Lodges, perfectly positioned for incredible sightings. This Wildebeest Migration Safari promises unforgettable game drives, breathtaking landscapes, and close encounters with the migrating herds, creating a truly remarkable safari experience. 

Day 01 : 
Nairobi - Amboseli National Park 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by Divulge Adventures representative for meet, greet andyour wildebeest migration safari briefing. Afterward, you will depart Nairobi for Amboseli National Park, approximately 4 hours. drive. Arriving at around lunchtime for check-in and lunch, followed by afternoon siesta. Later in the evening set out for evening game drive at Amboseli National Park, and later return to the lodge for dinner and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 02 : 
Full Day at Amboseli National Park 

After breakfast, set out for a full-day game drive into the home of large herds of elephants and the Kilimanjaro royal court. The game drive takes you to Kenya's premium park, which is rich in wildlife, from up close free-ranging African Elephants to experiencing the epic view of the tallest mountain in Africa, Mt Kilimanjaro. You will also visit Observation hill, where you can view the entire park in 360 degrees from the top. Lunch will be served as you proceed with the game drive and later return for dinner, and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Amboseli National Park - Lake Naivasha - Lake Nakuru National Park 

After a steamy hot delicious breakfast depart Amboseli for Lake Naivasha, which is approximately 5 hours away. There will be a stop to view the great rift valley scenery as you proceed to Lake Naivasha. You will arrive in time for lunch. After lunch, proceed for boat safari at Lake Naivasha before heading to Nakuru for check in at the Hotel, dinner and overnight at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Lake Nakuru National Park - Masai Mara National Reserve 

After a steamy hot delicious breakfast set exit Lake Nakuru National Park with and enroute game drive to Masai Mara Reserve approximately 5 hours drive. Check-in at the camp by lunchtime. Later in the afternoon, head for an afternoon game drive. The cooling air stirs wildlife into action—lions begin to hunt, elephants gather near waterholes, and herds of wildebeest move across the plains. The sky transforms into shades of orange and purple, creating a breathtaking backdrop for photography. It’s the perfect time to witness the Mara’s raw beauty and vibrant life come alive at dusk. Later, dinner and overnight at Masai Mara Sopa Lodge 

Day 05 & 06 : 
Two Full Days of Wildebeest Tracking at Masai Mara National Reserve 

Spend two unforgettable days tracking the iconic herds during your wildebeest migration safari at the magnificent Masai Mara National Reserve. These full days in the wild allow you to immerse yourself in one of nature’s greatest spectacles as millions of wildebeests, zebras, and gazelles move across the sweeping plains in search of fresh grazing. Follow the herds from sunrise to sunset, witnessing dramatic river crossings at the Mara River, intense predator action, and breathtaking scenes of the vast savannah alive with movement. Your experienced guide will position you at prime viewing spots to observe the rhythm of the migration as it unfolds. Capture stunning photographs of roaming elephants, big cats on the hunt, and the endless flow of wildlife. This immersive experience is a highlight of any wildebeest migration safari, offering unmatched encounters and lifelong memories. Overnight at Masai Mara Sopa Lodge 

Day 07 : 
Masai Mara National Reserve - Nairobi - Departure 

After breakfast at your camp, you will check out and head for Masai Village Visit; Immerse yourself in the rich culture of the Masai people by visiting a traditional village. Learn about their customs, traditions, and way of life, witness their intricate beadwork and warrior dances, and perhaps even purchase a handcrafted souvenir to support their community. Afterwards, exit Masai Mara for Nairobi approximately 5 hours’ drive. Arriving late in the afternoon, upon arrival at the Hotel or Airport for the Outbound Flight. This marks the end of your iconic 7 days wildebeest migration safari.
