## Overview 
This 7 day Kenya Sopa lodges safari is a private road trip from Nairobi with 2 nights at Amboseli Sopa Lodge, 1 night at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge and 3 nights at Masai Mara Sopa Lodge. A Lake Naivasha boat ride is included. The best season is July to October, and the price starts from USD 2,595 per adult. It suits couples, families and first-time visitors. 
Every night is spent in the same Sopa lodge group, rated 4 stars on the page, on full board, and you travel in a 4WD Land Cruiser with an English or bilingual driver guide. 

## Why choose a 7 day Kenya Sopa lodges safari? 
The Sopa lodges circuit links three of Kenya’s best-known parks with one lodge brand, so standards, meals and service feel familiar from the first night to the last. Amboseli gives you elephant herds below Mount Kilimanjaro, Lake Nakuru adds rhinos and flamingos, and the Masai Mara is the stage for the wildebeest migration from July to October. 
- Migration seekers: two full days in the Masai Mara in the crossing season. 
- First-time visitors: Amboseli, Lake Naivasha, Lake Nakuru and the Mara in one week. 
- Comfort lovers: 4-star Sopa lodges with full board throughout. 

## What to expect on the 7 day Kenya Sopa lodges safari 
Day 1 starts with a meet-and-greet and briefing, then a drive of about 4 hours to Amboseli National Park, lunch and an evening game drive, with the night at Amboseli Sopa Lodge. Day 2 is a full day in Amboseli for elephants, views of Mount Kilimanjaro and the 360-degree view from Observation Hill, with a second night at Amboseli Sopa Lodge. 
On day 3 you drive about 5 hours to Lake Naivasha, stopping at a Great Rift Valley viewpoint, take a boat safari after lunch and continue to Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge. Day 4 is a drive of about 5 hours to the Masai Mara with an en-route game drive, then an afternoon game drive and the first of three nights at Masai Mara Sopa Lodge. 
Days 5 and 6 are full days tracking the herds in the Masai Mara National Reserve, including time at the Mara River. On day 7 you visit a Maasai village after breakfast and drive about 5 hours back to Nairobi for your hotel or flight. 

### When is the best time for this Sopa lodges migration safari? 
The best season for this safari is July to October, as shown on the trip page, when the migration herds are usually in the Masai Mara. River crossings are never guaranteed because the herds decide when to cross. See our best time to visit the Masai Mara guide and our Amboseli National Park guide to plan your dates. 

## What is included in the Sopa lodges circuit? 
The price includes a meet-and-greet and safari briefing, all transport in a 4WD Land Cruiser, 6 nights of accommodation, all park entry and conservation fees, full board meals, an English or bilingual driver guide, unlimited game drives on the itinerary, unlimited drinking water and a complimentary Lake Naivasha boat ride. 
Not included are the Masai Mara hot air balloon safari (USD 500 per person), travel insurance, visas, international flights, tips (about USD 10 per person per day is suggested), drinks and personal items. Our Kenya safari cost guide explains what drives the price.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Enjoy 7 Days Wildebeest Migration Safari Sopa Lodge Circuit immerses you in the heart of East Africa’s most dramatic wildlife movement. Explore legendary parks while enjoying the comfort of Sopa Lodges, perfectly positioned for incredible sightings. This Wildebeest Migration Safari promises unforgettable game drives, breathtaking landscapes, and close encounters with the migrating herds, creating a truly remarkable safari experience. 

Day 01 : 
Nairobi - Amboseli National Park 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by Divulge Adventures representative for meet, greet andyour wildebeest migration safari briefing. Afterward, you will depart Nairobi for Amboseli National Park, approximately 4 hours. drive. Arriving at around lunchtime for check-in and lunch, followed by afternoon siesta. 
Later in the evening set out for evening game drive at Amboseli National Park, and later return to the lodge for dinner and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 02 : 
Full Day at Amboseli National Park 

After breakfast, set out for a full-day game drive into the home of large herds of elephants and the Kilimanjaro royal court. The game drive takes you to Kenya's premium park, which is rich in wildlife, from up close free-ranging African Elephants to experiencing the epic view of the tallest mountain in Africa, Mt Kilimanjaro. 
You will also visit Observation hill, where you can view the entire park in 360 degrees from the top. Lunch will be served as you proceed with the game drive and later return for dinner, and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Amboseli National Park - Lake Naivasha - Lake Nakuru National Park 

After a steamy hot delicious breakfast depart Amboseli for Lake Naivasha, which is approximately 5 hours away. There will be a stop to view the great rift valley scenery as you proceed to Lake Naivasha. You will arrive in time for lunch. After lunch, proceed for boat safari at Lake Naivasha before heading to Nakuru for check in at the Hotel, dinner and overnight at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Lake Nakuru National Park - Masai Mara National Reserve 

After a steamy hot delicious breakfast set exit Lake Nakuru National Park with and enroute game drive to Masai Mara Reserve approximately 5 hours drive. Check-in at the camp by lunchtime. Later in the afternoon, head for an afternoon game drive. 
The cooling air stirs wildlife into action—lions begin to hunt, elephants gather near waterholes, and herds of wildebeest move across the plains. The sky transforms into shades of orange and purple, creating a breathtaking backdrop for photography. It’s the perfect time to witness the Mara’s raw beauty and vibrant life come alive at dusk. 
Later, dinner and overnight at Masai Mara Sopa Lodge 

Day 05 & 06 : 
Two Full Days of Wildebeest Tracking at Masai Mara National Reserve 

Spend two unforgettable days tracking the iconic herds during your wildebeest migration safari at the magnificent Masai Mara National Reserve. These full days in the wild allow you to immerse yourself in one of nature’s greatest spectacles as millions of wildebeests, zebras, and gazelles move across the sweeping plains in search of fresh grazing. 
Follow the herds from sunrise to sunset, witnessing dramatic river crossings at the Mara River, intense predator action, and breathtaking scenes of the vast savannah alive with movement. Your experienced guide will position you at prime viewing spots to observe the rhythm of the migration as it unfolds. 
Capture stunning photographs of roaming elephants, big cats on the hunt, and the endless flow of wildlife. This immersive experience is a highlight of any wildebeest migration safari, offering unmatched encounters and lifelong memories. Overnight at Masai Mara Sopa Lodge 

Day 07 : 
Masai Mara National Reserve - Nairobi - Departure 

After breakfast at your camp, you will check out and head for Masai Village Visit; Immerse yourself in the rich culture of the Masai people by visiting a traditional village. Learn about their customs, traditions, and way of life, witness their intricate beadwork and warrior dances, and perhaps even purchase a handcrafted souvenir to support their community. 
Afterwards, exit Masai Mara for Nairobi approximately 5 hours’ drive. Arriving late in the afternoon, upon arrival at the Hotel or Airport for the Outbound Flight. This marks the end of your iconic 7 days wildebeest migration safari.
