## Overview 
This 6 day Amboseli and Masai Mara safari is a private road safari from Nairobi that also visits Lake Naivasha and Lake Nakuru. You stay 2 nights at Amboseli Sopa Lodge, 1 night at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge and 2 nights at PrideInn Mara Camp, on full board. The best season is July to September, during the Great Migration, and the price is on request. 
It suits first-time visitors, couples and families who want Kenya’s classic parks in under a week, with a private 4×4 Land Cruiser and an English or bilingual driver guide. 

## Why choose a 6 day Amboseli and Masai Mara safari? 
Amboseli and the Masai Mara are Kenya’s two best-known parks, and they are very different. Amboseli is famous for big elephant herds with Mount Kilimanjaro behind them, while the Masai Mara has lions, cheetahs, leopards and, from July to September, the wildebeest and zebra herds of the Great Migration. 
The route between them passes the Great Rift Valley and two lakes, so you add a boat safari and a rhino park without extra travel days. 
- First-time visitors: four parks and lakes in six days. 
- Short holidays: back in Nairobi in time for an evening flight on day 6. 
- Families: a private vehicle and a pace that suits you. 

## What to expect on the 6 day Amboseli and Masai Mara safari 
Day 1 starts with a meet-and-greet in Nairobi and a drive of about 4 hours to Amboseli National Park, arriving for lunch and an afternoon game drive. Day 2 is a full day in Amboseli for elephants, views of Mount Kilimanjaro and Observation Hill, which looks out over the whole park. 
On day 3 you drive about 5 hours to Lake Naivasha with a Rift Valley viewpoint stop, take a boat safari and a nature walk on Crescent Island, then continue to Lake Nakuru. Day 4 begins with a morning game drive in Lake Nakuru National Park for rhinos, giraffes, lions and flamingos, then a drive of about 5 hours to the Masai Mara. 
Day 5 is a full day in the Masai Mara National Reserve with a picnic lunch on the plains, and an optional hot air balloon safari at sunrise. On day 6 you visit a Maasai village and drive about 5 hours back to Nairobi for your flight. 

### Is 6 days enough for Amboseli and the Masai Mara? 
Yes, 6 days is enough for a full day in each of Amboseli and the Masai Mara, plus the two lakes. If you want more time at the Mara River during the migration, our 7 day Masai Mara river crossing safari adds a third night in the Mara. ## Day by Day

This 6 Days Wildebeest Migration Safari offers an exhilarating journey through the iconic Maasai Mara, home to one of the world’s greatest wildlife spectacles. Experience dramatic river crossings, abundant predators, and breathtaking landscapes. This Safari delivers unmatched encounters with nature’s raw drama, ensuring every moment is filled with excitement, discovery, and unforgettable adventure. 

Day 01 : 
Nairobi to Amboseli National Park 

Upon arrival at the airport, you will be met by a Divulge Adventures representative for a meet, greet, and safari briefing. Afterward, you will depart Nairobi for Amboseli National Park, approximately 4 hours . drive. Arriving at around lunchtime for check-in and lunch, followed by an afternoon siesta. Later, set out for an afternoon game drive at Amboseli National Park . 
This will be your first safari experience, to your wildebeest migration safari in Kenya. Afterwards, return to the lodge for dinner and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 02 : 
Full Day at Amboseli national Park 

After breakfast, set out for a full-day game drive into the home of large herds of elephants and the Kilimanjaro royal court. The game drive takes you to Kenya's premium park, which is rich in wildlife, from up close free-ranging African Elephants to experiencing the epic view of the tallest mountain in Africa, Mt Kilimanjaro. 
You will also visit Observation Hill, where you can view the entire park in 360 degrees from the top. Lunch will be served as you proceed with the game drive and later return for dinner, and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge 

Day 03 : 
Amboseli National Park to Lake Nakuru National Park via Lake Naivasha 

After a steamy, hot, delicious breakfast, depart Amboseli for Lake Naivasha, which is approximately 5 hours away. There will be a stop to view the great rift valley scenery as you proceed to Lake Naivasha. You will arrive in time for lunch. 
After lunch, proceed for a boat safari at Lake Naivasha and a nature safari walk at Crescent Island before heading to Nakuru for check-in at the Hotel, dinner, and overnight at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge 

Day 04 : 
Lake Nakuru National Park to Masai Mara National Reserve 

After an early and steamy breakfast, set out for Lake Nakuru National Park morning game drive . As the sun rises, the park comes alive with the sights and sounds of wildlife. Visitors can spot diverse animals, including rhinos, giraffes, lions, and flamingos. 
The park's stunning landscapes, ranging from lush forests to expansive grasslands and the picturesque lake itself, create a serene backdrop for an adventurous evening exploring Kenya's rich biodiversity. Later in the afternoon, exit for Masai Mara Reserve, approximately a 5-hour drive, arriving late in the evening. Check in at camp/ lodge, followed by relaxing moments. No evening game drive. 
Dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp 

Day 05 : 
Full Day at Masai Mara National Reserve 

Enjoy a full day of game viewing at the iconic Maasai Mara National Reserve, giving you ample time to explore its incredible wildlife. Witness thrilling scenes of big cats hunting, elephants, buffaloes, zebras, giraffes, and hippos, while crocodiles ambush wildebeests crossing the Mara River. 
Capture elephants, lion prides, impalas, topi, gazelles, and a variety of bird species on your camera. Picnic lunch will be served amidst the vast plains, with an optional balloon safari offering a breathtaking aerial perspective. This unforgettable experience is a highlight of your Kenya Wildebeest Migration Safari, providing up-close encounters with the spectacle of the Kenya Wildebeest Migration. 
Dinner and overnight at Prideinn Mara Camp 

Day 06 : 
Masai Mara National Reserve to Nairobi (Departure) 

After breakfast at your camp, you will check out and head for the Maasai Village Visit ; Immerse yourself in the rich culture of the Masai people by visiting a traditional village. Learn about their customs, traditions, and way of life, witness their intricate beadwork and warrior dances, and perhaps even purchase a handcrafted souvenir to support their community. 
Afterwards, exit the Masai Mara for Nairobi, approximately a 5-hour drive. Upon arrival, drop off at the airport for your outbound flight.

When is the best time for a 6 day Amboseli and Masai Mara safari? 

The best time for a 6 day Amboseli and Masai Mara safari is July to September, when the Great Migration herds are usually in the Masai Mara and the weather is dry. Amboseli is good all year, and the clearest views of Mount Kilimanjaro are usually early in the morning. 

What is included in the 6 day Amboseli and Masai Mara safari? 

The safari includes a private 4WD Land Cruiser with a driver guide, 5 nights on full board, all park and conservation fees, unlimited game drives on the itinerary, drinking water, a Lake Naivasha boat ride and a Maasai village visit. Flights, visas, insurance, tips, drinks and the balloon safari are extra. 

Which lodges are used on this Amboseli and Masai Mara safari? 

This Amboseli and Masai Mara safari uses Amboseli Sopa Lodge for 2 nights, Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge for 1 night and PrideInn Mara Camp for 2 nights. If a lodge is full for your dates, we offer a similar lodge and confirm it in your quote. 

Is the 6 day Amboseli and Masai Mara safari good for families? 

Yes, the 6 day Amboseli and Masai Mara safari works well for families because you travel in a private vehicle and can set the pace. Some drives take about 5 hours, so tell us your children’s ages and we will plan stops and rooms around them.
