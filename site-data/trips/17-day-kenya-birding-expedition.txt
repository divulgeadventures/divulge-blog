## 
This Kenya birding expedition runs for 17 days and visits every major birding region in Kenya except the coast. It is ideal from October to December, when migrants from Europe and Asia arrive and the rains turn the land green. 
The route starts with Mount Kenya’s forests, moves into the dry north, crosses the Rift Valley, spends three nights in Kakamega and finishes with the Masai Mara and Lake Naivasha. 

## Why choose this Kenya birding expedition? 
- Thika and Mount Kenya: Hinde’s babbler, Hartlaub’s turaco and Ayres’s hawk-eagle. 
- Samburu and Buffalo Springs: Somali specials, sandgrouse and pygmy falcon. 
- Nakuru and Baringo: flamingos, pelicans, owls and Heuglin’s courser. 
- Kakamega: three nights in Kenya’s only rainforest. 
- Masai Mara: three nights for raptors, cranes, longclaws and big cats. 

## Where you stay 
Lodges include Nairobi Safari Club, Castle Forest Lodge, Samburu Lodge, Naro Moru River Lodge, Sarova Lion Hill, Soi Lodge, Rondo Retreat, Mara Serena Lodge, Mara Sentrim Camp and Lake Naivasha Country Club, or similar. 

## Best time for a Kenya birding expedition 
October to December is ideal for migrants and breeding colours. January to March is also excellent. In the dry season from June to September, birding is easier but fewer migrants are present. 
Prefer something shorter? Try our 5 day Kenya birding safari . Read the Masai Mara entrance fee guide and browse all birding safaris . Nature Kenya has more on Kenya’s key birding sites.

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Arrival in Nairobi 

Airport pick-up and transfer to Nairobi Safari Club. 

Day 2 : 
Thika to Mount Kenya 

Bird the Blue Post grounds, Chania River and Wajee Camp for Hinde's babbler and turacos, then continue to Castle Forest Lodge on Mount Kenya. 

Day 3 : 
Mount Kenya to Samburu 

Early birding at Castle Forest, then drive north to Samburu Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Samburu National Reserve 

A full day along the Ewaso Ng'iro River for bateleurs, hornbills, sandgrouse and Somali buntings. 

Day 5 : 
Buffalo Springs to Naro Moru 

Morning in Buffalo Springs for pygmy falcon and Somali ostrich, a stop in Meru Forest, then Naro Moru River Lodge. 

Day 6 : 
Mount Kenya to Lake Nakuru 

Climb towards the Met Station for Jackson's francolin, then drive to Lake Nakuru. Overnight at Sarova Lion Hill. 

Day 7 : 
Lake Nakuru to Lake Baringo 

Morning at Nakuru, then north to Soi Lodge at Lake Baringo. 

Day 8 : 
Lake Baringo 

A full day on the lake and cliffs for white-faced owl, nightjars and Jackson's hornbill. 

Day 9 : 
Kerio Valley to Kakamega 

Birding stops in the Kerio Valley on the way to Rondo Retreat. 

Day 10 : 
Kakamega Forest 

A full day of rainforest birding for great blue turaco and casqued hornbills. 

Day 11 : 
Kakamega Forest 

More forest trails for flufftails, trogons and bee-eaters, plus primates. 

Day 12 : 
Lake Victoria to the Masai Mara 

Morning in the papyrus near Kisumu, then a long drive to the Masai Mara. Overnight at Mara Serena Lodge. 

Day 13 : 
Masai Mara 

A full day of birding and game drives for secretary birds, crowned cranes and big cats. 

Day 14 : 
Mara River and Hippo Pools 

Cross the Mara River, visit Hippo Pools and bird the bushy hills. Overnight at Mara Sentrim Camp. 

Day 15 : 
Siana to Lake Naivasha 

Morning birding near Siana, then drive to Lake Naivasha Country Club. 

Day 16 : 
Lake Naivasha to Nairobi 

Boat trip on Lake Naivasha, then birding at Kinangop, Gatamaiyu and Limuru. Overnight at Nairobi Safari Club. 

Day 17 : 
Nairobi National Park and departure 

Morning birding and a search for black rhino in Nairobi National Park, then transfer to the airport.

How long is the Kenya birding expedition? 

It is 17 days and 16 nights, from arrival in Nairobi to departure from Nairobi. 

Which lodges are used? 

Nairobi Safari Club, Castle Forest Lodge, Samburu Lodge, Naro Moru River Lodge, Sarova Lion Hill, Soi Lodge, Rondo Retreat, Mara Serena Lodge, Mara Sentrim Camp and Lake Naivasha Country Club, or similar. 

How many species can I expect? 

Keen birders often record 500 species or more on this route. 

Is it suitable for photographers? 

Yes. The private vehicle with a pop-up roof works well for bird and wildlife photography. 

Can we add the coast? 

Yes. Arabuko-Sokoke Forest and Mida Creek can be added at the end with a short flight to Malindi. 

What is the best time for this expedition? 

October to December, when migrants arrive and the land is green. 

Are boat trips included? 

The Lake Naivasha boat trip is included on the last full day. Other boat rides are optional.
