## 
This Lamu Island holiday takes you to the most atmospheric corner of the Kenya coast. Lamu Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the oldest living Swahili town in East Africa, with narrow lanes, carved wooden doors and donkeys instead of cars. You fly from Nairobi to Manda airstrip, stay four nights in a traditional Swahili house in Lamu or Shela village, take a guided walk through the Old Town, sail at sunset on a dhow and visit the Takwa Ruins on Manda Island. It suits couples and travellers who want culture and quiet beaches after a safari. 
Price: from USD 1,040 per adult sharing (low season estimate). Contact us for your exact dates. 

### Highlights 
- Guided walk through UNESCO-listed Lamu Old Town 
- Sunset dhow sail and a trip to Manda Island and the Takwa Ruins 
- Long, empty Shela Beach 
- Short flights from Nairobi, easy to add after a safari 

## 
Expand all 

Day 1 : 
Fly to Lamu 

Flight from Nairobi to Manda airstrip and a short boat transfer to Lamu. Evening at leisure on the waterfront. Overnight in Lamu Old Town or Shela. 

Day 2 : 
Lamu Old Town 

Guided walk through the Old Town: the Lamu Fort, the Lamu Museum, the donkey sanctuary and the lanes of carved Swahili houses. Overnight in Lamu or Shela. 

Day 3 : 
Dhow sail and Manda Island 

Morning by dhow to Manda Island to see the Takwa Ruins, with a seafood lunch. Sail back at sunset. Overnight in Lamu or Shela. 

Day 4 : 
Shela Beach 

Free day on the dunes and sand of Shela Beach, or optional snorkelling, fishing or a visit to Pate Island. Overnight in Lamu or Shela. 

Day 5 : 
Fly to Nairobi 

Boat to Manda airstrip for the flight to Nairobi.

How much does this Lamu Island holiday cost? 

It starts from USD 1,040 per adult sharing, based on low-season rates. Boutique and luxury houses in Shela cost more; we confirm options and prices when you enquire. 

Is Lamu safe to visit? 

Lamu Old Town and Shela are calm and welcoming. Check your government’s travel advice before booking; we fly you in and out to avoid the mainland road. 

What should we wear in Lamu? 

Lamu is a conservative Muslim town, so cover shoulders and knees in town. Swimwear is fine on the beach.
