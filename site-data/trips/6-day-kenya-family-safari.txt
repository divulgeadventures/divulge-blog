## 
This 6 day Kenya family safari is built for parents, children and grandparents travelling together. You see elephants below Kilimanjaro in Amboseli, ride a boat on Lake Naivasha, look for rhinos at Lake Nakuru and spend a full day in the Masai Mara, with a private 4×4 and the same driver guide throughout. 

## Why is this 6 day Kenya family safari good for children? 
Every day has something different, so younger travellers don’t get bored of game drives. There is a midday rest in Amboseli, a walk among zebras and giraffes on Crescent Island, a boat ride past hippos, and a Maasai village visit where children can try the jumping dance. Lodges have pools and family rooms, and meals are on full board. 
Your guide sets the pace. If the youngest need a break, you can return to the lodge early and head out again in the afternoon. 

## What does the family safari itinerary include? 
You spend two nights in Amboseli, one night at Lake Nakuru and two nights in the Masai Mara. Day 3 stops at Lake Naivasha for the boat ride and Crescent Island walk, and the last day includes the Maasai village before the drive back to Nairobi. 

### Which lodges are used? 
Amboseli Sopa Lodge, Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge and PrideInn Mara Camp, or similar. Child rates and family rooms depend on each lodge and your dates, and we confirm them in your quote. 
For more ideas, read our guide to planning a safe family safari and our comparison of the Masai Mara vs Amboseli . 

## Who is the 6 day kenya family safari best for? 
The 6 day kenya family safari is best for families who want to share a safari, from young children to grandparents. Tell us everyone’s ages and we will match vehicles, rooms and activities to your family, so the 6 day kenya family safari runs at the right pace for your group. 

### How do group bookings work? 
For every six paying travellers, the seventh place is free. Each traveller can pay separately, and people can arrive on different flights. A deposit of 30 to 40% holds the booking and the balance is due 2 to 4 weeks before travel. Full payment and cancellation rules are in our terms and conditions . 

## Can several families share this 6 day Kenya family safari? 
Yes. Two or more families often travel together in separate vehicles and meet at meals, or share one vehicle when the children are close in age. Grandparents can join for part of the trip. Tell us your group size and the children’s ages and we will plan vehicles and rooms around them.

Day 1 : 
Nairobi to Amboseli National Park 

Your 6 day kenya family safari begins today. Meet your driver guide in Nairobi for a safety and safari briefing, then drive about 4 hours to Amboseli. Check in for lunch and a rest by the pool, then go out on an afternoon game drive to see elephant families in front of Kilimanjaro. Dinner and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge. 

Day 2 : 
Full day in Amboseli 

Spend the day exploring Amboseli's swamps and plains, where large elephant herds come to drink. Climb Observation Hill for a 360-degree view of the park, a favourite with children. Lunch is served along the way, and the pace allows a break at the lodge. Dinner and overnight at Amboseli Sopa Lodge. 

Day 3 : 
Amboseli to Lake Nakuru via Lake Naivasha 

Drive about 5 hours to Lake Naivasha, stopping at a Great Rift Valley viewpoint. After lunch, take a boat ride past hippos and fish eagles, then walk among zebras, giraffes and wildebeest on Crescent Island. Continue to Lake Nakuru for dinner and overnight at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge. 

Day 4 : 
Lake Nakuru to the Masai Mara 

Morning game drive in Lake Nakuru National Park to look for rhinos, giraffes, lions and flamingos along the shore. In the afternoon, drive about 5 hours to the Masai Mara and settle into camp. Dinner and overnight at PrideInn Mara Camp. 

Day 5 : 
Full day in the Masai Mara 

A full day of game drives in the Masai Mara, looking for lions, cheetahs, elephants, giraffes and, from July to October, the wildebeest migration. Lunch is a picnic on the plains. An early-morning balloon flight can be added for older children and adults. Dinner and overnight at PrideInn Mara Camp. 

Day 6 : 
Maasai village and return to Nairobi 

After breakfast, visit a Maasai village to learn about daily life, see the beadwork and join the jumping dance. Then drive about 5 hours back to Nairobi for your onward journey. This is the end of your 6 day kenya family safari.

Is the 6 day Kenya family safari suitable for young children? 

Yes. The 6 day Kenya family safari mixes game drives with a boat ride, a walking safari on Crescent Island and a Maasai village visit, and there are midday breaks at the lodges. Some lodges set minimum ages for walks or rooms, so tell us your children’s ages and we will plan around them. 

How long are the drives between parks? 

The longest drives are Amboseli to Lake Naivasha (about 5 hours) and Lake Nakuru to the Masai Mara (about 5 hours). We stop for breaks and the Rift Valley viewpoint, and you can bring games, snacks and headphones for the children. 

Do children get lower rates? 

Many lodges and parks offer child rates and family rooms, but the rules differ by lodge, age and season. We include the right child rates for your dates in your quote rather than showing a fixed price. 

Can two families travel together on this safari? 

Yes. Families can share one vehicle or use two vehicles that meet at the lodges. On group bookings the seventh place is free for every six paying travellers, and each family can pay separately. 

When is the best time for a family safari in Kenya? 

July to October brings the Great Migration to the Masai Mara and fits the long school holidays. December to February is also dry and warm. The green season from March to May is quieter and lodges often have lower rates.
