## Overview 
This 3 day Sossusvlei tour from Windhoek is a short guided escape into the Namib Desert, with 2 nights at Hammerstein Lodge or a similar desert lodge on dinner, bed and breakfast. A full day in Namib-Naukluft National Park covers Sossusvlei, Deadvlei and a dune climb such as Dune 45 or Big Daddy. The price starts from USD 1,800 per adult, and it suits couples, photographers and travellers short on time. 
The tour works as a stand-alone desert break or as an add-on to a longer Namibia itinerary, with return transfers from Windhoek, a professional English-speaking guide and park entrance fees included. 

## Why choose a 3 day Sossusvlei tour from Windhoek? 
Sossusvlei is a clay pan ringed by some of the highest sand dunes in the world, and neighbouring Deadvlei is famous for its ancient camelthorn trees standing on white clay. Both lie inside Namib-Naukluft National Park, south-west of Windhoek, and they are Namibia’s most photographed landscapes. 
Two nights near the dunes let you enter the park early, when the low light turns the sand orange and red, instead of rushing there and back in one long day. 
- Photographers: early morning light on the dunes and the white pan of Deadvlei. 
- Short stays: the full Sossusvlei experience in 3 days from Windhoek. 
- Add-on travellers: it can end in Windhoek or continue to your next Namibian destination. 

## What to expect on the 3 day Sossusvlei tour from Windhoek 
Day 1 starts with a meeting by your Divulge Adventures representative and a scenic drive south-west from Windhoek into the Namib Desert. You check in at Hammerstein Lodge or similar and relax. An optional afternoon safari at We Kebi Safari Lodge offers a guided nature drive on private desert land, looking for desert-adapted wildlife at sunset. Dinner and overnight at the lodge. 
Day 2 is an early start into Namib-Naukluft National Park. You explore Sossusvlei and walk across the white clay pan of Deadvlei, then climb a famous dune such as Dune 45 or Big Daddy for wide views. After time for walking and photography, you return to the lodge in the afternoon for dinner under the desert stars. 
Day 3 begins with breakfast and, depending on your departure time, a short nature walk around the lodge. After check-out you drive back to Windhoek or continue to your next stop in Namibia. 

### When is the best time for a Sossusvlei tour? 
Sossusvlei can be visited all year, and the trip page does not set a season. The cooler months, roughly May to September, make dune climbs more comfortable, while December to February is hot by midday, so early starts matter even more then.
