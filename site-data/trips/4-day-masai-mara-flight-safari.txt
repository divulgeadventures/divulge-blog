## 
This Masai Mara flight safari is our recommended length for a first visit to the Mara. Three nights let you explore the plains, the river and, in season, the migration crossing points without rushing. 
Bush lunches on both full days mean you can stay out with the animals when the action begins. 

## Why choose this Masai Mara flight safari? 
- Two full days: time to explore the Mara River, the plains and the Mara Triangle. 
- Bush lunches: picnics under an acacia tree in the middle of the reserve. 
- Migration season: the best chance of river crossings from July to October. 
- Optional extras: balloon flight or a Maasai cultural visit. 

## Where you stay 
Three nights at Ashnil Mara Camp, or similar, a tented camp on the Talek River. 

## Best time for a Masai Mara flight safari 
The Mara is good all year. July to October brings the wildebeest migration and river crossings. January to March is quieter, with green plains and many young animals.

Is four days enough for the Masai Mara? 

Yes. Three nights give you two full days, which is enough to explore the main areas and see most of the big animals. 

How much luggage can I bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. Ask us if you need extra space. 

Where do the flights leave from in Nairobi? 

From Wilson Airport, not Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. We arrange the transfer from your hotel or from JKIA. 

When are the river crossings? 

Usually between July and October, but they depend on the herds and the rain. Nothing is guaranteed. 

Why must we leave early on the last day? 

Park fees are charged per 24 hours, so we time the last morning to avoid an extra day’s fee. 

Is emergency evacuation cover available? 

Yes. We recommend Flying Doctors evacuation cover for every safari and can arrange it for you. 

Can I visit a Maasai village? 

Yes, a cultural visit can be added on one of the full days.
