## Overview 
Discover the beauty of East Africa on this extraordinary 5 Days Northern Tanzania Safari, designed to take you deep into Tanzania’s most celebrated safari destinations. Explore the vast plains of the Serengeti, descend into the wildlife-rich Ngorongoro Crater, and enjoy the untamed charm of Tarangire National Park, famous for its giant elephant herds and iconic baobab trees. Along the way, enjoy exciting game drives, spectacular scenery, and close-up encounters with Africa’s incredible wildlife in their natural habitat. Whether it’s watching predators roam the savannah or soaking in the peaceful atmosphere of the African wilderness, this safari delivers an unforgettable escape filled with adventure, discovery, and lasting memories.

## Day by Day 
Expand all 

Day 01 : 
Arusha- Lake Manyara National Park 

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport met by our representative then drive to Lake Manyara National Park checking in the lodge Manyara secret Lodge and have a lunch and drive across the floor of the Great Rift Valley to Lake Manyara National Park for a game drive. 
This beautiful park with its lush vegetation and tropical forests of giant fig and mahogany trees, takes its name from the Maasai word for "euphorbia tirucalli", a bush which is used to make a thorny hedge to protect their cattle. Elephant, giraffe, buffalo, zebra and a variety of antelope inhabit the park as well as a variety of monkeys and a large population of hippos. 

If you are lucky you may spot the Manyara lions, well known for their tree climbing habits, lazily resting on the branches of an "Acacia tortilis" tree during your Northern Tanzania safari. The birdlife is also abundant, with flamingoes, pelicans, hornbills and plenty of forest species. Later evening drive back to the lodge for dinner and overnight at Omega View Hotel Karatu 

Day 02 : 
Lake Manyara - Serengeti Via Ngorongoro Crater 

After breakfast drive to Ngorongoro conservation area then you will descend into the crater for a wonderful crater tour with a picnic lunch inside the Ngorongoro Crater often described as one of the wonders of the world a designated UNESCO World Heritage; It is the world’s largest intact volcanic caldera . 

It is one of the few places where the rare black rhino can be observed in their natural setting. As very few animals migrate in and out of the crater you can expect to see lions, elephants, zebras, hippos, flamingos, jackals, rhinos, antelopes, many birds and other species. With a bit of luck, the ‘Big Five’ can be spotted during one game drive only. Later afternoon proceed to Serengeti Dinner and overnight at Moyo Tented Camp Serengeti 

Day 03 : 
Serengeti Full Day -The Ultimate Northern Tanzania Safari 

Early morning game drive to see also nocturnal predators returning from hunting and animals waking up in early morning sunrise followed by full day Serengeti National Park tour putting you in a position to see lots of the animals. Serengeti in Maasai meaning the endless plain; 14763sq km in size is the home to many animals including Lions, Elephants, Giraffes, Wildebeests, Zebras, Leopards, Cheetahs, Warthogs, Hyenas plus a lots of bird life. The great migration is taking place around Serengeti and during that particular time millions of Wildebeests, Zebras, Thompson’s Gazelles, Grants Gazelle, Elands and Impalas are mingling together followed by predators. Dinner and overnight Moyo tented camp 

Day 04 : 
Serengeti Full Day - Northern Tanzania safari 

After breakfast another full day game drive in Serengeti National Park with picnic lunch. Dinner and overnight at Moyo Tented Camp Serengeti 

Day 05 : 
Central Serengeti -Zanzibar 

After breakfast at the lodge set off for a morning game drive having completed the Northern Tanzania Safari .After lunch proceed to Seronera airstrip for Zanzibar flight.
