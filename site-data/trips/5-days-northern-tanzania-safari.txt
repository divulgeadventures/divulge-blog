## Overview 
This 5 day Lake Manyara Ngorongoro and Serengeti safari is a 4-night northern Tanzania safari from Kilimanjaro International Airport. You game drive in Lake Manyara National Park, spend a night near Karatu, tour the Ngorongoro Crater and stay 3 nights at Moyo Tented Camp in the Serengeti before flying from Seronera to Zanzibar. The page gives no fixed season, so it runs all year. It costs from USD 2,195 per adult. 
It suits first-time visitors, couples and families who want the crater and the Serengeti in a short trip and plan to continue to the beach, travelling in a modified 4WD Land Cruiser with a photographic roof and an English-speaking guide. 

## Why choose a 5 day Lake Manyara Ngorongoro and Serengeti safari? 
Lake Manyara, the Ngorongoro Crater and the Serengeti give three very different safaris in five days. Lake Manyara has forests of giant fig and mahogany trees, elephants, giraffes, hippos, monkeys and plenty of birds, and is known for lions that rest in acacia trees. 
The Ngorongoro Crater is the world’s largest intact volcanic caldera and one of the few places to see the rare black rhino. The Serengeti then gets 3 nights in one camp, with two full days of game drives across the endless plains, so you see the classic savannah wildlife without rushing. 
- Short-on-time travellers: the crater and 3 Serengeti nights in 5 days. 
- Beach lovers: the trip ends with a flight from Seronera to Zanzibar. 
- Bird lovers: flamingos, pelicans and hornbills at Lake Manyara. 

## What to expect on the 5 day Lake Manyara Ngorongoro and Serengeti safari 
Day 1 starts with a meet-and-greet at Kilimanjaro International Airport and a drive across the Great Rift Valley to Lake Manyara National Park for lunch and a game drive. In the evening you drive back for dinner and the night at Omega View Hotel in Karatu. 
Day 2 is a crater tour of the Ngorongoro Crater with a picnic lunch on the crater floor, looking for black rhino, lions, elephants, hippos and flamingos. In the late afternoon you continue to the Serengeti for dinner and the night at Moyo Tented Camp. 
Days 3 and 4 are full days of game drives in Serengeti National Park, starting early to catch predators returning from the night’s hunt, with picnic lunches and both nights at Moyo Tented Camp. On day 5 you have a morning game drive in the central Serengeti, then lunch and a transfer to Seronera airstrip for your flight to Zanzibar. 

### Are 5 days enough for Lake Manyara, Ngorongoro and the Serengeti? 
Five days are enough for Lake Manyara, Ngorongoro and the Serengeti if you want a first taste of each, because this route avoids the long drive back from the Serengeti by flying out from Seronera. The crater gets one game drive and the Serengeti two full days. If you want Tarangire as well, the 6 day Tarangire Serengeti and Ngorongoro safari adds it. Our Serengeti vs Ngorongoro guide explains how to split your days.

## Day by Day

Day 01 : 
Arusha- Lake Manyara National Park 

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport met by our representative then drive to Lake Manyara National Park checking in the lodge Manyara secret Lodge and have a lunch and drive across the floor of the Great Rift Valley to Lake Manyara National Park for a game drive. 
This beautiful park with its lush vegetation and tropical forests of giant fig and mahogany trees, takes its name from the Maasai word for "euphorbia tirucalli", a bush which is used to make a thorny hedge to protect their cattle. 
Elephant, giraffe, buffalo, zebra and a variety of antelope inhabit the park as well as a variety of monkeys and a large population of hippos. 

If you are lucky you may spot the Manyara lions, well known for their tree climbing habits, lazily resting on the branches of an "Acacia tortilis" tree during your Northern Tanzania safari. 
The birdlife is also abundant, with flamingoes, pelicans, hornbills and plenty of forest species. Later evening drive back to the lodge for dinner and overnight at Omega View Hotel Karatu 

Day 02 : 
Lake Manyara - Serengeti Via Ngorongoro Crater 

After breakfast drive to Ngorongoro conservation area then you will descend into the crater for a wonderful crater tour with a picnic lunch inside the Ngorongoro Crater often described as one of the wonders of the world a designated UNESCO World Heritage; It is the world’s largest intact volcanic caldera . 

It is one of the few places where the rare black rhino can be observed in their natural setting. 
As very few animals migrate in and out of the crater you can expect to see lions, elephants, zebras, hippos, flamingos, jackals, rhinos, antelopes, many birds and other species. With a bit of luck, the ‘Big Five’ can be spotted during one game drive only. Later afternoon proceed to Serengeti Dinner and overnight at Moyo Tented Camp Serengeti 

Day 03 : 
Serengeti Full Day -The Ultimate Northern Tanzania Safari 

Early morning game drive to see also nocturnal predators returning from hunting and animals waking up in early morning sunrise followed by full day Serengeti National Park tour putting you in a position to see lots of the animals. 
Serengeti in Maasai meaning the endless plain; 14763sq km in size is the home to many animals including Lions, Elephants, Giraffes, Wildebeests, Zebras, Leopards, Cheetahs, Warthogs, Hyenas plus a lots of bird life. 
The great migration is taking place around Serengeti and during that particular time millions of Wildebeests, Zebras, Thompson’s Gazelles, Grants Gazelle, Elands and Impalas are mingling together followed by predators. Dinner and overnight Moyo tented camp 

Day 04 : 
Serengeti Full Day - Northern Tanzania safari 

After breakfast another full day game drive in Serengeti National Park with picnic lunch. Dinner and overnight at Moyo Tented Camp Serengeti 

Day 05 : 
Central Serengeti -Zanzibar 

After breakfast at the lodge set off for a morning game drive having completed the Northern Tanzania Safari .After lunch proceed to Seronera airstrip for Zanzibar flight.
