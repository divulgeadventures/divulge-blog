## Overview 
This 7 day Uganda gorilla and chimpanzee safari is a private road trip from Entebbe with 2 nights in Kibale, 1 night in Queen Elizabeth National Park and 2 nights in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, including a chimpanzee trek, a Kazinga Channel cruise and a gorilla trek. The drier months, usually June to September and December to February, are best, and the price starts from USD 4,537 per adult. 
It suits first-time visitors to Uganda, couples and wildlife photographers who want both great apes and a savannah game drive in one week, travelling in an extended Land Cruiser with a pop-up roof on full board. 

## Why choose a 7 day Uganda gorilla and chimpanzee safari? 
Uganda is one of the few countries where you can track wild chimpanzees and mountain gorillas on the same trip. Kibale National Park is known for its habituated chimpanzee communities, and Bwindi Impenetrable National Park protects close to half of the world’s mountain gorillas. 
Between the two forests, Queen Elizabeth National Park adds a classic savannah safari with elephants, buffaloes, Uganda kobs and lions, plus hippos and crocodiles on the Kazinga Channel, so the week is a full primate and wildlife safari rather than a single trek. 
- Primate lovers: a chimpanzee trek in Kibale and a gorilla trek in Bwindi. 
- First-time visitors: three of Uganda’s best-known parks in one week. 
- Photographers: a pop-up roof vehicle and a boat cruise on the Kazinga Channel. 

## What to expect on the 7 day Uganda gorilla and chimpanzee safari 
Day 1 starts with a welcome at Entebbe International Airport and a transfer of 10 to 60 minutes to your hotel in Entebbe or Kampala, with the night at Hilton Gardens Hotel. On day 2 you travel west to Kibale National Park for your first chimpanzee activity and an afternoon at Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary, staying at Turaco TreeTops Lodge. 
Day 3 is a full day in Kibale for chimpanzee trekking with ranger guides and the community-run Bigodi nature and cultural walk, with a second night at Turaco TreeTops Lodge. On day 4 you drive about 2 to 3 hours past crater lakes to Queen Elizabeth National Park for an afternoon boat cruise on the Kazinga Channel, staying at Ihamba Safari Lodge. 
Day 5 begins with a morning game drive of 3 to 4 hours in Queen Elizabeth, then a drive of about 4 to 5 hours to Buhoma in Bwindi, staying at The Haven Lodge Buhoma. Day 6 is gorilla trekking day: the trek takes 2 to 6 hours, you spend one hour with a habituated gorilla family, and in the afternoon you visit the Batwa community, with the night at Gorilla Safari Lodge. On day 7 you drive about 8 to 9 hours back to Kampala or Entebbe, stopping at the Uganda Equator. 

### How fit do you need to be for this Uganda gorilla and chimpanzee safari? 
Gorilla trekking in Bwindi needs a reasonable level of fitness, because the trek can take 2 to 6 hours on steep, muddy forest trails. Chimpanzee trekking in Kibale is usually easier, on flatter ground. Porters can be hired at the trailhead, and our how hard is gorilla trekking guide explains what to expect and how to prepare.

Day 01 : 
Arrival at Uganda Pearl of Africa 

Upon arrival at Entebbe International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by a professional driver-guide who will brief you about your safari. You will then be transferred to your hotel in either Entebbe or Kampala, a journey that takes approximately 10 to 60 minutes depending on the location. 
Depending on your arrival time, you may choose to relax and recover from your journey or enjoy an optional evening city experience. Later, you will have a welcome dinner featuring a selection of both local and international cuisine as you settle into the Pearl of Africa. Overnight stay will be at Hilton Gardens Hotel. 

Day 02 : 
Entebbe - Kibale National Park 

Following an early breakfast, you will start your primate and wildlife safari with a transfer to the park headquarters in Kibale National Park for a detailed briefing by experienced ranger guides. You will then head into the dense tropical rainforest in search of chimpanzees, guided by trackers who are familiar with their movements. 
The trek can vary in duration, but once the chimpanzees are located, you will spend one unforgettable hour observing their behavior, including feeding, grooming, and social interactions. 
After the trek, you will return to your lodge for lunch and some relaxation. In the afternoon, you will visit the Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary for a guided nature and cultural walk. 
This community-run initiative offers opportunities to spot monkeys, birds, and other wetland wildlife while also experiencing local traditions such as coffee preparation, banana brewing, basket weaving, and learning about herbal medicine. You will later return to your lodge for dinner and overnight stay at Turaco TreeTops Lodge . 

Day 03 : 
Kibale National Park-Chimpanzee Trekking and Bigodi Community Experience 

After an early breakfast, you will transfer to the park headquarters in Kibale National Park for a detailed briefing on the primate and wildlife safari by experienced ranger guides. You will then head into the dense tropical rainforest in search of chimpanzees, guided by trackers who are familiar with their movements. 
The trek can vary in duration, but once the chimpanzees are located, you will spend one unforgettable hour observing their behavior, including feeding, grooming, and social interactions. 
After the trek, you will return to your lodge for lunch and some relaxation. In the afternoon, you will visit the Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary for a guided nature and cultural walk. 
This community-run initiative offers opportunities to spot monkeys, birds, and other wetland wildlife while also experiencing local traditions such as coffee preparation, banana brewing, basket weaving, and learning about herbal medicine. You will later return to your lodge for dinner and overnight stay at Turaco TreeTops Lodge. 

Day 04 : 
Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park and Kazinga Channel Cruise 

After breakfast, you will depart for Queen Elizabeth National Park for the primate and wildlife safari a journey of approximately two to three hours. The drive takes you through scenic crater lake regions and offers more stunning views of the Rwenzori Mountains. 
Upon arrival, you will check into your lodge and enjoy lunch before heading out for an afternoon boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel. This natural waterway connects Lake Edward and Lake George and is famous for its high concentration of wildlife. During the cruise, you can expect to see elephants, buffaloes, hippos, crocodiles, and a wide variety of bird species along the shores. 
After the cruise, you will return to your lodge for dinner and relaxation at Ihamba Safari Lodge 

Day 05 : 
Morning Game Drive and Transfer to Bwindi 

You will start the day early with breakfast before heading out for primate and wildlife safari ,morning game drive in Queen Elizabeth National Park when wildlife is most active. As you explore the savannah plains, you may encounter lions, leopards, elephants, buffaloes, Uganda kobs, hyenas, and other wildlife in their natural habitat. 
The game drive typically lasts three to four hours, after which you will return to the lodge, check out, and begin your journey to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. 
This drive takes approximately four to five hours and passes through beautiful rolling hills and terraced landscapes. You will arrive at your lodge in the evening, where you can relax and prepare for the next day’s gorilla trekking adventure. Dinner and overnight at your lodge at The Haven Lodge Buhoma. 

Day 06 : 
Buhoma- Gorilla Trekking and Batwa Cultural Experience 

After an early breakfast, you will head to the park headquarters in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park for a comprehensive briefing on the primate and wildlife safari before beginning your gorilla trek. Accompanied by experienced guides, you will hike through the dense forest in search of a habituated gorilla family, a journey that can take between two to six hours depending on their location. 
Once the gorillas are found, you will spend one magical hour observing them as they feed, groom, and interact within their group. After this extraordinary experience, you will return to the lodge for lunch and some rest. In the afternoon, you will visit the Batwa People to learn about their traditional forest lifestyle through storytelling, music, dance, and demonstrations of ancient survival skills. Dinner and overnight at Gorilla Safari Lodge 

Day 07 : 
Return to Kampala / Entebbe & Departure 

After breakfast, you will begin your return journey to Kampala or Entebbe, which takes approximately eight to nine hours. Along the way, you will make a stop at the Uganda Equator Line where you can take memorable photos while standing in both hemispheres, shop for souvenirs, and enjoy lunch 
. You will then proceed to your final destination, either your hotel or the airport, marking the end of your unforgettable primate and widlife safari through Uganda’s rich landscapes, wildlife, and cultural heritage.

When is the best time for the 7 day Uganda gorilla and chimpanzee safari? 

The best time for this 7 day Uganda gorilla and chimpanzee safari is usually the drier months, June to September and December to February, when forest trails in Kibale and Bwindi are less muddy. Gorillas and chimpanzees can be tracked all year, so the trip runs in every month, but rain is more likely in April, May, October and November.
