## 
This luxury Kenya fly-in safari is for travellers who want the best of Kenya in comfort. You fly between Samburu, the Masai Mara and Amboseli, and stay at small, beautifully designed lodges with excellent guiding. 
Expect private game drives, fine food, spacious rooms with views and time to relax between activities. 

## Why choose this luxury Kenya fly-in safari? 
- Boutique lodges: small camps with only a handful of rooms. 
- Three parks: the dry north, the Mara plains and Kilimanjaro’s elephants. 
- Private guiding: your own vehicle and guide on request. 
- Seamless travel: every flight and transfer arranged for you. 

## Where you stay 
Sasaab in Samburu, Angama Mara above the Mara Triangle and Tortilis Camp in Amboseli, or similar luxury lodges. 

## Best time for a luxury Kenya fly-in safari 
June to October is dry and excellent for wildlife, with the migration in the Mara from July. January to March is also very good and quieter. 
See our Kenya safaris and all flight packages , or add a beach stay after the safari. For travel information, visit Magical Kenya . 

## How much does the luxury Kenya fly-in safari cost? 

Price on request. The cost depends on your travel dates, group size, the season and the lodges you choose, so we prepare every quote personally rather than showing one fixed price.

Which lodges are used on the luxury Kenya fly-in safari? 

Sasaab in Samburu, Angama Mara in the Masai Mara and Tortilis Camp in Amboseli, or similar luxury lodges. 

How much luggage can I bring? 

Light aircraft usually allow 15 kg per person, including hand luggage, in soft bags without wheels. Ask us if you need extra space. 

Where do the flights leave from in Nairobi? 

From Wilson Airport, not Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. We arrange the transfer from your hotel or from JKIA. 

Are drinks included? 

Most luxury lodges include house drinks. Premium wines and spirits are usually extra. 

Can I have a private vehicle? 

Yes. Private vehicles and guides can be arranged at each lodge. 

Can I end on the beach? 

Yes. You can fly from Amboseli or Nairobi to Diani, Lamu or Zanzibar. 

Is emergency evacuation cover available? 

Yes. We recommend Flying Doctors evacuation cover for every safari and can arrange it for you.
