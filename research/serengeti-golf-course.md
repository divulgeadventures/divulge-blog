## Confirmed facts (reported by The Citizen / Mwananchi and Daily News, Oct 2026; Daily News June 2026)
- Name: Serengeti Golf Course (also called Serengeti National Park Golf Course), at Fort Ikoma, Serengeti District, Mara region.
- Built and run by Tanzania National Parks (TANAPA). Located in the Fort Ikoma area just OUTSIDE the national park boundary (a wildlife area bordering the park), not inside the protected area.
- 18 holes, par 72, about 7,097 m long, on 450 acres. Cost about TSh 8.5 billion.
- Facilities reported: playing and practice areas, a players' service building, access roads, power and water, irrigation and water reservoirs, about 4,000 indigenous trees planted. No clubhouse described.
- Construction supervised by engineer Enock Magile, who said it meets standards recognised by international golf bodies.
- Launched 18 Oct 2026 by the Minister for Natural Resources and Tourism, Dr Ashatu Kijaji, who played the opening shot. A launch tournament had 81 golfers from Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda and Malawi.
- TANAPA Conservation Commissioner Massana Mwishawa: the course is meant to diversify tourism and give visitors more reasons to extend their stay in the Serengeti. Part of Tanzania's goal of 8 million tourists a year by 2030.
- Golfers who played it (June 2026 report): forgiving layout with shallow bunkers, but thick savannah rough punishes wayward shots; greens smooth and true; fairways still maturing. Elephants, giraffes, zebras, antelopes, wildebeest and buffalo seen on and around the course; wildlife has right of way and players must not disturb animals. Lions, hyenas and elephants heard at night from the nearby lodge.
- Nearby stay named in reports: Ikoma Tented Lodge/Camp. Check other lodges near Fort Ikoma gate (Ikoma Wild Camp, Ikoma Bush Camp etc.) on their own official websites before naming them.
