## Facts found
- Twin Rivers Resort, Tigoni (Limuru highlands, Kiambu County, off Limuru Road), among tea farms where the Tigoni and Wakam rivers meet. Waterfall and river area, lawns, nature trails. (tigoni.ke guide, Aug 2026; tigoni.life listing)
- Activities: quad biking (ATV trails), ziplining, sky cycling (air bike on a suspended track), high ropes course, giant swing, climbing wall, archery, paintball, nature walks, picnics, camping, kids' playground and family activities. Adventure activities have age, height and safety requirements.
- Entry: KES 500 adults, KES 300 children, as published by the tigoni.ke guide (Aug 2026, "currently published", unconfirmed by the resort). Individual activities are charged separately; no prices published.
- Team building: whole programme on site, e.g. icebreakers and facilitated games on the lawns, then high ropes, ziplining, quad biking, archery, paintball or sky cycling, followed by lunch. Typical day 9:00 a.m. to about 5:00 p.m.
