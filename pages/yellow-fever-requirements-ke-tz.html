Do I Need a Yellow Fever Vaccine for Kenya?

Kenya requires a yellow fever certificate only from travellers aged 1 year or over who arrive from a country with yellow fever risk. If you fly to Nairobi directly from the UK, the USA, Canada, Europe or Asia, you do not need to show one on arrival.

Not required, however, is not the same as not recommended. Health authorities advise the vaccine for travellers aged 9 months and over who are visiting Kenya's main safari areas, because yellow fever is present in parts of the country. That advice covers most safari itineraries, including the Masai Mara, Tsavo East, Tsavo West and Lake Nakuru.

Is Kenya a Yellow Fever Risk Country?

Yes. Kenya appears on the World Health Organization list of countries with risk of yellow fever transmission, and Tanzania's Ministry of Health lists Kenya among the endemic countries whose travellers must show a certificate. The risk is not the same everywhere in Kenya: it is considered low in the cities of Nairobi and Mombasa, parts of the coast and the former North Eastern Province.

Is the Yellow Fever Vaccine Required for Kenya If I Fly from the USA or UK?

No certificate is required for entry if you fly directly from the USA or UK, because neither is a yellow fever risk country. Check your connection, though. A stopover of 12 hours or more in a risk country can trigger the requirement on later legs of your trip, and Tanzania applies this rule strictly.

Who Should Get the Yellow Fever Vaccine for Kenya?

The yellow fever vaccine for Kenya is recommended for most safari travellers to Kenya aged 9 months or over, and it is required for anyone combining Kenya with Tanzania. It is generally not recommended if your whole trip stays in low-risk areas.

  • Recommended: travellers visiting Kenya's safari areas, including the Masai Mara, Tsavo East, Tsavo West and Lake Nakuru.
  • Generally not recommended: trips limited to the cities of Nairobi and Mombasa, some coastal counties and the former North Eastern Province.
  • Travellers aged 60 and over: the vaccine carries a higher risk of side effects at this age, so health advice is not to have it for low-risk trips. Discuss it with your travel clinic if you are going on safari.
  • Babies under 9 months, pregnant women and people with weakened immunity: usually advised not to have it. A doctor can issue a medical exemption letter instead.

How Long Is a Yellow Fever Certificate Valid?

A yellow fever certificate is valid for life. One dose gives lifelong protection, so you do not need a booster for travel, and an older certificate still counts. The certificate becomes valid 10 days after your vaccination.

What Happens If I Arrive in Tanzania Without a Certificate?

Tanzania's Ministry of Health says travellers who need a certificate but arrive without one may be refused entry, or vaccinated on arrival for a fee and kept under public health observation for up to 10 days. Either outcome can wreck a safari schedule, so sort the certificate out before you fly.

Insider Tips: Your Yellow Fever Checklist

  • Book early: get your yellow fever vaccine for Kenya at least 10 days before you land; some travel clinics need a few weeks' notice.
  • Carry the original: keep the yellow ICVP card in your hand luggage with your passport, not in checked bags.
  • Photograph it: keep a photo on your phone as a backup in case the card is lost.
  • Check every leg: look at each flight, layover and border crossing, not just your first arrival.
  • Exemption letters: if you cannot have the vaccine, carry a signed medical exemption letter from your doctor.

Where Can I Get the Yellow Fever Vaccine for Kenya?

Get your yellow fever vaccine for Kenya at a registered yellow fever vaccination centre in your home country before you travel. Only these centres can issue the International Certificate of Vaccination or Prophylaxis (ICVP), the yellow card that border officials accept.

If you are already in Kenya, Kenya Port Health offers yellow fever vaccination at selected points of entry and designated facilities, and recommends doing it at least 10 days before onward travel. Ask us and we will point you to the nearest centre on your route.

Do I need a yellow fever vaccine for Kenya?

Is Kenya a yellow fever risk country?

The risk is considered low in Nairobi, Mombasa, parts of the coast and the former North Eastern Province, and higher in many safari areas, which is why the vaccine is recommended for most safari itineraries.

Do I need a yellow fever certificate to go from Kenya to Tanzania?

Yes. Tanzania requires a yellow fever certificate from travellers arriving from endemic countries, and Kenya is on that list. No. A traveller flying from the USA to Kenya does not need a yellow fever certificate to enter, because the USA is not a yellow fever risk country. How long is the yellow fever certificate valid for?

The yellow fever certificate is valid for life. A single dose of the vaccine gives lifelong protection, so no booster is needed for travel and older certificates remain accepted. The certificate only becomes valid 10 days after you are vaccinated, so plan the jab at least 10 days before you arrive in East Africa.

Final Thoughts

A yellow fever vaccine for Kenya is not required if you fly in directly from the UK, USA or Europe, but it is recommended for safari areas and becomes essential the moment your trip includes Tanzania or Zanzibar. Get vaccinated at least 10 days before travel, and carry the original certificate with your passport.
