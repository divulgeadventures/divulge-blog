# Field notes from the owner (Erick, Divulge Adventures)

First-hand, on-the-ground notes that make posts original: what guides saw this week,
road and park conditions, what clients loved or found hard, lodge observations, prices
seen on the ground, tips only a Nairobi operator would know.

Rules for writers (daily blog task and market agents):
- Before writing, look for notes that match the post's place, topic or season.
- When one fits, use it in your own words (attributed to "our guides" or "our team",
  never invented beyond what the note says) and list it in the post's
  "field_notes_used" field.
- Never invent field notes, quotes or client stories. Only use what is written here.
- Notes older than 12 months: only use them if they are clearly still true.

Format: one bullet per note, newest first.
`- YYYY-MM-DD | place/topic | the note`
